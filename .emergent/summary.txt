1.  **Vehicle & Customer Management:** Intake, status tracking, quick actions (e.g., approval requests), public tracking, customer database, receipts.
2.  **Financial Management:** Multi-account budgeting, detailed analytics (day/week/month/branch), import/export, accounting features (Sales, Expenses by category, Chart of Accounts), payroll.
3.  **AI Integration:** CEO bot for financial analysis, RAG search, extensive knowledge base (PDF, DOCX, Excel, video), vehicle comparison, intelligent DTC card extraction (causes/fixes). Integration of local LlamaIndex, and optional (with fallback) Brave Search API, You.com, and Perplexity AI for enhanced search/analysis.
4.  **User Experience:** Responsive design, multi-language support (Arabic, English, Hindi), dynamic sidebar, GoDaddy-inspired theme.
5.  **Authentication & Permissions:** Name-only login, granular three-tier user permissions (RBAC).
6.  **Approval Workflow:** Three-tier approval (technician -> manager -> client) with image attachments and real-time updates (SSE).
7.  **Invoicing & Templating:** Excel-based invoicing system, Invoice Template Studio for multi-format import, in-app editing, dynamic template saving, and generation (PDF/XLSX). Support for A4 designer.
