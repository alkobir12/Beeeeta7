<analysis>
The trajectory details the evolution of a Workshop Management System, initially built by an AI engineer, into a feature-rich application. The work began with integrating CEO analytics into the Operations page, then progressed to developing a comprehensive approval workflow with real-time updates (SSE) and customer logging. A major shift involved overhauling the invoice and templating system from HTML to a robust Excel-based approach, complete with a dedicated Invoice Template Studio for multi-format imports and in-app editing. Critical issues like frontend parsing errors, missing backend API routes, and language localization in menus were systematically addressed. The system was enhanced with accounting features, including a Chart of Accounts and categorized transactions. Performance for smart search and seamless WhatsApp integration were also identified as key improvement areas. The current state reflects a functional, albeit complex, application with a strong foundation for further development, facing an immediate need for comprehensive automated testing and significant UI/UX improvements to the Knowledge and References sections, along with deep-seated performance optimizations for AI features.
</analysis>

<product_requirements>
The Workshop Management System digitalizes auto repair, featuring vehicle management (intake, status, quick actions, public tracking, dynamic printing with editable templates). It includes customer and financial management (database, receipts, multi-account budgeting, detailed analytics by day/week/month/branch, import/export). Core AI integration offers a CEO bot for financial analysis, RAG search, a knowledge base (PDF, DOCX, Excel, video), vehicle comparison, and intelligent DTC card extraction with causes/fixes. UX prioritizes responsive design, multi-language support (Arabic, English, Hindi), and a dynamic sidebar. Authentication uses name-only login with granular three-tier user permissions and a new three-tier approval workflow (technician->manager->client) with image attachments. A GoDaddy-inspired theme was implemented. Recent additions include a full Excel-based invoicing system, detailed accounting (Sales, Operating/Personal Expenses, Electricity/Water/Fuel categories), and a Chart of Accounts.
</product_requirements>
