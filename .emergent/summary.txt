-   **AI Integration**: Features a floating AI Helper for problem-solving/text generation, a CEO bot for multi-account financial analysis, and future AI-driven Excel/CSV data analysis.
-   **Reporting & Printing**: A4 printable templates for various documents (invoice, diagnosis, quote, purchase order, vendor bill, receipt), print/export functionality, and a future template store.
-   **User Experience & Scalability**: Responsive design, multi-language support (Arabic/English), dynamic navigation controllable from settings, and database indexing/pagination for performance.
-   **User Authentication & Authorization**: WhatsApp OTP login, user management with roles and customizable permissions, and customizable message templates for WhatsApp notifications.
