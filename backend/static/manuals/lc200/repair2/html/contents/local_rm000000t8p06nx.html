DTC    P0560    System Voltage


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

INSPECT FUSE (EFI MAIN)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (ENGINE ROOM RELAY BLOCK - ECM, BATTERY)

INSPECT BATTERY

CHECK BATTERY TERMINAL

CHECK WHETHER DTC OUTPUT RECURS

REPLACE ECM

CHECK FOR SHORTS IN ALL HARNESSES AND CONNECTORS CONNECTED TO FUSE AND REPLACE FUSE

REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR

REPLACE BATTERY

REPAIR OR REPLACE BATTERY TERMINAL

CONFIRM WHETHER MALFUNCTION HAS BEEN SUCCESSFULLY REPAIRED
