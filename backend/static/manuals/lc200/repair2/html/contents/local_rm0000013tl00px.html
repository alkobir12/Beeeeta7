DTC    P1613    Secondary Air Injection Driver Malfunction


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

CHECK AIR INJECTION CONTROL DRIVER

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (AIR INJECTION CONTROL DRIVER - ECM)

CHECK ECM (AIDI VOLTAGE)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (AIR INJECTION CONTROL DRIVER - EFI NO. 2 FUSE)

CHECK HARNESS OR CONNECTOR (BATTERY - A/PUMP FUSE)

REPLACE AIR INJECTION CONTROL DRIVER

CHECK WHETHER DTC OUTPUT RECURS (DTC P1613)
