DTC    U1002 (CAN MS BUS)    Lost Communication with Gateway Module (Main body ECU)


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CHECK CAN BUS WIRE (MAIN WIRE FOR DISCONNECTION, BUS LINE FOR SHORT CIRCUIT)

CHECK FOR OPEN IN MAIN BUS WIRE (NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR - MAIN BODY ECU)

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR - COMBINATION METER)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 8 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - MAIN BODY ECU)

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 8 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (MAIN BODY ECU - NO. 8 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - COMBINATION METER)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (COMBINATION METER - NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS MAIN WIRE (NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR SIDE)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - COMBINATION METER)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 10 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR SIDE)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - POSITION CONTROL AND SWITCH)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - MULTIPLEX TILT AND TELESCOPIC ECU)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - OUTER MIRROR CONTROL ECU)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - CERTIFICATION ECU)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 9 JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR SIDE)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 8 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - NO. 2 CAN JUNCTION CONNECTOR)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 8 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - WINDSHIELD WIPER ECU)

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (NO. 8 JUNCTION CONNECTOR - MAIN BODY ECU)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (COMBINATION METER)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (POSITION CONTROL ECU AND SWITCH)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (MULTIPLEX TILT AND TELESCOPIC ECU)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (OUTER MIRROR CONTROL ECU)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (CERTIFICATION ECU)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (WINDSHIELD WIPER ECU)

CONNECT CONNECTOR

CHECK FOR SHORT IN CAN BUS WIRES (MAIN BODY ECU)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (MAIN BODY ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND)
