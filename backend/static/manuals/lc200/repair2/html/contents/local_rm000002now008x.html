DTC    P1251    Turbocharger / Supercharger Overboost Condition (Too High)


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

CHECK ANY OTHER DTCS OUTPUT (IN ADDITION TO DTC P1251)

INSPECT TURBOCHARGER SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 1 and/or Bank 2)

CHECK WHETHER DTC OUTPUT RECURS (DTC P1251)

INSPECT TURBO MOTOR DRIVER (POWER SOURCE CIRCUIT)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (TURBO MOTOR DRIVER - ECM)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (DC MOTOR - TURBO MOTOR DRIVER)

REPLACE TURBO MOTOR DRIVER (for Bank 1 and/or Bank 2)

CHECK WHETHER DTC OUTPUT RECURS (DTC P1251)

REPLACE TURBOCHARGER SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 1 and/or Bank 2) (DC MOTOR)

REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR

REPLACE ECM

CONFIRM WHETHER MALFUNCTION HAS BEEN SUCCESSFULLY REPAIRED
