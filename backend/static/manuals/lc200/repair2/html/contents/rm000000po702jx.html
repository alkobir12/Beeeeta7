INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK NAVIGATION SETTINGS



  1. Enter the "Menu" screen by pressing the "MENU" switch.

  1. Select "Set up".




  1. Check that "Voice Recognition Guidance" is not OFF.

    OK:
    Voice Recognition Guidance is not OFF.



NG
TURN VOICE RECOGNITION GUIDANCE "ON"
OK


2.CHECK MAP DISC



  1. Check that the map disc is not deformed or cracked.

    OK:
    No deformations or cracks on map disc.



NG
REPLACE MAP DISC
OK


3.CHECK MAP DISC



  1. Check for dirt on the map disc surface.

    OK:
    No dirt is on the map disc surface.

    HINT:
    If the disc is dirty, clean the disc by wiping the disc surface radially with a soft cloth.

    NOTICE:
    Do not use a conventional record cleaner or antistatic preservative.



NG
CLEAN MAP DISC
OK


4.CHECK MICROPHONE (NAVIGATION CHECK MODE)



  1. Enter the "Mic & Voice Recognition Check" mode (Click here).

  1. When a voice is input into the microphone, check that the microphone input level meter changes according to the input voice.

  1. Push the recording switch and perform voice recording.

    HINT:
    The recording limit is 5 seconds.

  1. Check that the recording indicator remains on while recording and that the recorded voice is played normally without noise or distortion.

    OK:
    All check results are normal.



NG
GO TO "MICROPHONE CIRCUIT BETWEEN OVERHEAD J/B AND MULTI-DISPLAY" (Click here)
OK

END
