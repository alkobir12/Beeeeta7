1.PRECAUTIONS WHEN USING THE INTELLIGENT TESTER

  1. When using the intelligent tester with the engine switch off to troubleshoot: Connect the intelligent tester to the vehicle, and turn a courtesy light switch on and off at 1.5 second intervals until communication between the intelligent tester and vehicle begins.

  2. After all DTCs are cleared, check if the trouble occurs again 6 seconds after turning the engine switch on (IG).


2.PRECAUTIONS FOR EACH FUNCTION

  1. Precautions for the key:

    1. The key is a precision instrument. Be sure to observe the following:

    1. Do not drop or strike the key.

    2. Do not keep the key in a high temperature area for a long time.

    3. Do not use an ultrasonic washing machine to clean the key.

    4. Keep the key away from magnets or magnetized items during use.

    5. Do not attach any stickers to the key.

  2. Precautions for the engine start function:

    1. Before starting the engine, firmly depress the brake pedal until the indicator of the engine switch illuminates in green.

    2. Be sure to turn the engine switch off before disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal. When the battery cable is disconnected, the power source mode (off, on (ACC), on (IG)) is memorized. When battery depletion causes the vehicle power to be cut, be careful to remember the power source mode.

    3. After the battery is removed and reinstalled, be sure to wait 10 seconds or more before the next engine start. The engine may not start immediately after the battery is reinstalled.

    4. When the key battery is depleted, affected by wave noise, etc. and it is held close to the engine switch to start the engine, several warnings may sound. However, the warnings most likely sound because the vehicle cannot determine if the key is in the vehicle or not, and not because there is an actual malfunction. The following warnings may sound:

      1. Driver side door open → closed:
        1. Warning to inform that the shift lever is in a position other than P, and the power source mode is not off.
        2. Warning to inform that the shift lever is in P but the power source mode is not off.
      2. Door other than the driver side door open → closed:
        1. Warning to inform that a passenger is carrying the key out of the vehicle.

  3. Precautions for the battery built into the key and the vehicle battery:

    1. When the door is being locked, the vehicle battery is used to transmit a radio wave. The battery may be fully depleted if the vehicle remains stopped for a long time. If the vehicle is not used for a long time, recharge or replace the battery or cancel the entry and start system (for Entry Function) (Click here).

    2. When the door is being locked with the key in the detection area of the door oscillator, the battery is used to regulate communication between the key and vehicle. If the vehicle is not used, keep the key away from the vehicle (more than 2 m (6.56 ft.)).


3.PRECAUTIONS FOR BATTERY

  1. Immediately after removing and installing the battery, door unlock by the entry and start system (for Entry Function) may not operate. In that case, after performing a door unlock/lock operation with the door control transmitter, door unlock operation by the smart key system returns.

  2. When the battery is fully depleted and it is recharged using another vehicle battery, start the engine by performing the following: (1) Put the shift lever in P; (2) with the engine switch off, open and close the driver door once; and (3) start the engine.

  3. When starting the engine after removing and installing the battery, open and close the driver door and wait 10 seconds before starting the system.

    HINT:
    If the engine does not start the first time after removing and installing the battery, the engine will start normally from the second time onward.
