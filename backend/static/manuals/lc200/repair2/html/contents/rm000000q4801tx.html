1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and under cover.



6. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL



  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.



7. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug and 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

  1. Remove the radiator cap. Then drain the coolant.

    HINT:
    Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.



  1. Tighten the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.



8. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the cowl top ventilator louver (Click here).



9. REMOVE V-BANK COVER



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



10. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps and remove the air cleaner hose.



11. REMOVE AIR CLEANER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the No. 2 ventilation hose.

  1. Detach the hose clamp.




  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose.

  1. Disconnect the MAF meter connector.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 2 wire harness clamps.




  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and air cleaner assembly.



12. REMOVE INTAKE AIR SURGE TANK



  1. Disconnect the throttle body connector.

  1. Disconnect the No. 4 water by-pass hose.

  1. Disconnect the No. 5 water by-pass hose.




  1. Disconnect the purge line hose.

  1. Disconnect the purge VSV connector.

  1. Disconnect the No. 1 ventilation hose.




  1. Disconnect the vacuum switching valve (for ACIS) connector.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 2 wire harness clamps.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and throttle body bracket.




  1. Using a clip remover, detach the wire harness clamp.




  1. Remove the bolt and bracket from the No. 1 surge tank stay.

  1. Remove the bolt and oil baffle plate.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 1 surge tank stay.




  1. for Manual Transmission:

    1. Remove the nut.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 surge tank stay.




  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  1. Using an 8 mm hexagon wrench, remove the 4 bolts. Then remove the intake air surge tank and gasket.



13. DISCONNECT NO. 1 FUEL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the fuel pipe clamp.

  1. Pinch the tube connector, and then pull the fuel pipe out of the delivery pipe as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any dirt and foreign objects from the fuel tube connector before performing this work.
    2. Do not allow any scratches or foreign objects on the parts when disconnecting, as the fuel tube connector has the O-ring that seals the pipe.
    3. Perform this work by hand. Do not use any tools.
    4. Do not forcibly bend, twist or turn the nylon tube.
    5. Keep the plug free from foreign objects.
    6. Protect the disconnected part by covering it with a plastic bag after disconnecting the fuel tube.
    7. If the fuel tube connector and pipe are stuck, push and pull to release them.



14. DISCONNECT NO. 2 FUEL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the fuel pipe clamp.

  1. Pinch the tube connector, and then pull the fuel pipe out of the pipe as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any dirt and foreign objects from the fuel tube connector before performing this work.
    2. Do not allow any scratches or foreign objects on the parts when disconnecting, as the fuel tube connector has the O-ring that seals the pipe.
    3. Perform this work by hand. Do not use any tools.
    4. Do not forcibly bend, twist or turn the nylon tube.
    5. Keep the plug free from foreign objects.
    6. Protect the disconnected part by covering it with a plastic bag after disconnecting the fuel tube.
    7. If the fuel tube connector and pipe are stuck, push and pull to release them.



15. REMOVE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 6 fuel injector connectors.

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and fuel delivery pipe together with the 6 fuel injectors.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to drop the injectors when removing the fuel delivery pipe.



16. REMOVE FUEL INJECTOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Pull the 6 fuel injectors from the fuel delivery pipe.

  1. Remove the O-ring and injector vibration insulator from each fuel injector.
