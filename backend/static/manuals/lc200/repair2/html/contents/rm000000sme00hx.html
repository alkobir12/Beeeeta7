CYLINDER BLOCK > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. REPLACE RING PIN

NOTICE:
It is not necessary to remove the ring pin unless it is being replaced.

  1. Remove the ring pin.



  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap in new ring pins until they stop.

    Standard Protrusion:
    Ring Pin
    		Specified Condition
    A
    		3.5 to 4.5 mm (0.138 to 0.177 in.)
    B
    		6.5 to 7.5 mm (0.256 to 0.295 in.)
    C
    		9.0 to 10.0 mm (0.354 to 0.394 in.)






2. REPLACE STRAIGHT PIN

NOTICE:
It is not necessary to remove the straight pin unless it is being replaced.

  1. Remove the straight pin.



  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap in new straight pins until they stop.

    Standard Protrusion:
    Straight Pin
    		Specified Condition
    A
    		5.5 to 6.5 mm (0.217 to 0.256 in.)
    B
    		10.5 to 11.5 mm (0.413 to 0.453 in.)






3. REPLACE CYLINDER BLOCK TIGHT PLUG

NOTICE:
If water leaks from the tight plug or the plug corrodes, replace it.

  1. Remove the tight plugs.




  1. Apply adhesive to the inner circumference of the holes where the tight plugs contact.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent



  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in new tight plugs as shown in the illustration.

    SST
    09950-60010   (09951-00340)
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)





4. REPLACE CONNECTING ROD SMALL END BUSH



  1. Using SST and a press, press out the bushing.

    SST
    09222-30010  




  1. Align the oil holes of a new bushing and the connecting rod.




  1. Using SST and a press, press in the bushing.

    SST
    09222-30010
