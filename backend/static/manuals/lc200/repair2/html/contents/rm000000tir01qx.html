for Preparation Click here


INSPECTION PROCEDURE

NOTICE:
  1. After replacing the ECM, the new ECM needs registration (Click here) and initialization (Click here).
  2. After replacing a fuel supply pump, the ECM needs initialization (Click here).
  3. After replacing a fuel injector, the ECM needs registration (Click here).

HINT:
Specified values in the following troubleshooting flowchart are for reference only. Variations in the Data List values may occur depending on the measuring conditions or the vehicle age. Do not assume the vehicle to be normal when the Data List outputs standard values. There may be concealed factors of the malfunction.

1.READ OUTPUT DTC (RELATED TO ENGINE)

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and turn the tester on.

  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / DTC.

  1. Read the DTCs.
    Result 
    Result
    		Proceed to
    No DTC is output

3.PERFORM ENGINE SPEED ACCELERATION

HINT:
If the exhaust gas contains excessive black smoke, perform the following operations.

  1. Accelerate the engine to the maximum speed with no load 20 times.

  1. Check the volume of black smoke in the exhaust gas.
    Result 
    Result
    		Proceed to
    Black smoke is not present
    		OK
    Black smoke remains in the exhaust gas
    		NG

    HINT:
    Soot deposits in the exhaust system may cause excessive black smoke.
