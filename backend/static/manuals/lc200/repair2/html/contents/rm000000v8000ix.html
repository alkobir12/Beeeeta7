2.INSPECT LIGHT CONTROL RHEOSTAT (TRIP SWITCH)



  1. Remove the light control rheostat (Click here).

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    4 (A/B) - 1 (E)
    		TRIP switch ON (Pushed)
    		Below 200 Ω
    TRIP switch OFF (Not pushed)
    		1 MΩ or higher

3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (COMBINATION METER - LIGHT CONTROL RHEOSTAT AND BODY GROUND)



  1. Disconnect the E8 ECU connector.

  1. Disconnect the E9 switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E8-4 (RSET) - E9-4 (A/B)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E8-23 (E2) - E9-1 (E)
    E8-23 (E2) - Body ground
    E8-4 (RSET) or E9-4 (A/B) - Body ground
    		10 kΩ or higher
