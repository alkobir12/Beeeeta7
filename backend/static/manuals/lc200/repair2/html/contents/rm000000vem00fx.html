1. INSPECT FRONT NO. 2 SPEAKER ASSEMBLY



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω*1
    3 - 4
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω*1
    2 - 4
    		Always
    		4 Ω*1
    1 - 2
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω*2
    3 - 4
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω*2
    2 - 4
    		Always
    		8 Ω*2

    HINT:
    1. *1: for 9 Speakers Models
    2. *2: except 9 Speakers Models

    If the result is not as specified, replace the speaker assembly.

    		Specified Condition
