1.TROUBLESHOOTING PRECAUTIONS

  1. If a terminal contact point malfunction or part installation problem occurs, removal and reinstallation of the suspected part may return the system to normal. However, be aware that the normal condition may only be temporary.

  2. Before disconnecting connectors or removing/installing parts, determine the malfunctioning area by checking and writing down the condition of the vehicle at the time of the malfunction (DTC outputs, etc.).

  3. Be sure to check for DTCs related to other systems. The pre-crash safety system may be influenced by malfunctions in other systems.


2.HANDLING PRECAUTIONS

  1. When removing and installing the ECUs, sensors, switches or other pre-crash safety system related parts, perform the following:

    1. Make sure the engine switch is off unless otherwise specified in the inspection procedures.
    2. After the removal/installation, check for DTCs and confirm that a normal system code is output.
