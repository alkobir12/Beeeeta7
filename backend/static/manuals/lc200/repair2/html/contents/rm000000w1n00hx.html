OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY LH (w/o Retract Mirror)



  1. Apply battery voltage and check the operation of the mirror.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (MV)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 1 (M+)
    		Turns upward (A)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1 (M+)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 3 (MV)
    		Turns downward (B)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 2 (MH)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 1 (M+)
    		Turns left (C)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1 (M+)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2 (MH)
    		Turns right (D)

    If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly.



2. INSPECT OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Retract Mirror)



  1. Apply battery voltage and check the operation of the mirror.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 5 (MV)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 6 (M+)
    		Turns upward (A)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 5 (MV)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 6 (M+)
    		Turns downward (B)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 7 (MH)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 6 (M+)
    		Turns left (C)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 7 (MH)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 6 (M+)
    		Turns right (D)

    If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly.




  1. For each position: Disconnect the battery, set the mirror position by hand, connect the battery, and check the retractable mirror movement.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Mirror Position
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Forward position (A)
    		Moves from (A) to retracted position (E)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Forward position (A)
    		Does not move
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Position between forward position (A) and driving position (C)
    		Moves from (B) to retracted position (E)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Position between forward position (A) and driving position (C)
    		Moves from (B) to forward position (A)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Driving position (C)
    		Moves from (C) to retracted position (E)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Driving position (C)
    		Does not move
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Position between driving position (C) and retracted position (E)
    		Moves from (D) to retracted position (E)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Position between driving position (C) and retracted position (E)
    		Moves from (D) to driving position (C)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Retracted position (E)
    		Does not move
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 15 (MR)
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 16 (MF)
    		Retracted position (E)
    		Moves from (E) to driving position (C)

    NOTICE:
    1. Disconnect and reconnect the battery between each mirror position check.
    1. Do not apply battery voltage when moving the mirror by hand into each mirror position.

    If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly.




  1. w/ Mirror Heater:
    Check the mirror heater.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value (s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Measurement Condition
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Terminal 4 (HTR+) - Terminal 13 (HTR-)
      		25°C (75°F)
      		3.0 to 3.9 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly.

    2. Apply battery voltage and check the operation of the mirror heater.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 4 (HTR+)
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 13 (HTR-)
      		Mirror becomes warm

      HINT:
      It will take a short time for the mirror to become warm.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly.




  1. w/ EC Mirror:
    Check the EC mirror operation.

    1. Apply battery voltage and check the operation of the mirror face, as shown in the table and illustration.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (ECM+)
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 12 (ECM-)
      		Mirror surface becomes dark

      If the result is not as specified, replace the outer rear view mirror assembly.
