OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL


2. REMOVE FRONT DOOR INSIDE HANDLE BEZEL



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the inside handle bezel.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE FRONT DOOR ARMREST BASE PANEL ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 5 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the front door armrest base panel and multiplex network master switch.



4. REMOVE DOOR ASSIST GRIP COVER



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 8 claws and remove the door assist grip cover.



5. REMOVE FRONT DOOR LOWER FRAME BRACKET GARNISH


6. REMOVE FRONT DOOR TRIM BOARD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Remove the 13 clips, 4 claws and trim board.

  1. Disconnect the connector.




  1. Disconnect the 2 cables from the inside handle.



7. REMOVE OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector labeled A.

  1. Detach the clamp.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts and claw.

  1. Remove the outer rear view mirror.
