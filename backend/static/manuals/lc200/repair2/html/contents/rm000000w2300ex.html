LIGHTING SYSTEM > Door Courtesy Light Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The door courtesy light turns on when the door is opened and turns off when closed.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK OPERATION OF DOOR COURTESY LIGHT

  1. When a door is opened, check that the following lights do not illuminate.

    Tester Connection
