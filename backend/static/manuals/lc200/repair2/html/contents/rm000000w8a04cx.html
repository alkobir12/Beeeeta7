DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
P2716
Conditions (a) or (b) below are detected for 1 sec. or more (1 trip detection logic).
(a) SLT - terminal: 0 V
(b) SLT - terminal: 12 V
  1. Open or short in shift solenoid valve SLT circuit
  2. Shift solenoid valve SLT
  3. ECM
Reference: Inspect using an oscilloscope.
Check the waveform of the ECM connector.

MONITOR DESCRIPTION

When an open or short in the shift solenoid valve SLT circuit is detected, the ECM interprets this as a fault. The ECM will turn on the MIL and store the DTC.
