AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION SYSTEM (for 1GR-FE) > Transmission Control Switch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When moving the shift lever to S using the transmission control switch, it is possible to switch the shift range between "1" (1st range) and "5" (5th range).
Shifting to "+" once raises the shift range by one, and shifting to "-" lowers the shift range by one.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT TRANSMISSION CONTROL SWITCH



  1. Disconnect the E52 transmission control switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    3 (IG) - 7 (S)
    		Shift lever in S, "+" or "-"
    		Below 1 Ω
    2 (SFTU) - 5 (E)
    		Shift lever held in "+"
    (Up-shift)
    		Below 1 Ω
    1 (SFTD) - 5 (E)
    		Shift lever held in "-"
    (Down-shift)
    		Below 1 Ω
    3 (IG) - 7 (S)
    		Shift lever not in S, "+" or "-"
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 (SFTU) - 5 (E)
    		Shift lever in S
    		10 kΩ or higher
    1 (SFTD) - 5 (E)
    		Shift lever in S
    		10 kΩ or higher



NG

2.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (TRANSMISSION CONTROL SWITCH - BATTERY, BODY GROUND)



  1. Disconnect the E52 transmission control switch connector.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E52-3 (IG) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    E52-3 (IG) - Body ground
    		Engine switch off
    		Below 1 V

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E52-5 (E) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (TRANSMISSION CONTROL SWITCH - ECM)



  1. Disconnect the A38 ECM connector.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A38-25 (S) - Body ground
    1. Engine switch on (IG)
    2. Shift lever in S, "+" or "-"
    		11 to 14 V
    A38-25 (S) - Body ground
    1. Engine switch on (IG)
    2. Shift lever not in S, "+" or "-"
    		Below 1 V

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A38-16 (SFTU) - Body ground
    		Shift lever held in "+"
    (Up-shift)
    		Below 1 Ω
    A38-51 (SFTD) - Body ground
    		Shift lever held in "-"
    (Down-shift)
    		Below 1 Ω
    A38-16 (SFTU) - Body ground
    		Shift lever in S
    		10 kΩ or higher
    A38-51 (SFTD) - Body ground
    		Shift lever in S
    		10 kΩ or higher



NG
