AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM > Air Conditioning Compressor Magnetic Clutch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When the air conditioning amplifier assembly is turned on, a magnetic clutch on signal is sent from the MGC terminal of the air conditioning amplifier assembly. Then, the magnetic clutch relay turns on to operate the magnetic clutch.
