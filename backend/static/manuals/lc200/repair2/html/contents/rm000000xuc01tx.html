ENTRY AND START SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

  1. In addition to conventional mechanical key and wireless door lock control functions, the entry and start system enables door locking/unlocking, steering lock releasing, engine control system starting and back door opening without operating the key. The only requirement is that the key is in the possession of the user.

    1. This system is controlled by the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly). When the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) detects the presence of the key in one of the detection areas, it identifies and checks the ID code, and outputs operation signals to the related ECU in accordance with their functions.
    2. For vehicle with rear entry function:
      The detection areas are formed by 4 oscillators and 4 antenna (4 door oscillators, back door antenna and 3 indoor antennas).
    3. For vehicle without rear entry function:
      The detection areas are formed by 2 oscillators and 4 antenna (2 door oscillators, back door antenna and 3 indoor antennas).


DETECTION AREA


  1. Precautions for the entire system:

    1. Be sure to carry the key during inspection of the entry and start system.
    2. As weak radio waves are used to detect the key, the key detection area may decrease, or the key may not be detected correctly in the following situations.
      1. The key battery is depleted.
      2. Any facilities that generate strong radio waves, such as a TV tower, power plant, a broadcast station or gas station, are located near the inspection site.
      3. A wireless device, such as a cell phone, is carried with the key.
      4. The key is in contact with or covered with metal.
      5. Electric wave type keyless entry systems are being operated nearby.
      6. The key is located near a device that generates high voltage or noise.
      7. Film with some metallic content is stuck to the back glass.

        HINT:
        In this case, remove the cause or unlock the door using the mechanical key.

    1. Even if the key is in the vehicle exterior key detection area, the key may not operate properly due to the vehicle body design.
    2. The key may not be detected correctly if it is around the vehicle's windows, the door handle or center of the bumper, even when it is in the detection area outside the vehicle.
    3. The key may not be detected correctly if it is on the instrument panel, rear package tray or floor, or in the glove box, even when it is in the detection area inside the vehicle.
    4. The key may not operate if the key is not held properly.
    5. The entry and start system does not operate in the following conditions:
      1. The entry and start system is canceled.
      2. The key battery is completely depleted (the indicator does not blink when pushing any of the switches on the key).

CONSTRUCTION AND OPERATION


  1. Key:
    A total of 7 keys can be registered.



    1. Electrical key transmitter:
      The electrical key transmitter consists of a mechanical key, a transmitter function for the wireless door lock control and a transceiver for the entry and start system.

      1. The transceiver function for the entry and start system receives the signals from the oscillators and sends the ID code to the door control receiver.
      2. The transmitter function for the wireless door lock control has a lock switch, unlock switch and panic switch.
      3. This mechanical key can lock/unlock all the doors. To lock or unlock the doors, remove the cap from the driver door outside handle, insert the key into the door key cylinder, and then turn it to the lock or unlock position.




  2. Oscillator (each door oscillator, front, rear 1 and rear 2 indoor antenna, and back door antenna)
    Each oscillator transmits the request signal received from the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly), and forms a key detection area to detect the presence of a key. The detection area formed by each door oscillator and back door outer antenna is approximately 0.7 to 1.0 m (2.30 to 3.28 ft.) from the outside handle of each door or the center of the rear bumper.

    1. The detection area of each door oscillator is formed by transmitting a request signal every 0.25 seconds while the engine switch is off and each door is locked. In this way, it detects the proximity of a key. During entry lock, the detection area is formed with the lock switch on.
    2. The detection area of the back door antenna is formed when the back door opener switch is on.
    3. The detection area of the indoor antenna (for front, rear 1 and rear 2) is formed when the driver door is opened or closed, during starting, when a warning is activated, or when the lock switch is on.


ENTRY FUNCTION OPERATION

  1. The entry and start system has the following functions.

    Function
    		Outline
    Mechanical Key
    		Operation is the same as a conventional mechanical key.
    Wireless Door Lock Control
    		This function remotely locks and unlocks all doors and the back door. Operation is the same as the wireless door lock control system. However, receiver in the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) uses the entry door control receiver to control locking and unlocking.
    Entry Illumination
    		When a person carrying the key enters the detection area, the door will enter unlock standby mode and room light will illuminate.
    Entry Unlock
    		When the key is located in the detection area of the door oscillator, the door will unlock by touching the inside of the outside door handle.
    Entry Unlock Mode Switching (Click here)
    		Changes the doors that can be unlocked with the entry unlock function between 2 modes.
    1. Driver door unlock mode
    2. All door unlock mode
    Entry Lock
    		When the key is located in the detection area of the door oscillator and the engine switch is off, the door can be locked by pressing the lock switch on the outside door handle.
    Entry Back Door Open
    		When the key is in the detection area of the back door antenna (outer), the back door opens by pressing the back door opener switch.
    Memory Call
    		This function operates the driving position memory system in accordance with the key ID code.
    1. Power Window Close Function*1
    2. Sliding Roof Close Function*1
    		Pressing the door handle entry lock switch for approximately 3 seconds or more causes all power windows and the sliding roof to close while sounding the wireless buzzer*2.
    Key Lock-in Prevention
    		Prevents lock-in of the key if the door is locked using the outside door handle while the key is still inside the vehicle.
    Warning
    		When any of the situations below occur, the entry and start system causes the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) to sound the buzzer in the combination meter and the wireless door lock buzzer*2, and display information in order to alert the driver.
    1. Shift lever not in P (power source cut reminder) vehicle exit warning
    2. Key reminder warning
    3. Shift lever in P (power source cut reminder) vehicle exit warning
    4. Entry door ajar warning
    5. Passenger has brought key out of vehicle warning
    6. Key is not within detection area warning
    7. Key lock-in prevention function buzzer
    8. Key battery low warning
    Battery Saving
    		If the key is constantly located within the actuation area of the door oscillator, the system maintains periodic communication with the key. Therefore, if the vehicle remains parked in that state for a long time, key battery and vehicle battery could be drained. For this reason, the vehicle has controls for saving battery power.
    Key Cancel (Click here)
    		The following key functions can be canceled:
    1. Entry Unlock/Lock
    2. Entry Ignition
    3. Memory Call
    4. Entry Back Door Open
    5. Key Lock-in Prevention
    6. Warning

    *1: w/ Remote Function
    *2: except China

ENTRY UNLOCK FUNCTION

  1. The detection area is formed by communication between a door oscillator and the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) when the door is locked in order to detect the person carrying the key.

  2. When the person carrying the key enters the detection area around the vehicle, the matching of the ID codes for a door oscillator and key will automatically be performed. After the matching is completed, the door will enter unlock standby mode.

  3. In unlock standby mode, the antenna built into the outside handle starts sensing. If the outside handle is held (the back side of the handle is touched), the door unlock operation will be performed. At this time, the hazard warning light blinks twice and the wireless door lock buzzer sounds twice*.

    HINT:
    *: except Europe and China

  4. If any of the doors are not opened after a door unlock operation, all doors will automatically lock after 30 seconds.


ENTRY LOCK FUNCTION

  1. After leaving the vehicle carrying the key, push the lock switch on the outside handle when all the doors are closed.

  2. The certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) determines whether the key is located inside or outside of the cabin based on the information from a door oscillator and indoor antenna. Then matching of the ID codes is performed.

  3. When the matching result shows that the ID codes of the key and indoor antenna do not match and the ID codes of the key and door oscillator match, the door lock function will operate. At this time, the hazard warning lights blink once and the wireless door lock buzzer sounds once*.

    HINT:
    *: except Europe and China

  4. If the key is located inside of the cabin, the door lock function will not operate and the alarm buzzer (beep sound) will sound for 2 seconds*.

    HINT:
    *: except China


ENTRY BACK DOOR OPEN FUNCTION
Stand in front of the back door with the key and push the back door opener switch to start the matching of the ID codes for the back door antenna. If the ID codes match, the back door open operation will be performed.

PREVENTION OF KEY LOCK-IN FUNCTION

  1. If you attempt to lock the door through keyless operation (move the lock knob to the lock position and then close the door) with the key in the cabin, the system determines that the key is located in the cabin and unlocks the door.

  2. If the back door is closed with the key in it, the alarm will sound and the back door can be opened only when all the doors are locked in order to prevent key lock-in in the vehicle.


ENTRY ILLUMINATION FUNCTION
In order to improve ease of entering and exiting the vehicle at night, the vehicle lights illuminate when the key is brought near the vehicle.

ENTRY POWER WINDOW FUNCTION* [REMOTE FUNCTION] (DOOR WINDOW AND SLIDING ROOF CLOSE OPERATION ONLY)

  1. Pressing the door handle lock switch for 3 seconds or more operates the door window close operation of all of the windows and the sliding roof close operation.

  2. If the entry power window function is operating and the door handle lock switch is released, the entry power window operation will stop.

    HINT:
    1. For more information about the entry power window function, refer to the Power Window Control System (Click here).
    2. *: w/ Remote Function


MEMORY CALL FUNCTION (w/ Seat Position Memory System)

  1. After unlocking the driver door using the key ID and opening the driver door, the seat automatically moves to the position associated with the key ID.

    HINT:
    For more information about the memory call function, refer to the Front Power Seat Control System (Click here).

BATTERY SAVING

  1. Vehicle battery saving function:
    In the entry and start system, signals are emitted outside of the vehicle at a prescribed interval (0.25 seconds) when the doors are locked. Therefore, the vehicle battery could be drained if the vehicle remains parked for a long time. For this reason, the controls listed below exist.

    Condition
    		Control
    Both conditions below are met for 10 days:
    1. Key is not in detection area
    2. Touch sensor or lock switch operation is not performed
    		Signal transmission interval is extended from 0.25 to 0.75 seconds
    Both conditions below are met for 15 days:
    1. Key is not in detection area
    2. Touch sensor or lock switch operation is not performed
    		Signal transmission interval is extended from 0.75 to 2.25 seconds
    Both conditions below are met for 30 days:
    1. Key is not in detection area
    2. Touch sensor or lock switch operation is not performed
    		Automatically deactivates the entry and start system
    Reinstatement Conditions:

    1. A wireless door lock control signal (lock, unlock) is input and the ID matches.
    2. A user carries the key and pushes a lock switch on an outside door handle.
    3. The driver door is locked or unlocked by the mechanical key.

  2. Key battery and vehicle battery saving function:
    In the entry and start system, if the key is constantly located within the vehicle exterior detection area of the doors, the system maintains periodic communication with the key. Therefore, if the vehicle remains parked in that state for a long time, the key battery and the vehicle battery could be drained. For this reason, if this state continues for more than 10 minutes, the entry and start system automatically becomes deactivated.
    Reinstatement Conditions:

    1. A wireless door lock control signal (lock, unlock) is input and the ID code matches.
    2. A user carries the key and pushes a lock switch on an outside handle.
    3. The driver door is locked or unlocked by the mechanical key.
