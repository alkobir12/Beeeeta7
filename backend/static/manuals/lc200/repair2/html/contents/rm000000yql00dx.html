INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
This problem may be caused by a malfunction in the vehicle stability control system or steering system.
Make sure that no DTC is output for the skid control ECU or steering control ECU before proceeding with the following procedures.
If a DTC is output for either of these ECUs, first troubleshoot it and then proceed to the troubleshooting below.

2.CHECK HEAT PROTECTION FUNCTION FOR STEERING ACTUATOR

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and wait for 30 minutes until the steering actuator cools down.

  1. Start the engine and slowly turn the steering wheel from lock to lock (approximately 90°/sec.) 3 times.

    HINT:
    If turning the steering wheel quickly while driving at low speeds, the steering angle may differ between the left and right, or the steering wheel may become off-center.
    However, this is not a malfunction.
