DTC    B1653/35    Seat Position Airbag Sensor Circuit Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The seat position sensor circuit consists of the center airbag sensor and the seat position sensor.
DTC B1653/35 is stored when a malfunction is detected in the seat position sensor circuit.

for LHD 
DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1653/35
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. The center airbag sensor receives a line short circuit signal, an open circuit signal, a short circuit to ground signal or a short circuit to B+ signal in the seat position sensor circuit for 2 seconds.
  2. A seat position sensor malfunction.
  3. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. Floor wire
  2. Seat position sensor
  3. Center airbag sensor assembly

for RHD 
DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1653/35
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. The center airbag sensor receives a line short circuit signal, an open circuit signal, a short circuit to ground signal or a short circuit to B+ signal in the seat position sensor circuit for 2 seconds.
  2. A seat position sensor malfunction.
  3. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. No. 2 floor wire
  2. Seat position sensor
  3. Center airbag sensor assembly

5.CHECK FLOOR WIRE (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR - SEAT POSITION SENSOR)


  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    K10-4 (LSP+) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		Below 1 V
    K10-8 (LSP-) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		Below 1 V

  1. w/ Side Airbag:

    1. Turn the engine switch off.

    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.

    3. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      K10-4 (LSP+) - K27-9 (LSP+)
      		Always
      		Below 1 Ω
      K10-4 (LSP+) - Body ground
      		Always
      		1 MΩ or higher
      K10-8 (LSP-) - Body ground
      		Always
      		Below 1 Ω

  1. w/o Side Airbag:

    1. Turn the engine switch off.

    2. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.

    3. Using a service wire, connect terminals 14 (LSP+) and 13 (LSP-) of connector B.
