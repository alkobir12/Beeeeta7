DTC    C15A3/63    Actuator Malfunction

DTC    C15C5/69    Motor Revolution Angle Signal (Test Mode DTC)

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The steering actuator drives the internal motor using the current output from the steering control ECU to change the relative angle between the tire angle and steering wheel angle.
The rotation angle sensor in the steering actuator detects the motor rotation angle and outputs this information to the steering control ECU.
If the steering control ECU detects a malfunction in the rotation angle sensor circuit, it will store DTC C15A3/63.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C15A3/63
The steering control ECU detects a malfunction in the rotation angle sensor circuit.
  1. Harness or connector
  2. Steering actuator
  3. Steering control ECU
C15C5/69
A steering signal indicating a motor rotation angle of 36° or more (to the left or right) is input after entering test mode.*
  1. Harness or connector
  2. Steering actuator
  3. Steering control ECU

HINT:
*: A tire angle of 36° and a motor rotation angle of 36° correspond to the steering wheel angle. The amount of change in tire angle and motor rotation angle is from when the mode is changed to test mode.
