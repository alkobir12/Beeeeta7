PARK / NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
  1. When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).
  2. After the engine switch is turned off, the navigation system requires approximately 90 seconds to record various types of memory and settings. As a result, after turning the engine switch off, wait 90 seconds or more before disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.



2. DISCONNECT FLOOR SHIFT GEAR SHIFTING ROD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clip and pin, and disconnect the shifting rod from the transmission control shaft lever RH.



3. REMOVE PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the switch connector.




  1. Remove the nut, spring washer and transmission control shaft lever RH.

  1. Remove the collar and transmission control rod bush from the transmission control shaft lever RH.




  1. Using a screwdriver, bend the tabs of the lock washer.




  1. Remove the lock nut and lock washer.




  1. Remove the bolt and switch.
