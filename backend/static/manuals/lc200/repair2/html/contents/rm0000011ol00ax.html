WIPER SWITCH > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE LOWER STEERING COLUMN COVER


2. REMOVE UPPER STEERING COLUMN COVER


3. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

  1. Using a screwdriver wrapped with protective tape, disengage the claw and remove the wiper switch.

    NOTICE:
    If the claw is pushed with excessive force, it may be broken.
