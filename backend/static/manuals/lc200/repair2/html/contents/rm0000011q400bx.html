CANISTER > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT CANISTER



  1. Visually check the canister for cracks or damage.

  1. Blow compressed air of 4.9 kPa (50 gf/cm2, 0.71 psi) into port A and check that air flows without resistance from port B and port C.
    If the result is not as specified, replace the canister.

  1. Apply a vacuum of 1.96 kPa (20 gf/cm2, 0.28 psi) to port A. Check that the vacuum does not decrease when ports B and C are closed, and check that the vacuum does not decrease when port B is released.
    If the result is not as specified, replace the canister.



2. CLEAN FILTER IN CANISTER



  1. Clean the filter by blowing compressed air of 4.9 kPa (50 gf/cm2, 0.71 psi) into port A while holding port B closed.

    NOTICE:
    Do not attempt to wash the canister.
