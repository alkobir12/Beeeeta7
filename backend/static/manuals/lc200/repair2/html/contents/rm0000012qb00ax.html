for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

  1. The theft warning siren (self power siren) has an internal battery. If the vehicle battery cable is disconnected or any of the communication lines are open, the theft warning siren (self power siren) detects this and sounds its siren.
  2. Although the theft warning siren (self power siren) usually sounds by receiving a signal from the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly), the theft warning siren (self power siren) can sound by its internal battery in case the vehicle battery cable is disconnected.
  3. The certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) sends an arming signal to the theft warning siren (self power siren) while transferring to the armed state, and it also sends a disarming signal to the siren while switching to the disarmed state. Also, the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) can cause the theft warning siren (self power siren) to sound by sending an alarm signal during the alarm sounding state.
