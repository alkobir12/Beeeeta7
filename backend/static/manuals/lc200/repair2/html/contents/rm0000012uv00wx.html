DTC    P0818    Driveline Disconnect Switch Input Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The ECM detects the signal indicating that the transfer is in neutral.
This DTC indicates that the signal indicating that the transfer is in neutral remains on.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
P0818
The signal indicating that the transfer is in neutral remains on while the vehicle is running under the following conditions for 30 sec. (2 trip detection logic):
  1. The vehicle speed is 25 km/h (16 mph) or more.
  2. The transfer position switch is in H4.
  1. Short in transfer neutral signal
  2. Transfer shift actuator assembly (transfer system)
  3. ECM

MONITOR DESCRIPTION

The ECM detects whether or not the transfer is in neutral by monitoring the signal from the transfer shift actuator assembly.
If the ECM detects that the transfer is in neutral under the following conditions, the ECM will conclude that there is a malfunction of the transfer shift actuator assembly, illuminate the MIL and store the DTC:
  1. The transfer shift actuator assembly indicates that the transfer is in neutral.
  2. The transfer position switch is in the H4 position.
  3. The vehicle is being driven at 25 km/h (16 mph) or more.
  4. The transfer neutral signal has been on for more than 30 seconds.
