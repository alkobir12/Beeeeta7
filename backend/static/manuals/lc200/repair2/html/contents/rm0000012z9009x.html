FRONT BRAKE > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL FRONT DISC



  1. Align the matchmarks, and then install the front disc.

    HINT:
    When replacing the front disc with a new one, select the installation position where the front disc has the minimum runout.



2. INSTALL DISC BRAKE CALIPER ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install the disc brake caliper with 2 new bolts.

    Torque:
    99 N*m{ 1,010 kgf*cm , 73 ft.*lbf }



3. CONNECT FRONT FLEXIBLE HOSE



  1. Install a new gasket and connect the flexible hose with the union bolt.

    Torque:
    30 N*m{ 306 kgf*cm , 22 ft.*lbf }



4. INSTALL FRONT DISC BRAKE PAD



  1. Install the No. 1 anti-squeal shims to each pad.




  1. Install the 2 pads to the disc brake caliper.




  1. Install the front disc brake anti-rattle spring between the 2 pads.

    NOTICE:
    The anti-rattle spring can be reused if it has sufficient rebound; no deformation, cracks or wear; and has had all rust, dirt and foreign particles cleaned off.




  1. Install the 2 hole pins.

  1. Install the pin hold clip.

    NOTICE:
    The pin hold clip can be reused if it has sufficient rebound; no deformation or wear; and has had all rust, dirt and foreign particles cleaned off.



5. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID

Fluid:
SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT3



6. BLEED BRAKE LINE


  1. Bleed air from the front brake system.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

      HINT:
      Perform this procedure with the engine switch on (IG), as the large piston of the master cylinder does not stroke with the engine switch off.




    2. Connect a vinyl tube to the brake caliper.

    3. Depress the brake pedal several times, then loosen the bleeder plug with the pedal held down (procedure "A").

      NOTICE:
      While performing the air bleeding procedure, make sure that the amount of fluid in the reservoir remains above the MIN line.

    4. At the point when the fluid stops coming out, tighten the bleeder plug, then release the brake pedal (procedure "B").

      Torque:
      11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

    5. Repeat procedure "A" to "B" until all the air in the fluid has been bled out.

    6. Repeat the above procedures to bleed the other front brake line.



  1. Bleed air from the rear brake system.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and depress the brake pedal.




    2. Connect a vinyl tube to the brake caliper.

    3. Loosen the bleeder plug and release air.

      NOTICE:
      While performing the air bleeding procedure, make sure that the amount of fluid in the reservoir remains above the MIN line.

      HINT:
      Brake fluid is sent through the pump, so keep the brake pedal depressed until the air is completely bled out.

    4. When the air is completely bled out of the brake fluid through the bleeder plug, tighten the bleeder plug.

      Torque:
      11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

    5. Repeat the above procedures to bleed the other rear brake line.

  1. Connect the intelligent tester.

    1. Turn the engine switch off.

    2. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / Utility / Air Bleeding.

      HINT:
      Please refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

  1. Bleed front right brake line.

    1. Select "FR Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "FR Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, depress the brake pedal and hold it to bleed the front right brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed front left brake line.

    1. Select "FL Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "FL Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, depress the brake pedal and hold it to bleed the front left brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed rear right brake line.

    1. Select "RR Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "RR Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, bleed the left brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed rear left brake line.

    1. Select "RL Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "RL Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, bleed the left brake line.

  1. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.

  1. Clear the DTCs (Click here).



7. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR



  1. When inspecting or refilling with the engine switch off, depress the brake pedal 40 times or more (there will be less pedal resistance and the pedal stroke will lengthen), and then add fluid until the fluid level is at the MAX line.

    HINT:
    When the engine switch is turned on (IG), fluid is sent to the accumulator causing the fluid level to decrease 10 mm (0.394 in.) from the level when the engine switch is off (this is normal).

    Brake Fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS NO. 116 DOT3



8. INSPECT FOR BRAKE FLUID LEAK


9. INSTALL FRONT WHEEL

Torque:
