AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION UNIT > DISASSEMBLY

1. REMOVE TRANSMISSION CONTROL SHAFT LEVER RH



  1. Remove the nut, spring washer and control shaft lever RH.



2. REMOVE PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a screwdriver, bend the tabs of the lock washer.

  1. Remove the nut, lock washer and bolt.

  1. Remove the park/neutral position switch.



3. REMOVE OIL COOLER TUBE UNION



  1. Remove the 2 oil cooler tube unions.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the oil cooler tube unions.



4. REMOVE SPEED SENSOR



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 speed sensors.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the sensors.



5. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION BREATHER TUBE



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Remove the breather tube.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the tube.



6. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION HOUSING



  1. Remove the 10 bolts.

  1. Remove the transmission housing.



7. REMOVE REAR ADAPTOR TRANSFER



  1. Remove the 8 bolts.

  1. Remove the transmission case adapter.

    HINT:
    Use a brass bar and a hammer to remove the transmission case adapter.



8. REMOVE TRANSMISSION CASE ADAPTER REAR OIL SEAL



  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the transmission case.

    HINT:
    Wrap the tip of the screwdriver with tape.



9. REMOVE TRANSFER CASE ADAPTER RADIAL BALL BEARING



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the snap ring.

  1. Using SST and a press, press out the bearing.

    SST
    09950-60010   (09951-00650)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)



10. FIX AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the transmission case to an overhaul attachment.



11. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION OIL PAN SUB-ASSEMBLY

NOTICE:
Do not turn the transmission over as this will contaminate the valve body with foreign matter on the bottom of the pan.

  1. Remove the drain plug and 20 bolts.



12. INSPECT AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION OIL PAN SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the magnets, and use them to collect steel particles.

  1. Carefully look at the foreign matter and particles in the pan and on the magnets to anticipate the type of wear you will find in the transmission.

    1. Steel (magnetic): bearing, gear and clutch plate wear
    2. Brass (non-magnetic): bushing wear



13. REMOVE VALVE BODY OIL STRAINER ASSEMBLY



  1. Turn over the transmission.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and oil strainer from the valve body.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the oil strainer.



14. REMOVE TRANSMISSION WIRE



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 clamps, and disconnect the 2 ATF temperature sensors.

  1. Disconnect the 7 connectors from the solenoid valves.




  1. Remove the bolt from the case.

  1. Pull the transmission wire out of the transmission case.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the transmission wire.



15. REMOVE TRANSMISSION VALVE BODY ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the bolt, detent spring cover and detent spring.




  1. Remove the 19 bolts.

  1. Remove the valve body.



16. REMOVE TRANSMISSION CASE GASKET



  1. Remove the 3 gaskets.



17. REMOVE BRAKE DRUM GASKET



  1. Remove the 3 gaskets.



18. REMOVE CHECK BALL BODY



  1. Remove the check ball body and spring.



19. REMOVE C-2 ACCUMULATOR PISTON



  1. Apply compressed air to the oil hole to remove the C-2 accumulator piston and spring.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the piston.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful as the C-3 and B-3 accumulator pistons may jump out.



20. REMOVE B-3 ACCUMULATOR PISTON



  1. Apply compressed air to the oil hole to remove the B-3 accumulator piston and spring.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the piston.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful as the C-3 accumulator piston may jump out.



21. REMOVE C-3 ACCUMULATOR PISTON



  1. Apply compressed air to the oil hole to remove the C-3 accumulator piston and 2 springs.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the piston.



22. REMOVE C-1 ACCUMULATOR VALVE



  1. Remove the C-1 accumulator valve and 2 springs.



23. REMOVE PARKING LOCK PAWL BRACKET



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bracket.



24. REMOVE PARKING LOCK ROD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the parking lock rod from the manual valve lever.



25. REMOVE PARKING LOCK PAWL SHAFT



  1. Pull out the parking lock pawl shaft from the front side, and then remove the lock pawl and spring.

  1. Remove the E-ring from the shaft.



26. REMOVE MANUAL VALVE LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a hammer and screwdriver, cut off the spacer and remove it from the shaft.




  1. Using a pin punch and hammer, tap out the spring pin.

    HINT:
    Slowly drive out the spring pin so that it does not fall into the transmission case.

  1. Pull the manual valve lever shaft out through the case, and remove the manual valve lever.



27. REMOVE MANUAL VALVE LEVER SHAFT OIL SEAL


  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the 2 oil seals.

    NOTICE:

    Be careful not to damage the transmission case.

    HINT:
    Wrap the tip of the screwdriver with tape.



28. REMOVE OIL PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts.




  1. Using SST, remove the oil pump.

    SST
    09350-30020   (09350-07020)




  1. Remove the 2 thrust bearing races from the front oil pump.



29. REMOVE CLUTCH DRUM AND INPUT SHAFT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clutch drum and input shaft assembly from the transmission case.




  1. Remove the clutch drum thrust washer and 2 thrust needle roller bearings.



30. INSPECT NO. 2 1-WAY CLUTCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Hold the reverse clutch hub and turn the 1-way clutch assembly.

  1. Check that the 1-way clutch turns freely clockwise and locks when turned counterclockwise.



31. REMOVE NO. 2 1-WAY CLUTCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 1-way clutch and No. 2 clutch drum thrust washer from the clutch drum and input shaft assembly.



32. FIX CLUTCH DRUM AND INPUT SHAFT ASSEMBLY



  1. Place the oil pump onto the torque converter, and then place the clutch drum and input shaft assembly onto the oil pump.



33. REMOVE REVERSE CLUTCH HUB SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the snap ring.




  1. Remove the reverse clutch hub sub-assembly, reverse clutch reaction sleeve, clutch cushion, plate reverse clutch flange, 5 reverse clutch discs and 4 clutch plates from the clutch drum.



34. REMOVE REVERSE CLUTCH REACTION SLEEVE



  1. Remove the reverse clutch reaction sleeve from the reverse clutch hub.



35. REMOVE REAR CLUTCH DISC



  1. Remove the clutch cushion plate, reverse clutch flange, 5 discs and 4 plates from the reverse clutch hub.



36. INSPECT REAR CLUTCH DISC



  1. Replace all discs if one of the following problems is present: 1) a disc, plate or flange is worn or burnt, 2) the lining of a disc is peeled off or discolored, or 3) the grooves or printed numbers have even a little bit of damage.

    NOTICE:
    Before assembling new discs, soak them in ATF for at least 15 minutes.



37. INSPECT REVERSE CLUTCH HUB SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the inside diameter of the reverse clutch hub bush.

    Standard inside diameter:
    35.812 to 35.837 mm (1.410 to 1.411 in.)

    Maximum inside diameter:
    35.887 mm (1.413 in.)

    1. If the inside diameter is more than the maximum, replace the reverse clutch hub.



38. REMOVE FORWARD CLUTCH HUB SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the forward clutch hub from the clutch drum.




  1. Remove the 2 thrust needle roller bearings from the forward clutch hub.



39. INSPECT FORWARD CLUTCH HUB SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the inside diameter of the forward clutch hub bush.

    Standard inside diameter:
    26.037 to 26.062 mm (1.025 to 1.026 in.)

    Maximum inside diameter:
    26.112 mm (1.028 in.)

    1. If the inside diameter is more than the maximum, replace the forward clutch hub.



40. REMOVE MULTIPLE DISC CLUTCH HUB



  1. Remove the multiple disc clutch hub from the clutch drum.




  1. Remove the 2 thrust bearing races from the multiple disc clutch hub.



41. REMOVE INPUT SHAFT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the thrust needle roller bearing from the clutch drum.




  1. Remove the input shaft assembly from the clutch drum.



42. REMOVE INPUT SHAFT OIL SEAL RING



  1. Remove the 3 oil seal rings from the input shaft.



43. REMOVE FORWARD MULTIPLE DISC CLUTCH DISC



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the hole snap ring.




  1. for 1GR-FE:
    Remove the 2 flanges, 6 discs and 5 plates from the input shaft.




  1. for 2UZ-FE:
    Remove the 2 flanges, 7 discs and 6 plates from the input shaft.



44. INSPECT FORWARD MULTIPLE DISC CLUTCH DISC



  1. Replace all discs if one of the following problems is present: 1) a disc, plate or flange is worn or burnt, 2) the lining of a disc is peeled off or discolored, or 3) the grooves or printed numbers have even a little bit of damage.

45. REMOVE NO. 1 CLUTCH BALANCER



  1. Place SST on the clutch balancer, and compress the return spring with a press.

    SST
    09350-30020   (09350-07040, 09350-07070)

  1. Using a snap ring expander, remove the snap ring.




  1. Remove the clutch balancer and forward clutch return spring from the input shaft assembly.




  1. Remove the O-ring from the No. 1 clutch balancer.



46. INSPECT FORWARD CLUTCH RETURN SPRING SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the free length of the spring together with the spring seat.

    Standard free length:
    26.74 mm (1.05 in.)



47. REMOVE FORWARD CLUTCH PISTON



  1. Hold the input shaft by hand and apply compressed air (392 kPa (4.0 kgf/cm2, 57 psi)) to the input shaft to remove the forward clutch piston.




  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the forward clutch piston.



48. REMOVE REVERSE CLUTCH FLANGE

49. REMOVE DIRECT CLUTCH DISC

  1. for 2UZ-FE:

65. REMOVE FRONT PLANETARY GEAR ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the front planetary gear assembly and 1-way clutch inner race from the case.




  1. Remove the No. 5 thrust bearing race, thrust needle roller bearing and No. 2 planetary carrier thrust washer from the front planetary gear assembly.



66. INSPECT FRONT PLANETARY GEAR ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a feeler gauge, measure the front planetary pinion gear thrust clearance.

    Standard clearance:
    0.2 to 0.6 mm (0.00787 to 0.0236 in.)

    Maximum clearance:
    0.65 mm (0.0256 in.)

    1. If the clearance is more than the maximum, replace the front planetary gear assembly.




  1. Using a cylinder gauge, measure the inside diameter of the front planetary gear bush.

    Maximum inside diameter:
    57.48 mm (2.26 in.)

    1. If the inside diameter is more than the maximum, replace the front planetary gear.



67. INSPECT 1-WAY CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

  1. Hold the 1-way clutch inner race and turn the 1-way clutch assembly. Check that the 1-way clutch turns freely counterclockwise and locks when turned clockwise.

  1. Remove the 1-way clutch from the 1-way clutch inner race.
