GLOW PLUG > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the intercooler (Click here).



2. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the air tube from the throttle body.



3. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the air tube from the throttle body.



4. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Disconnect the mass air flow meter connector and using a clip remover, detach the wire harness clamp from the air cleaner cap.

  1. Detach the 4 clamps and remove the air cleaner cap.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 1 air cleaner hose.



6. REMOVE INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR



  1. w/o Viscous Heater:

    1. Disconnect the connector from the water temperature sensor.

    2. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.

  1. w/ Viscous Heater:

    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors from the viscous with magnet clutch heater and water temperature sensor.

    2. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.




  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps and remove the 2 bolts and intake air connector.



7. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 1 air hose.



8. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 2 air hose.



9. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose from the oil separator.

  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 air cleaner pipe.



10. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 1 air cleaner pipe.



11. DISCONNECT NO. 4 WATER BY-PASS PIPE



  1. Detach the clamp and disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the nut, 4 bolts and water by-pass pipe so that the glow plug can be removed in a step below.



12. DISCONNECT ENGINE WIRE



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and engine wire so that the glow plug can be removed in a step below.




  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors and remove the 2 bolts and engine wire so that the glow plug can be removed in a step below.



13. REMOVE NO. 1 GLOW PLUG CONNECTOR



  1. Remove the 2 screw grommets and 2 nuts, and disconnect the 2 wire harnesses.

  1. Remove the 8 screw grommets, 8 nuts and 2 glow plug connectors.



14. REMOVE GLOW PLUG ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a 10 mm deep socket wrench, remove the 8 glow plugs.
