1. CHECK ILLUMINATION DURATION OF GLOW INDICATOR LIGHT


  1. Check the light illumination duration.




    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and measure the light illumination duration.

      Light illumination duration:
      See graph.

      1. If the result is not as specified, check the ECM, engine coolant temperature sensor, glow plug assembly and glow relay.

      HINT:
      Actual light illumination duration may vary slightly from the graph.

    2. Turn the engine switch off.



2. CHECK PRE-HEATING


  1. Inspect the pre-heating time.




    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG). Measure how long it takes for the battery voltage to be applied to the glow plugs.

      Standard Pre-Heating Time:
      Engine Coolant Temperature
      		Specified Condition
      40°C (104°F) or higher
      		1 second
      Below 40°C (104°F)
      		See graph*
      (Maximum: 21 seconds)

      HINT:
      *: The pre-heating time changes depending on the battery voltage.

      1. If the result is not as specified, check the ECM, engine coolant temperature sensor, glow plug assembly and glow relay.

    2. Turn the engine switch off.



3. CHECK AFTER GLOW TIME



  1. Remove the No. 1 glow relay (GLOW 1) from the engine room relay block.

  1. Remove the No. 2 glow relay (GLOW 2) from the engine room relay block.



  1. After the engine starts, measure the time duration that battery voltage remains present.
