GLOW PLUG > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL GLOW PLUG ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a 10 mm deep socket wrench, install the 8 glow plugs.

    Torque:
    12 N*m{ 125 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



2. INSTALL NO. 1 GLOW PLUG CONNECTOR



  1. Install the 2 glow plug connectors by uniformly tightening the 8 nuts.

    Torque:
    2.2 N*m{ 22 kgf*cm , 19 in.*lbf }

  1. Install the 8 screw grommets.

  1. Connect the 2 wire harnesses with the 2 nuts and 2 screw grommets.

    Torque:
    4.0 N*m{ 41 kgf*cm , 35 in.*lbf }



3. CONNECT ENGINE WIRE



  1. Connect the 2 connectors.

  1. Connect the engine wire with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the engine wire with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }



4. CONNECT NO. 4 WATER BY-PASS PIPE



  1. Connect the water by-pass pipe with the 4 bolts and nut.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Attach the clamp and connect the connector.



5. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the No. 1 air cleaner pipe to the No. 1 intake air connector pipe.

  1. Install the pipe with the bolt.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



6. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the No. 2 air cleaner pipe to the No. 2 intake air connector pipe.

  1. Connect the ventilation hose to the oil separator.

  1. Install the pipe with the bolt.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



7. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR HOSE



  1. Connect the No. 2 air hose with the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Align the paint mark of the air hose with the protrusion and push in the air hose so that distance B is 0 to 3 mm (0 to 0.118 in.).
    2. Position the clamp so that distance A is 2 to 6 mm (0.0787 to 0.236 in.).
    3. Make sure the direction of the hose clamp is as shown in the illustration.



8. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR HOSE



  1. Connect the No. 1 air hose with the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Align the paint mark of the air hose with the protrusion and push in the air hose so that distance B is 0 to 3 mm (0 to 0.118 in.).
    2. Position the clamp so that distance A is 2 to 6 mm (0.0787 to 0.236 in.).
    3. Make sure the direction of the hose clamp is as shown in the illustration.



9. INSTALL INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR



  1. Connect the intake air connector to the No. 1 and No. 2 air cleaner pipes.

  1. Install the connector with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the 2 hose clamps.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }




  1. Attach the 3 wire harness clamps.

  1. w/o Viscous Heater:
    Connect the connector to the water temperature sensor.

  1. w/ Viscous Heater:
    Connect the 2 connectors to the water temperature sensor and viscous with magnet clutch heater.



10. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Temporarily install the air cleaner hose to the intake air connector.



11. INSTALL AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the air cleaner cap to the air cleaner hose, and install the air cleaner cap with the 4 clamps.

  1. Connect the mass air flow meter connector and attach the wire harness clamp to the air cleaner cap.

  1. Attach the wire harness clamp.




  1. Align the protrusion of air cleaner cap and concave portion of air cleaner hose.

  1. Tighten the 2 hose clamps.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }



12. INSTALL AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install the air tube to the throttle body.

  1. Align the embossed mark of the throttle body with the paint mark of the No. 4 air hose.




  1. Tighten the hose clamp so that it is 7 to 11 mm (0.276 to 0.433 in.) from the end of the hose as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



13. INSTALL AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install the air tube to the throttle body.

  1. Align the embossed mark of the throttle body with the paint mark of the No. 4 air hose.




  1. Tighten the hose clamp so that it is 7 to 11 mm (0.276 to 0.433 in.) from the end of the hose as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }
