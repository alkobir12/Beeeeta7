VACUUM SWITCHING VALVE (for ACIS) > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT VACUUM SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY (for ACIS)

  1. Disconnect the VSV connector.




  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		20°C (68°F)
    		37 to 44 Ω
    1 - Body ground
    		Always
    		1 MΩ or higher
    2 - Body ground

    If the result is not as specified, replace the vacuum switching valve assembly.



  1. Check the VSV operation.




    1. When applying vacuum to port E, check that air is sucked into the filter.




    2. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the VSV operation.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2
      		When applying vacuum to port F, check that air is sucked into port E

      If the result is not as specified, replace the vacuum switching valve assembly.

  1. Connect the VSV connector.
