CLUTCH PEDAL (for LHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN CLUTCH FLUID


2. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



3. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



4. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL LH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 3 claws and remove the panel garnish.



6. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the panel garnish.



7. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Detach the 7 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



8. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Floor Under Cover)



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the under cover and disconnect the connectors.



9. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD LH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



10. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



11. REMOVE NO. 1 SWITCH HOLE BASE



  1. Detach the 4 claws.

  1. Remove the switch hole base and then disconnect the connectors.



12. REMOVE DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/ Driver Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and driver side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



13. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/o Driver Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the DLC3.

  1. Remove the 5 bolts and instrument panel.



14. REMOVE MAIN BODY ECU (COWL SIDE JUNCTION BLOCK LH)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts.




  1. Disconnect the 9 ECU connectors and remove the ECU.



15. DISCONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE AND CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE



  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the flexible hose tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.

  1. Disconnect the clutch reservoir tube.



16. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SUPPORT ASSEMBLY WITH CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER

  1. Disconnect the clutch start switch connector.

  1. w/ Cruise Control System:
    Disconnect the clutch switch connector.




  1. Remove the 2 nuts, bolt and clutch pedal support with clutch master cylinder.



17. REMOVE TURNOVER SPRING



  1. Move the pedal to the full stroke position.

  1. Remove the turnover spring.



18. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SPRING



  1. Remove the 2 E-rings and the spring with spacer.

  1. Remove the spacer from the spring.



19. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clip, wave washer and clevis pin.




  1. Remove the nut, washer and bolt.

  1. Remove the clutch pedal from the clutch pedal support.



20. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL PAD

  1. Remove the clutch pedal pad from the clutch pedal.



21. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SHAFT COLLAR



  1. Remove the clutch pedal shaft collar from the clutch pedal.



22. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL BUSH



  1. Remove the 2 bushes from the clutch pedal.



23. REMOVE NO. 2 CLUTCH PEDAL CUSHION



  1. Remove the 2 clutch pedal cushions from the clutch pedal.



24. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL TURNOVER BUSH



  1. Remove the clutch pedal turnover bush from the clutch pedal.



25. REMOVE CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER PUSH ROD CLEVIS BUSH



  1. Remove the push rod clevis bush from the clutch pedal.



26. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SPRING HOLDER



  1. Remove the clutch pedal spring holder from the clutch pedal support.
