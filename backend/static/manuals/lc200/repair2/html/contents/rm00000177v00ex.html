1. INSTALL NO. 2 TRANSMISSION HUB SLEEVE



  1. Apply a light coat of gear oil to the No. 2 transmission hub sleeve and No. 2 transmission clutch hub.

  1. Install the No. 2 transmission hub sleeve and 3 shifting keys to the No. 2 transmission clutch hub.

    NOTICE:
    Do not mistake the installation direction of the No. 2 transmission hub sleeve and No. 2 transmission clutch hub.




  1. Insert the 2 springs under the shifting keys.

    NOTICE:
    Position the key springs so that their end gaps are not aligned.



2. INSTALL NO. 3 TRANSMISSION HUB SLEEVE



  1. Apply a light coat of gear oil to the No. 3 transmission hub sleeve and No. 3 transmission clutch hub.

  1. Install the No. 3 transmission hub sleeve and 3 shifting keys to the No. 3 transmission clutch hub.

    NOTICE:
    Do not mistake the installation direction of the No. 3 transmission hub sleeve, No. 3 transmission clutch hub, and shifting keys.




  1. Insert the 2 springs under the shifting keys.

    NOTICE:
    Position the key springs so that their end gaps are not aligned.



3. INSTALL NO. 1 TRANSMISSION HUB SLEEVE

4. INSTALL 5TH COUNTER GEAR BEARING



  1. Coat the 5th counter gear bearing with gear oil, then install it to the 5th gear.



5. INSTALL 5TH GEAR

  1. Coat the 5th gear and No. 3 synchronizer ring with gear oil, and set them onto the output shaft.




  1. Using SST and a press, install the 5th gear, No. 3 synchronizer ring and transmission hub sleeve to the output shaft.

    SST
    09316-60011   (09316-00011)

    HINT:
    Align the convex portion of the synchronizer ring with the groove of the transmission hub sleeve.

  1. Check that the 5th gear, No. 3 synchronizer ring, and transmission hub sleeve move smoothly.

16. INSPECT 5TH GEAR THRUST CLEARANCE



  1. Using a feeler gauge, measure the 5th gear thrust clearance.

    Standard Clearance:
    0.10 to 0.35 mm (0.00394 to 0.0137 in.)

    If the clearance is outside the specification, replace the defective gear or shaft.



17. INSPECT 3RD GEAR THRUST CLEARANCE



  1. Using a feeler gauge, measure the 3rd gear thrust clearance.

    Standard Clearance:
    0.10 to 0.45 mm (0.00394 to 0.0177 in.)

    If the clearance is outside the specification, replace the defective gear, transmission clutch hub or shaft.



18. INSPECT 2ND GEAR THRUST CLEARANCE
