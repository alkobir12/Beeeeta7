1. CHECK ENGINE OIL QUALITY

  1. Check the engine oil for deterioration, water contamination, discoloration or thinning.
    If the quality is visibly poor, replace the oil and filter (Click here).



2. CHECK ENGINE OIL LEVEL

  1. Warm up the engine, then stop the engine and wait for 5 minutes.

  1. Check that the engine oil level is between the low level and full level marks on the dipstick.
    If low, check for leakage and add oil up to the full level mark.

    NOTICE:
    Do not fill engine oil above the full level mark.



3. INSPECT AND ADJUST OIL PRESSURE

  1. Remove the oil pressure sender gauge (Click here).




  1. Install an oil pressure gauge with an adapter.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Measure the oil pressure.

    Standard Oil Pressure:
    Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Idle
    		29 kPa (0.3 kgf/cm2, 4.2 psi) or more
    3000 rpm
    		294 to 588 kPa (3.0 to 6.0 kgf/cm2, 43 to 85 psi)

    If the pressure is not as specified, check the oil pump (Click here).

  1. Remove the oil pressure gauge and adapter.

  1. Install the oil pressure sender gauge (Click here).
