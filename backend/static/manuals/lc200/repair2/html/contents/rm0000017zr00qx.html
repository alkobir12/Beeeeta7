FRONT AIR CONDITIONING UNIT (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. for Standard:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).

  1. w/ Winch:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

  1. for Standard:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).

  1. w/ Winch:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



4. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL


  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.





5. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT

  1. for 1VD-FTV:
    Drain the engine coolant (Click here).

  1. for 2UZ-FE:
    Drain the engine coolant (Click here).

  1. for 1GR-FE:
    Drain the engine coolant (Click here).



6. RECOVER REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM

  1. Start the engine.

  1. Turn the A/C switch on.

  1. Operate the cooler compressor while the engine speed is approximately 1000 rpm for 5 to 6 minutes to circulate the refrigerant and collect the compressor oil remaining in each component into the cooler compressor.

  1. Stop the engine.

  1. Recover the refrigerant from the A/C system using a refrigerant recovery unit.



7. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



8. DISCONNECT AIR CONDITIONING TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt.

  1. Detach the plate as shown in the illustration.




  1. Disconnect the air conditioning tube assembly.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not use a screwdriver or similar tool to disconnect the tube.
    2. Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering them.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the air conditioning tube assembly.



9. DISCONNECT HEATER WATER INLET HOSE AND HEATER WATER OUTLET HOSE



  1. Using pliers, grip the claws of the clips and slide the 2 clips.

  1. Disconnect the 2 heater water hoses.



10. REMOVE FRONT WIPER MOTOR AND BRACKET

  1. Remove the front wiper motor and bracket (Click here).



11. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the instrument panel (Click here).



12. REMOVE NO. 3 AIR DUCT SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the duct.



13. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN ASSEMBLY

  1. for Power Tilt and Power Telescopic Steering Column:
    Remove the steering column (Click here).

  1. for Manual Tilt and Manual Telescopic Steering Column:
    Remove the steering column (Click here).



14. REMOVE HEATER TO REGISTER DUCT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 3 clips.



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the duct.





15. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL REINFORCEMENT ASSEMBLY


  1. Disconnect the clamps, connectors and wire harness.





  1. Remove the instrument panel reinforcement.

    1. for Driver Side:
      Remove the 3 caps and 3 bolts.

    2. for Passenger Side:
      Remove the 2 caps.

    3. for Passenger Side:
      Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 2 "TORX" bolts.

      HINT:
      When removing the bolts, the collars may come off with the bolts.

    4. for Passenger Side:
      Using a 12 mm hexagon wrench, remove the 2 collars.



    5. Remove the 7 bolts, screw and instrument panel reinforcement.





16. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING HOSE AND ACCESSORY (w/ Cool Box)



  1. Remove the bolt.



  1. Using SST, remove the piping clamp.

    SST
    09870-00025  




    1. Attach SST to the piping clamp.

      HINT:
      Confirm the direction of the piping clamp claw and SST by referring to the illustration on the caution label.




    2. Push down SST and release the clamp lock.

      NOTICE:
      Be careful not to deform the tubes when pushing SST.

    3. Pull SST slightly and push the release lever, then remove the piping clamp with SST.

    4. Remove the piping clamp from SST.

  1. Disconnect the hose and accessory from the liquid tube to remove it.

    NOTICE:
    Cap the open fittings immediately to keep moisture or dirt out of the system.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the air conditioning hose and accessory.



17. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING UNIT



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and air conditioning unit.
