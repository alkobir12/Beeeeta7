COMPRESSOR (for 1VD-FTV) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. RECOVER REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM

  1. Start the engine.

  1. Turn the A/C switch on.

  1. Operate the cooler compressor while the engine speed is approximately 1000 rpm for 5 to 6 minutes to circulate the refrigerant and collect the compressor oil remaining in each component into the cooler compressor.

  1. Stop the engine.

  1. Recover the refrigerant from the A/C system using a refrigerant recovery unit.



2. REMOVE RADIATOR ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the radiator assembly (Click here).



3. REMOVE INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the intercooler assembly (Click here).



4. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose from the oil separator.

  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 air cleaner pipe.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 air tube.

  1. Remove the O-ring.



6. DISCONNECT NO. 1 COOLER REFRIGERANT DISCHARGE HOSE



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the discharge hose from the cooler compressor.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the discharge hose.

    NOTICE:
    Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering them.



7. DISCONNECT SUCTION HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the suction hose from the cooler compressor.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the suction hose.

    NOTICE:
    Seal the openings of the disconnected parts using vinyl tape to prevent moisture and foreign matter from entering them.



8. REMOVE COOLER COMPRESSOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts.




  1. Remove the cooler compressor as shown in the illustration.
