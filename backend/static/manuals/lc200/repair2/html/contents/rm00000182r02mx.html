DTC    58-10    Gyro Error

DTC    80-10    Gyro Error

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
58-10
A ground short, power supply short, or open circuit in the gyro signal.
  1. Gyro sensor
  2. Navigation ECU
80-10
A ground short, power supply short, or open circuit in the gyro signal.
