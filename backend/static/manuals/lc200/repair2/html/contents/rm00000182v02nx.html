DTC No.

A difference between the GPS speed and SPD pulse is detected.

A difference between the GPS speed and SPD pulse is detected.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
After the inspection is completed, clear the DTCs (Click here).

1.CHECK VEHICLE SENSOR (NAVIGATION CHECK MODE)



  1. Enter the "Navigation Check" mode and select "Vehicle Sensors" (Click here).

  1. While driving the vehicle, compare the "SPD" indicator to the reading on the speedometer. Check if these readings are almost equal.

    OK:
    The readings are almost equal.
