DESCRIPTION


DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
61-40
A malfunction due to mechanical problem has occurred, or cassette tape is cut or entangled.
  1. Cassette tape
  2. Radio receiver assembly
61-41
A malfunction due to mechanical problem
61-42
Cassette tape is tangled.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
After the inspection is completed, clear the DTCs.

1.CHECK OPERATION

  1. Press the cassette tape EJECT switch of the radio receiver for 2 seconds or more and check that the cassette tape is ejected.

    OK:
    Cassette tape is ejected.
