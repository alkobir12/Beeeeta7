DTC    P0087    Fuel Rail / System Pressure - Too Low

DTC    P0190    Fuel Rail Pressure Sensor Circuit

DTC    P0192    Fuel Rail Pressure Sensor Circuit Low Input

DTC    P0193    Fuel Rail Pressure Sensor Circuit High Input

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The ECM monitors the internal fuel pressure of the common rail using the fuel pressure sensor, and controls the suction control valve to regulate the internal pressure to the target pressure. The pressure sensor is a semiconductor that varies electrical resistance when pressure is applied to its silicon chip. This sensor outputs the voltage in proportion to the internal fuel pressure.

  2. For more information on the fuel pressure sensor and common rail system, refer to the System Description (Click here).
  3. If DTC P0087 is stored, the following symptoms may appear:
    1. Black smoke
    2. Poor drivability
    3. Lack of power
  4. If DTC P0190, P0192 and/or P0193 is stored, the following symptoms may appear.
    1. Lack of power due to accelerator restriction of fail-safe function


MONITOR DESCRIPTION

  1. P0087 (Fuel pressure sensor output stays at fixed value):
    1. Under normal conditions, the internal fuel pressure of the common rail usually fluctuates 1 to 2 MPa (10 to 20 kgf/cm2, 145 to 290 psi) even when the driving conditions are constant. This DTC is stored if there is no fluctuation of the fuel pressure. The internal fuel pressure is approximately 27 to 37 MPa (275 to 377 kgf/cm2, 3915 to 5365 psi) when idling, and it increases to approximately 50 to 70 MPa (510 to 714 kgf/cm2, 7250 to 10150 psi) when running the engine at 2500 rpm.
      If this DTC is stored, the ECM enters fail-safe mode and limits engine power. The fail-safe mode continues until the engine switch is turned off.

    2. The diagram below indicates that fuel pressure rises as engine speed increases, but for the malfunction indicated by this DTC, that does not happen.



  1. P0190, P0192 and P0193 (Open or short in fuel pressure sensor circuit):
    These DTCs are stored if the fuel pressure sensor output voltage is out of the standard range due to an open or short malfunction of the sensor circuit.
    If these DTCs are stored, the ECM enters fail-safe mode and limits the engine power. The fail-safe mode continues until the engine switch is turned off.

8.CONFIRM WHETHER MALFUNCTION HAS BEEN SUCCESSFULLY REPAIRED

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Clear the DTCs (Click here).

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  1. Let the engine idle for 60 seconds, and then repeat quick engine speed accelerations (to 2500 rpm) for 30 seconds.

  1. Confirm that the DTC is not output again.

    HINT:
    Perform the following procedure using the tester to determine whether or not the DTC judgment has been carried out.

    1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Utility / All Readiness.

    2. Input DTC P0087, P0190, P0192 and/or P0193.

    3. Check that STATUS is NORMAL. If STATUS is INCOMPLETE or UNKNOWN, increase idling time.
