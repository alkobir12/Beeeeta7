REAR BRAKE > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. INSTALL REAR DISC



  1. Align the matchmarks, and then install the rear disc.

    HINT:
    When replacing the rear disc with a new one, select the installation position where the rear disc has the minimum runout.



2. INSTALL PARKING BRAKE SHOE ADJUSTING HOLE PLUG


3. INSTALL REAR DISC BRAKE CYLINDER MOUNTING LH

  1. Install a new boot and a new cylinder slide bush to the rear disc brake cylinder mounting.

    NOTICE:
    Install the boot securely to the grooves of the rear disc brake cylinder mounting.




  1. Install the rear disc brake cylinder mounting with 2 new bolts.

    Torque:
    95 N*m{ 969 kgf*cm , 70 ft.*lbf }



4. INSTALL REAR NO. 1 DISC BRAKE PAD SUPPORT PLATE

NOTICE:
  1. The pad support plates can be reused if they are securely attached to the cylinder mounting. Therefore, do not remove the pad support plates from the cylinder mounting.
  2. If a pad support plate is removed, replace it with a new one that is supplied with double-sided tape already attached.
  3. Make sure to clean the cylinder mounting contact surface before attaching the pad support plates.

  1. Clean the cylinder mounting surface where the pad support plates will be attached.

  1. Remove the peeling paper from the double-sided tape, and install 2 new pad support plates to the cylinder mounting.



5. INSTALL REAR NO. 2 DISK BRAKE PAD SUPPORT PLATE

NOTICE:
  1. The pad support plates can be reused if they are securely attached to the cylinder mounting. Therefore, do not remove the pad support plates from the cylinder mounting.
  2. If a pad support plate is removed, replace it with a new one that is supplied with double-sided tape already attached.
  3. Make sure to clean the cylinder mounting contact surface before attaching the pad support plates.

  1. Clean the cylinder mounting surface where the pad support plates will be attached.

  1. Remove the peeling paper from the double-sided tape, and install 2 new pad support plates to the cylinder mounting.



6. INSTALL REAR DISC BRAKE PAD WEAR INDICATOR PLATE

  1. Install the pad wear indicator plate to the inner side disc brake pad.



7. INSTALL REAR DISC BRAKE ANTI-SQUEAL SHIM KIT

  1. Install the anti-squeal shims to each brake pad.

    NOTICE:
    There should be no oil or grease on the friction surfaces of the brake pads and the rear disc.



8. INSTALL REAR DISC BRAKE PAD

NOTICE:
  1. When installing the pads, make sure that the No. 1 and No. 2 disc brake pad support plates are securely attached to the cylinder mounting. The pad support plates are attached to the cylinder mounting with double-sided tape.
  2. If a pad support plate is not securely attached to the cylinder mounting, replace it with a new one.

  1. Install the 2 disc brake pads to the disc brake cylinder mounting.



9. INSTALL REAR DISC BRAKE CYLINDER ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Apply lithium soap base glycol grease to the sliding part of the 2 cylinder slide pins.




  1. Install the cylinder with the 2 cylinder slide pins.

    Torque:
    88 N*m{ 897 kgf*cm , 65 ft.*lbf }



10. CONNECT REAR FLEXIBLE HOSE LH



  1. Install a new gasket and connect the flexible hose with the union bolt.

    Torque:
    30 N*m{ 306 kgf*cm , 22 ft.*lbf }



11. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID

Fluid:
SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT3



12. BLEED BRAKE LINE


  1. Bleed air from the front brake system.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

      HINT:
      Perform this procedure with the engine switch on (IG), as the large piston of the master cylinder does not stroke with the engine switch off.




    2. Connect a vinyl tube to the brake caliper.

    3. Depress the brake pedal several times, then loosen the bleeder plug with the pedal held down (procedure "A").

      NOTICE:
      While performing the air bleeding procedure, make sure that the amount of fluid in the reservoir remains above the MIN line.

    4. At the point when the fluid stops coming out, tighten the bleeder plug, then release the brake pedal (procedure "B").

      Torque:
      11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

    5. Repeat procedure "A" to "B" until all the air in the fluid has been bled out.

    6. Repeat the above procedures to bleed the other front brake line.



  1. Bleed air from the rear brake system.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and depress the brake pedal.




    2. Connect a vinyl tube to the brake caliper.

    3. Loosen the bleeder plug and release air.

      NOTICE:
      While performing the air bleeding procedure, make sure that the amount of fluid in the reservoir remains above the MIN line.

      HINT:
      Brake fluid is sent through the pump, so keep the brake pedal depressed until the air is completely bled out.

    4. When the air is completely bled out of the brake fluid through the bleeder plug, tighten the bleeder plug.

      Torque:
      11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

    5. Repeat the above procedures to bleed the other rear brake line.

  1. Connect the intelligent tester.

    1. Turn the engine switch off.

    2. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / Utility / Air Bleeding.

      HINT:
      Please refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

  1. Bleed front right brake line.

    1. Select "FR Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "FR Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, depress the brake pedal and hold it to bleed the front right brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed front left brake line.

    1. Select "FL Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "FL Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, depress the brake pedal and hold it to bleed the front left brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed rear right brake line.

    1. Select "RR Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "RR Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, bleed the left brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed rear left brake line.

    1. Select "RL Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "RL Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, bleed the left brake line.

  1. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.

  1. Clear the DTCs (Click here).



13. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR



  1. When inspecting or refilling with the engine switch off, depress the brake pedal 40 times or more (there will be less pedal resistance and the pedal stroke will lengthen), and then add fluid until the fluid level is at the MAX line.

    HINT:
    When the engine switch is turned on (IG), fluid is sent to the accumulator causing the fluid level to decrease 10 mm (0.394 in.) from the level when the engine switch is off (this is normal).

    Brake Fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS NO. 116 DOT3



14. INSPECT FOR BRAKE FLUID LEAK


15. ADJUST PARKING BRAKE LEVER TRAVEL

  1. Remove the console cup holder box sub-assembly (Click here).

  1. Completely release the parking brake lever.

  1. Loosen the adjusting nut to completely release the parking brake cable.




  1. Temporarily install the hub nuts.

  1. Remove the hole plug.




  1. Insert an adjustment tool into the adjustment hole of the disc. Rotate the adjustment wheel in the "X" direction until the shoes are locked. Then rotate the adjustment wheel in the "Y" direction 8 notches.

  1. Check that the disc can be rotated lightly. If not, rotate the adjustment wheel in the "Y" direction and check again.

  1. Install the hole plug.

  1. Remove the hub nuts.




  1. Turn the adjusting nut until the parking brake lever travel becomes correct.

    Standard Parking Brake Lever Travel when Pulled with a Force of 200 N (20 kgf, 45 lbf):
    5 to 7 clicks

  1. Operate the parking brake lever 3 to 4 times, and check the parking brake lever travel.

    Standard Parking Brake Lever Travel when Pulled with a Force of 200 N (20 kgf, 45 lbf):
    5 to 7 clicks

  1. Check whether the parking brake drags or not.

  1. When operating the parking brake lever, check that the brake warning light comes on.

    Standard Condition:
    The brake warning light always illuminates at the first click.

  1. Install the console cup holder box sub-assembly (Click here).
