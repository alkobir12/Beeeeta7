for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a vernier caliper and straightedge, measure dimension A between the end surface of the engine and the torque converter contact surface of the drive plate.

  1. Install the torque converter clutch to the transmission housing.




  1. Using a vernier caliper and straightedge, measure dimension B shown in the illustration and check that B is more than A measured in the first step.

    Standard distance:
    B = A + 1.00 mm (0.0394 in.) or more



2. INSTALL TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the transfer with the 8 bolts.

    Torque:
    40 N*m{ 408 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the oil seal of the transfer adapter.




  1. Install the transfer case lower protector with the 7 bolts.

    Torque:
    14 N*m{ 143 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the clamp of the ground cable to the transfer case lower protector.




  1. Attach the 2 breather hose clamps to connect the transfer breather hose to the automatic transmission breather tube.



3. INSTALL HARNESS CLAMP BRACKET

  1. Install the 2 harness clamp brackets with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    29 N*m{ 296 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }



4. INSTALL TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER ASSEMBLY (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Coat 2 new O-rings with ATF, and install them to the transmission oil cooler.

  1. Align the transmission oil cooler with the transmission oil thermostat and assemble them with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    8.0 N*m{ 82 kgf*cm , 71 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 oil cooler hoses to the transmission oil thermostat with the 2 clips.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the pinching portion of each clip is facing the direction shown in the illustration.




  1. Temporarily install the transmission oil cooler together with the transmission oil thermostat with the bolt A. Install the bolt B and tighten it to the specified torque. Then tighten the bolt A to the specified torque.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 hoses with the 2 clips.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the pinching portion of each clip is facing the direction shown in the illustration.



5. INSTALL AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



NOTICE:
Check that the 2 straight pins are attached to the engine side.

  1. Install the transmission with the 9 bolts.

    Torque:
    for 17 mm head bolt A:
    71 N*m{ 724 kgf*cm , 52 ft.*lbf }
    for 14 mm head bolt B:
    37 N*m{ 377 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }



6. CONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Connect the 2 water by-pass hoses with the 2 clips and install the water by-pass pipe with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure the pinching portion of each clip is facing the direction shown in the illustration.
    2. Make sure the paint mark of each hose is facing outward.



7. CONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Temporarily install the oil cooler tube to the engine with the bolt A. Install the bolt B and tighten it to the specified torque. Then tighten the bolt A to the specified torque.

    Torque:
    14 N*m{ 143 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 transmission oil cooler hoses to the transmission oil cooler.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the pinching portion of each clip is facing the direction shown in the illustration.



8. CONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Temporarily install the oil cooler tube to the engine with the bolt A. Install the bolt B and tighten it to the specified torque. Then tighten the bolt A to the specified torque.

    Torque:
    14 N*m{ 143 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 transmission oil cooler hoses to the oil cooler tube union.

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure the pinching portion of each clip is facing the direction shown in the illustration.
    2. Make sure the paint mark of each hose is facing outward.



9. CONNECT BREATHER PLUG HOSE



  1. Connect the breather plug hose and attach the transfer breather hose.



10. INSTALL NO. 2 MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Install the stay with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    40 N*m{ 408 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }



11. INSTALL MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Install the stay with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    40 N*m{ 408 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }



12. CONNECT WIRE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR


  1. Connect the park/neutral position switch connector, transmission wire connector, 2 speed sensor connectors and 2 transfer control side connectors.



    HINT:
    Push up the lever until the claw of the transmission wire connector makes a connection sound.

  1. Connect the harness with the bolt.

    Torque:
    8.0 N*m{ 82 kgf*cm , 71 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 connector clamps and 6 harness clamps.

  1. Tilt up the automatic transmission.




  1. Connect the ground cable with the bolt.

    Torque:
    8.4 N*m{ 86 kgf*cm , 74 in.*lbf }



13. INSTALL REAR NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR



  1. Install the rear engine mounting insulator to the transmission with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    59 N*m{ 602 kgf*cm , 44 ft.*lbf }



14. INSTALL NO. 2 FRAME CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the frame crossmember to the rear engine mounting insulator with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    37 N*m{ 377 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the frame crossmember to the frame with the 4 bolts and 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    110 N*m{ 1122 kgf*cm , 81 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the engine mounting hole cover.



15. INSTALL DRIVE PLATE AND TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH SETTING BOLT



  1. Turn the crankshaft to gain access to the installation locations of the 6 bolts and install each bolt while holding the crankshaft pulley setting bolt with a wrench.

    Torque:
    48 N*m{ 489 kgf*cm , 35 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    First install the black colored bolt and then install the remaining 5 bolts.




  1. Install the flywheel housing side cover.



16. INSTALL STARTER ASSEMBLY (for 2.0 kW Type)



  1. Install the starter with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    58 N*m{ 591 kgf*cm , 43 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the starter connector.

  1. Connect the starter wire with the nut.

    Torque:
    9.8 N*m{ 100 kgf*cm , 87 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the terminal cap.



17. INSTALL STARTER ASSEMBLY (for 1.6 kW Type)



  1. Install the starter with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    58 N*m{ 591 kgf*cm , 43 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the starter connector.

  1. Connect the starter wire with the nut.

    Torque:
    9.8 N*m{ 100 kgf*cm , 87 in.*lbf }

  1. Install the terminal cap.



18. CONNECT FLOOR SHIFT GEAR SHIFTING ROD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the gear shifting rod to the transmission control shaft lever RH with the pin and a new clip.



19. INSTALL FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Install a new gasket and the front exhaust pipe to the exhaust manifold RH with 2 new nuts.

    Torque:
    54 N*m{ 554 kgf*cm , 40 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the heated oxygen sensor connector.



20. INSTALL FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Install a new gasket and the front No. 2 exhaust pipe to the exhaust manifold LH with 2 new nuts.

    Torque:
    54 N*m{ 554 kgf*cm , 40 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

    HINT:
    Hook the wire harness to the bracket.



21. INSTALL CENTER EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the free length of the compression spring.

    Minimum free length:
    43 mm (1.693 in.)

    If the free length is less that the minimum, replace the compression spring.

  1. Connect the center exhaust pipe to the 3 exhaust pipe supports.

  1. Install 2 new gaskets to the front exhaust pipe and No. 2 front exhaust pipe.

  1. Install the center exhaust pipe with the 4 bolts and 2 compression springs.

    Torque:
    for center exhaust pipe and front exhaust pipe:
    49 N*m{ 496 kgf*cm , 36 ft.*lbf }
    for center exhaust pipe and No. 2 front exhaust pipe:
    48 N*m{ 489 kgf*cm , 35 ft.*lbf }

  1. Set a new gasket to the center exhaust pipe.




  1. Connect the tailpipe to the center pipe with a new clamp.

    Torque:
    32 N*m{ 326 kgf*cm , 24 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    Install the clamp within the angle range shown in the illustration.

26. INSPECT SHIFT LEVER POSITION

  1. When moving the shift lever from P to R with the engine switch on (IG) and the brake pedal depressed, make sure that it moves smoothly and correctly into position.

  1. Check that the shift lever does not stop when moving the shift lever from R to P, and check that the shift lever does not stick when moving the shift lever from D to S.



  1. Start the engine and make sure that the vehicle moves forward after moving the shift lever from N to D and moves rearward after moving the shift lever to R.
    If there are no problems during the above inspections, perform the adjustment using the following procedures.

    1. Move the shift lever to N




    2. Loosen the nut of the floor shift gear shifting rod. Then, with the lever of the floor shift assembly lightly pushed towards the rear of the vehicle, tighten the nut.

      Torque:
      13 N*m{ 130 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



27. INSPECT FOR EXHAUST GAS LEAK
If gas is leaking, tighten the areas necessary to stop the leak. Replace damaged parts as necessary.
