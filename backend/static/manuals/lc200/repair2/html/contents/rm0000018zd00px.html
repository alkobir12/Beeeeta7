AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY (for 2UZ-FE) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



6. DRAIN AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID

  1. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the ATF.

  1. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



7. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)


CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  1. Remove the radiator cap. Then drain the coolant.

    HINT:
    Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.

  1. Loosen the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs. Then drain the coolant from the engine.

  1. Tighten the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 130 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.



8. REMOVE FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the front propeller shaft (Click here).



9. REMOVE PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the propeller shaft (Click here).



10. REMOVE TAILPIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the bolt, clamp and gasket.

  1. Remove the tailpipe from the 2 exhaust pipe supports.



11. REMOVE CENTER EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Remove the center exhaust pipe from the 3 exhaust pipe supports.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the front exhaust pipe and front No. 2 exhaust pipe.



12. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts, front No. 2 exhaust pipe and gasket.



13. REMOVE FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Detach the heated oxygen sensor wire clamp.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts, front exhaust pipe and gasket.



14. DISCONNECT FLOOR SHIFT GEAR SHIFTING ROD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clip and pin, and disconnect the gear shifting rod from the transmission control shaft lever RH.



15. REMOVE DRIVE PLATE AND TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH SETTING BOLT



  1. Remove the bolt and flywheel housing under cover.




  1. Turn the crankshaft to gain access to the 6 bolts and remove each bolt while holding the crankshaft pulley setting bolt with a wrench.



16. SUPPORT AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



  1. Support the transmission with a transmission jack. Lift the transmission slightly from the crossmember.



17. REMOVE NO. 2 FRAME CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the engine mounting hole cover.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts of the rear engine mounting insulator.




  1. Remove the 4 nuts, 4 bolts and frame crossmember.



18. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear engine mounting insulator from the transmission.



19. DISCONNECT WIRE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground cable.




  1. Tilt the transmission downward.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the cooling fan does not contact the fan shroud.



  1. Disconnect the park/neutral position switch connector, transmission wire connector, 2 speed sensor connectors and 2 transfer control side connectors.



    HINT:
    Detach the claw, press down the lever, and then disconnect the transmission wire connector.

  1. Disconnect the 4 connector clamps and 7 harness clamps.

  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the wire harness.



20. DISCONNECT TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER HOSE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Disconnect the 2 transmission oil cooler hoses from the transmission.



21. DISCONNECT TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER HOSE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Disconnect the 2 transmission oil cooler hoses from the transmission oil thermostat.



22. DISCONNECT NO. 2 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the bolt and hose heat protector.




  1. Remove the bolt, and disconnect the 2 water by-pass hoses from the transmission oil cooler, and separate the by-pass pipe from the automatic transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Use a container to catch any coolant which flows out of the water by-pass hoses.



23. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER ASSEMBLY (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Disconnect the 2 oil cooler hoses.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and transmission oil cooler with transmission oil thermostat.




  1. Remove the 3 bolts and separate the transmission oil cooler from the transmission oil thermostat.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings.



24. REMOVE NO. 2 MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay.



25. REMOVE MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay.



26. DISCONNECT BREATHER PLUG HOSE





27. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Do not use excess force when prying the transmission assembly.



28. REMOVE HARNESS CLAMP BRACKET

  1. w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler:
    Remove the 4 bolts and 4 brackets.

  1. w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler:
    Remove the 3 bolts and 3 brackets.



29. REMOVE TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt, detach the 2 breather hose clamps and disconnect the transfer breather hose from the automatic transmission breather tube.




  1. Disconnect the clamp of the ground cable from the transfer case lower protector.

  1. Remove the 7 bolts and transfer case lower protector.




  1. Remove the 8 bolts and transfer.



30. REMOVE TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY


31. INSPECT TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

  1. Inspect the torque converter clutch (Click here).
