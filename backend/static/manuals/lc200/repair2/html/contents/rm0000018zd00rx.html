AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



6. REMOVE OIL PAN PROTECTOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and oil pan protector.



7. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL FRONT RH



  1. Remove the 3 clips and front fender apron seal front RH.



8. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR RH



  1. Remove the 4 clips and front fender apron seal rear RH.



9. DRAIN AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID

  1. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the ATF.

  1. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



10. DISCONNECT FLOOR SHIFT GEAR SHIFTING ROD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clip and pin, and disconnect the gear shifting rod from the transmission control shaft lever RH.



11. REMOVE FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the front propeller shaft (Click here).



12. REMOVE PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the propeller shaft (Click here).



13. REMOVE CENTER EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 compression springs.

  1. Disconnect the center exhaust pipe from the 3 exhaust pipe supports.

  1. Remove the gasket from the front exhaust pipe.



14. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the gasket.



15. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 2 nuts, 2 bolts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the gasket.



16. REMOVE OUTLET TURBINE ELBOW RH (w/o EGR System)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay RH.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts and outlet turbine elbow RH.

  1. Remove the gasket from the turbocharger.



17. REMOVE MONOLITHIC CONVERTER ASSEMBLY RH (w/ EGR System)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay RH.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts and monolithic converter RH.

  1. Remove the gasket from the turbocharger.



18. REMOVE OUTLET TURBINE ELBOW LH (w/o EGR System)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay LH.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts and outlet turbine elbow LH.

  1. Remove the gasket from the turbocharger.



19. REMOVE MONOLITHIC CONVERTER ASSEMBLY LH (w/ EGR System)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay LH.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts and monolithic converter LH.

  1. Remove the gasket from the turbocharger.



20. REMOVE DRIVE PLATE AND TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH SETTING BOLT



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and oil pan insulator.




  1. Turn the crankshaft to gain access to the 6 bolts and remove the bolts while holding the crankshaft pulley setting bolt with a wrench.



21. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE



  1. Disconnect the 2 transmission oil cooler hoses from the oil cooler tube union.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and separate the oil cooler tube from the engine.



22. SUPPORT AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



  1. Support the transmission with a transmission jack. Lift the transmission slightly from the crossmember.



23. REMOVE NO. 2 FRAME CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the engine mounting hole cover.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts of the rear engine mounting insulator.




  1. Remove the 4 nuts, 4 bolts and frame crossmember.



24. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear engine mounting insulator from the transmission.



25. DISCONNECT BREATHER PLUG HOSE



  1. Disconnect the 3 breather plug hoses from the wire harness.



26. DISCONNECT WIRE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground cable.




  1. Tilt the transmission downward.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the cooling fan does not contact the fan shroud.



  1. Disconnect the park/neutral position switch connector, 2 transmission wire connectors, 2 speed sensor connectors and 2 or 3 transfer control side connectors.



    HINT:
    Detach the claw, press down the lever, and then disconnect the transmission wire connector.

  1. Disconnect the 8 harness clamps.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the wire harness.



27. REMOVE OIL PAN COVER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and oil pan cover.



28. REMOVE TRANSMISSION HOUSING COVER



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and transmission housing cover.



29. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Do not use excess force to pry off the transmission assembly.



30. REMOVE TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 7 bolts and transfer case lower protector.




  1. Remove the 8 bolts and transfer.



31. REMOVE TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY


32. INSPECT TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

  1. Inspect the torque converter clutch (Click here).
