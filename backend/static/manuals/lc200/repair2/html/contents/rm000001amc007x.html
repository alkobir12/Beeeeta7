1. INSTALL REAR PROPELLER SHAFT



  1. Align the matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and transfer.

  1. Connect the propeller shaft with the 4 washers and 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    88 N*m{ 900 kgf*cm , 65 ft.*lbf }

    Torque:
    88 N*m{ 900 kgf*cm , 65 ft.*lbf }
