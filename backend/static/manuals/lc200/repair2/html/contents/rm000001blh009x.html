BACK DOOR > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE BACK DOOR GRIP



  1. Remove the 2 screws and back door grip.



3. REMOVE LOWER BACK DOOR STOPPER CUSHION

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 lower back door stopper cushions.



4. REMOVE CENTER BACK DOOR GARNISH



  1. Detach the 5 clips and 2 claws, and remove the back door garnish.



5. REMOVE CENTER STOP LIGHT ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the stop light.



6. REMOVE BACK DOOR SIDE GARNISH LH



  1. Detach the 3 clips and remove the back door side garnish.



7. REMOVE BACK DOOR SIDE GARNISH RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



8. REMOVE ASSIST GRIP (for Face to Face Seat Type)



  1. Remove the 2 screws and assist grip.



9. REMOVE NO. 2 BACK DOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER (for Face to Face Seat Type)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the back door service hole cover.

  1. Disconnect connector.



10. REMOVE POWER BACK DOOR MAIN SWITCH (for Face to Face Seat Type)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the power back door main switch.



11. REMOVE BACK DOOR GARNISH



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 14 clips and remove the back door garnish.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



12. REMOVE REAR ASSIST GRIP REINFORCEMENT (for Face to Face Seat Type)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the rear assist grip reinforcement.



13. REMOVE REAR WIPER ARM (w/ Rear Wiper)



  1. Open the cover.

  1. Remove the nut and rear wiper arm.



14. REMOVE REAR WIPER MOTOR GROMMET (w/ Rear Wiper)



  1. Detach the wiper motor grommet.



15. REMOVE REAR WIPER MOTOR ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Wiper)



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and rear wiper motor assembly.



16. REMOVE BACK DOOR LOCK COVER



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the back door lock cover.



17. REMOVE BACK DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and back door lock.



18. REMOVE LICENSE PLATE LIGHT LENS (for Standard)



  1. Detach the 2 claws in the order shown in the illustration and remove the license plate light.

  1. Disconnect the connector.



19. REMOVE BACK DOOR OPENER SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Disconnect the connector and then remove the back door opener switch.



20. REMOVE REAR TELEVISION CAMERA ASSEMBLY (w/ Parking Assist Monitor System or Rear Veiw Monitor System)



  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the 2 screws.




  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the rear television camera assembly.

  1. Disconnect the connector.



21. REMOVE LIFT GATE WEATHERSTRIP



  1. Detach the 25 clips and remove the lift gate weatherstrip.

    NOTICE:
    Do not pull strongly on the weatherstrip as it may tear.



22. REMOVE LOWER BACK DOOR STOPPER



  1. Remove the bolt and lower back door stopper.



23. REMOVE BACK DOOR GARNISH SUB-ASSEMBLY OUTSIDE

  1. Put protective tape around the back door garnish.



  1. Detach the 4 clips and remove the double-sided tape to remove the back door garnish.





24. REMOVE REAR SPOILER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Spoiler)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.




  1. Detach the 3 clips and remove the rear spoiler.



25. REMOVE REAR WASHER NOZZLE (w/ Rear Wiper)



  1. Detach the hose.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the washer nozzle.



26. REMOVE BACK DOOR STAY ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and stay.

    CAUTION:
    Remove the door stay while supporting the back door with one hand.




  1. Remove the bolt and stay.

    CAUTION:
    Remove the door stay while supporting the back door with one hand.



27. REMOVE BACK DOOR STAY ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



28. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



29. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM COVER LH



  1. Remove the back door trim cover LH as shown in the illustration.



30. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM COVER RH



  1. Remove the back door trim cover RH as shown in the illustration.



31. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 16 clips and remove the back door trim panel.



32. REMOVE NO. 2 BACK DOOR GARNISH SUB-ASSEMBLY OUTSIDE (w/ Tire Carrier)


33. REMOVE LICENSE PLATE LIGHT ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)


34. REMOVE REAR LICENSE LIGHT COVER (w/ Tire Carrier)


35. REMOVE REAR BUMPER COVER

  1. for Standard:

    1. Remove the bumper cover (Click here).

  1. w/ Towing Hitch:

    1. Remove the bumper cover (Click here)

  1. w/ Pintle Hook:

    1. Remove the bumper cover (Click here)



36. REMOVE SPARE TIRE


37. REMOVE LOWER BACK DOOR TORSION BAR ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and lower back door torsion bar.



38. REMOVE BACK DOOR TORSION BAR GUIDE



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the back door torsion bar guide.



39. REMOVE BACK DOOR INSIDE HANDLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the screw and back door inside handle.

  1. Remove the back door handle grommet.



40. REMOVE LOWER TAIL GATE LOCK ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Using a T30 "TORX" wrench, remove the 3 screws and lower tail gate lock.




  1. Disconnect the cable from the the lower tail gate lock.



41. REMOVE BACK DOOR REMOTE CONTROL ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws.




  1. Disconnect the left side cable.

  1. Remove the back door remote control.



42. REMOVE LOWER TAIL GATE LOCK ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using a T30 "TORX" wrench, remove the 3 screws and lower tail gate lock.



43. REMOVE TAIL GATE STAY SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 2 screws and tail gate stay.



44. REMOVE TAIL GATE STAY SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
