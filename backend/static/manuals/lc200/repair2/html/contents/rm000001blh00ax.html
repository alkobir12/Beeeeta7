1. INSTALL TAIL GATE STAY SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Clean the threaded surface on the vehicle body with a non-residue solvent.

  1. Apply adhesive to the threads of the screws.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent




  1. Using a T40 "TORX" wrench, install the 2 screws and tail gate stay.

11. INSTALL REAR LICENSE LIGHT COVER (w/ Tire Carrier)

  1. Clean the vehicle body surface.

    1. Using a heat light, heat the vehicle body surface.

    2. Remove the double-sided tape from the vehicle body.

    3. Wipe off any tape adhesive residue with cleaner.




  1. Install a new rear license light cover.

    1. Using a heat light, heat the vehicle body and a new rear license light cover.

    2. Remove the peeling paper from the face of the emblem.

      HINT:
      After removing the peeling paper, keep the exposed adhesive free from foreign matter.

    3. Attach the 7 claws to install the rear license light cover.

      HINT:
      Press the rear license light cover firmly to install it.

18. INSTALL BACK DOOR STAY ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Horizontally fix the stay in a vise with the piston-rod pulled out.

  1. Using a metal saw, gradually cut any area between A and B to release the gas.

    Standard Measurement:
    Area
    		Specified Condition
    a
    		150 mm (5.91 in.)
    b
    		20 mm (0.787 in.)

    CAUTION:
    As metal debris may be blown outward by the gas, you must:
    1. Wear protective glasses.
    2. Cover the area being cut.

    NOTICE:
    The gas inside the hood support is colorless, odorless and harmless. As there is a possibility that metal debris could scatter, cover the back door stay with a piece of cloth or other material.

22. INSTALL BACK DOOR GARNISH SUB-ASSEMBLY OUTSIDE
