DTC    P1442    Stuck Close in Secondary Air Injection Vacuum Switching Valve Bank 1

DTC    P1445    Stuck Close in Secondary Air Injection Vacuum Switching Valve Bank 2

DTC    P2441    Secondary Air Injection System Switching Valve Stuck Close Bank1

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The diagnostic trouble code (DTC) indicates that the electric air switching valve (E-ASV) and air switching valves (ASVs) of the secondary air injection (AIR) system are stuck closed. The engine control module (ECM) operates entire components of this system in a sequence, and monitors the system pressure and exhaust pulse (AIR monitor). This monitor will run just after a cold engine start. If abnormal pressure and exhaust pulse are detected, the ECM interprets this as a malfunction. When the malfunction is detected in consecutive driving cycles, the ECM illuminates the malfunction indicator lamp (MIL), and stores a DTC.
The secondary air injection (AIR) system introduces fresh air into the exhaust manifold in order to reduce carbon monoxide (CO) and hydrocarbon (HC) in the exhaust emission. This system is activated just after engine start, and is stopped when the catalyst converter is warmed-up. This system consists of the following components: 1) the air pump which sends fresh air to the exhaust manifold, 2) the electric air switching valve (E-ASV) which switches the fresh air flow, 3) the air switching valve (ASV) which switches the air passage to the exhaust manifold of each bank, 4) the vacuum switching valve (VSV) which switches the vacuum (intake manifold pressure) to operate the ASV, 5) the pressure sensor which detects the fresh air pressure and exhaust pulse, 6) the air injection control driver which controls the power sources of the air pump and E-ASV, 7) the engine control module (ECM) which controls and monitors this system.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
Read freeze frame data using the intelligent tester. Freeze frame data records the engine conditions when malfunctions are detected. When troubleshooting, freeze frame data can help determine if the vehicle was running or stopped, if the engine was warmed up or not, if the air-fuel ratio was lean or rich, and other data from the time the malfunction occurred.

1.PERFORM SECONDARY AIR INJECTION SYSTEM CHECK (AUTOMATIC MODE)

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and turn the tester on.

  1. Record the DTCs and freeze frame data.

  1. Clear the DTCs.

  1. Warm-up the engine.

  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Utility / Secondary Air Injection Check (Automatic Mode).

  1. Perform the Active Test and check the pending DTC.

2.CHECK VACUUM HOSES (VSV)

  1. Check that the vacuum hoses between the throttle body and VSV for the secondary air injection system are securely connected.

    OK:

  1. Inspect the vacuum hoses for blockages or damage.

3.CHECK VACUUM SWITCHING VALVE (VSV)

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the vacuum hoses from the VSV for the secondary air injection system.

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Start the engine and turn the tester on.

  1. Perform the Active Test to operate the secondary air injection system, and check that there is negative pressure from ports A. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Utility / Secondary Air Injection Check (Manual Mode) / Operation 5 or 6.

    OK:
    Negative pressure from ports A.

    NOTICE:
    1. This test only allows technicians to operate the AIR system for 5 seconds. Furthermore, the test can be performed 4 times a trip. If the test is repeated, intervals of at least 30 seconds are required between tests.
      While the AIR system operation using the intelligent tester is prohibited, the tester displays the prohibition (WAIT or ERROR). If ERROR (AI Status NG) is displayed on the tester, stop the engine for 10 minutes and then try again.
    2. When performing the Secondary Air Injection Check operation after the battery cable has been reconnected, wait for 7 minutes with the engine switch turned on (IG) or the engine running.
    3. Turn the engine switch off when the Secondary Air Injection Check operation finishes.
