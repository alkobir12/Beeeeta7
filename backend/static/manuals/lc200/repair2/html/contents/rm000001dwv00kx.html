  1. The DTCs are simultaneously stored in memory. The DTCs can be read by connecting SST between terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of the DLC3 and observing the blinking of the ABS warning light, or by connecting the intelligent tester.

WARNING LIGHT CHECK



  1. Release the parking brake lever.

    NOTICE:
    When releasing the parking brake, set chocks to hold the vehicle for safety.

    HINT:
    When the parking brake is applied or the level of the brake fluid is low, the BRAKE warning light comes on.

  2. When the engine switch is turned on (IG), check that the ABS and BRAKE warning lights turn on and then turn off after the engine starts.
