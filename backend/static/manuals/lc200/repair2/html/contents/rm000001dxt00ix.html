DTC    C1380/64    Stop Light Control Relay Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

Upon receiving the CRAWL, downhill assist control or hill-start assist control operating signal from the skid control ECU, the relay contact turns on and the stop light comes on.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C1380/64
  1. When the voltage at the IG1 terminal is between 10 V and 14 V, the stop light driving output (STPO) is on, and a signal has not been input to the STP terminal for 5 seconds or more.
  2. When the voltage at the IG1 terminal is between 10 V and 14 V, the stop light driving output (STPO) is off, and the input signal at the STP terminal is different from the input signal at the STP2 terminal for 5 seconds or more.
  1. BRK LP relay
  2. BRK LP relay circuit
  3. Master cylinder solenoid (Skid control ECU)
