DTC    C1257/57    Power Supply Drive Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The motor relay (semiconductor relay) is built into the master cylinder solenoid and drives the pump motor based on a signal from the skid control ECU.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C1257/57
There is an open in the motor system circuit (motor input circuit).
Hydraulic brake booster pump motor circuit

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

NOTICE:
When replacing the master cylinder solenoid, perform zero point calibration (Click here).

1.CHECK PUMP MOTOR OPERATION

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the A24 skid control ECU connector.

  1. Depress the brake pedal more than 20 times.

  1. Turn the engine switch on.

  1. Check pump motor operation.

    OK:
    Pump motor normally operates.
