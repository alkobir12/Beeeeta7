REAR EVAPORATOR TEMPERATURE SENSOR (w/ Heater) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE REAR SIDE NO. 2 AIR DUCT



  1. Remove the 3 screws and duct.



2. REMOVE REAR SIDE NO. 3 AIR DUCT



  1. Remove the 2 screws and duct.



3. REMOVE DRAIN COOLER HOSE



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the drain hose.



4. REMOVE REAR AIR MIX DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and damper servo.



5. REMOVE REAR MODE DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws and plate cover.




  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and damper servo.



6. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR MIX DAMPER SERVO SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and damper servo.



7. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONER ACCESSORY ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the packing.




  1. Remove the bolt and 4 screws.

  1. Using pliers, grip the claws of the clips and slide the 2 clips.

  1. Remove the air conditioner accessory assembly.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the air conditioner accessory assembly.



8. REMOVE REAR COOLING UNIT EXPANSION VALVE



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Detach the clamp.

  1. Using a 4 mm hexagon wrench, remove the 2 hexagon bolts and expansion valve.



9. REMOVE HEATER RADIATOR UNIT SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the screw and detach the claw.

  1. Remove the heater clamp.




  1. Remove the heater radiator.



10. REMOVE REAR BLOWER MOTOR



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 3 screws and blower motor.



11. REMOVE BLOWER MOTOR CONTROLLER



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and blower motor controller.



12. REMOVE EVAPORATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 12 screws and rear cooling unit case LH.

  1. Remove the evaporator.




  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the evaporator.



13. REMOVE REAR EVAPORATOR TEMPERATURE SENSOR



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.



14. REMOVE WIRING AIR CONDITIONING HARNESS SUB-ASSEMBLY
