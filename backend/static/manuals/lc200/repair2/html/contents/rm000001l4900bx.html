DTC    C1248/48    Open Circuit in Rear Differential Lock

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C1248/48
With the vehicle speed at 50 km/h (30 mph) or more, the rear differential lock position switch changes from off to on and 3 seconds or more elapse.
  1. Rear differential lock position switch (No. 1 transfer indicator switch)
  2. Master cylinder solenoid (Skid control ECU)
  3. Harness or connector
