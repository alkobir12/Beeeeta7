1. INSPECT REAR WIPER MOTOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Check the wiper operation.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the rear wiper motor and check the speed of the rear wiper motor.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (+B)
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2 (LS) and Terminal 4 (E)
      		Motor operates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear wiper motor assembly.

  1. Check the INT operation.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the rear wiper motor and check the speed of the rear wiper motor.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (+B)
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 1 (C1) and Terminal 4 (E)
      		Motor operates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear wiper motor assembly.

  1. Automatic Stop Position Operation Check

    1. Connect the battery positive (+) lead to terminal 3 (+B), and the negative (-) lead to terminal 2 (LS) and terminal 4 (E). With the motor rotating, disconnect terminal 2 (LS) to stop the wiper motor operation at any position other than the matchmark.

    2. Check that the motor stops automatically at the matchmark.

      OK:
      Motor stops automatically at the matchmark.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear wiper motor assembly.
