Main Body ECU Communication Stop Mode
  1. "Main Body" is not displayed on "Bus Check".
  1. "Main Body ECU Communication Stop Mode" in "DTC Combination Table" applies.
  1. Power source or inside main body ECU
  2. Main body ECU branch wire or connector
  3. Main body ECU

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
Operating the engine switch, any switches or any doors triggers related ECU and sensor communication with the CAN, which causes resistance variation.

1.DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal before measuring the resistances of the main wire and the branch wire.

    CAUTION:
    Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).


NEXT


2.CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS WIRE (MAIN BODY ECU BRANCH WIRE)



  1. Disconnect the E3 main body ECU (cowl side junction block LH) connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E3-5 (CANH) - E3-6 (CANL)
    		Engine switch off
    		54 to 69 Ω



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE MAIN BODY ECU (COWL SIDE JUNCTION BLOCK LH) BRANCH WIRE OR CONNECTOR (CANH, CANL)
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (MAIN BODY ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND)

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



  1. Disconnect the 2A and 2D main body ECU (cowl side junction block LH) connectors.



  1. Disconnect the E3 main body ECU (cowl side junction block LH) connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    2D-62 (GND2) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E3-1 (GND3) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    2A-1 (ALTB) - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK

REPLACE MAIN BODY ECU
