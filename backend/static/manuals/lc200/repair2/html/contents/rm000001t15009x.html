CHECK STEERING LOCK ECU


  1. Disconnect the E26 steering lock ECU connector.

  2. Measure the voltage and resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E26-1 (GND) - Body ground
    		W-B - Body ground
    		Ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E26-2 (SGND) - Body ground
    		BR - Body ground
    		Signal ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E26-6 (IG2) - E26-1 (GND)
    		L - W-B
    		IG signal input
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    E26-7 (B) - E26-1 (GND)
    		P - W-B
    		Power source
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction on the wire harness side.

  3. Reconnect the E26 steering lock ECU connector.

  4. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E26-3 (IGE) - E26-1 (GND)
    		SB - W-B
    		Power source for motor drive
    		Motor operating
    		Below 1 V
    E26-3 (IGE) - E26-1 (GND)
    		SB - W-B
    		Power source for motor drive
    		Motor not operating
    		11 to 14 V
    E26-4 (SLP1) - E26-2 (SGND)
    		LG - BR
    		Unlock position sensor output signal
    		Steering locked
    		11 to 14 V
    E26-4 (SLP1) - E26-2 (SGND)
    		LG - BR
    		Unlock position sensor output signal
    		Steering lock unlocked
    		Below 1 V
    E26-5 (LIN) - E26-2 (SGND)
    		O - BR
    		LIN communication bus
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		Pulse generation
    If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction on the wire harness side.
