DTC    5C-40    Camera Picture Error

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION


DTC No.
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
5C-40
A synchronous signal from the camera cannot be transmitted.
  1. Harness or connector
  2. Television camera
  3. Multi-display
