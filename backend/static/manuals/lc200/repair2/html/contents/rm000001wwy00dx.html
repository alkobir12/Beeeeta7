DESCRIPTION

This circuit provides power to the radio receiver.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT FUSE (RAD NO. 1, ACC, AMP)

  1. Disconnect the RAD NO. 1 and AMP fuses from the engine room relay block.

  1. Disconnect the ACC fuse from the main body ECU.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    RADIO NO. 1 fuse
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    ACC fuse
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    AMP fuse
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω



NG
REPLACE FUSE
OK


2.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (RADIO RECEIVER - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND)



  1. Disconnect the F35*1 and F1*2 receiver connectors.

    HINT:
    *1: for Built-in Type Amplifier System
    *2: for Separate Type Amplifier System

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    for Built-in Type Amplifier System:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F35-7 (GND) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    for Separate Type Amplifier System:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F1-20 (GND) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    for Built-in Type Amplifier System:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F35-4 (B) - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    F35-3 (ACC) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (ACC)
    for Separate Type Amplifier System:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F1-1 (B) - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    F1-11 (ACC) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (ACC)



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK
