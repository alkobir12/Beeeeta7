DESCRIPTION

This circuit is necessary for the ASL (Automatic Sound Levelizer). Speed signals are received from the combination meter and used for the ASL.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT COMBINATION METER (SPEEDOMETER)

  1. Drive the vehicle and check that the function of the speedometer on the combination meter is normal.

    OK:
    Actual vehicle speed and the speed indicated on the speedometer are the same.
