1. INSPECT REAR DOOR LOCK ASSEMBLY LH (w/o Double Locking System)




  1. Check the door lock motor operation.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the door lock motor connector and check the operation of the door lock motor.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → 4 (L)
      Battery negative (-) → 1 (UL)
      		Lock
      Battery positive (+) → 1 (UL)
      Battery negative (-) → 4 (L)
      		Unlock

      1. If the result is not as specified, replace the door lock assembly.

  1. Check the detection switch.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Switch Condition
      		Specified Condition
      6 (LSSR) - 9 (E)
      		Unlock
      		Below 1 Ω
      6 (LSSR) - 9 (E)
      		Lock
      		10 kΩ or higher

      1. If the result is not as specified, replace the door lock assembly.
