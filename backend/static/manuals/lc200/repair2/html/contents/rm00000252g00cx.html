DTC    C1340/47    Center Differential Lock Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When the center differential is locked, VSC control turns off and "VSC OFF" appears on the multi-information display*1 or the VSC OFF indicator light*2 illuminates.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C1340/47
When the terminal IG1 voltage is between 10 and 14 V and there is an open in the center differential lock circuit for 2 seconds or more.
  1. Center differential lock switch
  2. Center differential lock switch circuit
  3. Four wheel drive control ECU

HINT:
*1: w/ Multi-information Display
*2: w/o Multi-information Display
