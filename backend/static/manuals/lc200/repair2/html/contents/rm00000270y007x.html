1. INSPECT REAR AXLE SHAFT LH



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the rear axle shaft runout and flange runout.

    Maximum runout:
    Shaft runout:
    1.50 mm (0.0591 in.)
    Flange runout:
    0.05 mm (0.00197 in.)

    If the rear axle shaft or flange is damaged or worn, or if the runout is more than the maximum, replace the rear axle shaft.
