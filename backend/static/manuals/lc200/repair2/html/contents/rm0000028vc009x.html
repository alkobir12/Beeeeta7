1. INSPECT INJECTION VOLUME AND LEAKAGE


  1. Check the injector injection.

    CAUTION:
    1. This test involves high-pressure fuel and electricity.
    2. Take every precaution regarding safe handling of both the fuel and the electrical parts.
    3. Perform this test in a safe area, and avoid any sparks or flame.
    4. Do not smoke.





    1. Assemble SST as shown in the illustration.

      SST
      09268-31012   (09268-41091, 09268-41110, 09268-41300, 09268-41500, 95336-08070)

    2. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).

    3. Disconnect the fuel inlet hose (fuel tube connector) from the fuel pipe.

    4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the fuel pressure regulator from the fuel delivery pipe.

      NOTICE:
      Do not disconnect the No. 2 fuel hose from the fuel pressure regulator.




    5. Connect SST to the fuel pipe.

      SST
      09268-31012   (09268-41091, 09268-41110, 09268-41300, 09268-41500, 95336-08070)

      CAUTION:
      Always read the precautions before connecting the fuel tube connector.




    6. Connect SST (hose) to the fuel inlet of the pressure regulator with another SST (union), a new O-ring and the 2 bolts.

      Torque:
      7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    7. Connect the fuel return hose to the fuel outlet of the pressure regulator.




    8. Install the O-ring to the injector.

    9. Connect SST (adaptor and hose) to the injector, and hold the injector and union with another SST (clamp).

      SST
      09268-31012   (95336-08070, 09268-41110, 09268-41300)

    10. Put the injector into a graduated cylinder.

      CAUTION:
      Install a suitable vinyl hose onto the injector to prevent gasoline from splashing out.

    11. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    12. Operate the fuel pump (Click here).




    13. Connect SST (wire) to the injector and battery for 15 seconds, and measure the injection volume with the graduated cylinder. Test each injector 2 or 3 times.

      SST
      09842-30070  

      Standard injection volume:
      56 to 69 cm3 (3.4 to 4.2 cu. in.) per 15 seconds

      Difference between each injector:
      13 cm3 (0.8 cu. in.) or less

      If the injection volume is not as specified, replace the injector.



  1. Check for fuel drop.




    1. In the condition above, disconnect the tester probes of SST (wire) from the battery and check for fuel drop from the injector.

      SST
      09842-30070  

      Standard fuel drop:
      1 drop or less per 12 minutes

      If the fuel drop is not as specified, replace the injector assembly.

    2. Turn the engine switch off.

    3. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.

      NOTICE:
      When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

    4. Remove SST and fuel tube connector.

      SST
      09842-30070  

    5. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.

    6. Reconnect the fuel inlet pipe to the fuel tube.
