OIL PUMP > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE ENGINE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the engine assembly from the vehicle (Click here).



4. REMOVE TIMING BELT

  1. Remove the timing belt (Click here).



5. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT POSITION SENSOR



  1. Disconnect the sensor connector.

  1. Remove the bolt and sensor.



6. REMOVE ENGINE OIL LEVEL DIPSTICK GUIDE



  1. Remove the dipstick.

  1. Remove the bolt and dipstick guide.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the dipstick guide.



7. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT TIMING PULLEY



  1. Using SST, remove the timing pulley.

    SST
    09950-50013   (09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05011)

    HINT:
    Apply a lubricant to the threads and end of SST.

    Parts Number:
    Item
    		Parts number
    SST (Bolt A)
    		09953-05020
    SST (Bolt B)
    		09953-05010



8. REMOVE OIL PRESSURE SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the sender gauge connector.

  1. Remove the sender gauge.



9. REMOVE OIL COOLER HOSE



  1. Using needle-nose pliers, grip the claws of the clamps and slide the clamps to remove the oil cooler hose.



10. REMOVE OIL FILTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, remove the oil filter.

    SST
    09228-07501  



11. REMOVE OIL FILTER BRACKET SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt, nut, stud bolt, oil filter bracket and gasket.



12. REMOVE NO. 2 OIL PAN SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 8 bolts and 2 nuts.




  1. Insert the blade of an oil pan seal cutter between the oil pans, cut off applied sealer and remove the No. 2 oil pan.

    NOTICE:
    1. Be careful not to damage the No. 2 oil pan contact surface of the oil pan.
    2. Be careful not to damage the No. 2 oil pan flange.



13. REMOVE OIL PAN SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 19 bolts and 2 nuts.




  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the oil pan by prying between the oil pan and cylinder block as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the contact surfaces of the cylinder block and oil pan.



14. REMOVE OIL STRAINER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts, oil strainer and gasket.



15. REMOVE OIL PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a 6 mm hexagon wrench, remove bolt A.

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and stud bolt.




  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the oil pump by prying the portions between the oil pump and cylinder block shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the contact surfaces of the cylinder block and oil pump.




  1. Remove the O-ring from the cylinder block.
