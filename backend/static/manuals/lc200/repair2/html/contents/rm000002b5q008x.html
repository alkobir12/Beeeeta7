OIL PUMP > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE OIL PUMP SEAL



  1. Using a screwdriver and wooden block, pry out the oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the surface of the oil seal press fit hole.



2. REMOVE OIL PUMP RELIEF VALVE



  1. Remove the plug, compression spring and relief valve.



3. REMOVE OIL PUMP COVER



  1. Remove the 10 screws and pump cover.



4. REMOVE OIL PUMP ROTOR SET

  1. Remove the drive and driven rotors.
