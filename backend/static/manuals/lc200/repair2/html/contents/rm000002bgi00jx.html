RADIATOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and under cover.



4. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL



  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.



5. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug and 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

  1. Remove the radiator cap. Then drain the coolant.

    HINT:
    Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.



  1. Tighten the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.



6. REMOVE V-BANK COVER



  1. Remove the 2 cap nuts and V-bank cover.



7. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER WINCH COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Winch)


8. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE



  1. Put protective tape around the radiator grille.

  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 8 claws, and remove the radiator grille.



9. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER COVER

HINT:
For the front bumper cover with garnish, use the procedures described below.



  1. Put protective tape around the bumper cover.





  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 6 screws.



  1. Remove the 3 clips, 4 screws and 4 bolts.

  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Fog Light:
    Detach the 10 claws and remove the bumper cover.



  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System or w/ Fog Light:
    Detach the 10 claws, disconnect the No. 4 engine room wire connector and remove the bumper cover.






  1. w/ Headlight Cleaner System:
    Disconnect the headlight cleaner hose and remove the bumper cover.



10. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER COVER (w/ Winch)


  1. Put protective tape around the bumper cover.





  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 6 screws.



  1. Remove the 3 clips, 4 screws and 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 10 claws.

  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Fog Light:
    Disconnect the winch control wire connector and remove the bumper cover.



  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System or w/ Fog Light:
    Disconnect the No. 4 engine room wire connector and winch control wire connector and remove the bumper cover.





11. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER AIR DUCT (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and oil cooler air duct.



12. REMOVE RADIATOR SIDE DEFLECTOR RH (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and move the side deflector so that the radiator can be removed in the step below.



13. REMOVE RADIATOR SIDE DEFLECTOR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and move the side deflector so that the radiator can be removed in the step below.



14. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Remove the radiator hose.



15. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Remove the radiator hose.



16. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Detach the claw to open the flexible hose clamp, and then remove the 2 bolts to disconnect the oil cooler tube from the fan shroud.



17. REMOVE FAN SHROUD



  1. Loosen the 4 nuts holding the fluid coupling fan.

  1. Remove the fan and generator V-belt (Click here).




  1. Disconnect the reservoir hose from the upper side of the radiator tank.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts holding the fan shroud.

  1. Remove the 4 nuts of the fluid coupling fan, and then remove the shroud together with the coupling fan.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the radiator core.

  1. Remove the fan pulley from the water pump.



18. DISCONNECT INLET NO. 3 OIL COOLER HOSE (for Automatic Transmission)



  1. Disconnect the oil cooler hose.



19. DISCONNECT OUTLET NO. 2 OIL COOLER HOSE (for Automatic Transmission)

  1. Disconnect the oil cooler hose.



20. REMOVE RADIATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and radiator.
