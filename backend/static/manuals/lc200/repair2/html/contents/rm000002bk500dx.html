CYLINDER HEAD > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE VALVE LIFTER

HINT:
Arrange the valve lifters in the correct order.



2. REMOVE VALVE

HINT:
Arrange the valves, inner compression springs, valve spring retainers and valve spring retainer locks in the correct order.




  1. Place the cylinder head on wooden blocks.

  1. Using SST, compress the inner compression spring and remove the valve spring retainer lock.

    SST
    09202-70020   (09202-00010)

  1. Remove the valve, inner compression spring and valve spring retainer.



3. REMOVE VALVE SPRING SEAT



  1. Using compressed air and a magnetic finger, remove the valve spring seat by blowing air.



4. REMOVE VALVE STEM OIL SEAL



  1. Using needle-nose pliers, remove the valve stem oil seal.
