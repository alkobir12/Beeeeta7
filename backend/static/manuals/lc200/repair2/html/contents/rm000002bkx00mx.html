1. INSPECT PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    2 (RB) - 6 (PL)
    		Shift lever in P
    		Below 1 Ω
    2 (RB) - 6 (PL)
    		Shift lever not in P
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 (RB) - 1 (RL)
    		Shift lever in R
    		Below 1 Ω
    2 (RB) - 1 (RL)
    		Shift lever not in R
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 (RB) - 9 (NL)
    		Shift lever in N
    		Below 1 Ω
    2 (RB) - 9 (NL)
    		Shift lever not in N
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 (RB) - 7 (DL)
    		Shift lever in D or S
    		Below 1 Ω
    2 (RB) - 7 (DL)
    		Shift lever not in D or S
    		10 kΩ or higher
    4 (B) - 5 (L)
    		Shift lever in P or N
    		Below 1 Ω
    4 (B) - 5 (L)
    		Shift lever not in P or N
    		10 kΩ or higher

    If the result is not as specified, replace the switch.
