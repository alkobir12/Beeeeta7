for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL OIL PUMP SEAL



  1. Apply MP grease to the lip of a new oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the lip of the oil seal.
    2. Do not allow MP grease to contact the dust seal.

  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the oil seal until its surface is flush with the oil pump body edge.

    SST
    09316-60011   (09316-00011)

    NOTICE:
    1. Keep the lip of the oil seal free from foreign matter.
    2. Do not tap the oil seal at an angle.



2. INSTALL CRANKSHAFT TIMING PULLEY



  1. Align the timing pulley set key with the key groove of the pulley.

  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the timing pulley with the flange side facing inward.

    SST
    09223-46011
