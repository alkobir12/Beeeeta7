1. INSTALL ENGINE REAR OIL SEAL



  1. Apply MP grease to the lip of a new oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the lip of the oil seal.
    2. Do not allow MP grease to contact the dust seal.

  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the oil seal until its surface is flush with the rear oil seal retainer edge.

    SST
    09223-56010  

    NOTICE:
    1. Keep the lip of the oil seal free from foreign matter.
    2. Do not tap on the oil seal at an angle.



2. INSTALL DRIVE PLATE AND RING GEAR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, hold the crankshaft.

    SST
    09213-70011   (09213-70020)
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)

  1. Install the front drive plate spacer, drive plate and rear drive plate spacer to the crankshaft.




  1. Install and uniformly tighten 8 new bolts in the sequence shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    30 N*m{ 306 kgf*cm , 22 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not reuse the drive plate installation bolts.
    2. Do not impact or damage the drive plate installation bolts. Be sure to handle them carefully.




  1. Mark the upside of each drive plate installation bolt with paint.

  1. Tighten the drive plate installation bolts another 90° as shown.

  1. Check that the painted marks are now at a 90° angle to the upside.
