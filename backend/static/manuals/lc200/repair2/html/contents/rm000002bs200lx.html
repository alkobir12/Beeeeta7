POWER STEERING OIL PRESSURE SWITCH > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL POWER STEERING OIL PRESSURE SWITCH

  1. Install a new O-ring to the oil pressure switch.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the O-ring of the oil pressure switch.




  1. Using a 19 mm deep socket wrench, install the oil pressure switch.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the oil seal face of the power steering oil pressure switch (connecting surface with pressure feed tube).
    2. Be careful not to damage the O-ring when installing the power steering oil pressure switch.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the oil pressure switch connector.



2. INSTALL RADIATOR RESERVOIR ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the radiator reservoir with the 3 bolts.

  1. Connect the 2 hoses to the upper radiator tank and water inlet.

    HINT:
    Make sure the directions of the hose clamps are as shown in the illustration.



3. INSTALL NO. 1 OIL RESERVOIR BRACKET



  1. Install a new bracket with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    4.5 N*m{ 46 kgf*cm , 40 in.*lbf }



4. INSTALL VANE PUMP OIL RESERVOIR ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the vane pump oil reservoir to the bracket.



5. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the No. 1 air cleaner pipe to the No. 1 intake air connector pipe.

  1. Install the pipe with the bolt.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



6. INSTALL HEATER WATER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Connect the 4 water hose ends, and install the water pipe with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    9.8 N*m{ 100 kgf*cm , 87 in.*lbf }



7. INSTALL NO. 3 AIR TUBE



  1. Install the No. 3 air tube with the bolt.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }

    HINT:
    Make sure the direction of the hose clamp is as shown in the illustration.




  1. Install the wire harness bracket with the bolt.




  1. Install the ground wire with the nut, and attach the wire harness clamp.

    Torque:
    8.4 N*m{ 87 kgf*cm , 74 in.*lbf }



8. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR HOSE



  1. Connect the No. 1 air hose with the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Align the paint mark of the air hose with the protrusion and push in the air hose so that distance B is 0 to 3 mm (0 to 0.118 in.).
    2. Position the clamp so that distance A is 2 to 6 mm (0.0787 to 0.236 in.).
    3. Make sure the direction of the hose clamp is as shown in the illustration.



9. INSTALL INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR



  1. Connect the intake air connector to the No. 1 and No. 2 air cleaner pipes.

  1. Install the connector with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the 2 hose clamps.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }




  1. Attach the 3 wire harness clamps.

  1. w/o Viscous Heater:
    Connect the connector to the water temperature sensor.

  1. w/ Viscous Heater:
    Connect the 2 connectors to the water temperature sensor and viscous with magnet clutch heater.



10. TEMPORARILY INSTALL NO. 1 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Temporarily install the air cleaner hose to the intake air connector.



11. INSTALL AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the air cleaner cap to the air cleaner hose, and install the air cleaner cap with the 4 clamps.

  1. Connect the mass air flow meter connector and attach the wire harness clamp to the air cleaner cap.

  1. Attach the wire harness clamp.




  1. Align the protrusion of air cleaner cap and concave portion of air cleaner hose.

  1. Tighten the 2 hose clamps.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }



12. CONNECT WATER HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Connect the 2 water hoses.



13. INSTALL INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the intercooler assembly (Click here).



14. INSTALL V-RIBBED BELT



  1. Attach a wrench to the V-ribbed belt tensioner bracket and turn the wrench clockwise.




  1. Install the V-ribbed belt as shown in the illustration.




  1. Check that the belt fits properly in the ribbed grooves.

    HINT:
    Check with your hand to confirm that the belt has not slipped out of the groove on the bottom of the pulley.
    If it has slipped out, replace the V-ribbed belt. Install a new V-ribbed belt.



15. INSTALL NO. 3 IDLER PULLEY (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Align the No. 3 idler pulley bracket knock pin and No. 1 idler pulley bracket knock pin hole and install the No. 3 idler pulley with the nut.

    Torque:
    88 N*m{ 898 kgf*cm , 64 ft.*lbf }



16. INSTALL NO. 1 IDLER PULLEY (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Install the collar, No. 1 idler pulley and cover with the bolt.

    Torque:
    49 N*m{ 495 kgf*cm , 36 ft.*lbf }



17. INSTALL V-RIBBED BELT (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Install the V-ribbed belt as shown in the illustration.




  1. Temporarily install the lock nut, and turn the bolt clockwise.




  1. Using a belt tension gauge, adjust belt tension.

    Standard Belt Tension:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    New belt
    		400 to 650 N (41 to 66 kgf, 89.9 to 146.1 lbf)
    Used belt
    		300 to 500 N (31 to 51 kgf, 67.4 to 112.4 lbf)

    HINT:
    1. A "new belt" is a belt which has been used for less than 5 minutes on a running engine.
    2. A "used belt" is a belt which has been used on a running engine for 5 minutes or more.
    3. After installing a new belt, run the engine for approximately 5 minutes and then recheck the tension.




  1. Tighten the nut.

    Torque:
    40 N*m{ 408 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }




  1. Check that the belt fits properly in the ribbed grooves.

    HINT:
    Check with your hand to confirm that the belt has not slipped out of the groove on the bottom of the pulley.
    If it has slipped out, replace the V-ribbed belt. Install a new V-ribbed belt.



18. ADD ENGINE COOLANT



  1. Remove the engine air bleed cap.




  1. Connect a clear hose to the engine air bleed pipe.




  1. Using a wrench, remove the vent plug.




  1. Fill the radiator with TOYOTA SLLC to the radiator reservoir filler neck.

    HINT:
    Pour TOYOTA SLLC until it spills out of the engine air bleed pipe.

    Standard Capacity (for Automatic Transmission):
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Front heater only
    		14.8 liters (15.6 US qts, 13.0 Imp. qts)
    Front heater and rear heater
    		17.6 liters (18.6 US qts, 15.5 Imp. qts)
    Front heater with viscous heater
    		15.2 liters (16.1 US qts, 13.4 Imp. qts)
    Front heater and rear heater with viscous heater
    		18.0 liters (19.0 US qts, 15.4 Imp. qts)

    Standard capacity (for Manual Transmission):
    15.4 liters (16.3 US qts, 13.5 Imp. qts)

    NOTICE:
    Do not substitute plain water for engine coolant.

    HINT:
    TOYOTA vehicles are filled with TOYOTA SLLC at the factory. In order to avoid damage to the engine cooling system and other technical problems, only use TOYOTA SLLC or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology consists of a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).




  1. Install the vent plug.

    Torque:
    2.0 N*m{ 20 kgf*cm , 18 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Do not tighten the plug to 5.0 N*m (51 kgf*cm, 44 in.*lbf) or more, as the plug will be damaged.




  1. Disconnect the clear hose from the engine air bleed pipe.




  1. Install the engine air bleed cap when coolant comes out.

  1. Install the radiator reservoir cap.

  1. Start the engine.

    NOTICE:
    Immediately after starting the engine, if the radiator reservoir does not have any coolant, perform the following: 1) stop the engine, 2) wait until the coolant has cooled down, and 3) add coolant until the coolant is filled to the FULL line.

  1. Maintain an engine speed of 3000 rpm for approximately 10 minutes so that the thermostat opens and air bleeding is performed.

    CAUTION:
    When pressing the radiator hoses:
    1. Wear protective gloves.
    2. Be careful as the radiator hoses are hot.
    3. Keep your hands away from the radiator fan

    NOTICE:
    1. Pay attention to the needle of the water temperature meter. Make sure that the needle does not show an abnormally high temperature.
    2. If there is not enough coolant, the engine may burn out or overheat.

    HINT:
    The thermostat opening timing can be confirmed by pressing the No. 2 radiator hose by hand, and checking when the engine coolant starts to flow inside the hose.

  1. Stop the engine, and wait until the engine coolant cools down to ambient temperature.

    CAUTION:
    Do not remove the radiator reservoir cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Check that the coolant level is between the FULL and LOW lines.
    If the coolant level is above the FULL line, drain coolant so that the coolant level is between the FULL and LOW lines.



19. INSPECT FOR COOLANT LEAK

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator reservoir cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and attach a radiator cap tester to the radiator reservoir.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Using the radiator cap tester, increase the pressure inside the radiator to 123 kPa (1.3 kgf/cm2, 17.8 psi), and check that the pressure does not drop.
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks.
    If no external leaks are found, check the cylinder block and cylinder head.
