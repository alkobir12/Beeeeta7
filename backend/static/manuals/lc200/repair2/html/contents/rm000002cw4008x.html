1. INSPECT THERMOSTAT



HINT:
The valve opening temperature is inscribed on the thermostat.

  1. Check that the valve is fully closed when the thermostat is at a low temperature (below 77°C (171°F)).
    If not fully closed, replace the thermostat.




  1. Immerse the thermostat in water, then gradually heat the water.

  1. Check the valve opening temperature of the thermostat.

    Standard valve opening temperature:
    80 to 84°C (176 to 183°F)

    If the valve opening temperature is not as specified, replace the thermostat.




  1. Check the valve lift.

    Standard valve lift:
    10.0 to 11.3 mm (0.394 to 0.445 in.) or more at 95°C (203°F)

    If the valve lift is not as specified, replace the thermostat.
