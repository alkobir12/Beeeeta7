STARTER (for 1.6 kW Type) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE REPAIR SERVICE STARTER KIT



  1. Remove the nut, and disconnect the lead wire from the repair service starter kit.




  1. Remove the 2 screws which are used to secure the repair service starter kit to the starter drive housing.

  1. Remove the repair service starter kit.

  1. Remove the return spring and plunger.



2. REMOVE STARTER YOKE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 through bolts, and pull out the starter yoke together with the starter commutator end frame.



3. REMOVE STARTER COMMUTATOR END FRAME ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the starter commutator end frame from the starter yoke.



4. REMOVE STARTER ARMATURE PLATE



  1. Remove the starter armature plate from the starter yoke.



5. REMOVE STARTER COMMUTATOR END FRAME COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the starter commutator end frame cover.



6. REMOVE STARTER ARMATURE ASSEMBLY

  1. Hold the starter commutator end frame in a vise between aluminum plates.

    NOTICE:
    Do not overtighten the vise.




  1. Using snap ring pliers, remove the snap ring and plate washer.

  1. Remove the starter armature from the commutator end frame.

    NOTICE:
    Do not drop the starter armature.



7. REMOVE PLANETARY GEAR



  1. Remove the 3 planetary gears from the starter center bearing clutch.



8. REMOVE STARTER CENTER BEARING CLUTCH SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the starter center bearing clutch and starter drive lever set pin from the starter drive housing.

  1. Remove the starter drive lever set pin from the starter center bearing clutch.
