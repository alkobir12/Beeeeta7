STARTER (for 2.0 kW Type) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE STARTER YOKE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the lead wire from terminal C.

  1. Remove the 2 through bolts.

  1. Pull out the starter yoke and the armature together.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the starter yoke.



2. REMOVE MAGNET STARTER SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws and magnet starter switch.




  1. Remove the return spring, idler gear, clutch roller, retainer and starter clutch from the starter housing.




  1. Using a magnetic finger, remove the steel ball from the clutch shaft hole.



3. REMOVE STARTER BRUSH HOLDER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws and end frame from the starter yoke.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the starter yoke.




  1. Using a screwdriver, hold the spring back and disconnect the 4 brushes from the brush holder.

  1. Remove the brush holder from the armature.



4. REMOVE STARTER ARMATURE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the starter armature from the starter yoke.
