2UZ-FE VANE PUMP > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



3. REMOVE AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the vacuum transmitting hose and No. 2 ventilation hose.

  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps.

  1. Remove the air cleaner hose.



4. REMOVE AIR CLEANER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the clamp and MAF connector.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and air cleaner.



5. REMOVE FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT



  1. Loosen the belt tension by turning the belt tensioner counterclockwise, and then remove the fan and generator V belt.

    HINT:
    The pulley bolt for the belt tensioner has left-hand thread.



6. DRAIN POWER STEERING FLUID


7. DISCONNECT NO. 1 OIL RESERVOIR TO PUMP HOSE



  1. Slide the clip and disconnect the hose from the vane pump.



8. DISCONNECT POWER STEERING OIL PRESSURE SWITCH CONNECTOR



  1. Disconnect the connector.



9. DISCONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE



  1. Remove the union bolt and disconnect the pressure feed tube.

  1. Remove the gasket.



10. REMOVE VANE PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, nut and vane pump.
