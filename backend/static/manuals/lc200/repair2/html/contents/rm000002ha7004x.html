EXHAUST PIPE > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


CAUTION:
  1. Wear protective gloves when removing the exhaust pipe.
  2. The exhaust pipe is extremely hot immediately after the engine has stopped.
  3. Confirm that the exhaust pipe has cooled down before removing it.



1. REMOVE HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR (for Bank 1 Sensor 2)



  1. Disconnect the sensor connector.




  1. Using SST, remove the sensor.

    SST
    09224-00010  



2. REMOVE TAILPIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the bolt, clamp and gasket.

  1. Remove the tailpipe from the 2 exhaust pipe supports.



3. REMOVE CENTER EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 compression springs.

  1. Remove the center exhaust pipe from the 3 exhaust pipe supports.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the front exhaust pipe and front No. 2 exhaust pipe.



4. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the gasket from the front exhaust pipe.



5. REMOVE HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR (for Bank 2 Sensor 2)



  1. Using SST, remove the sensor.

    SST
    09224-00010  



6. REMOVE FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the gasket from the front exhaust pipe.
