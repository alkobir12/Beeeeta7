IGNITION COIL AND SPARK PLUG > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the vacuum transmitting hose and No. 2 ventilation hose.

  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps.

  1. Remove the air cleaner hose.



3. REMOVE AIR CLEANER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the clamp and MAF connector.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and air cleaner.



4. REMOVE IGNITION COIL ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 8 ignition coil connectors.

  1. Remove the 8 bolts and pull out the 8 ignition coils.



5. REMOVE SPARK PLUG

  1. Remove the 8 spark plugs.
