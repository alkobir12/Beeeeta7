DTC    B2075    Passenger Side Seat Belt Buckle Switch Circuit Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This DTC is stored when there is a malfunction in the front seat inner belt buckle switch circuit for the passenger side.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B2075
Malfunction in the front seat inner belt (buckle switch) circuit.
  1. Harness or connector
  2. Front seat inner belt (for passenger side)
  3. Seat belt control ECU

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (FRONT SEAT INNER BELT - SEAT BELT CONTROL ECU)



  1. Disconnect the c30*1 or c24*2 front seat inner belt connector.

    HINT:
    1. *1: for LHD
    2. *2: for RHD

  1. Disconnect the E41 seat belt control ECU connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.
