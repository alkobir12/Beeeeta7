FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.



2. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY (for Manual Seat)

  1. Remove the front seat assembly (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY (for Power Seat)

  1. Remove the front seat assembly (Click here).



4. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY (for Manual Seat)

  1. Disconnect the connectors and detach the clamps.




  1. Remove the nut and front seat inner belt assembly.



5. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY (for Power Seat)

  1. Disconnect the connectors and detach the clamps.




  1. Remove the nut and front seat inner belt assembly.
