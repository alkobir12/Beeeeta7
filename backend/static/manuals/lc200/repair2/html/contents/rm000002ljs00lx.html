FRONT SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.



2. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE



  1. Detach the 7 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



3. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



4. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



5. REMOVE CENTER PILLAR GARNISH COVER

HINT:
  1. for Manual Seat:
    When removing the center pillar garnish cover, move the front seat and seatback to the foremost upright position.
  2. for Power Seat:
    When removing the center pillar garnish cover, connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal and operate the power seat switch to move the front seat and seatback to the foremost upright position.
  3. for Power Seat:
    After moving the front seat, disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.




  1. Detach the 2 clips and 2 guides, and remove the cover.



6. REMOVE CENTER LOWER PILLAR GARNISH



HINT:
  1. for Manual Seat:
    When removing the center pillar garnish cover, move the front seat and seatback to the foremost upright position.
  2. for Power Seat:
    When removing the center pillar garnish cover, connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal and operate the power seat switch to move the front seat and seatback to the foremost upright position.
  3. for Power Seat:
    After moving the front seat, disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.

  1. Remove the bolt and seat belt's anchor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and 7 clips, and remove the center lower pillar garnish.



7. REMOVE REAR ASSIST GRIP ASSEMBLY



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the rear assist grip on the other side.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 assist grip plugs.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the rear assist grip.



8. REMOVE CENTER PILLAR GARNISH



  1. Remove the bolt.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 2 guides.

  1. Pass the seat belt anchor through the center pillar garnish, and remove the center pillar garnish.



9. REMOVE FRONT SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the nut and shoulder anchor.




  1. Disconnect the pretensioner connector labeled A as shown in the illustration.

  1. Disconnect the tension reducer connector labeled B.

  1. w/ Pre-Crash Safety System:
    Disconnect the pre-crash safety connector labeled C.




  1. Remove the bolt and seat belt.



10. REMOVE FRONT SHOULDER BELT ANCHOR ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and adjuster.
