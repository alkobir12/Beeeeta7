LIST OF OPERATION METHODS
By operating each of the air conditioning control switches as shown in the diagram below, it is possible to enter the diagnostic check mode.

HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LHD vehicle and RHD vehicle.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LHD vehicle.




INDICATOR CHECK

  1. Press and hold the A/C control AUTO switch and recirculation/fresh switch simultaneously, and turn the engine switch on (IG). Continue holding the 2 switches until the panel diagnosis mode begins.

    HINT:
    When the above operation is performed, each mode switch indicator light illuminates. For vehicles equipped with multi-display, each mode switch indicator light illuminates and an operation confirmation buzzer sounds.




  2. Check that all indicators turn on and off 4 times in succession at 1 second intervals.

    HINT:
    1. After the indicator check is completed, the system enters the DTC mode automatically.
    2. Press the "Off" switch to cancel the indicator check.
