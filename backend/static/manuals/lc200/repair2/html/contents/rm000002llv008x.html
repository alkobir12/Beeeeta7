DESCRIPTION

The back-up power source circuit for the air conditioning amplifier assembly is shown below. Power is supplied even when turning the engine switch off and is used for diagnostic trouble code memory, etc.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT FUSE (A/C)

  1. Remove the A/C fuse from the cowl side junction block LH.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A/C fuse
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

2.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER - BATTERY)

  1. w/ Rear Heater

    1. Disconnect the E36 amplifier connector.

    2. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Voltage:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      E36-6 (+B1) - Body ground
      		Always
      		11 to 14 V
      E36-7 (+B2) - Body ground




  1. w/o Rear Heater

    1. Disconnect the E81amplifier connector.

    2. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Voltage:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      E81-21 (+B1) - Body ground
      		Always
      		11 to 14 V
