1. INSPECT HEADLIGHT CLEANER MOTOR AND PUMP ASSEMBLY

HINT:
This check should be performed with the headlight cleaner motor and pump installed to the washer jar.




  1. Fill the washer jar with washer fluid.

  1. Apply battery voltage to the headlight cleaner motor and pump and check the operation of the headlight cleaner motor and pump assembly.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2
    		Headlight cleaner motor and pump operation is normal

    If the result is not as specified, replace the headlight cleaner motor and pump assembly.
