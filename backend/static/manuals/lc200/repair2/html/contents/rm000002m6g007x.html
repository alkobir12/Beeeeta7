FRONT WIPER RUBBER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for both the RH and LH sides.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE FRONT WIPER BLADE LH



  1. Remove the wiper blade holder.

  1. Remove the wiper blade as shown in the illustration.



2. REMOVE WIPER RUBBER LH

  1. Detach the head part (large side) of the wiper rubber from the wiper blade.




  1. Remove the wiper rubber in the direction indicated by the arrow in the illustration.




  1. Remove the 2 wiper rubber backing plates from the wiper rubber.
