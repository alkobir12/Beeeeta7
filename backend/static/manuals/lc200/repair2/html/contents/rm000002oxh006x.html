for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This circuit detects the conditions of the headlight cleaner switch.
  1. Headlight cleaner switch signal
  2. Headlight operating signal
  3. Daytime running light operating signal
  4. Front washer motor operating signal

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY (LO BEAM)

  1. Check that the headlights illuminate in LO beam.

    OK:
    Headlights illuminate in LO beam



NG

2.INSPECT HEADLIGHT CLEANER SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the G4 cleaner switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    8 (B) - 5 (L)
    		Headlight cleaner switch off
    		10 kΩ or higher
    8 (B) - 5 (L)
    		Headlight cleaner switch on
    		Below 1 Ω



NG
REPLACE HEADLIGHT CLEANER SWITCH ASSEMBLY (Click here)
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (HEADLIGHT CLEANER SWITCH ASSEMBLY - HEADLIGHT CLEANER CONTROL RELAY AND MAIN BODY ECU)



  1. Disconnect the A17 relay connector.

  1. Disconnect the G4 switch connector.

  1. Disconnect the 2D main body ECU connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    2 (H) - G4-8 (B)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    G4-5 (L) - 2D-23 (HRLY)
    2 (H) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    G4-8 (B) - Body ground
    G4-5 (L) - Body ground
    2D-23 (HRLY) - Body ground



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
