DTC    B1632/81    Lost Communication with Rear Airbag Sensor RH

DTC    B1633/81    Rear Airbag Sensor Assembly RH Initialization Incomplete

DTC    B1697/82    Lost Communication with Door Side Airbag Sensor LH

DTC    B1698/82    Door Side Airbag Sensor LH Initialization Incomplete

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

HINT:
"Door side airbag sensor LH" refer to the side airbag sensor LH.

  1. The circuit for the side collision sensor LH or RH (to determine deployment of the front seat side airbag LH or RH, rear seat side airbag LH or RH and curtain shield airbag LH or RH) is composed of the center airbag sensor, side airbag sensor LH or RH, and rear airbag sensor LH or RH.
    The side airbag sensor LH or RH and rear airbag sensor LH or RH detect impacts to the vehicle and send signals to the center airbag sensor to determine if the airbag should be deployed.
    DTC B1632/81 or B1697/82 is stored when a malfunction is detected in the circuit for the side collision sensor LH or RH (to determine deployment of the front seat side airbag LH or RH, rear seat side airbag LH or RH and curtain shield airbag LH or RH).

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1632/81
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. The center airbag sensor receives a line short circuit signal, an open circuit signal, a short circuit to ground signal or a short circuit to B+ signal in the circuit for the side collision sensor RH (to determine deployment of the front seat side airbag RH and curtain shield airbag RH) for 2 seconds.
  2. A side airbag sensor RH malfunction.
  3. A rear airbag sensor RH malfunction.
  4. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. No. 2 floor wire
  2. Front door wire RH
  3. Side airbag sensor RH
  4. Rear airbag sensor RH
  5. Center airbag sensor assembly

5.CHECK REAR AIRBAG SENSOR RH CIRCUIT


  1. Disconnect the connectors from the side airbag sensor RH and rear airbag sensor RH.

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    I9-1 (BCR+) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		Below 1 V
    I9-2 (BCR-) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		Below 1 V

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.

  1. Using a service wire, connect terminals 2 (BCR+) and 1 (BCR-) of connector E.

    NOTICE:
    Do not forcibly insert the service wire into the terminals of the connector when connecting a service wire.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    I9-1 (BCR+) - I9-2 (BCR-)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

  1. Disconnect the service wire from connector E.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    I9-1 (BCR+) - I9-2 (BCR-)
    		Always
    		1 MΩ or higher
    I9-1 (BCR+) - Body ground
    		Always
    		1 MΩ or higher
    I9-2 (BCR-) - Body ground
    		Always
    		1 MΩ or higher
