DTC    B1467/67    Rear Air Foot Duct Sensor Circuit on Driver Side

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The air duct temperature sensor LH*1 or RH*2 (for driver side) detects the duct temperature and sends the appropriate signals to the air conditioning amplifier assembly.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1467/67
An open or short in the air duct temperature sensor LH*1 or RH*2 circuit.
  1. Air duct temperature sensor LH*1
  2. Air duct temperature sensor RH*2
  3. Harness or connector (air duct temperature sensor LH)*1
  4. Harness or connector (air duct temperature sensor RH)*2
  5. Air conditioning amplifier assembly

HINT:
*1: for LHD
*2: for RHD
