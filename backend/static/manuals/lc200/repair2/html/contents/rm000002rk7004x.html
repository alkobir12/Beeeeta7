1. INSPECT EGR VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Check the EGR valve position sensor.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1 (+B) - 5 (EGR)
      		20°C (68°F)
      		6.5 to 7.5 Ω
      3 (VC) - 2 (E2)
      		Always
      		2.45 to 4.55 kΩ

      HINT:
      The temperature indicated under "Condition" is the temperature of the EGR valve assembly.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the EGR valve assembly.
