REAR POWER WINDOW SWITCH > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE REAR DOOR ARMREST BASE PANEL ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the armrest base panel.



2. REMOVE REAR POWER WINDOW REGULATOR SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the regulator switch.
