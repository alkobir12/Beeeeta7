LIN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM > Rear Heater Control Panel LIN Communication Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The LIN communication of the components related to the rear air conditioning occurs between the air conditioning amplifier (A/C ECU), rear air conditioning control panel and front air conditioning control panel.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

NOTICE:
When using the intelligent tester with the engine switch off to troubleshoot:
Connect the intelligent tester to the vehicle, and turn a courtesy switch on and off at 1.5 second intervals until communication between the intelligent tester and vehicle begins.

1.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (A/C ECU - REAR AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL PANEL AND FRONT AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL PANEL*)

  1. Disconnect the E36 amplifier connector.

  1. Disconnect the F10* and E55 panel connector.

  1. Measure the resistance and voltage according to the value(s) in the tables below.
