AIR FUEL RATIO SENSOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL FRONT RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and fender apron seal.



4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



5. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts from the center exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and front No. 2 exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the front No. 2 exhaust pipe.



6. REMOVE FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts with the 2 compression springs from the center exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the front exhaust pipe.



7. REMOVE MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and No. 1 manifold stay.



8. REMOVE EXHAUST MANIFOLD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.




  1. Remove the 6 nuts, exhaust manifold and gasket from the cylinder head RH.



9. REMOVE NO. 2 MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and No. 2 manifold stay.



10. REMOVE EXHAUST MANIFOLD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.




  1. Remove the 6 nuts, exhaust manifold and gasket from the cylinder head LH.



11. REMOVE AIR FUEL RATIO SENSOR (for Bank 1 Sensor 1)



  1. Using SST, remove the sensor.

    SST
    09224-00010  



12. REMOVE AIR FUEL RATIO SENSOR (for Bank 2 Sensor 1)



  1. Using SST, remove the sensor.

    SST
    09224-00010
