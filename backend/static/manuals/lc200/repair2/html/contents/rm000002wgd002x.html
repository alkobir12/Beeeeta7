1. INSPECT CYLINDER HEAD SET BOLT



  1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the thread outside diameter of the bolt.

    Measuring Point:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Intake side
    		82 mm (3.23 in.)
    Exhaust side
    		86 mm (3.39 in.)

    Standard outside diameter:
    9.810 to 9.960 mm (0.386 to 0.392 in.)

    Minimum outside diameter:
    9.700 mm (0.382 in.)

    If the outside diameter is less than the minimum, replace the cylinder head set bolt.

    HINT:
    If a visual check reveals no excessively thin areas, check the center of the bolt (see illustration) and find the area that has the minimum diameter.



2. INSPECT CYLINDER HEAD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a precision straightedge and feeler gauge, measure the surfaces contacting the cylinder block and manifolds for warpage.

    Standard Warpage:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Cylinder block side
    		0 to 0.05 mm (0 to 0.00197 in.)
    Intake manifold side
    		0 to 0.08 mm (0 to 0.00315 in.)
    Exhaust manifold side
    		0 to 0.08 mm (0 to 0.00315 in.)

    Maximum warpage:
    0.10 mm (0.00394 in.)

    1. If the warpage is more than the maximum, replace the cylinder head sub-assembly.




  1. Using a dye penetrant, check the combustion chamber, intake ports, exhaust ports and cylinder block surface for cracks.
    If the cylinder head is cracked, replace it.



3. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD GASKET LH



  1. Clean the cylinder block and cylinder head with solvent.

  1. Place a new cylinder head gasket in position on the cylinder block.

    HINT:
    A mark is placed on the rear side of the cylinder head gasket to distinguish the LH and RH sides, a "2R" mark for the RH side and a "2L" mark for the LH side.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful of the installation direction.



4. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD LH

HINT:
The cylinder head bolts are tightened in 3 progressive steps.




  1. Check that the timing mark of the crankshaft timing pulley is in the position shown in the illustration, and that the piston is below the TDC of compression.

  1. Install the cylinder head with the exhaust manifold to the cylinder block.

    NOTICE:
    Ensure that no oil is on the mounting surface of the cylinder head.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the threads and under the heads of the cylinder head bolts.



  1. Step 1:

    1. Set the plate washer to the cylinder head bolt.




    2. Install and uniformly tighten the 10 cylinder head bolts and plate washers in the sequence shown in the illustration.

      Torque:
      40 N*m{ 408 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }

      If any one of the cylinder head bolts does not meet the torque specification, replace the cylinder head bolt.

      NOTICE:
      Do not drop the plate washer for the cylinder head bolt of the cylinder head.



  1. Step 2:




    1. Mark the cylinder head bolt head with paint as shown in the illustration.

    2. Tighten the cylinder head bolts 90° in the sequence shown in step 1.

  1. Step 3:

    1. Tighten the cylinder head bolts another 90° in the sequence shown in step 1.

    2. Check that the painted marks are now facing rearward.

7. INSTALL VALVE LIFTER



  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the valve lifters and adjusting shims.

  1. Install the valve lifters and shims to the cylinder head.

  1. Check that the valve lifter rotates smoothly by hand.



8. INSTALL CAMSHAFT SETTING OIL SEAL

  1. Apply MP grease to the lip of a new oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the lip of the oil seal.
    2. Do not allow MP grease to contact the dust seal.




  1. Insert the oil seal into the camshaft timing tube until it reaches the stopper.

    NOTICE:
    1. Be careful of the installation direction.
    2. Do not turn over the lip of the oil seal.



9. INSTALL CAMSHAFT DRIVE GEAR



  1. Align the timing tube knock pin with the knock pin groove of the drive gear, and temporarily install the drive gear with the 4 bolts.

  1. Using SST and a 5 mm hexagon wrench, uniformly tighten the 4 bolts in several steps, in the sequence shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    SST
    09960-10010   (09962-01000, 09963-00500)

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the timing tube.



10. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Mount the hexagonal portion of the camshaft in a vise.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the camshaft.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the camshaft and camshaft timing tube.




  1. Align the camshaft knock pin with the knock pin hole of the timing tube, and push the timing tube by hand until the camshaft knock pin touches the bottom of the timing tube knock pin hole.

    NOTICE:
    Do not forcefully push on the camshaft timing tube. This may cause the camshaft knock pin tip to damage the installation surface of the camshaft timing tube.

  1. Using a 10 mm socket hexagon wrench, install the bolt.

    Torque:
    78 N*m{ 795 kgf*cm , 58 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the seal washer and screw plug.

    Torque:
    15 N*m{ 153 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }

28. ADD ENGINE COOLANT

  1. Add engine coolant.

    Standard Capacity:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    w/o Transmission oil cooler
    		w/o Rear Heater
    		13.4 liters (14.2 US qts, 11.8 Imp. qts)
    w/ Rear Heater
    		16.3 liters (17.2 US qts, 14.3 Imp. qts)
    w/ Transmission oil cooler
    		w/o Rear Heater
    		14.1 liters (14.9 US qts, 12.4 Imp. qts)
    w/ Rear Heater
    		16.9 liters (17.9 US qts, 14.9 Imp. qts)

    HINT:
    TOYOTA vehicles are filled with TOYOTA SLLC at the factory. In order to avoid damage to the engine cooling system and other technical problems, only use TOYOTA SLLC or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology consists of a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).




  1. Slowly pour coolant into the radiator reservoir until it reaches the F line.

  1. Install the radiator reservoir cap.

  1. Press the inlet and outlet radiator hoses several times by hand, and then check the coolant level. If the coolant level is low, add coolant.

  1. Install the radiator cap.

  1. Start the engine and warm it up until the thermostat opens.

    NOTICE:
    Switch off the A/C.

  1. Maintain the engine speed at 2000 to 2500 rpm.

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure that the radiator reservoir still has some coolant in it.
    2. Pay attention to the needle of the water temperature meter. Make sure that the needle does not show an abnormally high temperature.
    3. If there is not enough coolant, the engine may burn out or overheat.
    4. Immediately after starting the engine, if the radiator reservoir does not have any coolant, perform the following: 1) stop the engine, 2) wait until the coolant has cooled down, and 3) add coolant until the coolant is filled to the F line.
    5. Run the engine at 2000 rpm until the coolant level has stabilized.

  1. Press the inlet and outlet radiator hoses several times by hand to bleed air.

    CAUTION:
    1. Wear protective gloves.
    2. Be careful as the radiator hoses are hot.
    3. Keep your hands away from the cooling fan.

  1. Stop the engine, and wait until the engine coolant cools down to the ambient temperature.

    CAUTION:
    Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Check that the coolant level is between the F and L lines. If the coolant level is below the L line, repeat all of the procedures above. If the coolant level is above the F line, drain coolant so that the coolant level is between the F and L lines.

30. INSPECT FOR OIL LEAK

  1. Start the engine. Make sure that there are no oil leaks from the area that was worked on.



31. INSPECT FOR COOLANT LEAK



  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and attach a radiator cap tester.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Using the radiator cap tester, increase the pressure inside the radiator to 123 kPa (1.3 kgf/cm2, 18 psi), and check that the pressure does not drop.
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks. If no external leaks are found, check the heater core, cylinder block and head.



32. INSPECT FOR FUEL LEAK

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and intelligent tester ON.

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine.

  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Active Test / Activate the Fuel Pump Speed Control.

  1. Check that there are no fuel leaks after doing maintenance anywhere on the fuel system.



33. INSPECT FOR EXHAUST GAS LEAK
If gas is leaking, tighten the areas necessary to stop the leak. Replace damaged parts as necessary.


34. CHECK ENGINE OIL LEVEL

  1. Warm up the engine, then stop the engine and wait for 5 minutes.

  1. Check that the engine oil level is between the low level and full level marks on the dipstick.
    If low, check for leakage and add oil up to the full level mark.

    NOTICE:
    Do not fill engine oil above the full level mark.



35. INSPECT IGNITION TIMING

  1. Warm up and stop the engine.

    1. Allow the engine to warm up to a normal operating temperature.

  1. When using the intelligent tester:

    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

      NOTICE:
      Switch off all accessories and the A/C before connecting the intelligent tester.

    2. Start the engine and idle it.

    3. Push the intelligent tester main switch ON.

    4. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Data List / Primary / IGN Advance.

      HINT:
      Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

      Standard ignition timing:
      5 to 15° BTDC @ idling (transmission in neutral and A/C switch off)

    5. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.




  1. When not using the intelligent tester:

    1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.

    2. Connect the tester probe of a timing light to the wire of the ignition coil connector for the No. 1 cylinder.

      NOTICE:
      Use a timing light that detects primary signals.




    3. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of the DLC3.

      SST
      09843-18040  

      NOTICE:
      1. Confirm the terminal numbers before connecting them. Connecting the wrong terminals can damage the engine.
      2. Switch off all accessories and the A/C.




    4. Using the timing light, check the ignition timing.

      Ignition timing:
      5 to 15° BTDC @ idling (transmission in neutral and A/C switch off)

    5. Remove SST from the DLC3.

    6. Disconnect the timing light from the engine.

    7. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 nuts.

      Torque:
      7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }



36. INSPECT ENGINE IDLE SPEED

  1. Warm up and stop the engine.

    1. Allow the engine to warm up to a normal operating temperature.

  1. When using the intelligent tester:

    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

      NOTICE:
      Switch off all accessories and the A/C before connecting the intelligent tester.

    2. Race the engine at 2500 rpm for approximately 90 seconds.

    3. Push the intelligent tester main switch ON.

    4. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Data List / Primary / Engine Speed.

      Standard idle speed:
      650 to 750 rpm (transmission in neutral and A/C switch off)

      HINT:
      Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

      If the idle speed is not as specified, check the air intake system.

    5. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.




  1. When not using the intelligent tester:

    1. Using SST, connect a tachometer probe to terminal 9 (TAC) of the DLC3.

      SST
      09843-18030  

      NOTICE:
      1. Confirm the terminal number before connecting the probe. Connecting the probe to the wrong terminal can damage the engine.
      2. Switch off all accessories and the A/C.

    2. Race the engine at 2500 rpm for approximately 90 seconds.

    3. Check the idle speed.

      Standard idle speed:
      650 to 750 rpm (transmission in neutral and A/C switch off)

      If the idle speed is not as specified, check the air intake system.

    4. Disconnect the tachometer from the DLC3.

  1. Check that the engine coolant level is between the L and F lines when the engine is cold.
    If the engine coolant is low, check for leaks and add "TOYOTA Super Long Life Coolant" or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite and non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology to the F line.

    NOTICE:

  1. Remove the radiator cap, and check that the coolant level is up to the radiator filler hole.
