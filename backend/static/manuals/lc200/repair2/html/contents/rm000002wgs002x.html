OIL PRESSURE SENSOR > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL OIL PRESSURE SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the sender gauge.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1344, Three Bond 1344 or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    Do not let adhesive adhere to the oil hole.




  1. Install the sender gauge.

    Torque:
    15 N*m{ 153 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine within 1 hour of installation.

  1. Connect the sender gauge connector.



2. CHECK ENGINE OIL LEVEL

  1. Warm up the engine, then stop the engine and wait for 5 minutes.

  1. Check that the engine oil level is between the low level and full level marks on the dipstick.
    If low, check for leakage and add oil up to the full level mark.

    NOTICE:
    Do not fill engine oil above the full level mark.



3. INSPECT FOR OIL LEAK

  1. Start the engine. Make sure that there are no oil leaks from the area that was worked on.



4. INSTALL NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the No. 1 engine under cover with the 10 bolts.

    Torque:
    29 N*m{ 296 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }



5. INSTALL FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



6. INSTALL FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Push in the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to install the fender splash shield.

  1. Install the 3 bolts and screw.
