LIGHTING SYSTEM > Headlight Signal Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The main body ECU receives a headlight Lo switch information signal from the light control switch, and illuminates the headlight low beam.

6.CHECK HEADLIGHT (LOW BEAM)



  1. Disconnect the 2B ECU connector.

  1. Using a service wire, connect terminal 2B-18 (HRLY) on the wire harness side and body ground.

  1. Check that the headlight low beam illuminates.

    OK:
    Headlight low beam illuminates.
