CHECK ILLUMINATED ENTRY SYSTEM OPERATION

  1. The illuminated entry system controls the following lights:

    1. Engine switch illumination
    2. Map light
    3. Room light

  2. Check the illuminated entry system with the engine switch off.

    1. Check that the lights turn on when unlocking any of the doors that are closed and locked with the engine switch off. Then check that the lights fade out under the following conditions:

      1. Leave the doors unlocked for 15 seconds.
      2. Turn the engine switch on (ACC) or on (IG).
      3. Lock all the doors.

    2. Check that the lights stay on for at least 15 seconds after opening any of the doors before fading out. Then check that the lights fade out 15 seconds after closing all the doors.

  3. Check the illuminated entry system with the engine switch on (ACC) or on (IG).

    1. Check that the lights stay lit after turning the engine switch off with all the doors closed and the engine switch on (ACC) or on (IG). Then check that the lights fade out under the following conditions:

      1. Leave the doors unlocked for 15 seconds.
      2. Turn the engine switch on (ACC) or on (IG).
      3. Lock all the doors.

    2. Check that the lights stay on for at least 15 seconds after opening any of the doors. Then check that the lights fade out 15 seconds after closing all the doors.

    3. Check that the lights turn on when opening any of the doors and fade out when closing and locking all the doors or turning the engine switch on (ACC) or on (IG).


CHECK BATTERY SAVER OPERATION

  1. Turn the engine switch off and close all the doors.

  2. Open the driver side door to turn the room light on, and leave the door open. Check that the light turns off after about 20 minutes.

  3. After the room light turns off, close the driver door.

  4. Open any door to turn the room light on, and then open another door. Check that the room light turns off within 20 minutes after opening the doors.


CHECK LIGHT AUTO TURN OFF OPERATION

  1. Prepare the vehicle so that the engine switch is on (IG) and the driver door is closed.

  2. Set the headlight dimmer switch assembly to TAIL, HEAD or AUTO to illuminate the lights.

  3. Turn the engine switch off and open the driver door. When the headlight dimmer switch is on AUTO, check that the headlights, fog lights, and taillights turn off. When the headlight dimmer switch is on head or tail, check that the headlights and front fog lights turn off.
