1. INSPECT HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY (ENGINE HOOD COURTESY SWITCH)


  1. for LHD:




    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1 - 2
      		Unlock
      		Below 1 Ω
      1 - 2
      		Lock
      		10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the hood lock assembly.



  1. for RHD:




    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1 - 2
      		Unlock
      		Below 1 Ω
      1 - 2
      		Lock
      		10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the hood lock assembly.
