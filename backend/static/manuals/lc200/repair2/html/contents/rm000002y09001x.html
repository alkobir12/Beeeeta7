CAUTION:
  1. Prolonged and repeated contact with engine oil will result in the removal of natural oils from the skin, leading to dryness, irritation and dermatitis. In addition, used engine oil contains potentially harmful contaminants which may cause skin cancer.
  2. Precautions should be taken when replacing engine oil to minimize the risk of your skin making contact with used engine oil. Protective clothing and gloves that cannot be penetrated by oil should be worn. The skin should be washed with soap and water, or use waterless hand cleaner, to remove any used engine oil thoroughly. Do not use gasoline, thinners, or solvents.
  3. In order to protect the environment, used oil and used oil filters must be disposed of at designated disposal sites.



1. REMOVE OIL FILLER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY


2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



5. REMOVE REAR ENGINE UNDER COVER SEAL



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and cover seal.



6. DRAIN ENGINE OIL



  1. Remove the oil drain plug and gasket, and drain the engine oil into a container.



7. REMOVE OIL FILTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, remove the oil filter.

    SST
    09228-07501  



8. INSTALL OIL FILTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Clean and check the oil filter installation surface.

  1. Apply clean engine oil to the gasket of a new oil filter.

  1. Lightly screw the oil filter into place by hand. Tighten it until the gasket contacts the seat.




  1. Using SST, tighten the oil filter. Choose from the following to tighten the oil filter.

    SST
    09228-07501  

    1. Using a torque wrench, tighten the oil filter.

      Torque:
      17 N*m{ 175 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    2. Tighten the oil filter a 3/4 turn by hand or with a common wrench.



9. ADD ENGINE OIL

  1. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

    Torque:
    39 N*m{ 398 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

  1. Add new engine oil.

    Standard Engine Oil:
    Oil Grade
    		Oil Viscosity (SAE)
    API grade SL "Energy-Conserving", SM "Energy-Conserving" or ILSAC multigrade engine oil
    		10W-30
    (10W-30 is best choice for fuel economy and good starting in cold weather).
    API grade SL or SM multigrade engine oil
    		10W-30

    Standard Oil Capacity:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Drain and refill with oil filter change
    		6.2 liters (6.6 US qts, 5.5 Imp. qts)
    Drain and refill without oil filter change
    		5.7 liters (6.0 US qts, 5.0 Imp. qts)
    Dry fill
    		7.1 liters (7.5 US qts, 6.2 Imp. qts)



10. INSTALL OIL FILLER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY


11. CHECK ENGINE OIL LEVEL

  1. Warm up the engine, then stop the engine and wait for 5 minutes.

  1. Check that the engine oil level is between the low level and full level marks on the dipstick.
    If low, check for leakage and add oil up to the full level mark.

    NOTICE:
    Do not fill engine oil above the full level mark.



12. INSPECT FOR OIL LEAK

  1. Start the engine. Make sure that there are no oil leaks from the area that was worked on.



13. INSTALL REAR ENGINE UNDER COVER SEAL



  1. Install the cover seal with the 2 bolts.



14. INSTALL NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the No. 1 engine under cover with the 10 bolts.

    Torque:
    29 N*m{ 296 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }



15. INSTALL FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Push in the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to install the fender splash shield.

  1. Install the 3 bolts and screw.



16. INSTALL FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
