SHIFT LEVER > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY





2. REMOVE INDICATOR LIGHT WIRE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a thin-bladed screwdriver, release the connector housing retainer.

  1. Using a thin-bladed screwdriver, pull out the 4 (ILL+) and 8 (ILL-) terminals from the rear as shown in the illustration.




  1. Rotate the indicator light wire counterclockwise to align the key part and remove the wire.



3. REMOVE POSITION INDICATOR HOUSING ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the position indicator housing from the floor shift.



4. REMOVE POSITION INDICATOR LENS



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the position indicator lens from the floor shift.



5. REMOVE POSITION INDICATOR SLIDE COVER AND NO. 2 POSITION INDICATOR SLIDE COVER



  1. Remove the position indicator slide cover with No. 2 position indicator slide cover.

  1. Remove the No. 2 position indicator slide cover from the position indicator slide cover.



6. REMOVE SHIFT LOCK RELEASE BUTTON



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the button and spring.



7. REMOVE LOWER POSITION INDICATOR HOUSING



  1. Using a small screwdriver, detach the 6 claws and remove the housing.



8. REMOVE SHIFT LOCK CONTROL ECU



  1. Disconnect the shift lock solenoid connector.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the ECU.
