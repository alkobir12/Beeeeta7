CYLINDER HEAD > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. REPLACE VALVE STEM OIL SEAL



  1. Using needle-nose pliers, remove the valve stem oil seal.




  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the valve stem oil seal.

    HINT:
    The intake valve stem oil seal is light brown and the exhaust valve stem oil seal is gray.




  1. Using SST and a hammer, push in a new valve stem oil seal.

    SST
    09201-41020  



2. REPLACE VALVE GUIDE BUSH



  1. Gradually heat the cylinder head to 80 to 90°C (176 to 212°F).

  1. Place the cylinder head on wooden blocks.




  1. Using SST and hummer, tap out the valve guide bush.

    SST
    09201-10000  
    09201-01055  
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)




  1. Using a caliper gauge, measure the bush bore diameter of the cylinder head.

    Standard bush bore diameter:
    10.295 to 10.315 mm (0.405 to 0.406 in.)

    If the bush bore diameter of the cylinder head is more than 10.315 mm (0.406 in.), machine the bush bore to a diameter of 10.345 to 10.365 mm (0.407 to 0.408 in.).

    Standard Valve Guide Bush Diameter:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    STD
    		10.333 to 10.344 mm (0.4068 to 0.4072 in.)
    O/S 0.05
    		10.383 to 10.394 mm (0.4088 to 0.4092 in.)

    If the bush bore diameter of the cylinder head is more than 10.365 mm (0.4081 in.), replace the cylinder head.




  1. Gradually heat the cylinder head to 80 to 100°C (176 to 212°F).

  1. Place the cylinder head on wooden blocks.




  1. Using SST, tap in a new valve guide bush to the standard protrusion height labeled A shown in the illustration.

    SST
    09201-10000  
    09201-01055  
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)

    Standard protrusion height:
    9.3 to 9.7 mm (0.366 to 0.382 in.)

  1. Using a sharp 5.5 mm reamer, ream the valve guide bush to obtain the standard specified clearance between the valve guide bush and valve stem.

    Standard Overall Length:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Intake
    		0.025 to 0.060 mm (0.00984 to 0.00236 in.)
    Exhaust
    		0.030 to 0.065 mm (0.00118 to 0.00256 in.)



3. REPLACE UNION

  1. Remove the union from the bank 1 cylinder head (front side) and bank 2 cylinder head (intake port side).

  1. Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the bolt ends of new unions.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent



  1. Using a 12 mm deep socket wrench, install the 2 unions.

    Torque:
    15 N*m{ 150 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }






4. REPLACE TIGHT PLUG

NOTICE:
If water leaks from the tight plug or the plug corrodes, replace it.

  1. Remove the tight plugs.




  1. Apply adhesive around new tight plugs.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent



  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the tight plugs to the standard depth.

    SST
    09950-60010   (09951-00250)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)

    Standard depth:
    1.5 mm (0.0591 in.)






5. REPLACE HEAD STRAIGHT SCREW PLUG



  1. Using a 14 mm hexagon wrench, remove the straight screw plug and gasket.

  1. Using a 14 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and straight screw plug.

    Torque:
    80 N*m{ 816 kgf*cm , 59 ft.*lbf }




  1. Using a 14 mm hexagon wrench, remove the straight screw plug and gasket.

  1. Using a 14 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and straight screw plug.

    Torque:
    85 N*m{ 867 kgf*cm , 63 ft.*lbf }



6. REPLACE RING PIN

NOTICE:
It is not necessary to remove the ring pin unless it is being replaced.

  1. Remove the ring pins.



  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap in new ring pins until they reach the standard protrusion height labeled A shown in the illustration.

    Standard protrusion height:
    2.7 to 3.3 mm (0.106 to 0.0969 in.)






7. REPLACE STRAIGHT PIN

NOTICE:
It is not necessary to remove the straight pin unless it is being replaced.

  1. Remove the straight pins.



  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap in new straight pins until they reach the standard protrusion height labeled A shown in the illustration below.

    Standard Protrusion Height:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Pin "A"
    		17.5 to 19.5 mm (0.689 to 0.768 in.)
    Pin "B"
    		7.5 to 8.5 mm (0.295 to 0.335 in.)
    Pin "C"
    		7.0 to 9.0 mm (0.276 to 0.354 in.)






8. REPLACE STUD BOLT

NOTICE:
If a stud bolt is deformed or its threads are damaged, replace it.

  1. Using an E5 and E7 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the stud bolts.



  1. Using an E5 and E7 "TORX" socket wrench, install new stud bolts.

    Torque:
    for stud bolt A:
    4.0 N*m{ 41 kgf*cm , 35 in.*lbf }
    for stud bolt B:
    9.5 N*m{ 97 kgf*cm , 84 in.*lbf }
    for stud bolt C:
    4.0 N*m{ 41 kgf*cm , 35 in.*lbf }
