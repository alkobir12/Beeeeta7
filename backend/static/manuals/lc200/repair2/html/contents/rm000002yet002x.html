1. REPAIR VALVE SPRING SEAT



  1. If the seating is too high to the valve face, use 30° and 45° cutters to correct the seat.




  1. Intake Side:
    If the seating is too low to the valve face, use 60° and 45° cutters to correct the seat.




  1. Exhaust Side:
    If the seating is too low to the valve face, use 75° and 45° cutters to correct the seat.




  1. Hand-lap the valve and valve seat with an abrasive compound.

  1. After lapping the valve by hand, clean the valve and valve seat.
