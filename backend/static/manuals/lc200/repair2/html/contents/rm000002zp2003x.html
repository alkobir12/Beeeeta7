LIGHTING SYSTEM > Turn Signal Light Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The turn signal flasher receives a turn signal switch signal from the headlight dimmer switch, and illuminates the front turn signal light, outer rear view mirror and rear combination light.
