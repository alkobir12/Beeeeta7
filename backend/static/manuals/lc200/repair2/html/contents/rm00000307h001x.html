1. REPAIR INTAKE VALVE SEAT

NOTICE:
  1. Repair the seat while checking the seating position.
  2. Keep the lip free of foreign matter.
  3. Take off a cutter gradually to make the intake valve seat smooth.




  1. Using a 45° cutter, resurface the valve seats.

    HINT:
    Only remove the amount of metal necessary to clean the seats.




  1. If the seating is too high on the valve face:
    Use 30° and 45° cutters to correct the seat.




  1. If the seating is too low on the valve face:
    Use 60° and 45° cutters to correct the seat.

  1. Check that the seat contact is in the middle of the valve face and has the following width.

    Standard width:
    1.0 to 1.4 mm (0.0394 to 0.0551 in.)




  1. Hand-lap the valve and valve seat with an abrasive compound.

  1. Clean and check the valve seating position.
