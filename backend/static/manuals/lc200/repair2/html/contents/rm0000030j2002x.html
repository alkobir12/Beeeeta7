4WD CONTROL ECU > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



2. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (for LHD)

  1. w/ Floor Under Cover:

    1. Remove the No. 2 instrument panel under cover (Click here).



3. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



4. REMOVE FRONT PASSENGER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (for LHD)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the front passenger side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



5. REMOVE NO. 3 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH (for LHD)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the panel garnish.



6. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL BOX DOOR KNOB (for LHD)



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the knob.



7. REMOVE LOWER NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL (for LHD)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connector.



8. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL (for RHD)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



9. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (for RHD)



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



10. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH (for RHD)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 3 claws and remove the panel garnish.



11. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH (for RHD)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the panel garnish.



12. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (for RHD)

  1. w/ Floor Under Cover:

    1. Remove the No. 1 instrument panel under cover (Click here).



13. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL (for RHD)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



14. REMOVE DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (for RHD)



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and driver side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



15. REMOVE FOUR WHEEL DRIVE CONTROL ECU



  1. Disconnect the 2 ECU connectors.

  1. Remove the bolt and ECU.
