TRANSFER CASE FRONT OIL SEAL > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN TRANSFER OIL



  1. except 1GR-FE:
    Remove the 2 bolts and transfer dynamic damper.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and transfer heat insulator.




  1. Remove the 7 bolts and lower transfer case protector.




  1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.




  1. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the transfer oil.



2. REMOVE FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the front propeller shaft assembly (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT OUTPUT SHAFT COMPANION FLANGE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST and a hammer, release the staked part of the nut.

    SST
    09930-00010   (09931-00010, 09931-00020)

    NOTICE:
    1. Be sure to use SST with the tapered surface facing the shaft.
    2. Do not grind the tip of SST with a grinder, etc.
    3. Completely loosen the staked part of the nut when removing it.
    4. Do not damage the threads of the transfer driven sprocket.




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, remove the lock nut.

    SST
    09330-00021  




  1. Using SST, remove the companion flange.

    SST
    09950-40011   (09951-04020, 09952-04010, 09953-04030, 09954-04010, 09955-04051, 09957-04010, 09958-04011)

  1. Remove the O-ring from the companion flange.



4. REMOVE TRANSFER CASE FRONT OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST, tap out the oil seal.

    SST
    09308-00010  

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the oil seal and front transfer case contact surface.



5. INSTALL TRANSFER CASE FRONT OIL SEAL

  1. Apply transfer oil to the spline of the driven sprocket.




  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in a new oil seal until its surface is 1 to 1.5 mm (0.0394 to 0.0590 in.) from the case upper surface.

    SST
    09649-17010  
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)



6. INSTALL FRONT OUTPUT SHAFT COMPANION FLANGE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the companion flange onto the drive sprocket.




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, install a new O-ring and a new companion flange lock nut.

    SST
    09330-00021  

    Torque:
    127 N*m{ 1299 kgf*cm , 94 ft.*lbf }




  1. Using a chisel and hammer, stake the companion flange lock nut.



7. INSTALL FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the front propeller shaft assembly (Click here).



8. ADD TRANSFER OIL



  1. Install a new gasket onto the drain plug and then install the plug.

    Torque:
    37 N*m{ 377 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }




  1. Add oil so that the oil level is between 0 to 5.0 mm (0 to 0.197 in.) from the bottom lip of the filler plug hole.

    NOTICE:
    1. Add oil slowly.
    2. Add oil a little at a time, waiting several minutes between each addition of oil.

  1. Wait approximately 5 minutes and check that the oil level has not changed.




  1. Install a new gasket onto the filler plug and then install the plug.

    Torque:
    37 N*m{ 377 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the lower transfer case protector with the 7 bolts.

    Torque:
    14 N*m{ 139 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the transfer heat insulator with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    30 N*m{ 306 kgf*cm , 22 ft.*lbf }




  1. except 1GR-FE:
    Install the transfer dynamic damper with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    12 N*m{ 122 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



9. CHECK FOR TRANSFER OIL LEAK
