REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY (for Face to Face Seat Type) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION LEG SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the 2 E-rings.

  1. Remove the 2 hinge pins.




  1. Remove the 2 seat leg plates.

  1. Remove the seat cushion leg with the spring.

  1. Remove the spring from the seat cushion leg.



2. REMOVE REAR SIDE SEAT CUSHION COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the hog rings.




  1. Open the 2 fasteners, and then remove the seat cushion cover.



3. REMOVE REAR SIDE SEAT CUSHION PAD LH


4. REMOVE REAR SIDE SEATBACK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the hog rings.

  1. Pass the side seat band into the seatback cover.

  1. Remove the seatback cover.



5. REMOVE REAR SIDE SEAT BAND SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the screw and seat band.



6. REMOVE REAR SIDE SEATBACK PAD LH



  1. Remove the hog rings and seatback pad.
