REAR DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER OIL SEAL > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN DIFFERENTIAL OIL

  1. Stop the vehicle on a level place.




  1. for Front Differential:

    1. Remove the engine under cover.

    2. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, remove the filler plug and gasket.

    3. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the oil.

    4. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and the drain plug.

      Torque:
      39 N*m{ 400 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

  1. for Rear Differential:

    1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.

    2. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the oil.

    3. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

      Torque:
      49 N*m{ 500 kgf*cm , 36 ft.*lbf }



2. DISCONNECT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY



  1. Place matchmarks on the rear propeller shaft and companion flange.

  1. Remove the 4 nuts, 4 washers and 4 bolts, and disconnect the propeller shaft from the differential side.

  1. Support the propeller shaft securely.



3. REMOVE DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE REAR NUT



  1. Using SST and a hammer, loosen the staked part of the nut.

    SST
    09930-00010  




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, remove the nut.

    SST
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)



4. REMOVE REAR DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE REAR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, remove the companion flange.

    SST
    09950-30012   (09951-03010, 09953-03010, 09954-03010, 09955-03030, 09956-03040)



5. REMOVE REAR DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST, remove the oil seal.

    SST
    09308-10010  



6. REMOVE REAR DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION OIL SLINGER


7. REMOVE REAR DRIVE PINION FRONT TAPERED ROLLER BEARING



  1. Using SST, remove the front bearing (inner race) from the drive pinion.

    SST
    09556-22010  




  1. Using SST, remove the front bearing (outer race) from the drive pinion.

    SST
    09308-00010  



8. REPLACE REAR DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION BEARING SPACER



  1. Replace the bearing spacer with a new one.



9. INSTALL REAR DRIVE PINION FRONT TAPERED ROLLER BEARING



  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the bearing (outer race).

    SST
    09316-60011   (09316-00011, 09316-00021)

  1. Install the bearing (inner race).



10. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION OIL SLINGER


11. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in a new oil seal.

    SST
    09214-76011  

    Standard oil seal depth:
    0.05 to 0.95 mm (0.00197 to 0.0374 in.)

  1. Coat the lip of the oil seal with MP grease.



12. INSTALL REAR DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE REAR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, install the companion flange to the drive pinion.

    SST
    09950-30012   (09951-03010, 09953-03010, 09954-03010, 09955-03030, 09956-03040)



13. ADJUST DRIVE PINION PRELOAD

  1. Coat the threads of a new nut with hypoid gear oil.




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, install the nut by tightening it until the standard preload is reached.

    SST
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)

    Torque:
    441 N*m{ 4497 kgf*cm , 325 ft.*lbf } or less




  1. Using a torque wrench, measure the preload.

    Standard Preload (at Starting):
    Bearing
    		Specified Condition
    New
    		1.0 to 1.7 N*m (11 to 17 kgf*cm, 10 to 14 in.*lbf)
    Reused
    		0.9 to 1.4 N*m (9 to 13 kgf*cm, 8 to 12 in.*lbf)

    If the preload is not within the specification, adjust the preload.



14. STAKE DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE REAR NUT



  1. Using a chisel and hammer, stake the nut.



15. CONNECT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY



  1. Align the matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and differential flange.

  1. Connect the propeller shaft with the 4 bolts, 4 washers and 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    88 N*m{ 900 kgf*cm , 65 ft.*lbf }



16. ADD DIFFERENTIAL OIL



  1. Add differential oil so that the oil level is between 0 to 5 mm (0 to 0.197 in.) from the bottom lip of the differential filler plug hole.

    NOTICE:
    1. Too much or too little oil will lead to differential problems.
    2. After changing the oil, drive the vehicle and then check the oil level again.

    Front differential oil type and viscosity:
    Toyota Genuine Differential gear oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent

    Rear Differential Oil Type and Viscosity:
    Item
    		Oil Type and Viscosity
    w/o LSD
    		Toyota Genuine Differential gear oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent
    w/ LSD
    		Toyota Genuine Differential gear oil LX 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent

    Front differential capacity:
    1.85 to 1.95 liters (1.96 to 2.06 US qts., 1.63 to 1.71 Imp. qts.)

    Rear Differential Capacity:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Standard
    		4.15 to 4.25 liters (4.39 to 4.49 US qts., 3.66 to 3.74 Imp. qts.)
    w/ LSD
    		4.10 to 4.20 liters (4.34 to 4.43 US qts., 3.60 to 3.69 Imp. qts.)
    w/ Differential lock

  1. for Front Differential:

    1. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and the filler plug.

      Torque:
      39 N*m{ 400 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

    2. Install the engine under cover.

  1. for Rear Differential:

    1. Install a new gasket and the filler plug.

      Torque:
      49 N*m{ 500 kgf*cm , 36 ft.*lbf }
