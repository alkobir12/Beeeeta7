1. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR BACKLASH



  1. Install the 2 thrust washers to the 2 side gears.

  1. Install the 4 thrust washers to the 4 pinion gears.




  1. Install the 2 side gears into the differential case.

  1. Install the holder into the differential case.

  1. Install the 4 pinion gears.

  1. Align the holes of the differential case and pinion shaft, and install the 3 pinion shafts.

  1. Align the matchmarks and install the differential cover to the differential case.

  1. Temporarily install the 5 bolts and 3 pinion shaft pins.

  1. Using a T10 "TORX" socket, tighten the 5 bolts and 3 pinion shaft pins.

    Torque:
    58 N*m{ 590 kgf*cm , 43 ft.*lbf }




  1. Using a dial indicator, while holding the side gear, measure the backlash.

    Standard backlash:
    0.02 to 0.15 (0.000787 to 0.00591 in.)

    If the backlash is not within the specification, replace the side gear thrust washers with washers of a different thickness.

    HINT:
    1. Make sure the washers on both sides are the same size.
    2. Measure at all 4 pinion gear locations.
    3. Apply hypoid gear oil to each sliding surface and rotating part.


    Standard Thrust Washer Thickness:

    Specified Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1.53 to 1.57 mm (0.0602 to 0.0618 in.)
    		1.83 to 1.87 mm (0.0721 to 0.0737 in.)
    1.58 to 1.62 mm (0.0622 to 0.0638 in.)
    		1.88 to 1.92 mm (0.0740 to 0.0756 in.)
    1.63 to 1.67 mm (0.0641 to 0.0657 in.)
    		1.93 to 1.97 mm (0.0760 to 0.0776 in.)
    1.68 to 1.72 mm (0.0661 to 0.0677 in.)

    1.78 to 1.82 mm (0.0701 to 0.0717 in.)
    		2.08 to 2.12 mm (0.0820 to 0.0835 in.)

  1. After measuring the backlash, remove the 5 set bolts and 3 pinion shaft pins.



2. ASSEMBLE DIFFERENTIAL CASE

  1. Clean the threads of the bolts, pinion shaft pins, case and cover with non-residue solvent.




  1. Coat the threads of the bolts and pinion shafts with adhesive.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent

  1. Align the matchmarks of the case and cover.

  1. Using a T10 "TORX" socket, install the 5 bolts and 3 pinion shaft pins.

    Torque:
    58 N*m{ 590 kgf*cm , 43 ft.*lbf }



3. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL CASE BEARING



  1. Differential Cover Side:
    Using SST and a press, press the side bearing onto the differential cover.

    SST
    09550-60010  




  1. Ring Gear Side:
    Using SST and a press, press the side bearing onto the differential case.

    SST
    09550-60010  



4. INSTALL DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR

  1. Clean the threads of the bolts and differential case with non-residue solvent.

  1. Clean the contact surface of the differential case and ring gear.




  1. Heat the ring gear to about 100°C (212°F) in boiling water.

  1. Carefully take the ring gear out of the boiling water.

  1. After the moisture on the ring gear has completely evaporated, quickly align the matchmarks on the ring gear and differential case and set the ring gear onto the differential case. Then temporarily install the 12 bolts so that the bolt holes in the ring gear and differential case are aligned.




  1. After the ring gear cools down sufficiently, remove the 12 bolts. Then apply thread lock adhesive to the 12 bolts and install them.

    Thread lock:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1360K, Three Bond 1360K or equivalent

    Torque:
    137 N*m{ 1397 kgf*cm , 101 ft.*lbf }



5. INSPECT RUNOUT OF DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR

  1. Place the bearing outer races on their respective bearings.

    HINT:
    Do not interchange the left and right outer races.

  1. Install the assembled plate washers to the side bearing.

  1. Install the differential case to the differential carrier.

    HINT:
    If it is difficult to install the differential case to the carrier, replace the plate washer with a thinner one.
    However, select a plate washer that allows no clearance between it and the carrier.

  1. Align the matchmarks on the bearing caps and differential carrier.

  1. Install and uniformly tighten the 4 bolts a little at a time.




  1. Using a dial indicator, check the ring gear runout.

    Maximum runout:
    0.05 mm (0.00197 in.)

    If the runout is more than the maximum, replace the ring gear.

  1. Remove the differential case.

8. INSTALL REAR DRIVE PINION REAR TAPERED ROLLER BEARING

    HINT:

  1. Using SST and a press, press the rear bearing into the drive pinion.

    SST

9. INSPECT DRIVE PINION PRELOAD

  1. Install the drive pinion and front bearing.

    HINT:
    Assemble the spacer and oil seal after adjusting the gear contact pattern.

  1. Install the oil slinger.




  1. Using SST, install the companion flange.

    SST
    09950-30012   (09951-03010, 09953-03010, 09954-03010, 09955-03030, 09956-03040)




  1. Using SST to hold the flange, adjust the drive pinion preload by tightening the nut.

    SST
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)

    NOTICE:
    1. Coat the nut and threads of the drive pinion with gear oil.
    2. As there is no spacer, tighten the nut a little at a time, being careful not to overtighten it.




  1. Using a torque wrench, measure the preload.

    Standard Preload (at Starting):
    Bearing
    		Specified Condition
    New
    		1.0 to 1.7 N*m (11 to 17 kgf*cm, 10 to 14 in.*lbf)
    Reused
    		0.9 to 1.4 N*m (9 to 13 kgf*cm, 8 to 12 in.*lbf)

    NOTICE:
    Measure the total preload after turning the bearing clockwise and counterclockwise several times to make the bearing smooth.



10. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Place the bearing outer races on their respective bearings.

    HINT:
    Do not interchange the left and right outer races.

  1. Install the assembled plate washer to the side bearing.

  1. Install the differential case to the carrier.




  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap on the ring gear to make sure the differential case is aligned with the carrier, and fit the bearings and plate washers into the carrier.

    HINT:
    If it is difficult to install the differential case to the carrier, replace the plate washer with a thinner one.
    However, select a plate washer that allows no clearance between it and the carrier.

  1. Remove the 2 plate washers and differential carrier.

  1. Install the selected plate washer into the lower part of the carrier.

  1. Place the selected plate washer onto the differential case together with the outer races, and install the differential case with the outer races to the carrier.

  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap on the ring gear to make sure the differential case is aligned with the carrier, and fit the bearings and plate washers into the carrier.




  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the ring gear backlash.

    Standard backlash:
    0.10 to 0.20 mm (0.00394 to 0.00787 in.)

    If the backlash is not within the specification, adjust it by either increasing or decreasing the thickness of washers on both sides by an equal amount.

    HINT:

  1. Ensure that there is ring gear backlash.



12. ADJUST SIDE BEARING PRELOAD

  1. Remove the ring gear side plate washer.




  1. Using a micrometer, measure the thickness.

  1. Using the backlash as a reference, select a washer that is 0.06 to 0.09 mm (0.00236 to 0.00354 in.) thicker than the removed washer.

    HINT:
    Select a washer which can be pressed in 2/3 of the full amount with your finger.

  1. Install the selected plate washer.




  1. Using SST and plastic-faced hammer, tap the plate washer so that it fits to the bearing.

    SST
    09504-22011  




  1. Align the matchmarks on the caps and carrier.

  1. Install the 4 bearing cap bolts to the specified torque.

    Torque:
    113 N*m{ 1150 kgf*cm , 83 ft.*lbf }

  1. Using a dial indicator, adjust the ring gear backlash until it is within the specification.

    Standard backlash:
    0.10 to 0.20 mm (0.00394 to 0.00787 in.)



13. INSPECT TOTAL PRELOAD



  1. Using a torque wrench, measure the preload with the teeth of the drive pinion and ring gear in contact.

    Standard total preload (at starting):
    Standard drive pinion preload plus 0.29 to 0.49 N*m (3 to 5 kgf*cm, 3 to 4 in.*lbf)

26. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR BACKLASH



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the ring gear backlash.

    Standard backlash:
    0.10 to 0.20 mm (0.00394 to 0.00787 in.)

    HINT:
    Measure at 3 or more positions around the circumference of the ring gear.

    If the backlash is not as specified, adjust the side bearing preload or repair as necessary.

29. INSTALL DIFFERENTIAL LOCK SHIFT ACTUATOR

  1. Remove any old FIPG material.

  1. Clean the contact surfaces of any residual FIPG material using non-residue solvent.




  1. Apply seal packing to the actuator.

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing 1281, Three Bond 1281 or equivalent

    HINT:
    Install the actuator within 10 minutes after applying seal packing.




  1. Install the shift fork and actuator to the differential and align the shift fork hole with the shift lock.

  1. Clean the threads of the set bolt and fork shaft with non-residue solvent.




  1. Coat the threads of the set bolt with adhesive.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent

  1. Install the shift fork shaft set bolt.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Engage the sleeve with the dog clutch of the differential case.




  1. Install the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    24 N*m{ 245 kgf*cm , 18 ft.*lbf }



30. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL LOCK COVER

  1. Remove any old FIPG material.

  1. Clean the contact surfaces of any residual FIPG material using non-residue solvent.




  1. Apply seal packing to the cover

    HINT:

  1. Install the cover with the 3 bolts.

    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }
