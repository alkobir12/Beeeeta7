1. INSPECT AND ADJUST ADJUSTING SHIM



  1. Measure the differential case LH and RH dimensions labeled X shown in the illustration.




  1. Install the 3 thrust washers and 2 clutch plates to the side gear.

  1. Using SST to press down the thrust washers and clutch plates with a force of approximately 98 N*m (1000 kgf*cm, 72 ft.*lbf), measure dimension "Y" shown in the illustration.

    SST
    09649-17010  



  1. Referring to the following table, select the proper adjusting shim.

    Standard Adjusting Shim Thickness:
    Adjusting shim thickness = X - Y - 19.08 mm (0.751 in.)
    Mark
    		Specified Condition
    A
    		0.20 mm (0.00787 in.)
    B
    		0.25 mm (0.00984 in.)
    C
    		0.30 mm (0.0118 in.)
    D
    		0.35 mm (0.0138 in.)









2. INSPECT REAR DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR



  1. Install the thrust washers, clutch plates and adjusting shim to the side gear.

    HINT:
    When installing the adjusting shim, make sure the surface with no oil groove faces the differential case side.

  1. Install the side gear to the differential case.

  1. Install the 4 pinion gears and thrust washers to the spider.




  1. Align the spring retainer holes with the straight pins and install the retainer.

  1. Install the spider assembly to the differential case.

    HINT:
    Install the spider to the differential case tightly and do not move the spring retainer.




  1. Using the dial indicator, measure the side gear backlash while holding the side gear and spider.

    Standard backlash:
    0.02 to 0.15 mm (0.000787 to 0.00591 in.)

    HINT:
    1. Measure at all 4 pinion gear locations.
    2. Measure the backlash at the differential case LH and RH.
    3. Apply hypoid gear oil LSD to each sliding surface and rotating part.

    If the backlash is not within the specification, replace the side gear thrust washers with washers of a different thickness.



3. ASSEMBLE DIFFERENTIAL CASE

  1. Install the spider assembly to the differential case LH.

    HINT:
    Install the spider to the differential case LH tightly and do not move the spring retainer.

  1. Install the compression spring and spring retainer RH.

  1. Install the side gear assembly RH.

    NOTICE:
    1. Be careful not to drop the side gear.
    2. Check the pinion and side gear alignment.




  1. Align the matchmarks and assemble the differential case LH and RH.

  1. Install the 8 bolts and tighten them uniformly, a little at a time.

    Torque:
    47 N*m{ 479 kgf*cm , 35 ft.*lbf }



4. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL CASE BEARING



  1. Using SST and a press, press the RH side bearing (inner race) into the differential case.

  1. Using SST and a press, press the LH side bearing (inner race) into the differential case.

    SST
    09710-30050  
    09950-60010   (09951-00480)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)



5. INSTALL DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR

  1. Clean the threads of the bolts and differential case with non-residue solvent.

  1. Clean the contact surface of the differential case and ring gear.




  1. Heat the ring gear to about 100°C (212°F) in boiling water.

  1. Carefully take the ring gear out of the boiling water.

  1. After the moisture on the ring gear has completely evaporated, quickly align the matchmarks on the ring gear and differential case and set the ring gear onto the differential case. Then temporarily install the 12 bolts so that the bolt holes in the ring gear and differential case are aligned.




  1. After the ring gear cools down sufficiently, remove the 12 bolts. Then apply thread lock adhesive to the 12 bolts and install them.

    Thread lock:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1360 K, Three Bond 1360 K or equivalent

    Torque:
    137 N*m{ 1397 kgf*cm , 101 ft.*lbf }



6. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR RUNOUT

  1. Place the bearing outer races on their respective bearings.

    HINT:
    Do not interchange the left and right outer races.

  1. Install the assembled plate washers to the side bearing.

  1. Install the differential case to the differential carrier.

    HINT:
    If it is difficult to install the differential case to the carrier, replace the plate washer with a thinner one. However, select a plate washer that allows no clearance between it and the carrier.

  1. Align the matchmarks on the bearing caps and differential carrier.

  1. Install and uniformly tighten the 4 bolts a little at a time.




  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the ring gear runout.

    Maximum runout:
    0.05 mm (0.00197 in.)

    If the runout is more than the maximum, replace the ring gear.

  1. Remove the differential case.

9. INSTALL REAR DRIVE PINION REAR TAPERED ROLLER BEARING

  1. Install the washer to the drive pinion.

    HINT:
    After installing the washer, check the tooth contact pattern. If necessary, replace the washer with one of a different thickness.




  1. Using SST and a press, press the rear bearing (inner race) into the drive pinion.

    SST
    09506-35010  



10. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION PRELOAD



  1. Install the drive pinion, front bearing (inner race) and oil slinger.

    HINT:
    Install the spacer and oil seal after adjusting the gear contact pattern.




  1. Using SST, install the companion flange.

    SST
    09950-30012   (09951-03010, 09953-03010, 09954-03010, 09955-03030, 09956-03040)




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, install the nut.

    SST
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)

    Torque:
    441 N*m{ 4497 kgf*cm , 325 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Apply hypoid gear oil LSD to the nut.
    2. As there is no spacer, tighten a little at a time, being careful not to overtighten it.




  1. Using a torque wrench, measure the preload.

    Standard Preload (at Starting):
    Bearing
    		Specified Condition
    New
    		1.0 to 1.7 N*m (11 to 17 kgf*cm, 10 to 14 in.*lbf)
    Reused
    		0.9 to 1.4 N*m (9 to 13 kgf*cm, 8 to 12 in.*lbf)

    HINT:
    Measure the total preload after turning the bearing clockwise and counterclockwise several times to make the bearing smooth.



11. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Place the bearing outer races on their respective bearings.

    HINT:
    Do not interchange the left and right outer races.

  1. Install the differential case to the carrier.

    HINT:
    Make sure that there is backlash between the ring gear and drive pinion.



12. INSTALL REAR DIFFERENTIAL BEARING ADJUSTING NUT



  1. Install the 2 adjusting nuts to the carrier, making sure the nuts are threaded properly.



13. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR BACKLASH



  1. Align the matchmarks on the bearing caps and carrier. Screw in the 4 bearing cap bolts 2 or 3 turns and press down the bearing caps by hand.

    HINT:
    If the bearing caps do not fit tightly on the carrier, the adjusting nuts are not engaged properly. Reinstall the adjusting nuts if necessary.




  1. Tighten the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    90 N*m{ 918 kgf*cm , 66 ft.*lbf }




  1. Loosen the 4 bolts to the point where the adjusting nuts can be turned by SST.

    SST
    09504-00011  

  1. Using SST, tighten the adjusting nut on the ring gear side until the ring gear has a backlash of about 0.2 mm (0.00787 in.).




  1. While turning the ring gear, use SST to tighten the adjusting nut on the drive pinion side. After the bearings are settled, loosen the adjusting nut on the drive pinion side.

    SST
    09504-00011  




  1. Place a dial indicator on the top of the adjusting nut on the ring gear side.

  1. Adjust the side bearing to 0 preload by tightening the other adjusting nut until the pointer on the indicator begins to move.




  1. Using SST, tighten the adjusting nut 1 to 1.5 notches from the 0 preload position.

    SST
    09504-00011  




  1. Using a dial indicator, adjust the ring gear backlash until it is within the specification.

    SST
    09504-00011  

    Standard backlash:
    0.10 to 0.20 mm (0.00394 to 0.00787 in.)

    HINT:
    The backlash is adjusted by turning the left and right adjusting nuts by an equal amount. For example, loosen the nut on the left side 1 notch and tighten the nut on the right side 1 notch.

  1. Tighten the 4 bearing cap bolts.

    Torque:
    90 N*m{ 918 kgf*cm , 66 ft.*lbf }

  1. After rotating the ring gear 5 turns or more, recheck the ring gear backlash.

    Standard backlash:
    0.10 to 0.20 mm (0.00394 to 0.00787 in.)

27. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the ring gear backlash.

    Standard backlash:
    0.10 to 0.20 mm (0.00394 to 0.00787 in.)

    HINT:
    Measure at 3 or more positions around the circumference of the ring gear.

    If the backlash is not as specified, adjust the side bearing preload or repair as necessary.



28. INSPECT RUNOUT OF REAR DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE REAR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the runout of the companion flange vertically and laterally.

    Maximum Runout:
    Runout
    		Specified Condition
    Vertical runout
    		0.10 mm (0.00394 in.)
    Lateral runout
    		0.10 mm (0.00394 in.)

    If the runout is more than the maximum, replace the companion flange.



29. STAKE DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE REAR NUT
