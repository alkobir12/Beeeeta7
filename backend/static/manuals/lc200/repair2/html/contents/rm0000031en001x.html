1. INSPECT COMMON RAIL ASSEMBLY RH


  1. Inspect the fuel pressure sensor.




    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1 - 2
      		25°C (77°F)
      		16.4 kΩ or less
      2 - 3
      		25°C (77°F)
      		3 kΩ or less

      If the result is not as specified, replace the common rail assembly RH.
