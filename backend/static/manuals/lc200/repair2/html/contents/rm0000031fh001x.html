  1. Check for leakage or clogging between the air cleaner housing and inlet turbocharger and between the outlet turbocharger and cylinder head.
    Condition

    Leakage from connections
    		Check each connection and repair
    Cracks in components
    		Check and replace

    		Operation

    		Repair or replace

3. CHECK TURBOCHARGING PRESSURE

  1. Warm up the engine.




  1. Using a 3-way connector, connect SST (turbocharger pressure gauge) to the hose between the diesel turbo pressure sensor and the gas filter leading to the intake air connector.

    SST
    09992-00242  

  1. for Automatic Transmission:
    Fully depress the accelerator pedal. Measure the turbocharging pressure at maximum speed (4700 to 4900 rpm).
    for Manual Transmission:
    While depressing the clutch pedal, fully depress the accelerator pedal. Measure the turbocharging pressure at maximum speed (4700 to 4900 rpm).

    Standard pressure:
    10 to 40 kPa (0.1 to 0.4 kgf/cm2, 1.5 to 5.8 psi)

    If the pressure is less than the specification, check both the intake air and exhaust systems for leakage. If there is no leakage, check if the vacuum hose has been disconnected. If it has not, check the turbocharger.
