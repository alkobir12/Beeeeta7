NOTICE:
When replacing an injector (including interchanging injectors between cylinders), common rail, replace the corresponding injection pipes with a new one.



1. INSTALL COMMON RAIL ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install the common rail with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



2. INSTALL INJECTION PIPE RH



  1. Using a union nut wrench, install the 4 injection pipes.

    Torque:
    without union nut wrench:
    34 N*m{ 347 kgf*cm , 25 ft.*lbf }
    with union nut wrench:
    31 N*m{ 316 kgf*cm , 23 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure there is no damage or foreign matter on the seal surfaces.
    2. The injection pipe can only be reused 5 times. After the fifth time, the injection pipe must be replaced.


    HINT:

    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).
    2. Make sure the union nut wrench and the torque wrench are connected in a straight line.




  1. Install the 4 injection pipe clamps with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }



3. INSTALL NO. 5 INJECTION PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Temporarily install the No. 5 injection pipe by hand.

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure there is no damage or foreign matter on the seal surfaces.
    2. The injection pipe can only be reused 5 times. After the fifth time, the injection pipe must be replaced.




  1. Temporarily install the used gasket and No. 2 fuel pipe with the nut, bolt and union bolt.




  1. Temporarily install the No. 2 injection pipe clamp with the bolt.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }




  1. Using a union nut wrench, tighten the No. 5 injection pipe ends.

    Torque:
    without union nut wrench:
    34 N*m{ 347 kgf*cm , 25 ft.*lbf }
    with union nut wrench:
    31 N*m{ 316 kgf*cm , 23 ft.*lbf }


    HINT:

    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).
    2. Make sure the union nut wrench and the torque wrench are connected in a straight line.




  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 injection pipe clamp.




  1. Remove the union bolt and gasket.

  1. Remove the bolt, nut and No. 2 fuel pipe.

35. BLEED CLUTCH LINE (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Remove the release cylinder bleeder plug cap.

  1. Connect a vinyl tube to the bleeder plug.

  1. Depress the clutch pedal several times, and then loosen the bleeder plug while the pedal is depressed.

  1. When fluid no longer comes out, tighten the bleeder plug, and then release the clutch pedal.

  1. Repeat the previous 2 steps until all the air in the fluid is completely bled.

  1. Tighten the bleeder plug.

    Torque:
    11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the bleeder plug cap.

  1. Check that all the air has been bled from the clutch line.

38. ADD ENGINE COOLANT



  1. Remove the engine air bleed cap.




  1. Connect a clear hose to the engine air bleed pipe.




  1. Using a wrench, remove the vent plug.




  1. Fill the radiator with TOYOTA SLLC to the radiator reservoir filler neck.

    HINT:
    Pour TOYOTA SLLC until it spills out of the engine air bleed pipe.

    Standard Capacity (for Automatic Transmission):
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Front heater only
    		14.8 liters (15.6 US qts, 13.0 Imp. qts)
    Front heater and rear heater
    		17.6 liters (18.6 US qts, 15.5 Imp. qts)
    Front heater with viscous heater
    		15.2 liters (16.1 US qts, 13.4 Imp. qts)
    Front heater and rear heater with viscous heater
    		18.0 liters (19.0 US qts, 15.4 Imp. qts)

    Standard capacity (for Manual Transmission):
    15.4 liters (16.3 US qts, 13.5 Imp. qts)

    NOTICE:
    Do not substitute plain water for engine coolant.

    HINT:
    TOYOTA vehicles are filled with TOYOTA SLLC at the factory. In order to avoid damage to the engine cooling system and other technical problems, only use TOYOTA SLLC or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology consists of a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).




  1. Install the vent plug.

    Torque:
    2.0 N*m{ 20 kgf*cm , 18 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Do not tighten the plug to 5.0 N*m (51 kgf*cm, 44 in.*lbf) or more, as the plug will be damaged.




  1. Disconnect the clear hose from the engine air bleed pipe.




  1. Install the engine air bleed cap when coolant comes out.

  1. Install the radiator reservoir cap.

  1. Start the engine.

    NOTICE:
    Immediately after starting the engine, if the radiator reservoir does not have any coolant, perform the following: 1) stop the engine, 2) wait until the coolant has cooled down, and 3) add coolant until the coolant is filled to the FULL line.

  1. Maintain an engine speed of 3000 rpm for approximately 10 minutes so that the thermostat opens and air bleeding is performed.

    CAUTION:
    When pressing the radiator hoses:
    1. Wear protective gloves.
    2. Be careful as the radiator hoses are hot.
    3. Keep your hands away from the radiator fan

    NOTICE:
    1. Pay attention to the needle of the water temperature meter. Make sure that the needle does not show an abnormally high temperature.
    2. If there is not enough coolant, the engine may burn out or overheat.

    HINT:
    The thermostat opening timing can be confirmed by pressing the No. 2 radiator hose by hand, and checking when the engine coolant starts to flow inside the hose.

  1. Stop the engine, and wait until the engine coolant cools down to ambient temperature.

    CAUTION:
    Do not remove the radiator reservoir cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Check that the coolant level is between the FULL and LOW lines.
    If the coolant level is above the FULL line, drain coolant so that the coolant level is between the FULL and LOW lines.

      Tester Display

      		Diagnostic Note
      Test the Fuel Leak

      		Stop/Start

      2. The above conditions are preserved while Start is selected.



40. CHECK FOR ENGINE COOLANT LEAKS

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator reservoir cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and attach a radiator cap tester to the radiator reservoir.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Using the radiator cap tester, increase the pressure inside the radiator to 123 kPa (1.3 kgf/cm2, 17.8 psi), and check that the pressure does not drop.
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks.
    If no external leaks are found, check the cylinder block and cylinder head.
