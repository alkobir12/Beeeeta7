INTERCOOLER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL



  1. Remove the 7 clips and upper radiator support seal.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MAIN SEAL LH



  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 clips and remove the fender main seal.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MAIN SEAL RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM LH

  1. Remove the nut, wiper arm and blade.



5. REMOVE FRONT WIPER ARM RH

  1. Remove the nut, wiper arm and blade.



6. REMOVE HOOD TO COWL TOP SEAL



  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 12 clips, 4 clamps and remove the hood to cowl top seal.



7. REMOVE COWL TOP VENTILATOR LOUVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the washer hose.




  1. Remove the 2 clips.

  1. Detach the 17 claws and remove the cowl top ventilator louver sub-assembly.



8. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and No. 1 engine cover.



9. REMOVE INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the No. 1 air hose clamp.




  1. Loosen the No. 2 air hose clamp.




  1. Disconnect the turbo pressure sensor connector, intake air temperature sensor connector and vacuum hose.




  1. Remove the 6 nuts, 2 bolts and intercooler.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the air tube LH and RH.



10. REMOVE ENGINE COVER BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and engine cover bracket.
