1. INSPECT CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		Hot
    		835 to 1400 Ω
    Cold
    		1060 to 1645 Ω

    NOTICE:
    In the table above, the terms "cold" and "hot" refer to the temperature of the sensor. "Cold" means approximately -10 to 50°C (14 to 122°F). "Hot" means approximately 50 to 100°C (122 to 212°F).

    If the result is not as specified, replace the sensor.
