1. INSTALL CLUTCH RELEASE FORK SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply release hub grease to the release fork, release bearing, push rod contact point and pivot points shown in the illustration.

    Grease:
    Toyota Genuine Release Hub Grease or equivalent




  1. Apply clutch spline grease to the input shaft spline.

    Grease:
    Toyota Genuine Clutch Spline Grease or equivalent




  1. Install the release bearing to the clutch release fork with the clip.




  1. Install the release fork support to the transmission unit.

    Torque:
    47 N*m{ 480 kgf*cm , 35 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the clutch release fork with the clutch release bearing to the transmission unit.

    HINT:
    After installation, move the fork back and forth to check that the release bearing slides smoothly.



2. INSTALL TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the transfer with the 8 bolts.

    Torque:
    40 N*m{ 408 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Take care not to damage the adaptor oil seal with the transfer input shaft spline.



3. INSTALL MANUAL TRANSMISSION WITH TRANSFER (for 1GR-FE)



NOTICE:
Check that the 2 straight pins are attached to the engine side.

  1. Install the manual transmission with transfer with the 9 bolts.

    Torque:
    for bolt A:
    72 N*m{ 730 kgf*cm , 53 ft.*lbf }
    for bolt B:
    37 N*m{ 380 kgf*cm , 28 ft.*lbf }

    Bolt Length:
    Item
    		Length
    Bolt A
    		50 mm (1.97 in.)
    Bolt B
    		45 mm (1.77 in.)

8. INSTALL CLUTCH ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the clutch accumulator with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:

  1. Using a union nut wrench, connect the flexible hose tube.

9. INSTALL CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER TO ACCUMULATOR TUBE

  1. Install the tube with the bolt.

18. INSTALL TUBE CONNECTOR TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE (for 1VD-FTV)

19. INSTALL AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Install the air tube to the throttle body.

  1. Align the embossed mark of the throttle body with the paint mark of the No. 4 air hose.




  1. Tighten the hose clamp so that it is 7 to 11 mm (0.276 to 0.433 in.) from the end of the hose as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



20. INSTALL CLUTCH HOSE (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Connect the clutch hose to the air tube with the bolt labeled A.

  1. Temporarily install the clutch hose to the clutch master cylinder tube to flexible hose tube and tube connector to flexible hose tube, and fix it in place with the 2 clips.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the bolt labeled B.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



  1. Tighten the flexible hose tube.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the flexible hose tube.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the flexible hose tube from the connecting points.

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.). If using a torque wrench with a length that is not 30 cm (11.8 in.), calculate the torque specification for the torque wrench and SST based on the "without SST" torque specification (Click here).
    2. Make sure union nut wrench and the wrench are connected in a straight line.





    1. Using a 10 mm union nut wrench, tighten the 2 flare nuts of the flexible hose tube.

      Torque:
      without union nut wrench:
      15 N*m{ 154 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
      with union nut wrench:
      14 N*m{ 141 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




    2. Using a 10 mm union nut wrench, tighten the flare nut of the flexible hose tube.

      Torque:
      without union nut wrench:
      15 N*m{ 154 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
      with union nut wrench:
      14 N*m{ 141 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }

29. INSPECT TRANSMISSION OIL

  1. Park the vehicle in a level place.




  1. Check that the oil level is within 5 mm (0.196 in.) of the lowest point of the transmission filler plug opening.

    NOTICE:
    1. Problems may occur when the oil level is too high or low.
    2. After replacing the oil, drive the vehicle and check the oil level again.

  1. Inspect for oil leaks when the oil level is low.




  1. Install a new gasket and the filler plug.

    Torque:

44. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID

  1. Fill the reservoir with brake fluid.

    Brake Fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3



45. BLEED CLUTCH LINE



  1. Remove the release cylinder bleeder plug cap.

  1. Connect a vinyl tube to the bleeder plug.

  1. Depress the clutch pedal several times, and then loosen the bleeder plug while the pedal is depressed.

  1. When fluid no longer comes out, tighten the bleeder plug, and then release the clutch pedal.

  1. Repeat the previous 2 steps until all the air in the fluid is completely bled.

  1. Tighten the bleeder plug.

    Torque:
    11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the bleeder plug cap.

  1. Check that all the air has been bled from the clutch line.



46. CHECK FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR

  1. Check the fluid level.
    If the brake fluid level is low, check for leaks and inspect the disc brake pad. If necessary, refill the reservoir with brake fluid after repair or replacement.

    Brake fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3
