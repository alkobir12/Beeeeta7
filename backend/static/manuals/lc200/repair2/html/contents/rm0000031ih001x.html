MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



3. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



5. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



6. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. Twist the shift lever knob in the direction indicated by the arrow and remove it.





7. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



8. REMOVE REAR UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 9 claws and remove the panel.



9. REMOVE CONSOLE CUP HOLDER BOX SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the box.



10. REMOVE UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 14 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console panel.



11. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER BOOT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 screws, shift lever retainer and shift lever boot.



12. REMOVE FLOOR SHIFT LEVER ASSEMBLY



  1. Cover the shift lever cap with a cloth.

  1. Press down on the shift lever cap and rotate it counterclockwise to remove it.

  1. Pull out the shift lever.



13. REMOVE TRANSFER HEAT INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and insulator.



14. REMOVE TRANSFER DYNAMIC DAMPER (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and transfer dynamic damper.



15. REMOVE LOWER TRANSFER CASE PROTECTOR



  1. Remove the 7 bolts and lower transfer case protector.



16. DRAIN MANUAL TRANSMISSION OIL



  1. Remove the drain plug and gasket and then drain the manual transmission oil.




  1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.

  1. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

    Torque:
    37 N*m{ 380 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }



17. REMOVE PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY



  1. Place matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and transfer.

  1. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers.




  1. Place matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and rear differential.

  1. Remove the 4 nuts, 4 bolts and 4 washers.

  1. Remove the rear propeller shaft.



18. REMOVE FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the front propeller shaft assembly (Click here).



19. REMOVE EXHAUST PIPE (for 1GR-FE)

  1. Remove the exhaust pipe (Click here).



20. REMOVE EXHAUST PIPE (for 1VD-FTV)

  1. Remove the exhaust pipe (Click here).



21. REMOVE MANIFOLD STAY (for 1GR-FE)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and No. 1 manifold stay.



22. REMOVE NO. 2 MANIFOLD STAY (for 1GR-FE)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and No. 2 manifold stay.



23. REMOVE INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY (for 1VD-FTV)

  1. Remove the intercooler (Click here).



24. DRAIN CLUTCH FLUID


25. REMOVE CLUTCH HOSE (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Disconnect the 2 flexible hose tubes from the clutch hose with a 10 mm union nut wrench while holding the flexible hose with a wrench.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the flexible hose tubes.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the flexible hose tubes from the connecting point.

  1. Remove the 2 clips.




  1. Remove the bolt and clutch hose.



26. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the air tube from the throttle body.



27. REMOVE TUBE CONNECTOR TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Using a 10 mm union nut wrench, disconnect the tube connector to flexible hose tube from the clutch tube to release cylinder 2 way.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the flexible hose tube.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the flexible hose tube from the connecting point.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and tube connector to flexible hose tube.



28. REMOVE STARTER ASSEMBLY (for 1GR-FE)

  1. for 1.6 kW Type:
    Remove the starter assembly (Click here).

  1. for 2.0 kW Type:
    Remove the starter assembly (Click here).



29. REMOVE NO. 2 FRAME CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Support the transmission with a transmission jack. Lift the transmission slightly from the crossmember.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts of the rear engine mounting insulator.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts, 4 nuts, 4 washers and frame crossmember.



30. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and engine mounting insulator.



31. REMOVE TRANSFER BREATHER HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for 1GR-FE)



  1. Disconnect the breather hose from the transfer and actuator, and disconnect the 2 clamps.




  1. Disconnect the 5 clamps.




  1. Disconnect the 2 breather hoses from the bracket.



32. REMOVE TRANSFER BREATHER HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Disconnect the breather hose from the transfer and actuator, and disconnect the 2 clamps.




  1. Disconnect the 3 clamps.




  1. Disconnect the 3 breather hoses from the bracket.

  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the bracket from the transmission.



33. DISCONNECT WIRE HARNESS (for 1GR-FE)



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors and 3 clamps.




  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground cable.

  1. Tilt the transmission downward.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the cooling fan does not contact the fan shroud.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Disconnect the back-up light switch connector.

  1. Disconnect the 6 clamps.



34. DISCONNECT WIRE HARNESS (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors and 3 clamps.




  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground cable.

  1. Tilt the transmission downward.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the cooling fan does not contact the fan shroud.




  1. Disconnect the shift position switch connector.

  1. Disconnect the back-up light switch connector.

  1. Disconnect the 5 clamps.



35. REMOVE NO. 1 CLUTCH HOUSING COVER



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and clutch housing cover.



36. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER TO ACCUMULATOR TUBE



  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.

  1. Remove the bolt and tube.



37. REMOVE CLUTCH ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the flexible hose tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.




  1. Remove the 3 bolts and clutch accumulator.



38. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and pull out the clutch release cylinder.



39. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and flexible hose tube.



40. REMOVE OIL PAN COVER (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and oil pan cover.



41. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION WITH TRANSFER (for 1GR-FE)



  1. Remove the 9 bolts and manual transmission with transfer.



42. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION WITH TRANSFER (for 1VD-FTV)



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and manual transmission with transfer.



43. REMOVE TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 8 transfer adaptor rear mounting bolts.

  1. Pull the transfer straight up and remove it from the transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Take care not to damage the adaptor oil seal with the transfer input shaft spline.



44. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE FORK SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clutch release fork with the clutch release bearing from the transmission unit.

  1. Remove the release fork support from the transmission unit.




  1. Remove the clip and release bearing from the clutch release fork.
