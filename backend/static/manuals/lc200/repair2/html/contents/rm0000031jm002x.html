EGR VALVE (w/ EGR System) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnect the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Disconnect the cables from the negative (-) main battery and sub-battery terminals.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and under cover.



5. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL



  1. Remove the 7 clips and upper radiator support seal.



6. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator reservoir cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.

HINT:
Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  1. Remove the radiator reservoir cap to drain the coolant in the radiator.



  1. Loosen the oil filter bracket drain cock plug to drain the coolant in the engine.



  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.




  1. Tighten the oil filter bracket drain cock plug.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }



7. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Disconnect the mass air flow meter connector and using a clip remover, detach the wire harness clamp from the air cleaner cap.

  1. Detach the 4 clamps and remove the air cleaner cap.



8. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 1 air cleaner hose.



9. REMOVE INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR



  1. w/o Viscous Heater:

    1. Disconnect the connector from the water temperature sensor.

    2. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.

  1. w/ Viscous Heater:

    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors from the viscous with magnet clutch heater and water temperature sensor.

    2. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.




  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps and remove the 2 bolts and intake air connector.



10. REMOVE INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the intercooler assembly (Click here).



11. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE OIL DIPSTICK GUIDE



  1. Disconnect the wire harness clamp from the No. 2 engine oil level dipstick guide.




  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose from the cylinder head cover RH.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 engine oil level dipstick guide.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the No. 2 engine oil level dipstick guide.



12. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the air tube from the throttle body.



13. REMOVE DIESEL THROTTLE BODY ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Disconnect the throttle position sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the throttle motor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and throttle body.

  1. Remove the gasket from the intake pipe.



14. DRAIN CLUTCH FLUID (for Manual Transmission)


15. REMOVE CLUTCH HOSE (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Disconnect the 2 flexible hose tubes from the clutch hose with a 10 mm union nut wrench while holding the flexible hose with a wrench.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the flexible hose tubes.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the flexible hose tubes from the connecting point.

  1. Remove the 2 clips.




  1. Remove the bolt and clutch hose.



16. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the air tube from the throttle body.



17. REMOVE TUBE CONNECTOR TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE (for Manual Transaxle)



  1. Using a 10 mm union nut wrench, disconnect the tube connector to flexible hose tube from the clutch tube to release cylinder 2 way.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the flexible hose tube.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the flexible hose tube from the connecting point.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and tube connector to flexible hose tube.



18. REMOVE DIESEL THROTTLE BODY ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Disconnect the throttle position sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the throttle motor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and throttle body.

  1. Remove the gasket from the intake pipe.



19. REMOVE NO. 1 GAS FILTER



  1. Disconnect the hose from the intake pipe.

  1. Remove the gas filter.



20. REMOVE NO. 3 INTERCOOLER SUPPORT BRACKET



  1. for Manual Transmission:

    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the clutch tube to release cylinder 2 way from the bracket.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and bracket.



21. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 1 air hose.



22. REMOVE NO. 3 AIR TUBE



  1. Disconnect the wire harness from the clamp.

  1. Remove the nut and ground wire.




  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the wire harness bracket.




  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the bolt and No. 3 air tube.



23. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 2 air hose.



24. REMOVE NO. 4 AIR TUBE



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the suction hose.




  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the bolt and No. 4 air tube.



25. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose from the oil separator.

  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 air cleaner pipe.



26. DISCONNECT WATER HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY





27. REMOVE HEATER WATER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and disconnect the 4 water hose ends, and then remove the heater water pipe.



28. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 1 air cleaner pipe.



29. REMOVE FUEL HOSE





30. REMOVE NO. 3 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Viscous Heater)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 water hose ends, and then remove the No. 3 water by-pass pipe.



31. REMOVE NO. 4 WATER BY-PASS PIPE



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and nut.

  1. Disconnect the 4 water hose ends, then remove the No. 4 water by-pass pipe.



32. DISCONNECT ENGINE WIRE


  1. LH Side:




    1. Disconnect the 4 connectors.

    2. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.




    3. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    4. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.




    5. Disconnect the 8 connectors.

    6. Remove the bolt and engine wire harness bracket labeled B.

    7. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the engine wire protector labeled A.

    8. for RHD:
      Disconnect the wire harness from the wire harness clamp holder labeled C.



  1. RH Side:




    1. Disconnect the 7 connectors.

    2. Using a clip remover, detach the wire harness clamp.

    3. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the wire harness protector labeled A.

    4. Remove the bolt and wire harness bracket labeled B.

    5. Remove the nut and glow plug wire harness labeled C.




    6. for RHD:
      Disconnect the wire harness from the wire harness clamp holder.



  1. Rear Side:




    1. Using a clip remover, detach the wire harness clamp.

    2. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 ground wires labeled C.

    3. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the engine wire harness protector labeled B.

    4. Disconnect the 2 common rail connector RH and LH.

    5. Remove the nut and disconnect the glow plug wire harness labeled A.



33. REMOVE NO. 2 FUEL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY

NOTICE:

  1. After removing an fuel pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the fuel pipe ends, cover the fuel pipe ends with plastic bags.
  2. After removing an fuel pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the fuel supply pump hole, cover the hole with protective tape.

  1. Disconnect the fuel hose from the No. 5 nozzle leakage pipe.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 injection pipe clamp.

  1. Remove the union bolt and gasket.

  1. Remove the bolt, nut and No. 2 fuel pipe.



34. REMOVE NO. 6 INJECTION PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY

NOTICE:
  1. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the injection pipe ends, cover the injection pipe ends with plastic bags.
  2. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the common rail holes, cover the holes with protective tape.




  1. w/ EGR System:

    1. Using a union nut wrench, loosen the No. 6 injection pipe ends.

    2. Remove the 2 nuts and 2 No. 2 injection pipe clamps.

    3. Remove the No. 6 injection pipe.




  1. w/o EGR System:

    1. Using a union nut wrench, loosen the No. 6 injection pipe ends.

    2. Remove the nut and No. 2 injection pipe clamp.

    3. Remove the bolt and No. 6 injection pipe.



35. REMOVE INJECTION PIPE LH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and 4 injection pipe clamps.




  1. Using a union nut wrench, remove the 4 injection pipes.

    NOTICE:
    1. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the injection pipe ends, cover the injection pipe ends with plastic bags.
    2. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the common rail holes or injector holes, cover the holes with protective tape.



36. REMOVE COMMON RAIL ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and common rail.

    NOTICE:
    Do not remove the fuel pressure limiter from the common rail. If it is removed, replace the common rail.



37. REMOVE FUEL FILTER TO INJECTION PUMP FUEL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 2 hoses from the fuel pipe.

  1. Remove the bolt and fuel filter to injection pump fuel pipe.



38. DISCONNECT FUEL PUMP MOTOR WIRE



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the connector from the fuel supply pump.



39. REMOVE NO. 5 INJECTION PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a union nut wrench, remove the No. 5 injection pipe.

    NOTICE:
    1. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the injection pipe ends, cover the injection pipe ends with plastic bags.
    2. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the common rail hole or injection pump hole, cover the holes with protective tape.



40. REMOVE INJECTION PIPE RH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and 4 injection pipe clamps.




  1. Using a union nut wrench, remove the 4 injection pipes.

    NOTICE:
    1. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the injection pipe ends, cover the injection pipe ends with plastic bags.
    2. After removing an injection pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the common rail holes or injector holes, cover the holes with protective tape.



41. REMOVE COMMON RAIL ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and common rail.

    NOTICE:
    Do not remove the fuel pressure sensor from the common rail. If it is removed, replace the common rail.
