OIL LEVEL SENSOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL RH


2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL FRONT RH



  1. Remove the 3 clips and front fender apron seal front RH.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR RH



  1. Remove the 4 clips and front fender apron seal rear RH.



4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Loosen the clip and remove the splash shield.



5. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Loosen the clip and remove the splash shield.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and under cover.



7. DRAIN ENGINE OIL



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 engine under cover seal from the No. 2 engine under cover.

  1. Remove the oil filler cap.

  1. Remove the oil pan drain plug and gasket, and then drain the engine oil into a container.

  1. Install a new gasket and the oil pan drain plug.

    Torque:
    38 N*m{ 387 kgf*cm , 28 ft.*lbf }



8. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and under cover.



9. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (for Automatic Transmission)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and disconnect the oil cooler tube from the engine.



10. REMOVE ENGINE OIL LEVEL SENSOR



  1. Disconnect the sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and sensor.

  1. Remove the gasket from the sensor.
