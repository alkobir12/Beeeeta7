for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN WINCH FLUID



  1. Using a hexagon wrench, remove the drain plug.

  1. Drain the winch fluid.



2. ADD WINCH FLUID



  1. Fill the winch with ATF and install the drain plug.

    Torque:
    14.7 to 19.6 N*m{ 150 to 199 kgf*cm , 11 to 14 ft.*lbf }

    Fluid type:
    ATF D-II or "DEXRON III (DEXRON II)"

    Standard Capacity:
    0.9 liters (1.0 US qts, 0.8 Imp. qts)

    NOTICE:
    Do not overfill the winch.
