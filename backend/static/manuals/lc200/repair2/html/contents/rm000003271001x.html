WINCH UNIT > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and member.




  1. Remove the 8 bolts and winch base.




  1. Remove the 3 bolts and winch motor.



2. REMOVE NO. 2 WINCH EARTH WIRE



  1. Remove the bolt and winch earth wire.



3. REMOVE WINCH ROLLER BRACKET ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts, 4 bolts and winch roller bracket.



4. REMOVE MAGNET SWITCH WINCH COVER



  1. Remove the 4 screws and cover.



5. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR MAGNET SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts, 2 spring washers, 2 washers and wire harness.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and magnet switch from the motor.



6. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR COMMUTATOR END FRAME



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and commutator end frame.



7. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR O-RING

  1. Remove the O-ring.



8. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR BRUSH



  1. Using a screwdriver, pry the bush spring forward and pull the brush out of the holder.




  1. Remove the bolt and brush from the brush holder.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures to remove the 3 remaining brushes.



9. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR BRUSH HOLDER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the brush holder in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration.



10. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR YOKE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the yoke in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration.



11. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR ARMATURE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the armature in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration.



12. REMOVE NO. 1 WINCH MOTOR ARMATURE BEARING



  1. Using SST, remove the bearing.

    SST
    09286-46011  



13. REMOVE NO. 2 WINCH MOTOR ARMATURE BEARING



  1. Using SST, remove the bearing.

    SST
    09628-62011  



14. REMOVE WINCH MOTOR BRUSH SPRING



  1. Insert a screwdriver, labeled A, as shown in the illustration and pry the spring forward, then move the spring in the direction of the arrow shown in the illustration with a screwdriver, labeled B, to remove it from the holder.
