REMOTE CONTROL SWITCH > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT OVERHEAT TEMPERATURE INDICATOR



  1. Apply battery voltage and check operation of the remote control switch, as shown in the table below.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal B
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal E
    		Overheat temperature indicator light illuminates and buzzer sounds

    If the result is not as specified, replace the remote control switch assembly.



2. INSPECT REMOTE CONTROL SWITCH CIRCUIT



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A - B*1
    B - C
    B - E*2
    		Not pushed
    		Below 1 Ω
    B - D
    		Pushed to IN
    		Below 1 Ω
    B - F
    		Pushed to OUT
    		Below 1 Ω

    HINT:
    1. *1: Connect the positive (+) lead of the ohmmeter to terminal B and the negative (-) lead to terminal A.
    2. *2: Connect the positive (+) lead of the ohmmeter to terminal E and the negative (-) lead to terminal B.

    If the result is not as specified, replace the remote control switch assembly.
