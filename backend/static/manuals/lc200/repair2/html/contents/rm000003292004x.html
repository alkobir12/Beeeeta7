ECM > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CONNECTOR HOLDER BLOCK



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and move the connector holder block so that the ECM can be removed in the next step.



3. REMOVE ECM



  1. Raise the 2 levers while pushing the locks on the 2 levers.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure that the lock levers are raised 90° as shown in the illustration before disconnecting the ECM connectors. Failure to do this may cause the ECM connectors to break.

  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and ECM.



4. REMOVE GASKET



  1. Peel off the gasket.




  1. Spray gasket remover or equivalent on the remaining tape of the gasket.

    NOTICE:
    When using gasket remover or equivalent, cover the ECM connectors with a cloth.




  1. Remove the tape of the gasket without using bladed objects.
