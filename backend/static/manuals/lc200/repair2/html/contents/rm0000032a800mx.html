4. INSTALL OIL PRESSURE REGULATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Hold the oil pressure regulator body in a vise between aluminum plates.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the oil pump relief valve labeled C.

  1. Install the oil pump relief valve labeled C to the oil pressure regulator body.

  1. Install the 2 springs labeled B to the oil pressure regulator body.

  1. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and the oil pump relief valve plug labeled A.

    Torque:
    50 N*m{ 510 kgf*cm , 37 ft.*lbf }

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to a new O-ring, and install it to the oil pressure regulator body.




  1. Using a 26 mm wrench, install a new gasket and the oil pressure regulator.

    Torque:
    86 N*m{ 877 kgf*cm , 63 ft.*lbf }



5. INSTALL OIL FILTER BRACKET DRAIN COCK ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the drain cock.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Using a 17 mm wrench, rotate the drain cock clockwise (360°) and align the position of the drain hole so that it is within the range shown in the illustration after tightening the drain cock to the specified torque.

    NOTICE:
    1. As the oil filter bracket may be damaged, the maximum tightening torque should be 80 N*m (816 kgf*cm, 59 ft.*lbf) or less.
    2. Do not rotate the drain cock more than 1 revolution (360°) after tightening the drain cock with the specified torque.
    3. Do not expose the drain cock to coolant within 1 hour after installing.




  1. Install the oil filter drain cock plug.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 130 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }
