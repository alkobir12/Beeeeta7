1. INSPECT STARTER ASSEMBLY




CAUTION:
As a large electric current passes through the cable during this inspection, a thick cable must be used. If not, the cable may become hot and cause injury.

NOTICE:
The following tests must be done within 3 to 5 seconds to prevent the coil from burning out.



  1. Perform the operation test without load.




    1. Grip the starter in a vise.

    2. Connect the battery and an ammeter to the starter with terminal 30 disconnected.

      NOTICE:
      Do not connect terminal 30 to the battery in this step.

    3. Connect terminal 30 to the battery and check that the starter rotates smoothly and steadily while the pinion gear is moving outward. Then measure the current.

      Standard current:
      200 A or less at 11 V

      If the result is not as specified, repair or replace the starter assembly.



2. INSPECT STARTER ARMATURE ASSEMBLY



  1. Inspect the commutator for an open circuit.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Commutator
      		Always
      		Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the armature assembly.




  1. Inspect the commutator for a short circuit.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Commutator - Armature coil core
      		Always
      		10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the armature assembly.




  1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the commutator diameter.

    Standard diameter:
    36.0 mm (1.42 in.)

    Minimum diameter:
    35.0 mm (1.38 in.)

    If the diameter is less than the minimum, replace the armature assembly.




  1. Measure the undercut depth of the commutator.

    Standard undercut depth:
    0.7 mm (0.0276 in.)

    Minimum undercut depth:
    0.2 mm (0.00787 in.)

    If the undercut depth is less than the minimum, increase the depth using a hacksaw blade.




  1. Check that the bearing rotates smoothly.
    If necessary, replace the starter armature assembly.




  1. Inspect the commutator circle runout.

    1. Place the commutator on V-blocks.

    2. Using a dial indicator, measure the circle runout.

      Maximum circle runout:
      0.05 mm (0.00197 in.)

      If the runout is more than the maximum, correct it with sandpaper (#400) or replace the armature assembly.



3. INSPECT STARTER YOKE ASSEMBLY



  1. Inspect the field coil for an open circuit.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Terminal C plate - Brushes
      		Always
      		Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the starter yoke assembly.




  1. Inspect if the field coil is grounded.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Brushes - Starter yoke body
      		Always
      		10 kΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the starter yoke assembly.



4. INSPECT STARTER BRUSH



  1. Using a vernier caliper, measure the brush length.

    Standard length:
    21.0 mm (0.827 in.)

    Minimum length:
    12.0 mm (0.472 in.)

    If the length is less than the minimum, replace the brush holder and starter yoke assembly.



5. INSPECT STARTER BRUSH HOLDER ASSEMBLY



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Positive (+) brush holder - Negative (-) brush holder
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher

    If the result is not as specified, replace the brush holder assembly.




  1. Inspect the load of the brush spring.

    1. Take a pull scale reading immediately after the brush spring separates from the brush.

      Standard spring installed load:
      36 N (3.6 kgf, 8.0 lbf)

      Minimum spring installed load:
      12 N (1.2 kgf, 2.7 lbf)

      If the spring installed load is less than the minimum, replace the brush holder assembly.



6. INSPECT STARTER CLUTCH SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Check the starter clutch operation.

    1. Hold the clutch and bearing with one hand and turn the shaft with the other. Check that the inner sleeve turns easily in the "free" direction but cannot turn in the "lock" direction as shown in the illustration.
      If the starter clutch sub-assembly does not operate as specified, replace the starter clutch sub-assembly.



7. INSPECT MAGNET STARTER SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Check the pull-in coil for an open circuit.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Terminal 50 - Terminal C
      		Always
      		Below 0.3 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the magnet starter switch assembly.




  1. Check the hold-in coil for an open circuit.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Terminal 50 - Housing Body
      		Always
      		Below 0.8 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the magnet starter switch assembly.
