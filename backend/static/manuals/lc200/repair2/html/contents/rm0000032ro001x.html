CLUTCH ACCUMULATOR > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL CLUTCH ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the clutch accumulator with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    12 N*m{ 120 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }




  1. Using a union nut wrench, connect the flexible hose tube.

    Torque:
    without union nut wrench:
    15 N*m{ 155 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
    with union nut wrench:
    14 N*m{ 144 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).
    2. This torque value is effective when the union nut wrench is parallel to the torque wrench.



2. INSTALL CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER TO ACCUMULATOR TUBE



  1. Using a union nut wrench, connect the tube to the clutch accumulator and release cylinder.

    Torque:
    without a union nut wrench:
    16 N*m{ 158 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
    with a union nut wrench:
    14 N*m{ 147 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).
    2. This torque value is effective when the union nut wrench is parallel to the torque wrench.

  1. Install the tube with the bolt.

    Torque:
    12 N*m{ 120 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



3. INSTALL NO. 1 CLUTCH HOUSING COVER



  1. Install the housing cover with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    12 N*m{ 120 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



4. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID

  1. Fill the reservoir with brake fluid.

    Brake Fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3



5. BLEED CLUTCH LINE



  1. Remove the release cylinder bleeder plug cap.

  1. Connect a vinyl tube to the bleeder plug.

  1. Depress the clutch pedal several times, and then loosen the bleeder plug while the pedal is depressed.

  1. When fluid no longer comes out, tighten the bleeder plug, and then release the clutch pedal.

  1. Repeat the previous 2 steps until all the air in the fluid is completely bled.

  1. Tighten the bleeder plug.

    Torque:
    11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the bleeder plug cap.

  1. Check that all the air has been bled from the clutch line.



6. CHECK FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR

  1. Check the fluid level.
    If the brake fluid level is low, check for leaks and inspect the disc brake pad. If necessary, refill the reservoir with brake fluid after repair or replacement.

    Brake fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3
