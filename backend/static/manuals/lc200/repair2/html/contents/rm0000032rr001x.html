CLUTCH ACCUMULATOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN CLUTCH FLUID


2. REMOVE NO. 1 CLUTCH HOUSING COVER



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and housing cover.



3. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER TO ACCUMULATOR TUBE



  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.

  1. Remove the bolt and tube.



4. REMOVE CLUTCH ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the flexible hose tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.




  1. Remove the 3 bolts and clutch accumulator.
