AIR PUMP (w/ Secondary Air Injection System) > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL AIR PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the air pump, 3 insulators and 3 collars with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the air hose.



2. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR INJECTION SYSTEM HOSE

  1. Install the hose to the air pump assembly.



3. INSTALL AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the air switching valve to the No. 1 air injection system hose.

  1. Install the air switching valve to the air pump bracket with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    16 N*m{ 163 kgf*cm , 12 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the air pressure sensor to the air switching valve with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }

  1. Install the vacuum hose to the air pressure sensor and air switching valve.

  1. Connect the air pressure sensor connector.

  1. Connect the air switching valve connector.



4. INSTALL AIR PUMP WITH BRACKET AND AIR SWITCHING VALVE



  1. Install the air pump with bracket with the 2 bolts and 2 nuts in the order shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    16 N*m{ 163 kgf*cm , 12 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the pressure sensor connector.

  1. Connect the air switching valve connector.



5. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR INJECTION SYSTEM HOSE



  1. Install the hose.



6. INSTALL WATER BY-PASS PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Apply soapy water to the O-ring.

  1. Install a new O-ring to the water by-pass pipe.




  1. Push in the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Install the water by-pass pipe with the bolt.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 vacuum switching valve hoses to the water by-pass pipe clamp.

  1. Connect the wire harness clamp.

  1. Connect the connector.
