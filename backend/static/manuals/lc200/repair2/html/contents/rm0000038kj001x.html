1. REMOVE FRONT ROOF RACK LEG COVER LH



  1. Detach the 2 clips and 2 guides, and remove the cover.



2. REMOVE FRONT ROOF RACK LEG COVER RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



3. REMOVE CENTER ROOF RACK LEG COVER LH



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.



4. REMOVE CENTER ROOF RACK LEG COVER RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



5. REMOVE REAR ROOF RACK LEG COVER LH



  1. Detach the 2 clips and 2 guides, and remove the cover.



6. REMOVE REAR ROOF RACK LEG COVER RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



7. REMOVE ROOF CARRIER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH


  1. Remove the 5 bolts and roof carrier.



    NOTICE:


    1. Loosen bolt A so that it protrudes a distance of 5 to 7 mm (0.197 to 0.276 in.) as shown in the illustration.


    2. Remove bolt B.


    3. Remove bolt A.



8. REMOVE ROOF CARRIER SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
