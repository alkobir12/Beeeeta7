ROCKER PANEL MOULDING > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MUDGUARD LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 4 screws and mudguard.



2. REMOVE SIDE STEP ASSEMBLY LH


  1. Remove the 6 bolts and side step.





3. REMOVE BODY ROCKER PANEL MOULDING ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the 8 side mudguard retainers and screw.



  1. Detach the 10 clips and remove the rocker panel moulding.
