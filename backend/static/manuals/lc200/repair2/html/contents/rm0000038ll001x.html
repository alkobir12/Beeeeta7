MUDGUARD > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MUDGUARD LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 4 screws and mudguard.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MUDGUARD RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



3. REMOVE REAR QUARTER PANEL MUDGUARD LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 6 screws and mudguard.



4. REMOVE REAR QUARTER PANEL MUDGUARD RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 7 screws and mudguard.
