36. INSTALL ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY



HINT:
  1. for Manual Seat:
    When installing the roof headlining, move the front seat and rear No. 1 seat to the position shown in the illustration.
  2. for Power Seat:
    When installing the roof headlining, connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal and move the front seat and No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.
  3. for Power Seat:
    After moving the seats, disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.




  1. Place the headlining in the cabin from the rear of the vehicle as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the roof headlining when placing it in the cabin.



  1. for 3 Person Seat Type Rear No. 2 Seat:
    Attach the 12 claws, 2 clips, 2 guides and 16 fasteners to install the headlining.





  1. except 3 Person Seat Type Rear No. 2 Seat:
    Attach the 12 claws, 3 clips, 2 guides and 16 fasteners to install the headlining.






  1. Connect the washer hose.




  1. Connect the antenna cord connector.




  1. Connect the antenna cord connector and attach the 2 clamps.

  1. Install the bolt.




  1. Connect the 3 roof wire connectors and washer hose.

  1. Attach the 4 clamps.




  1. Connect the drive gear connector.




  1. w/ Rain Sensor:
    Disconnect the rain sensor connector.




  1. w/ EC Mirror:
    Connect the inner mirror connector.

59. INSTALL FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH



HINT:
When installing the front quarter trim panel, operate the reclining adjuster release handle and move the No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.



  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. w/o Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Connect the rear seat lock control lever cable.

    2. w/ Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Connect the thermistor connector and rear seat lock control lever cable.

    3. Attach the 18 clips and 2 claws to install the quarter trim panel.



    4. Install the clip and bolt.

      Torque:
      12 N*m{ 122 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }






  1. w/o Rear No. 2 Seat or w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, for Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Attach the 18 clips and 2 claws to install the quarter trim panel.



    2. Install the clip.






  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type:
    Install the rear No. 2 seat belt anchor with the bolt.

    Torque:
    42 N*m{ 428 kgf*cm , 31 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Do not overlap the anchor part of the seat belt and protruding parts of the vehicle body.




  1. Install the rear No. 1 seat belt anchor with the bolt.

    Torque:
    42 N*m{ 428 kgf*cm , 31 ft.*lbf }




  1. Attach the 3 claws to install the cover.
