1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

45. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY



HINT:
  1. for Manual Seat:
    When removing the roof headlining, move the front seat and rear No. 1 seat to the position shown in the illustration.
  2. for Power Seat:
    When removing the roof headlining, connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal and move the front seat and No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.
  3. for Power Seat:
    After moving the seats, disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal.




  1. w/ EC Mirror:
    Disconnect the inner mirror connector.




  1. w/ Rain Sensor:
    Disconnect the rain sensor connector.




  1. Disconnect the drive gear connector.




  1. Disconnect the 3 roof wire connectors and washer hose.

  1. Detach the 4 clamps.




  1. Remove the bolt.

  1. Disconnect the antenna cord connector and detach the 2 clamps.




  1. Disconnect the antenna cord connector.




  1. Disconnect the washer hose.



  1. for 3 Person Seat Type Rear No. 2 Seat:
    Detach the 12 claws, 2 clips, 2 guides and 16 fasteners.





  1. except 3 Person Seat Type Rear No. 2 Seat:
    Detach the 12 claws, 3 clips, 2 guides and 16 fasteners.






  1. Remove the headlining from the rear of the vehicle as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the roof headlining when taking it out.
