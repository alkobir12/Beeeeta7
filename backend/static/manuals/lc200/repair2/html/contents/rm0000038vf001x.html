1. INSPECT GLOVE BOX LIGHT ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the LED illumination condition.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1 (B)
    Battery positive (-) → Terminal 2 (E)
    		Not pushed
    		Light illuminates

    If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction in the glove box light assembly.
