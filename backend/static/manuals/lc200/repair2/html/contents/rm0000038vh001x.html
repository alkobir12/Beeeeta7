GLOVE BOX LIGHT > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the RHD and LHD.
  2. The procedures listed below are the LHD side.



1. REMOVE FRONT PASSENGER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the front passenger side knee airbag assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL BOX DOOR KNOB



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the knob.



3. REMOVE LOWER NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connector.



4. REMOVE GLOVE BOX LIGHT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws and glove box light.
