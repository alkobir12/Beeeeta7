for Preparation Click here


HINT:
When only replacing the room light bulbs, use the following procedures.



1. REMOVE MAP LIGHT ASSEMBLY (w/ Sliding Roof)

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and open the 2 covers.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 2 guides.



  1. Disconnect the connector and then remove the map light.





2. REMOVE MAP LIGHT ASSEMBLY (w/o Sliding Roof)

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and open the 2 covers.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 2 guides.



  1. Disconnect the connector and then remove the map light.





3. REMOVE MAP LIGHT BULB



  1. Turn the 2 map light bulbs in the direction indicated by the arrows and remove them.
