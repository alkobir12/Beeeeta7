1. INSPECT REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Check the stop light.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the LED illumination condition.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → 4 (STOP)
      Battery negative (-) → 6 (E)
      		Light illuminates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear combination light assembly.




  1. Check the taillight.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the LED illumination condition.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → 3 (TAIL)
      Battery negative (-) → 6 (E)
      		Light illuminates

      If the result is not as specified, replace the rear combination light.
