1. INSPECT HAZARD WARNING SIGNAL SWITCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Inspect the hazard warning switch LED illumination.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the LED illumination condition.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (ILL+)
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2 (ILL-)
      		Light illuminates




    2. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Switch Condition
      		Specified Condition
      4 (F) - 1 (E)
      		Hazard warning signal switch off
      		10 kΩ or higher
      4 (F) - 1 (E)
      		Hazard warning signal switch on
      		Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction in the hazard warning switch.
