CLOCK > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE NO. 1 SPEAKER OPENING COVER ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 8 claws and remove the opening cover.



2. REMOVE NO. 3 INSTRUMENT PANEL REGISTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the register and then disconnect the connector.



3. REMOVE NO. 4 INSTRUMENT PANEL REGISTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the register and then disconnect the connector.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL



  1. Detach the 10 claws.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



5. REMOVE CLOCK ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws and clock.



6. REMOVE RADIO RECEIVER ASSEMBLY (w/o Multi-display)



  1. Remove the 2 screws and 2 bolts.




  1. Pull the radio receiver to detach the 10 claws on the backside of the radio receiver.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the radio receiver.



7. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIO BRACKET (w/o Multi-display)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bracket.



8. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIO BRACKET (w/o Multi-display)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bracket.



9. REMOVE CLOCK ASSEMBLY (w/o Multi-display)



  1. Remove the 2 screws and clock.
