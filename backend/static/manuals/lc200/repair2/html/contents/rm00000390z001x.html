REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY (for 60/40 Split Seat Type 60 Side) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE REAR NO. 3 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 6 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE REAR NO. 5 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 6 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



4. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Operate the reclining adjuster release handle to move the seat into the position shown in the illustration.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and 2 clips, and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



5. REMOVE REAR NO. 4 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and 2 clips, and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



6. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. w/ Rear Seat Side Airbag:
    Disconnect the connector.

  1. w/ Seat Heater System:
    Disconnect the connector.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and seat assembly.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the vehicle body.



7. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH

  1. Remove the rear No. 1 seat assembly RH (Click here).



8. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT PROTECTOR



  1. Detach the 10 claws and remove the 2 seat protectors.



9. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT PROTECTOR



  1. Detach the 10 claws and remove the 2 seat protectors.



10. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



11. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



12. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



13. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY

  1. for Face to Face Seat Type:
    Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly (Click here).

  1. except Face to Face Seat Type:
    Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly (Click here).



14. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



15. REMOVE REAR SEAT COVER CAP (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the rear seat cover cap on the other side.

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws and remove the rear seat cover cap.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



16. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).



17. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY RH

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly RH (Click here).

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly RH (Click here).



18. REMOVE AIR DUCT PLUG (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the plug.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures for both sides.



19. REMOVE REAR AIR DUCT GUIDE (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the guide.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures for both sides.



20. REMOVE FRONT FLOOR CARPET ASSEMBLY



  1. Fold back the floor carpet.

    HINT:
    Fold back the carpet until it is possible to remove the rear No. 6 air duct.



21. REMOVE REAR NO. 6 AIR DUCT (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)

  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.



  1. Remove the 5 screws and duct.





22. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION LOCK STRIKER SUB-ASSEMBLY




  1. Remove the 10 bolts and 4 lock strikers.
