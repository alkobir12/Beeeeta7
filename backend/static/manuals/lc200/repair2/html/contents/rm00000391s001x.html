REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY (except Face to Face Seat Type) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT HEADREST ASSEMBLY LH


2. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER LH



  1. Operate the rear seat headrest handle and lower the headrest.




  1. Operate the reclining adjuster release handle and fold down the seat.




  1. Operate the seat cushion lock release lever and move the seat to the storage position.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT HINGE COVER LH



  1. Detach the 2 claws and clip, and then remove the cover.



4. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and seat assembly.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the vehicle body.



5. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



6. INSTALL REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



7. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



8. REMOVE REAR SEAT COVER CAP



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the rear seat cover cap on the other side.

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws and remove the rear seat cover cap.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



9. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).



10. REMOVE REAR SEAT LOCK CONTROL LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the seat lock control lever.



11. REMOVE REAR ROOF NO. 1 AIR DUCT (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cooler plate.




  1. Remove the clip and duct.



12. REMOVE FOLD SEAT LEG LOCK SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Detach the 2 cable clamps.




  1. Remove the 2 nuts and fold seat leg lock.



13. REMOVE NO. 3 SEAT CUSHION COVER



  1. Detach the 4 clips and 16 claws, and then remove the 4 seat cushion covers.



14. REMOVE REAR SEAT LOCK STRIKER SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Fold back the floor carpet.




  1. Remove the 8 bolts and 4 seat lock strikers.
