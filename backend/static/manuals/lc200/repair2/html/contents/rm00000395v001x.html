REAR SEAT CUSHION HEATER (for 60/40 Split Seat Type 40 Side) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


CAUTION:
Wear protective gloves. Sharp areas on the parts may injure your hands.

HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH

  1. Remove the rear No. 1 seat assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE SEAT ADJUSTER BOLT COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER RELEASE HANDLE RH



  1. Remove the screw and release handle.



4. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT RECLINING COVER RH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws in the order shown in the illustration, and then remove the cover.



5. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT RECLINING COVER LH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the cover.



6. REMOVE UPPER SEAT TRACK RAIL COVER LH



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



7. REMOVE UPPER SEAT TRACK RAIL COVER RH



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



8. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT LEG SIDE COVER



  1. Remove the 4 screws and detach the 3 claws.




  1. Remove the bolt that is securing the fold seat stopper band and remove the seat leg side cover together with the fold seat stopper band.



9. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT PROTECTOR



  1. w/ Rear Seat Side Airbag:
    Detach the claw and remove the wire harness connector.

  1. w/ Seat Heater System:
    Detach the claw and remove the wire harness connector.




  1. Using a clip remover, remove the clip.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the seat protector from the seat hinge.

  1. Detach the 2 claws, open the protector and remove the wire harness.



10. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT HINGE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and hinge.



11. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT HINGE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. w/ Rear Seat Side Airbag:
    Detach the wire harness clamp.

  1. w/ Seat Heater System:
    Detach the wire harness clamp.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and hinge.




  1. Detach the cable clamp and disconnect the cable.



12. REMOVE REAR SEAT COVER



  1. Detach the 12 claws.




  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.



13. REMOVE REAR UNDER SIDE COVER LH



  1. Remove the 5 screws.

  1. Detach the clip and 2 claws, and remove the cover.



14. REMOVE REAR SEAT UNDER TRAY COVER RH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the cover.



15. REMOVE SEAT CUSHION COVER WITH PAD

  1. w/ Rear Seat Side Airbag:

    1. Detach the side airbag wire harness clamps.

    2. Remove the side airbag wire harness through the hole in the seat cushion.



  1. w/ Seat Heater System:




    1. Disconnect the 2 seat heater connectors.




    2. Remove the seatback heater wire harness through the hole in the seat cushion.

  1. Remove the seat cushion cover with pad.



16. REMOVE REAR SEPARATE TYPE SEAT CUSHION COVER



  1. Detach the 3 pieces of fastening tape.




  1. Remove the hog rings and cover.



17. REMOVE REAR SEAT CUSHION HEATER ASSEMBLY



  1. Cut off the tack pins which fasten the seat heater to the seat cushion cover, and then remove the seat cushion heater from the seat cushion cover.
