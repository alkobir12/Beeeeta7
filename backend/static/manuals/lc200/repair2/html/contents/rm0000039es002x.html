REAR NO. 1 SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.



2. REMOVE TONNEAU COVER ASSEMBLY (w/ Tonneau Cover)

  1. Remove the tonneau cover.



3. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly (Click here).



4. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



5. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



6. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



7. REMOVE NO. 1 TONNEAU COVER HOLDER CAP (w/ Tonneau Cover)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the tonneau cover holder cap on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the tonneau cover holder cap.



8. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT COVER BEZEL (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat)



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the rear No. 2 seat cover bezel.



9. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH




HINT:
When removing the front quarter trim panel, operate the reclining adjuster release handle and move the No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear seat outer belt floor anchor.




  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, for LH side:

    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear No. 2 seat outer belt floor anchor LH.




  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, for RH side:

    1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




    2. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear No. 2 seat outer belt floor anchor RH.



  1. w/o Tonneau Cover:

    1. Remove the clip and bolt.

    2. Detach the 18 clips and 2 claws.

    3. w/o Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Disconnect the rear seat lock control lever cable and then remove the quarter trim panel.



    4. w/ Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Disconnect the thermistor connector and rear seat lock control lever cable, and then remove the quarter trim panel.





  1. w/ Tonneau Cover:

    1. Remove the screw and clip.



    2. Detach the 18 clips and 2 claws, and remove the quarter trim panel.





10. REMOVE REAR FRONT QUARTER TRIM GARNISH LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the clip and 2 guides.

  1. Pass the seat belt anchor through the quarter trim garnish, and remove the quarter trim garnish.



11. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the nut and seat belt shoulder anchor.




  1. w/ Pretensioner:
    Disconnect the pretensioner connector as shown in the illustration.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and rear No. 1 seat outer belt.



12. REMOVE REAR SHOULDER BELT ANCHOR PLATE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and anchor plate.
