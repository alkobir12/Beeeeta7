DOOR CONTROL RECEIVER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH (except Face to Face Seat Type)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly RH (Click here ).



2. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH (for Face to Face Seat Type)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly RH (Click here).



3. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



4. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



5. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 TONNEAU COVER HOLDER CAP (w/ Tonneau Cover)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the tonneau cover holder cap on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the tonneau cover holder cap.



7. REMOVE REAR SEAT COVER CAP (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the rear seat cover cap on the other side.

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws and remove the rear seat cover cap.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



8. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY RH



HINT:
When removing the front quarter trim panel, operate the reclining adjuster release handle and move the No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.




  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




  1. Remove the bolt and rear No. 1 seat belt anchor.




  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




    2. Remove the bolt and rear No. 2 seat belt anchor.



  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Remove the clip and bolt.

    2. Detach the 16 clips and 2 claws.

    3. w/o Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Disconnect the rear seat lock control lever cable and then remove the quarter trim panel.



    4. w/ Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Disconnect the thermistor connector and rear seat lock control lever cable, and then remove the quarter trim panel.





  1. w/o Rear No. 2 Seat or w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, for Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Remove the clip.



    2. Detach the 16 clips and 2 claws, and remove the quarter trim panel.





9. REMOVE REAR UPPER PILLAR GARNISH RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



10. REMOVE DOOR CONTROL RECEIVER



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the bolt and receiver.
