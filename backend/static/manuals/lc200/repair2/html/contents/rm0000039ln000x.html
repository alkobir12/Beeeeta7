1. INSPECT NO. 2 ULTRASONIC SENSOR



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 (BI) - 3 (SI)
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher

    If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 2 ultrasonic sensor.



2. INSTALL NO. 3 ULTRASONIC SENSOR



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 (BI) - 3 (SI)
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher

    If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 3 ultrasonic sensor.
