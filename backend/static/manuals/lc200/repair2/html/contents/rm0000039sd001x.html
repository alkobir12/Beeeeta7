MULTIPLEX TILT AND TELESCOPIC ECU > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE STEERING COLUMN ASSEMBLY

HINT:
Refer to the instructions for removal of the steering column assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE STEERING LOCK ACTUATOR ASSEMBLY

  1. Using a center punch, mark the center of the 2 tapered-head bolts.

  1. Using a 3 to 4 mm (0.118 to 0.157 in.) drill, drill a hole in the 2 bolts.




  1. Using a screw extractor, remove the 2 bolts and the steering lock actuator assembly from the steering column assembly.



3. REMOVE MULTIPLEX TILT AND TELESCOPIC ECU



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and ECU.
