1. INSTALL POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the power steering link with the 3 bolts and 3 nuts.

    Torque:
    120 N*m{ 1224 kgf*cm , 89 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    Hold the bolts and tighten the nuts to install the power steering link.



2. INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION REBOUND STOPPER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install the suspension rebound stopper LH with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    58 N*m{ 591 kgf*cm , 43 ft.*lbf }



3. INSTALL FRONT SUSPENSION REBOUND STOPPER SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install the suspension rebound stopper RH with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    58 N*m{ 591 kgf*cm , 43 ft.*lbf }



4. CONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the pressure feed tube clamp with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    8.8 N*m{ 90 kgf*cm , 78 in.*lbf }




  1. Connect the pressure feed tube (return tube side) to the power steering link with the clamp.




  1. Using a union nut wrench, connect the pressure feed tube (pressure feed tube side) to the power steering link.

    Torque:
    without union nut wrench:
    44 N*m{ 449 kgf*cm , 32 ft.*lbf }
    with union nut wrench:
    40 N*m{ 409 kgf*cm , 30 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 300 mm (11.8 in.).
    2. This torque value is effective when the union nut wrench is perpendicular to the torque wrench.



5. INSTALL TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Align the matchmarks of the tie rod and rack end, and temporarily install the tie rod with the lock nut.

    HINT:
    After adjusting toe-in, tighten the lock nut.

    Torque:
    82 N*m{ 836 kgf*cm , 60 ft.*lbf }

  1. Check that length A is the same as the length measured previously.



6. INSTALL TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the RH side.



7. CONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Connect the tie rod end LH to the steering knuckle with the nut.

    Torque:
    69 N*m{ 704 kgf*cm , 51 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install a new cotter pin.

    HINT:
    If the holes for the cotter pin are not aligned, tighten the nut up to another 60°.



8. CONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.

20. BLEED POWER STEERING FLUID

  1. Check the fluid level.

  1. Jack up the front of the vehicle and support it with stands.

  1. Turn the steering wheel.

    1. With the engine stopped, turn the wheel slowly from lock to lock several times.

  1. Lower the vehicle.

  1. Start the engine.

  1. Idle the engine for a few minutes.

  1. Turn the steering wheel.

    1. With the engine idling, turn the wheel left or right to the full lock position and keep it there for 2 to 3 seconds, then turn the wheel to the opposite full lock position and keep it there for 2 to 3 seconds. *1

    2. Repeat *1 several times.

      NOTICE:
      For vehicles with VGRS, if the steering wheel is turned from lock to lock repeatedly, the system may stop operating and the amount of rotation before the steering wheel locks may increase due to operation of the overheating prevention function. When the system temperature drops, the system operation automatically returns to normal.

  1. Stop the engine.




  1. Check for foaming or emulsification.
    If the system has to be bled twice because of foaming or emulsification, check for fluid leaks in the system.

  1. Check the fluid level.



21. CHECK POWER STEERING FLUID LEVEL



  1. Keep the vehicle horizontal.

  1. With the engine stopped, check the fluid level in the reservoir.
    If necessary, add fluid.

    Fluid:
    ATF DEXRON II or III, or equivalent

    HINT:
    If the fluid is hot, check that the fluid level is within the HOT range. If the fluid is cold, check that the fluid level is within the COLD range.

  1. Start the engine and idle it.

  1. Turn the steering wheel from lock to lock several times to raise the fluid temperature.

    Fluid temperature:
    80°C (176°F)




  1. Check for foaming or emulsification.
    If foaming or emulsification is identified, bleed the power steering system.




  1. With the engine idling, measure the fluid level in the reservoir.

  1. Stop the engine.

  1. Wait a few minutes and remeasure the fluid level in the reservoir.

    Maximum fluid level rise:
    5.0 mm (0.197 in.)

    If the fluid level rise is more than the maximum, bleed the power steering system.

  1. Check the fluid level.

27. CLOSE STABILIZER CONTROL WITH ACCUMULATOR HOUSING SHUTTER VALVE

NOTICE:
  1. Perform the inspection on a level surface.
  2. Perform the inspection with the vehicle empty.
  3. Ensure that the wheels are on the ground and facing straight ahead.
  4. Perform the inspection with the vehicle load completely on the suspension.

  1. Using a 5 mm hexagon socket wrench, tighten the lower and upper chamber shutter valves of the stabilizer control with accumulator housing.

    Torque:
    14 N*m{ 143 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }
