FUEL PUMP (for Single Tank Type) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



NOTICE:

Do not disconnect the hoses indicated in the illustration.



1. REMOVE FUEL SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the fuel sender gauge connector.




  1. Press down on the sender gauge claw labeled A. Then slide the sender gauge upward to remove it.



2. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL SUB-TANK



  1. Disconnect the fuel pump connector.




  1. Disconnect the jet pump from the sub-tank.

  1. Using a small screwdriver, detach the claw from the sub-tank.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws from the claw holes.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the fuel sub-tank.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws from the claw holes.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the fuel sub-tank.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE FUEL PUMP



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 5 claws from the claw holes and disconnect the fuel pump from the fuel filter case.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Disconnect the fuel pump wire harness connector from the fuel pump to remove it.




  1. Remove the O-ring and spacer from the fuel pump.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL SUCTION SUPPORT



  1. Detach the 2 claws from the claw holes to remove the fuel suction support.
