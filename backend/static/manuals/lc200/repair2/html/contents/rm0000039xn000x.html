FUEL FILTER > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TUBE PROTECTOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and No. 1 fuel tube protector.



4. DISCONNECT NO. 2 FUEL MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Pull up the retainer and disconnect the No. 2 fuel main tube.

    NOTICE:
    1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
    2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
    3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
    4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
    5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
    6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.



5. DISCONNECT FUEL MAIN TUBE



  1. Pull up the retainer and disconnect the fuel main tube.

    NOTICE:
    1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
    2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
    3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
    4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
    5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
    6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.



6. REMOVE FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and fuel filter.



7. INSTALL FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the fuel filter with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 199 kgf*cm , 14 ft.*lbf }



8. CONNECT FUEL MAIN TUBE



  1. Connect the fuel main tube and push the retainer.

    HINT:
    Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

    NOTICE:
    1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
    2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.



9. CONNECT NO. 2 FUEL MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the No. 2 fuel main tube and push the retainer.

    HINT:
    Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

    NOTICE:
    1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
    2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.



10. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



11. INSPECT FOR FUEL LEAK

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and intelligent tester ON.

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine.

  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Active Test / Activate the Fuel Pump Speed Control.

  1. Check that there are no fuel leaks after doing maintenance anywhere on the fuel system.



12. INSTALL NO. 1 FUEL TUBE PROTECTOR



  1. Install the No. 1 fuel tube protector with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    29 N*m{ 296 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }
