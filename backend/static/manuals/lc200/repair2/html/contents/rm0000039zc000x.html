1. INSPECT HEIGHT CONTROL PUMP AND MOTOR



  1. Check the pump and motor operation.

    1. Apply battery voltage to the pump and motor and check the operating sound of the height control pump and motor.

      NOTICE:
      1. Be careful of fluid spray when the height control pump and motor is operated.
      2. Do not allow the motor to operate for 60 seconds or more.
      3. If the height control pump and motor is shorted, locked, or has a similar type of malfunction, a large amount of current will be flowing. Therefore, if the motor does not operate, immediately stop this inspection.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 2
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 1
      		Motor operates

      1. If the result is not as specified, replace the height control pump and motor.
