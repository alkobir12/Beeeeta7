1. DISPOSE OF SUSPENSION CONTROL PUMP ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Place the accumulator in a vise and saw a hole between A and B shown in the illustration using a saw to discharge the gas inside.

    CAUTION:
    1. Before discharging the gas, loosen the bleeder plug to bleed high pressure oil.
    2. The gas is colorless, odorless and harmless.
    3. Saw a hole while covering the saw with a cloth because chips may fly up.
