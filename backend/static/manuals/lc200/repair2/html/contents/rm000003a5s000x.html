NETWORK GATEWAY ECU (for LHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL (w/ Passenger Side Knee Airbag)

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front door scuff plate (Click here).

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front door scuff plate (Click here).



3. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



4. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Floor Under Cover)



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the under cover.



6. REMOVE FRONT PASSENGER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/ Passenger Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the front passenger side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



7. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL (w/o Passenger Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the instrument panel.



8. REMOVE NO. 3 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the panel garnish.



9. REMOVE LOWER NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connector.



10. REMOVE NETWORK GATEWAY ECU



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the nut and ECU.
