NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



7. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 engine under cover.



8. REMOVE FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT



  1. Loosen the belt tension by turning the belt tensioner counterclockwise, and then remove the fan and generator V belt.

    HINT:
    The pulley bolt for the belt tensioner has left-hand thread.



9. DRAIN ENGINE OIL



  1. Remove the oil drain plug and gasket, and drain the engine oil into a container.



10. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT


CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  1. Remove the radiator cap. Then drain the coolant.

    HINT:
    Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.

  1. Loosen the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs. Then drain the coolant from the engine.

  1. Tighten the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 130 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.



11. REMOVE HOOD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 hood stay bolts. Then disconnect the 2 hood supports.

    NOTICE:
    1. Avoid touching the stroke portions of the rod as much as possible to prevent foreign matter from attaching to it. Be sure to hold the cylinders while servicing.
    2. Do not wear cotton gloves or other similar materials when handling the rod. Fibers may attach to the rod and result in gas leaks.
    3. Do not apply any load to the cylinders in the horizontal direction in order to prevent the rod from being deformed.
    4. Remove the hood support while supporting the hood by hand.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and hood.

35. DISCONNECT FUEL HOSE



  1. Remove the fuel hose clamp.

  1. Pinch and pull the fuel hose connector to disconnect the connector from the fuel pipe.

    NOTICE:
    1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
    2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
    3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
    4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
    5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
    6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.

    1. Detach the fuel hose clamp.

    3. Pinch and pull the No. 2 fuel hose connector to disconnect the connector from the No. 3 fuel pipe.

      NOTICE:

      4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
      5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.

  1. for body side:




    1. Pinch and pull the No. 2 fuel hose connector to disconnect the connector from the fuel pipe.

      NOTICE:
      1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
      2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
      3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
      4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
      5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.

52. REMOVE DRIVE PLATE AND RING GEAR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, hold the crankshaft damper.

    SST
    09213-70011   (09213-70020)
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)




  1. Remove the 8 bolts, front drive plate spacer, drive plate and rear drive plate spacer.



53. REMOVE ENGINE ASSEMBLY

  1. Attach an engine sling device and hang the engine with a chain block.

    NOTICE:
    Pay attention to the angle of the sling device, as the engine assembly or engine hanger No. 1 may be damaged or deformed.

  1. Remove the 4 engine mounting insulator bolts.

  1. Lift the engine out from the vehicle slowly and carefully.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the engine is clear of all wiring, hoses and cables.



54. INSTALL ENGINE ON ENGINE STAND

  1. Install the engine onto an engine stand with bolts.
