1. INSTALL KNOCK CONTROL SENSOR



  1. Install the 2 sensors with the 2 nuts as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 sensor connectors.



2. INSTALL CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR



  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the O-ring.

    NOTICE:
    When reusing the sensor, inspect the O-ring.
    If the O-ring has scratches or cuts, replace the sensor.




  1. Install the 2 sensors with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 sensor connectors.



3. INSTALL VENTILATION VALVE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the ventilation valve.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent




  1. Install the ventilation valve to the cylinder head cover sub-assembly LH.

    Torque:
    27 N*m{ 275 kgf*cm , 20 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    After applying the specified torque, rotate the ventilation valve clockwise until the port facing in the direction indicated in the illustration.



4. INSTALL VENTILATION VALVE COVER

  1. Install the ventilation valve cover to the ventilation valve.



5. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install a new O-ring to the camshaft timing oil control valve.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install the camshaft timing oil control valve with the bolt.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the oil seal face of the oil control valve (connecting surface with cylinder head cover).
    2. Be careful that the O-ring is not cracked when installing the oil control valve.

  1. Connect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector and wire harness clamp.



6. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install a new O-ring to the camshaft timing oil control valve.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install the oil control valve with the bolt.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the oil seal face of the oil control valve (connecting surface with cylinder head cover).
    2. Be careful that the O-ring is not cracked when installing the oil control valve.

  1. Connect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector.



7. INSTALL ENGINE OIL LEVEL SENSOR



  1. Clean the oil level sensor installation surface.

  1. Install a new gasket and the oil level sensor with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    7.0 N*m{ 71 kgf*cm , 62 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the oil level sensor connector.



8. INSTALL CRANKSHAFT POSITION SENSOR



  1. Install the sensor with the bolt.

    Torque:
    6.5 N*m{ 66 kgf*cm , 58 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the sensor connector.



9. INSTALL STARTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the starter wire with the nut.

    Torque:
    9.8 N*m{ 100 kgf*cm , 87 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the starter connector.

  1. Connect the starter to the cylinder block.

  1. Connect the ground wire with the bolt.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 173 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the engine wire protector to the starter with the bolt.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 173 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the starter with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    25 N*m{ 255 kgf*cm , 18 ft.*lbf }



10. INSTALL REAR WATER BY-PASS JOINT




  1. Install 2 new gaskets and the water by-pass joint with the 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    With the flat-bottom of each new gasket facing downward as shown in the illustration, install the 2 gaskets to the engine rear side.



11. INSTALL AIR PUMP WITH BRACKET AND AIR SWITCHING VALVE (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install the air pump with bracket with the 2 bolts and 2 nuts in the order shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    16 N*m{ 163 kgf*cm , 12 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the pressure sensor connector.

  1. Connect the air switching valve connector.



12. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR INJECTION SYSTEM HOSE (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install the hose.



13. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install a new gasket and the No. 1 air switching valve to the rear water by-pass joint with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the vacuum switching valve hose to the No. 1 air switching valve.



14. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install a new gasket and the No. 2 air switching valve to the rear water by-pass joint with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:

15. INSTALL WATER BY-PASS PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Apply soapy water to the O-ring.

  1. Install a new O-ring to the water by-pass pipe.




  1. Push in the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Install the water by-pass pipe with the bolt.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 vacuum switching valve hoses to the water by-pass pipe clamp.

  1. Connect the wire harness clamp.

  1. Connect the connector.



16. INSTALL FRONT WATER BY-PASS JOINT



  1. Install 2 new gaskets and the water by-pass joint with the 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    With the flat-bottom of each new gasket facing downward as shown in the illustration, install the 2 gaskets to the engine front side.



17. INSTALL ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR

  1. Install a new gasket to the sensor.




  1. Using a 19 mm deep socket wrench, install the sensor.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 200 kgf*cm , 14 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the sensor connector.



18. INSTALL WATER INLET HOUSING



  1. Apply soapy water to a new O-ring and install it to the water inlet housing.

  1. Apply seal packing in a continuous line to the places shown in the illustration.

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing 1282B, Three Bond 1282B or equivalent

    Standard seal diameter:
    2.0 to 3.0 mm (0.0787 to 0.118 in.)

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any oil from the contact surface.
    2. Install the water inlet housing within 3 minutes after applying seal packing.




  1. Temporarily install the water inlet housing with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    Bolt Length:
    Item
    		Length
    Bolt A
    		77 mm (3.03 in.)
    Bolt B
    		22 mm (0.866 in.)



19. CONNECT ENGINE WIRE


  1. Connect the ground wire to the No. 5 bearing cap and install the bolt, as shown in the illustration.



    Torque:
    8.4 N*m{ 86 kgf*cm , 74 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the ground wire to the left side of the engine and install the bolt.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 173 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the ground wire to the inside left side of the V-bank and install the bolt, as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 173 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect each ground wire to the cylinder head and cylinder head LH rear side and install the 2 bolts, as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    8.4 N*m{ 86 kgf*cm , 74 in.*lbf }

22. INSTALL OIL FILTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Clean and check the oil filter installation surface.

  1. Apply clean engine oil to the gasket of a new oil filter.

  1. Lightly screw the oil filter into place by hand. Tighten it until the gasket contacts the seat.




  1. Using SST, tighten the oil filter. Choose from the following to tighten the oil filter.

    SST
    09228-07501  

    1. Using a torque wrench, tighten the oil filter.

      Torque:
      17 N*m{ 175 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    2. Tighten the oil filter a 3/4 turn by hand or with a common wrench.
