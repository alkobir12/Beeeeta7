OIL PUMP > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the manual transmission assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE TRANSFER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the transfer assembly (Click here).



3. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and oil pump cover.



4. REMOVE OIL PUMP DRIVE ROTOR STRAIGHT PIN



  1. Using a magnetic finger, remove the 2 pins.



5. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER O-RING

  1. Remove the oil pump cover O-ring from the oil pump cover.
