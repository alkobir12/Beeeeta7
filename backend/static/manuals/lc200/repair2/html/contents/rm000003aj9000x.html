INJECTOR DRIVER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Disconnect the cables from the negative (-) main battery and sub-battery terminals.



2. REMOVE INJECTOR DRIVER



  1. Disconnect the 4 injector driver connectors.




  1. Detach the fuel hose clamp and wire harness clamp.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and injector driver assembly.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts, No. 2 injector driver and No. 2 injector driver bracket from the No. 1 injector driver bracket.




  1. Remove the 4 screws, No. 1 injector driver bracket, No. 1 injector driver and injector driver bracket.




  1. Remove the 4 screws, No. 2 injector driver bracket, No. 2 injector driver and injector driver bracket.
