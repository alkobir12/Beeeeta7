HEATER WATER PUMP (w/ Viscous Heater) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. for Standard:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).

  1. w/ Winch:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

  1. for Standard:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).

  1. w/ Winch:
    Remove the front fender splash shield (Click here).



4. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



5. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT

  1. for 1VD-FTV:
    Drain engine coolant (Click here).

  1. for 2UZ-FE:
    Drain engine coolant (Click here).

  1. for 1GR-FE:
    Drain engine coolant (Click here).



6. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL


  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.





7. REMOVE NO. 3 ENGINE ROOM WIRE

  1. Disconnect the 4 wire harness clamps.



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and No. 3 engine room wire.





8. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and No. 1 engine cover.



9. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and disconnect the connector.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 clamps and remove the air cleaner cap.



10. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 1 air cleaner hose.



11. REMOVE INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors from the viscous heater and water temperature sensor.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.




  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps and remove the 2 bolts and intake air connector.



12. REMOVE V-RIBBED BELT



  1. Loosen the lock nut and turn the bolt counterclockwise.

  1. Remove the V-ribbed belt.



13. REMOVE VANE PUMP OIL RESERVOIR ASSEMBLY



  1. Insert a screwdriver between the reservoir and oil reservoir bracket, push the claw, and then disconnect the reservoir by pulling it upwards.



14. REMOVE VANE PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and vane pump.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the vane pump.



15. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIATOR HOSE


16. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIATOR HOSE


17. REMOVE OIL COOLER HOSE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Disconnect the inlet and outlet No. 1 oil cooler hose.




  1. Disconnect the inlet No. 2 and No. 3 oil cooler hoses.




  1. Disconnect the inlet No. 4 oil cooler hose.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and transmission oil cooler tube.



18. REMOVE FAN SHROUD WITH FAN



  1. Loosen the 4 nuts holding the fluid coupling fan.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts holding the fan shroud.




  1. Remove the 4 nuts of the fluid coupling fan, and then remove the shroud together with the fluid coupling fan.



19. REMOVE RADIATOR RESERVE TANK ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 2 hoses.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and radiator reservoir.



20. REMOVE RADIATOR ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the radiator assembly (Click here).



21. REMOVE IDLER PULLEY ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 idler pulleys.



22. REMOVE VISCOUS WITH MAGNET CLUTCH HEATER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector and detach the clamp.

  1. Using pliers, grip the claws of the clips and slide the 2 clips.

  1. Disconnect the 2 heater hoses.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and heater assembly.



23. REMOVE IDLER PULLEY BRACKET



  1. Remove the bolt and bracket.



24. REMOVE IDLER PULLEY BRACKET (for Belt Tension Side)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bracket.
