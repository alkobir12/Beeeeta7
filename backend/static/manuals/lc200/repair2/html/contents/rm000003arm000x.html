REAR DIFFERENTIAL LOCK ACTUATOR (w/ Differential Lock) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL PROTECTOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 connector clamps and wire harness clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt, nut and protector.



2. REMOVE REAR DIFFERENTIAL LOCK ACTUATOR PROTECTOR



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and protector.



3. REMOVE REAR DIFFERENTIAL LOCK COVER WITH POSITION SWITCH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and differential lock cover with position switch.

  1. Using a brass bar and hammer, tap on the cover to remove it.



4. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL LOCK SHIFT ACTUATOR



  1. Remove the shift fork shaft bolt.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and differential lock shift actuator.

  1. Using a screwdriver, pull out the actuator and remove the sleeve and shift fork.
