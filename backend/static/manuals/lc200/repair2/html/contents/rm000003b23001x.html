GRAPHIC DECAL > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  3. When removing the stripe tape, heat the vehicle body and stripe tape using a heat light.

Standard Heating Temperature:
Item
Temperature
Vehicle Body
40 to 60°C (104 to 140°F)
Stripe Tape
20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)

NOTICE:
Do not heat the vehicle body and stripe tape excessively.



1. REMOVE FENDER STRIPE LH



  1. for Type A:
    Remove the fender stripe.

    1. Pull back an edge of the stripe tape and pull it parallel to the vehicle body to remove it.




  1. for Type B:
    Remove the fender stripe.

    1. Pull back an edge of the stripe tape and pull it parallel to the vehicle body to remove it.

3. REMOVE REAR DOOR OUTSIDE STRIPE LH

  1. for Type A:
