FRONT CONSOLE BOX (w/ Cool Box) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. RECOVER REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM

  1. Start the engine.

  1. Turn the A/C switch on.

  1. Operate the cooler compressor while the engine speed is approximately 1000 rpm for 5 to 6 minutes to circulate the refrigerant and collect the compressor oil remaining in each component into the cooler compressor.

  1. Stop the engine.

  1. Recover the refrigerant from the A/C system using a refrigerant recovery unit.



2. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (for Power Seat)

  1. Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (for Manual Seat)

  1. Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).



4. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



5. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



7. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



8. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. Twist the shift lever knob in the direction indicated by the arrow and remove it.





9. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



10. REMOVE REAR UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 9 claws and remove the panel.



11. REMOVE CONSOLE CUP HOLDER BOX SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the box.



12. REMOVE UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 14 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console panel.



13. REMOVE NO. 1 COOLER COVER



  1. Detach the clamp on the lower part of the cooler cover, and then pull the cooler cover upward to remove it.



14. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE END PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the end panel and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



15. REMOVE COOLING BOX ASSEMBLY




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 cooler pipes.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the cooler pipes.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cooling box.
