HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY (for HID Headlight) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE NO. 1 HEADLIGHT BULB



  1. Turn the bulb in the direction indicated by arrow in the illustration.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the bulb.



2. REMOVE FRONT TURN SIGNAL LIGHT BULB



  1. Turn the bulb in the direction indicated by arrow in the illustration and remove it.



3. REMOVE CLEARANCE LIGHT BULB



  1. Turn the bulb in the direction indicated by arrow in the illustration and remove it.



4. REMOVE DISCHARGE HEADLIGHT BULB



  1. Turn the back cover in the direction indicated by the arrow and remove it.




  1. Turn the headlight control ECU socket in the direction indicated by the arrow and disconnect it.




  1. Release the set spring lock as shown in the illustration and remove the bulb.

    NOTICE:
    Do not touch the glass part of the bulb.



5. REMOVE HEADLIGHT LEVELING MOTOR LH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Turn the motor in the direction indicated by the arrow (1) in the illustration to disconnect it.

  1. Turn the aiming screw in the direction indicated by the arrow (2) in the illustration to disconnect the shaft.

  1. Pull the headlight leveling motor LH in the direction indicated by the arrow (3) in the illustration to remove it.
