for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT SUPPORT PLATE REAR (w/ Sliding Roof)



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



2. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT SUPPORT PLATE REAR (w/o Sliding Roof)



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



3. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM COVER LH



  1. Remove the back door trim cover LH as shown in the illustration.



4. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM COVER RH



  1. Remove the back door trim cover RH as shown in the illustration.



5. REMOVE BACK DOOR TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 16 clips and remove the back door trim panel.



6. REMOVE TIRE CARRIER COURTESY LIGHT SWITCH



  1. Disconnect the connector

  1. Remove the bolt and switch.
