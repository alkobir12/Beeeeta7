POWER STEERING OIL PRESSURE SWITCH > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 10 bolts and engine under cover.



4. REMOVE V-BANK COVER



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps and remove the air cleaner hose.



6. REMOVE FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT



  1. While releasing the belt tension by turning the belt tensioner counterclockwise, remove the fan and generator V belt from the belt tensioner.



7. DRAIN POWER STEERING FLUID


8. REMOVE POWER STEERING OIL PRESSURE SWITCH



  1. Disconnect the oil pressure switch connector.

  1. Using a 19 mm deep socket wrench, remove the oil pressure switch.
