CRUISE CONTROL SYSTEM > Clutch Switch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When the clutch pedal is released, the ECM receives positive (+) battery voltage through the LH-IG fuse. While depressing the clutch pedal, the clutch switch sends a signal to terminal D*1 or CLSW*2 of the ECM. The ECM cancels cruise control when terminal D*1 or CLSW*2 receives the signal.

HINT:
  1. *1: for 1GR-FE
  2. *2: for 1VD-FTV
