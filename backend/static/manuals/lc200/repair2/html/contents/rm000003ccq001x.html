FUEL LID LOCK CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE TONNEAU COVER ASSEMBLY (w/ Tonneau Cover)

  1. Remove the tonneau cover.



3. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH (w/o Airbag Cut Off Switch)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



4. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH (w/ Airbag Cut Off Switch)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the side panel and disconnect the connector.



5. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



6. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



7. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



8. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



9. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



10. REMOVE NO. 1 TONNEAU COVER HOLDER CAP (w/ Tonneau Cover)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the tonneau cover holder cap on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the tonneau cover holder cap.



11. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT COVER BEZEL (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat)

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the rear No. 2 seat cover bezel.



12. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



13. REMOVE FUEL FILLER OPENING LID LOCK RETAINER



  1. Turn the cable counterclockwise and remove the cable from the retainer.




  1. Turn the lock retainer counterclockwise and remove the lock retainer.



14. REMOVE FUEL LID LOCK OPEN LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 claws.

  1. Disconnect the fuel lid lock control cable and remove the fuel lid lock open lever.



15. REMOVE FUEL LID LOCK CONTROL CABLE SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the clamps shown in the illustration.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Remove the lock control cable.
