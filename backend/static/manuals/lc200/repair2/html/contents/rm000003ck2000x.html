1. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR OUTLET GRILLE ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



HINT:
Use the same procedures to remove the air outlet grille on the other side.

  1. Detach 6 claws and remove the air outlet grille.



2. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR OUTLET GRILLE ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the No. 2 air outlet grille.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ROOF SILENCER PAD



  1. Remove the roof silencer pad.



4. REMOVE NO. 4 ROOF SILENCER PAD



  1. Remove the roof silencer pad.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ROOF WIRE

  1. Turn the visor connectors approximately 90° clockwise and remove them from the roof headlining.

  1. Remove the 6 pieces of tape from the roof headlining.

  1. Detach each clamp, and remove the roof wire and washer hose.



  1. Remove the double-sided tape remaining on the headlining.





6. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. w/o Sliding Roof:



    1. Detach the 2 clamps and remove the antenna from the notch on the rear of the roof headlining.



    2. Remove the antenna cord from the roof headlining.

    3. Remove the double-sided tape from the roof headlining.



  1. w/ Sliding Roof:



    1. Detach the 4 clamps and remove the antenna from the notch on the rear of the roof headlining.



    2. Remove the antenna cord from the roof headlining.

    3. Remove the double-sided tape from the roof headlining.
