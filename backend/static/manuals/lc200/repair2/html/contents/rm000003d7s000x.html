BACK DOOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER HINGE BRACKET (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the bracket.



2. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  1. Detach the clip and remove the cover.



3. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)

  1. Remove the spare tire.



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and spare wheel carrier.





4. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER LOCK CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Detach the clamp.

  1. Remove the 5 bolts, then remove the spare wheel carrier lock control cable together with the spare wheel carrier lock and spare wheel carrier lock cover.




  1. Open the spare wheel carrier lock cover, detach the clamp, and remove the spare wheel carrier lock control cable from the spare wheel carrier lock.



5. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER LOCK ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the spare wheel carrier lock from the spare wheel carrier lock cover.



6. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER AUXILIARY STOPPER (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and stopper.



7. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER LOCK STRIKER ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and striker.



8. REMOVE SPARE PLATE SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and plate.
