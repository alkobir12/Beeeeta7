AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM > Heater Control Switch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The temperature control switch assembly LH and RH sends the temperature up and down signal to the air conditioning amplifier assembly through the multi-display*1 or the air conditioning control assembly*2.

HINT:
*1: w/ Multi-display
*2: w/o Multi-display
