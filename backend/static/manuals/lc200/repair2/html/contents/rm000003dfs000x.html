for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. DISPOSE OF NO. 2 SEAT STAY SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Horizontally fix the seat stay in a vise with the piston-rod pulled out.

  1. Wear safety glasses. Gradually cut a part between A and B shown in the illustration using a metal saw and gradually release the gas.

    Standard:
    Area
    		Specified Condition
    a
    		80 mm (3.15 in.)
    b
    		10 mm (0.394 in.)

    CAUTION:
    As metal debris may be blown outward by the gas, you must:
    1. Wear protective glasses.
    2. Cover the area being cut.

    NOTICE:
    The gas inside the seat stay is colorless, odorless and harmless. There is a possibility that metal debris could scatter, so cover the part with a piece of cloth or other material.
