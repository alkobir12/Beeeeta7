LIN COMMUNICATION SYSTEM > Rain Sensor LIN Communication Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The LIN communication of the components related to the rain sensor occurs between the rain sensor and windshield wiper ECU.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

NOTICE:
When using the intelligent tester with the engine switch off to troubleshoot:
Connect the intelligent tester to the vehicle, and turn a courtesy switch on and off at 1.5 second intervals until communication between the intelligent tester and vehicle begins.

1.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (RAIN SENSOR - WIND SHIELD WIPER ECU AND OVERHEAD JUNCTION BLOCK)


  1. Disconnect the E85 ECU connector.

  1. Disconnect the R11 sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the R7 junction block.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    R11-3 (MPX) - E85-7 (MPX1)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    R11-4 (SIG) - R7-29 (IG2)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    R11-1 (ES) - R7-4 (GND2)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    R11-3 (MPX) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    R11-4 (SIG) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    R11-1 (ES) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher

  1. Reconnect the R7 junction block connector.

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    R11-4 (SIG) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK

  1. Temporarily replace the rain sensor with a new one (Click here).

  1. Check the rain sensor function is normal.
