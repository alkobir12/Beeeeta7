LUMBAR SUPPORT ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY (for Manual Seat) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the front seat assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE VERTICAL ADJUSTER COVER LH (for 8 Way Seat Type)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the vertical adjuster cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE VERTICAL ADJUSTING HANDLE LH (for 8 Way Seat Type)



  1. Remove the 2 screws and vertical adjusting handle.



4. REMOVE VERTICAL SEAT ADJUSTER KNOB (for 8 Way Seat Type)



  1. Remove the snap ring and vertical seat adjuster knob.



5. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER RELEASE HANDLE LH



  1. Lift the reclining adjuster release handle.

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the reclining adjuster release handle.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



6. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH



  1. w/ Seatback Board:
    Remove the 2 rubber bands from the hooks.




  1. w/o Seatback Board:
    Remove the 2 rubber bands from the hooks.




  1. for 4 Way Seat Type:

    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Detach the 3 claws in the order shown in the illustration, and then remove the cushion shield.




  1. for 8 Way Seat Type:

    1. Remove the 3 screws.

    2. Detach the 3 claws in the order shown in the illustration, and then remove the cushion shield.

  1. w/ Lumbar Support:
    Disconnect the lumbar switch connector.



7. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Disconnect the connectors and detach the clamps.




  1. Remove the nut and front seat inner belt assembly.



8. REMOVE FRONT INNER SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and clip, and then remove the cushion shield.



9. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK BOARD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Seatback Board)



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Move the seatback board in the direction of the arrow to detach the 2 hooks and remove the seatback board.



10. REMOVE FRONT SEAT HEADREST SUPPORT (w/ Active Headrest)



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 headrest supports.



11. REMOVE SEATBACK COVER WITH PAD


  1. w/ Seatback Board:




    1. Remove the 3 hog rings and detach the 9 hooks.




    2. w/ Front Seat Side Airbag:
      Remove the nut and seatback cover bracket from the seat frame.



  1. w/o Seatback Board:




    1. Remove the 3 hog rings.




    2. Open the 2 fasteners, and then open the seatback cover.




    3. w/ Front Seat Side Airbag:
      Remove the nut and seatback cover bracket from the seat frame.




    4. Detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 headrest supports.

  1. Remove the seatback cover with pad from the seatback frame.



12. REMOVE LUMBAR SUPPORT ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Lumbar Support)



  1. Remove the 4 springs.

  1. Detach the 4 hooks and remove the seatback spring.




  1. Disconnect the lumbar support adjuster connector.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and lumbar support adjuster.
