HINT:
  1. Bore all 8 cylinders to fit the O/S piston outside diameter.
  2. Replace all the piston rings with ones to match the O/S pistons.



1. BORE CYLINDER

  1. Prepare 4 new O/S pistons.




  1. Using a micrometer, measure the piston diameter at a position that is 6.7 mm (0.264 in.) downward from the center of the piston pin hole.

    Standard piston diameter (O/S 0.50):
    94.468 to 94.496 mm (3.719 to 3.720 in.)

  1. Calculate the amount each cylinder is to be rebored as follows.

    HINT:
    1. Size to be rebored = P + C - H
      P = Piston diameter
      C = Piston oil clearance: 0.050 to 0.068 mm (0.00197 to 0.00268 in.)
      H = Allowance for honing: 0.02 mm (0.000787 in.) or less

  1. Bore and hone the cylinders to the calculated dimensions.

    NOTICE:
    Excess honing will destroy the finished roundness.

  1. Using a cylinder gauge, measure the cylinder bore diameter and calculate the honing allowance.

    HINT:
    Honing allowance = cylinder bore diameter - (piston diameter + oil clearance)

  1. Finish the cylinder bore using the calculated value.




  1. Using a cylinder gauge, measure the cylinder bore diameter.
