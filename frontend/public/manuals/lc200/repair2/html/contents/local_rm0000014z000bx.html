DTC    P0660    Intake Manifold Tuning Valve Control Circuit / Open (Bank 1)


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

PERFORM ACTIVE TEST USING INTELLIGENT TESTER (OPERATE VSV FOR ACIS)

INSPECT VSV FOR ACIS (OPERATION)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (VSV FOR ACIS - ECM, VSV FOR ACIS - EFI NO. 2 FUSE)

CHECK VACUUM HOSE

INSPECT INTAKE AIR SURGE TANK (INTAKE AIR CONTROL VALVE)
