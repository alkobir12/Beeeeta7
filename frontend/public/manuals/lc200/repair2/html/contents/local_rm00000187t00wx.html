DTC    P0405    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Sensor "A" Circuit Low


DESCRIPTION

MONITOR DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (POWER SOURCE)

INSPECT EGR VALVE ASSEMBLY (No. 1 or No. 2) (POSITION SENSOR)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (EGR VALVE POSITION SENSOR - ECM)

REPLACE ECM

REPLACE EGR VALVE ASSEMBLY (No. 1 or No. 2)

REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR

CONFIRM WHETHER MALFUNCTION HAS BEEN SUCCESSFULLY REPAIRED
