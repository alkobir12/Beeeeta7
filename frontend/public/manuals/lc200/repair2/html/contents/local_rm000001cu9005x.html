DTC    C1763    Abnormal Pump Motor Oil Pressure


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

INSPECT SUSPENSION CONTROL RELAY (AHC)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (SUSPENSION CONTROL RELAY - PUMP AND MOTOR AND BODY GROUND)

CHECK OPERATION OF HEIGHT CONTROL PUMP AND MOTOR
