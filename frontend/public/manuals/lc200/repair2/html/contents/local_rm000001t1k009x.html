DTC    B2788    IG2 Signal Malfunction


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

INSPECT STEERING LOCK ECU

INSPECT FUSE (IGN, IG2 MAIN)

INSPECT INTEGRATION RELAY (IG2 RELAY)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (STEERING LOCK ECU - INTEGRATION RELAY (IG2 RELAY) AND BODY GROUND)

CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (MAIN BODY ECU - INTEGRATION RELAY (IG2 RELAY) AND BODY GROUND)
