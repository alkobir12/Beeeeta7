DTC    B1661/44    Roll Over Cut Off Indicator Malfunction


DESCRIPTION

WIRING DIAGRAM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

CHECK COMBINATION METER (SOURCE VOLTAGE)

CHECK RSCA OFF INDICATOR CIRCUIT

INSPECT CURTAIN SHIELD AIRBAG CUTOFF SWITCH

CHECK CURTAIN SHIELD AIRBAG CUTOFF SWITCH CIRCUIT

CHECK FLOOR WIRE (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR - INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE)

CHECK NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR - INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE)

CHECK NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE (CENTER AIRBAG SENSOR - INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE)

CHECK INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE (NO. 2 FLOOR WIRE - NO. 3 INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE AND BODY GROUND)
