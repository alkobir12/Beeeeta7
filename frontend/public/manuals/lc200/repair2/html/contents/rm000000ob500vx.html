1.CHECK NAVIGATION SYSTEM SETTING

  1. Check that "OFF" is not selected.

    OK:

  1. Check that "Voice Guidance in All Modes" is not OFF.

    OK:

NG

  1. Enter the "Menu" screen by pressing the "MENU" switch.

  1. Select "Setup".

    OK:
    "Auto Voice Guidance" is not OFF.



NG
