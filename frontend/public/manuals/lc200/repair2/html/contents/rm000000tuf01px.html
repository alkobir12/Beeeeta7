DTC    B278A    Short to GND in Immobiliser System Power Source Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This DTC is output when the engine switch power source supply line is open or shorted.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B278A
The engine switch power source supply line is open or shorted.
  1. Harness or connector
  1. Engine switch
  1. Certification ECU (Smart key ECU)

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (CERTIFICATION ECU (SMART KEY ECU ASSEMBLY) - ENGINE SWITCH)


  1. Disconnect the E29 ECU connector.

  1. Disconnect the E18 switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E29-30 (VC5) - E18-14 (VC5)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E29-40 (AGND) - E18-8 (AGND)
    E29-30 (VC5) or E18-14 (VC5) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    E29-40 (AGND) or E18-8 (AGND) - Body ground
