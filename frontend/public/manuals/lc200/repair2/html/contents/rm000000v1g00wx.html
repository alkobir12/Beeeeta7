WATER PUMP > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and under cover.



4. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL



  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.



5. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.

  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug and 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

  1. Remove the radiator cap. Then drain the coolant.

    HINT:
    Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.



  1. Tighten the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.



6. REMOVE V-BANK COVER



  1. Remove the 2 cap nuts and V-bank cover.



7. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Remove the radiator hose.



8. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Remove the radiator hose.



9. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Detach the claw to open the flexible hose clamp, and then remove the 2 bolts to disconnect the oil cooler tube from the fan shroud.



10. REMOVE FAN SHROUD



  1. Loosen the 4 nuts holding the fluid coupling fan.

  1. Remove the fan and generator V-belt (Click here).




  1. Disconnect the reservoir hose from the upper side of the radiator tank.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts holding the fan shroud.

  1. Remove the 4 nuts of the fluid coupling fan, and then remove the shroud together with the coupling fan.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the radiator core.

  1. Remove the fan pulley from the water pump.



11. INSPECT WATER PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Visually check the air hole and water hole for coolant leakage.
    If leakage is found, replace the water pump assembly.

  1. Turn the pulley and check that the water pump bearing moves smoothly and quietly.
    If necessary, replace the water pump assembly.



12. INSTALL FAN SHROUD

  1. Install the fan pulley to the water pump.

  1. Place the shroud together with the coupling fan between the radiator and engine.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the radiator core.

  1. Temporarily install the fluid coupling fan to the water pump with the 4 nuts. Tighten the nuts as much as possible by hand.




  1. Attach the claws of the shroud to the radiator as shown in the illustration.

  1. Install the shroud with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    8.0 N*m{ 82 kgf*cm , 71 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the reservoir hose to upper side of the radiator tank upper.

  1. Install the fan and generator V-belt (Click here).

  1. Tighten the 4 nuts of the fluid coupling fan.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



13. CONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Connect the oil cooler tube with the 2 bolts, and attach the claw to close the flexible hose clamp.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }



14. INSTALL NO. 2 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Install the radiator hose.



15. INSTALL NO. 1 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Install the radiator hose.



16. ADD ENGINE COOLANT

  1. Add engine coolant.

    Standard Capacity:
    Item
    		Specified condition
    for Automatic transmission
    		w/o Rear Heater
    		11.2 liters (11.8 US qts, 9.9 Imp. qts)
    w/ Rear Heater
    		14.4 liters (15.2 US qts, 12.7 Imp. qts)
    for Manual transmission
    		w/o Rear Heater
    		12.0 liters (12.7 US qts, 10.6 Imp. qts)
    w/ Rear Heater
    		14.6 liters (15.4 US qts, 12.8 Imp. qts)

    NOTICE:
    Do not substitute plain water for engine coolant.

    HINT:
    TOYOTA vehicles are filled with TOYOTA SLLC at the factory. In order to avoid damage to the engine cooling system and other technical problems, only use TOYOTA SLLC or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology consists of a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).




  1. Slowly pour coolant into the radiator reservoir until it reaches the F line.

  1. Install the reservoir cap.

  1. Press the No. 1 and No. 2 radiator hoses several times by hand, and then check the coolant level. If the coolant level is low, add coolant.

  1. Install the radiator cap.

  1. Set the air conditioning as follows while warming up the engine.
    Item
    		Condition
    Fan speed
    		Any setting except off
    Temperature
    		Toward WARM
    Air conditioning switch
    		Off

  1. Start the engine and warm it up until the thermostat opens.

    HINT:
    The thermostat opening timing can be confirmed by pressing the No. 2 radiator hose by hand, and checking when the engine coolant starts to flow inside the hose.

  1. Maintain the engine speed at 2000 to 2500 rpm.

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure that the radiator reservoir still has some coolant in it.
    2. Pay attention to the needle of the water temperature meter. Make sure that the needle does not show an abnormally high temperature.
    3. If there is not enough coolant, the engine may burn out or overheat.
    4. Immediately after starting the engine, if the radiator reservoir does not have any coolant, perform the following: 1) stop the engine, 2) wait until the coolant has cooled down, and 3) add coolant until the coolant is filled to the F line.
    5. Run the engine at 2000 rpm until the coolant level has stabilized.

  1. Press the No. 1 and No. 2 radiator hoses several times by hand to bleed air.

    CAUTION:
    1. Wear protective gloves. Heat areas on the parts may injure your hands.
    2. Be careful as the radiator hoses are hot.
    3. Keep your hands away from the fan.

  1. Stop the engine, and wait until the engine coolant cools down to ambient temperature.

    CAUTION:
    Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Check that the coolant level is between the F and L lines.
    If the coolant level is below the L line, repeat all of the procedures above.
    If the coolant level is above the F line, drain coolant so that the coolant level is between the F and L lines.



17. INSPECT FOR COOLANT LEAK

CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and attach a radiator cap tester.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Using the radiator cap tester, increase the pressure inside the radiator to 123 kPa (1.3 kgf/cm2, 18 psi), and check that the pressure does not drop.
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks. If no external leaks are found, check the heater core, cylinder block and head.



18. INSTALL UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL



  1. Install the radiator support seal with the 7 clips.



19. INSTALL V-BANK COVER



  1. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 cap nuts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }



20. INSTALL NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the under cover with the 10 bolts.



21. INSTALL FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



22. INSTALL FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Push in the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to install the fender splash shield.

  1. Install the 3 bolts and screw.
