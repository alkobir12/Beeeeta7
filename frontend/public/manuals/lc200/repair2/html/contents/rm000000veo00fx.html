INSTRUMENT PANEL SPEAKER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE NO. 1 SPEAKER OPENING COVER ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 8 claws and remove the opening cover.



3. REMOVE FRONT NO. 4 SPEAKER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Remove the speaker and then disconnect the speaker connector.

    NOTICE:
    Do not touch the cone part of the speaker.



4. REMOVE ASSIST GRIP SUB-ASSEMBLY



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the front assist grip on the other side.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 assist grip plugs.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the front assist grip.



5. REMOVE FRONT PILLAR GARNISH LH



  1. Detach the clip 3 guides, and remove the front pillar garnish.

  1. for 9 Speakers:
    Disconnect the speaker connector and then remove the front pillar garnish.




  1. Protect the curtain shield airbag.

    1. Thoroughly cover the airbag with a cloth or nylon sheet and fix the ends of the cover with adhesive tape, as shown in the illustration.

      NOTICE:
      Cover the curtain shield airbag with a protective cover as soon as the front pillar garnish is removed.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL SPEAKER PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the speaker panel.



7. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 SPEAKER ASSEMBLY



  1. for 9 Speakers Models:

    1. Remove the 2 bolts.

    2. Remove the speaker and then disconnect the speaker connector.

      NOTICE:
      Do not touch the cone part of the speaker.



  1. except 9 Speakers Models:




    1. Remove the 2 bolts.

    2. Remove the speaker and then disconnect the speaker connector.

      NOTICE:
      Do not touch the cone part of the speaker.
