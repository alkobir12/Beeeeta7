CHECK CERTIFICATION ECU (SMART KEY ECU ASSEMBLY)


  1. Disconnect the E29 and E30 ECU connectors.

  2. Measure the resistance and voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E29-1 (+B) - Body ground
    		B - Body ground
    		+B power supply
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    E29-18 (IG) - Body ground
    		B - Body ground
    		IG power supply
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    Engine switch off
    		Below 1 Ω
    E29-17 (E) - Body ground
    		W-B - Body ground
    		Ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

    1. If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction on the wire harness side.

  3. Reconnect the E29 and E30 ECU connectors.

  4. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E29-2 (IND) - Body ground
    		V - Body ground
    		Security indicator signal
    		Security indicator illuminates (illuminates only for 60 sec. in alarm sounding state (blinking when system in armed state))
    		6 to 11 V
    E30-20 (SH-) - Body ground*1
    		LG - Body ground
    		Security horn signal
    		Security horn not sounding (theft deterrent system in alarm sounding state)
    		11 to 14 V
    Security horn sounding (theft deterrent system in alarm sounding state)
    		0.5 to 4 V
    E30-24 (HSW) - Body ground
    		W - Body ground
    		Engine hood courtesy switch signal
    		Engine hood closed
    		10 kΩ or higher
    Engine hood open
    		Below 1 Ω
    E30-7 (SSCL) - Body ground*2
    		L - Body ground
    		Theft warning siren (self power siren) signal
    		Theft warning siren (self power siren) sounding (theft warning system in alarm sounding state)
    		Below 2 V
    Theft warning siren (self power siren) not sounding (theft warning system in alarm sounding state)
    		4.5 to 14 V
    E30-4 (IOUT) - Body ground*3
    		P - Body ground
    		Intrusion sensor signal input
    		No moving object detected
    		9 to 14 V
    Moving object detected during arming preparation state or armed state
    		Pulse generation (see waveform 1)
    E30-5 (SSW1) - Body ground*3
    		V - Body ground
    		Intrusion sensor cancel switch signal
    		Intrusion sensor cancel switch on
    		Below 1.5 V
    Intrusion sensor cancel switch off
    		4.5 to 14 V
    E30-21 (CSIF) - Body ground*3
    		P - Body ground
    		Tilt sensor signal input
    		Vehicle tilt not detected
    		9 to 14 V
    Vehicle tilt detected during arming preparation state or armed state
    		Pulse generation (see waveform 2)
    E30-23 (GBS) - Body ground*4
    		V - Body ground
    		Glass breakage sensor signal
    		Glass breakage sensor normal
    		Below 2 V
    Glass breakage sensor malfunctioning
    		4.5 to 14 V

    HINT:
    *1: w/ Security Horn
    *2: w/ Theft Warning Siren (Self Power Siren)
    *3: w/ Intrusion Sensor
    *4: w/ Glass Breakage Sensor

    1. If the result is not as specified, the ECU may have a malfunction.




  5. Using an oscilloscope, check waveform 1.
    Waveform 1 (Reference) 
    Item
    		Content
    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		E30-4 (IOUT) - Body ground
    Tool Setting
    		2 V/DIV., 500 ms/DIV.
    Condition
    		Moving object detected during arming preparation state or armed state




  6. Using an oscilloscope, check waveform 2.
    Waveform 2 (Reference) 
    Item
    		Content
    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		E30-21 (CSIF) - Body ground
    Tool Setting
    		2 V/DIV., 500 ms/DIV.
    Condition
    		Vehicle tilt detected during arming preparation state or armed state

    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition

    		B - Body ground
    		Battery power supply
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    2B-20 (BATB) - Body ground
    		L - Body ground
    		Battery power supply
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    2D-7 (IG) - Body ground

    2D-8 (ACC) - Body ground
    		GR - Body ground
    		ACC power supply

    		11 to 14 V
    Engine switch off
    		Below 1 V
    2D-62 (GND2) - Body ground

    		Ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E3-1 (GND3) - Body ground
    		BR - Body ground
    		Ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
