    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection

    If the result is not as specified, replace the injector assembly.



2. INSPECT INJECTION VOLUME AND LEAKAGE



  1. Check the injector injection.

    CAUTION:
    1. This test involves high-pressure fuel and electricity.
    2. Take every precaution regarding safe handling of both the fuel and the electrical parts.
    3. Perform this test in a safe area, and avoid any sparks or flame.
    4. Do not smoke.


    1. Assemble SST as shown in the illustration.

      SST
      09268-31012   (09268-41091, 09268-41120, 09268-41140, 09268-41400, 09268-41500, 90467-13001, 95333-06030, 95336-08070)

    2. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).

    3. Disconnect the fuel inlet hose (fuel tube connector) from the fuel pipe.

    4. Remove the bolt and disconnect the fuel pressure regulator from the fuel delivery pipe.

      NOTICE:
      Do not disconnect the No. 2 fuel pipe from the fuel pressure regulator.




    5. Connect SST to the fuel pipe.

      CAUTION:
      Always read the precautions before connecting the fuel tube connector.




    6. Connect SST (hose) to the fuel inlet of the pressure regulator with another SST (union) and the 2 bolts.

      SST
      09268-31012   (09268-41091, 95336-08070)

      Torque:
      9.0 N*m{ 92 kgf*cm , 80 in.*lbf }

    7. Connect the fuel return hose to the fuel outlet of the pressure regulator.




    8. Install a new O-ring to the injector.

    9. Connect SST (adapter and hose) to the injector, and hold the injector and adapter with SST (clamp).

      SST
      09268-31012   (09268-41140, 09268-41400, 95336-08070)

    10. Put the injector into a graduated cylinder.

      CAUTION:
      Install a suitable vinyl tube onto the injector to contain any gasoline spray.

    11. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    12. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

      NOTICE:
      Do not start the engine.

    13. Turn the intelligent tester ON.

    14. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Active Test / Activate the Fuel Pump Speed Control.




    15. Connect SST (wire) to the injector and the battery for 15 seconds, and measure the injection volume with a graduated cylinder. Test each injector 2 or 3 times.

      SST
      09842-30080  

      Standard injection volume:
      71 to 86 cm3 (4.3 to 5.2 cu. in.) per 15 seconds

      Difference between each injector:
      15 cm3 (0.9 cu. in.) or less

      NOTICE:
      Always turn the voltage on and off on the battery side, not the fuel pump side.

      If the result is not as specified, replace the injector assembly.




  1. Check for fuel drop.

    1. In the condition above, disconnect the tester probes of SST (wire) from the battery and check for fuel drop from the injector.

      Standard fuel drop:
      1 drop or less per 12 minutes

      If the fuel drop is not as specified, replace the injector assembly.
