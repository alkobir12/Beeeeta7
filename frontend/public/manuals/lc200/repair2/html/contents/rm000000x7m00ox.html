DTC    B2000    Short to B+ in Seat Belt Motor RH Circuit

DTC    B2005    Short to B+ in Seat Belt Motor LH Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The seat belt control ECU receives information from the master cylinder solenoid (skid control ECU) via the CAN communication line and tightens the seat belt by operating the motor in the front seat outer belt assembly RH or LH.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B2000
  1. Short to B+ in the seat belt motor RH circuit continues for 1 second or more.
  2. Short in the seat belt motor RH circuit.
  1. Front seat outer belt assembly RH
  2. Harness or connector
  3. Seat belt control ECU
B2005
  1. Short to B+ in the seat belt motor LH circuit continues for 1 second or more.
  2. Short in the seat belt motor LH circuit.
  1. Front seat outer belt assembly LH
  2. Harness or connector
  3. Seat belt control ECU
