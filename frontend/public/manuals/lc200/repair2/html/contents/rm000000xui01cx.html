ENTRY AND START SYSTEM (for Entry Function) > Driver Side Door Entry Lock and Unlock Functions do not Operate

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When the driver side door entry lock and unlock functions do not operate, one of the following may be malfunctioning: 1) power door lock system, 2) driver side door electrical key oscillator, or 3) certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly).

3.CHECK WAVE ENVIRONMENT



  1. Bring the electrical key transmitter near the driver side door outside handle, and perform a driver side door entry lock and unlock operation check.

    NOTICE:
    If the key is brought within 0.2 m (0.656 ft.) of the door handle, communication is not possible.

    HINT:
    1. When the electrical key transmitter is brought near the driver side door outside handle, the possibility of wave interference decreases, and it can be determined if wave interference is causing the problem symptom.
    2. If the operation check is normal, the possibility of wave interference is high. Also, added vehicle components may cause wave interference. If installed, remove them and perform the operation check.
