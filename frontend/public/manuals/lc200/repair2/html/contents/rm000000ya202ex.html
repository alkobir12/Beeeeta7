INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT ENGINE SWITCH

  1. Disconnect the E18 engine switch connector.

  1. Apply battery voltage between the terminals of the engine switch, and check the illumination condition of the engine switch indicator light.

    NOTICE:
    1. If the positive (+) lead and the negative (-) lead are incorrectly connected, the engine switch indicator light will not illuminate.
    2. If the voltage is too low, the indicator light will not illuminate.
