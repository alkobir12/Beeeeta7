CHECK DTC

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  2. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  3. Turn the intelligent tester on.

  4. Enter the following menus: Body / (desired system) / Trouble Codes.

  5. Check for DTCs by following the directions on the tester screen.

    HINT:
    Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.


CLEAR DTC

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  2. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  3. Turn the intelligent tester on.

  4. Enter the following menus: Body / (desired system) / Trouble Codes.

  5. Clear the DTCs by following the directions on the tester screen.

    HINT:
    Refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.
