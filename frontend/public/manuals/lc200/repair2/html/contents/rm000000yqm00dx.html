INSPECTION PROCEDURE

This problem may be caused by a malfunction in the vehicle stability control system or steering system.
Make sure that no DTC is output for the skid control ECU or steering control ECU before proceeding with the following procedures.
If a DTC is output for either of these ECUs, first troubleshoot it and then proceed to the troubleshooting below.
1.CHECK DRIVE VEHICLE

  1. Drive the vehicle straight at 35 km/h (22 mph) or more for 5 seconds or more.

  1. Turn the steering wheel slowly from left to right several times, and then check if the steering wheel is centered.
