VARIABLE GEAR RATIO STEERING SYSTEM > Steering Wheel Vibrates or Abnormal Noise is Heard from Steering Wheel

for Preparation Click here


INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
Check that no DTC is output for the VGRS system and vehicle stability control system.

1.CHECK STEERING WHEEL CONDITION

  1. Center the steering wheel.

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the steering actuator connector.

  1. Start the engine.

  1. Check for abnormal noise or vibration while turning the steering wheel.

    OK:
    No abnormal noise or vibration.

    NOTICE:
    DTC C15A5/65 is stored when the engine is running with the connector disconnected from the steering actuator. Clear the DTC after this check.
