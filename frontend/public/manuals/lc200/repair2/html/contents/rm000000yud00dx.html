DTC    C15C6/77    IG Power Supply Voltage Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The steering control ECU monitors the IG power supply voltage.
If the steering control ECU detects a malfunction, it will turn on the master warning light, store DTC C15C6/77, and stop VGRS operation.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C15C6/77
The steering control ECU detects that the battery voltage is normal and the IG terminal voltage is below 4 V for 6 seconds.
  1. Harness or connector
  2. Steering control ECU
