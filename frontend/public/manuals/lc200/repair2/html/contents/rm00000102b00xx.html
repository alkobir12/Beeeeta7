CHECK RADIO RECEIVER (for Separate Type Amplifier System)


  1. Disconnect the F1 receiver connector.

  2. Measure the resistance and voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specification
    F1-1 (B) - F1-20 (GND)
    		L - BR
    		Battery
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    F1-20 (GND) - Body ground
    		BR - Body ground
    		Ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    F1-11 (ACC) - F1-20 (GND)
    		GR - BR
    		Accessory (ON)
    		Engine switch off
    		Below 1 V
    F1-11 (ACC) - F1-20 (GND)
    		GR - BR
    		Accessory (ON)
    		Engine switch on (ACC)
    		11 to 14 V

    1. If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction on the wire harness side.

  3. Reconnect the F1 receiver connector.

  4. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specification

    		Engine switch on (IG), light control switch off
    		Below 1 V
    F1-2 (ILL+) - F1-20 (GND)
    		G - BR
    		Illumination signal
    		Engine switch on (IG), light control switch tail or on
    		11 to 14 V
    F1-7 (MUTE) - F1-20 (GND)
    		B - BR
    		Mute signal
    		Audio system playing → changing modes
    		3.5 V or higher → Below 1 V
    F1-8 (R+) - F1-20 (GND)
    		B - BR
    		Sound signal (right)
    		Audio system playing

    F1-9 (L+) - F1-20 (GND)
    		R - BR

    		Audio system playing
    		Waveform synchronized with sound is output
    F1-18 (R-) - F1-20 (GND)
    		W - BR
    		Sound signal (right)
    		Audio system playing
    		Waveform synchronized with sound is output
    F1-19 (L-) - F1-20 (GND)
    		G - BR
    		Sound signal (left)
    		Audio system playing
    		Waveform synchronized with sound is output
    F1-12 (ILL- ) - F1-20 ( GND)

    F1-12 (ILL-) - F1-20 (GND)

    		Pulse generation
    F1-5 (ATX+) - F1-20 (GND)
    		R - BR

    F1-15 (ATX-) - F1-20 (GND)

    		Engine switch on (ACC)

    F1-13 (ANT) - F1-20 (GND)

    F19-6 (SWG) - F1-20 (GND)
    		W - BR

    		Always
    		Below 1 V
    F19-7 (SW1) - F1-20 (GND)
    		L - BR

    F19-7 (SW1) - F1-20 (GND)

    F19-7 (SW1) - F1-20 (GND)
    		L - BR
