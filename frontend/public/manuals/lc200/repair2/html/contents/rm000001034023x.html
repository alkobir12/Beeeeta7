2.IDENTIFY NOISE SOURCE

  1. Check the noise condition.

    HINT:
    The radio has a noise prevention function to reduce noise when listening to the radio. If a loud noise occurs, check whether the ground at the antenna mounting base and the noise prevention unit are installed and wired correctly.


    Condition in Which Noise Occurs
    		Noise Type

    1. When horn switch is pressed and released

    		Engine coolant temperature sensor noise
    When wiper is operating
    		Wiper noise
    When brake pedal is depressed
    		Stop light switch noise
    Other
    		Static electricity on vehicle

    HINT:
    1. In the left column of the chart, find the situation that matches the vehicle of the customer. Then, in the right column, find the part that is causing the noise. Check the noise filter on the part.
    2. To save time and avoid a misdiagnosis, first make sure that the noise is not originating from outside the vehicle.
    3. Troubleshoot noise problems in order of loudness, starting with the loudest noise.
    4. Setting the radio to a frequency that receives no signal may make recognition of the noise problem easier.
