PARKING BRAKE CABLE > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE REAR WHEEL


2. DISCONNECT REAR DISC BRAKE CYLINDER ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the rear disc brake cylinder.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not disconnect the flexible hose from the disc brake cylinder.
    2. Do not twist or bend the flexible hose.

  1. Hang the disc brake cylinder with a piece of wire.



3. REMOVE REAR DISC



  1. Put matchmarks on the rear disc and axle hub if planning to reuse the disc.




  1. Turn the shoe adjuster as shown in the illustration until the disc turns freely, and then remove the disc.



4. REMOVE PARKING BRAKE SHOE RETURN SPRING



  1. Using SST, remove the return spring.

    SST
    09703-30011  



5. REMOVE NO. 1 PARKING BRAKE SHOE ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using SST, remove the shoe hold down spring cup, compression spring and shoe hold down spring pin.

    SST
    09718-00011  




  1. Disconnect the tension spring from the No. 1 parking brake shoe.




  1. Remove the No. 1 parking brake shoe and shoe adjuster screw set.



6. REMOVE NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE SHOE ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using SST, remove the shoe hold down spring cup, compression spring and shoe hold down spring pin.

    SST
    09718-00011  

  1. Remove the No. 2 parking brake shoe.



7. REMOVE PARKING BRAKE SHOE LEVER LH

  1. Disconnect the No. 3 parking brake cable from the parking brake shoe lever.




  1. Remove the parking brake shoe lever.



8. DISCONNECT NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt and No. 3 parking brake cable.



9. REMOVE HEIGHT CONTROL UNIT PROTECTOR PIPE


  1. Remove the 6 bolts and protector pipe.





10. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SUSPENSION CONTROL ACCUMULATOR BRACKET

  1. Remove the bolt and bracket from the shock absorber control valve.



11. RECOVER REFRIGERANT FROM REFRIGERATION SYSTEM (w/ Cool Box)

  1. Start the engine.

  1. Turn the A/C switch on.

  1. Operate the cooler compressor while the engine speed is approximately 1000 rpm for 5 to 6 minutes to circulate the refrigerant and collect the compressor oil remaining in each component into the cooler compressor.

  1. Stop the engine.

  1. Recover the refrigerant from the A/C system using a refrigerant recovery unit.



12. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (for Power Seat)

  1. Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).



13. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (for Manual Seat)

  1. Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).



14. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



15. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



16. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



17. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



18. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Automatic Transmission)


  1. Twist the shift lever knob in the direction indicated by the arrow and remove it.





19. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transmission)


  1. Twist the shift lever knob in the direction indicated by the arrow and remove it.





20. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



21. REMOVE REAR UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 9 claws and remove the panel.



22. REMOVE CONSOLE CUP HOLDER BOX SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the box.



23. REMOVE UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 14 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console panel.



24. REMOVE NO. 1 COOLER COVER (w/ Cool Box)



  1. Detach the clamp on the lower part of the cooler cover, and then pull the cooler cover upward to remove it.



25. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE END PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the end panel and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



26. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE BOX SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/o Cool Box)




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the console box.



27. REMOVE COOLING BOX ASSEMBLY (w/ Cool Box)




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and 2 screws.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 cooler pipes.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings from the cooler pipes.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cooling box.



28. REMOVE NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the adjusting nut.




  1. Disconnect the No. 2 parking brake cable from the No. 1 parking brake pull rod.




  1. Pinch the claws of the No. 2 parking brake cable and push the cable outside of the vehicle slightly.



  1. Tilt the fuel tank (Click here) so that bolt A in the illustration can be removed.



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and No. 2 parking brake cable.

  1. Remove the No. 2 parking brake cable clamp from the No. 2 parking brake cable.



29. REMOVE NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the adjusting nut.




  1. Disconnect the No. 3 parking brake cable from the No. 1 parking brake pull rod.




  1. Pinch the claws of the No. 3 parking brake cable and push the cable outside of the vehicle slightly.



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and No. 3 parking brake cable.



  1. Remove the No. 3 parking brake cable clamp from the No. 3 parking brake cable.
