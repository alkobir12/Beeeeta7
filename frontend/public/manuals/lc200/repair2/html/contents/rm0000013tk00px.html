DTC    P0418    Secondary Air Injection System Control "A" Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The diagnostic trouble code (DTC) indicates an open or short in the air pump circuit of the secondary air injection (AIR) system. The air injection control driver diagnoses the air pump circuit, E-ASV circuit and itself, and sends the diagnosis signal to the engine control module (ECM). If the ECM receives a malfunction signal from the air injection control driver, the ECM illuminates the malfunction indicator lamp (MIL), and stores a DTC.
The secondary air injection (AIR) system introduces fresh air into the exhaust manifold in order to reduce carbon monoxide (CO) and hydrocarbon (HC) in the exhaust emission. This system is activated just after the engine start, and is stopped when the catalyst converter is warmed-up. This system consists of the following components: 1) the air pump which sends fresh air to the exhaust manifold, 2) the electric air switching valve (E-ASV) which switches the fresh air flow, 3) the air switching valve (ASV) which switches the air passage to the exhaust manifold of each bank, 4) the vacuum switching valve (VSV) which switches the vacuum (intake manifold pressure) to operate the ASV, 5) the pressure sensor which detects the fresh air pressure and exhaust pulse, 6) the air injection control driver which controls the power sources of the air pump and E-ASV, 7) the engine control module (ECM) which controls and monitors this system.
