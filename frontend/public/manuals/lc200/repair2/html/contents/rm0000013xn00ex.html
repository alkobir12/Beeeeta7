1. CHECK GLOW PLUG ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the screw grommet.

  1. Remove the glow plug connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Glow plug terminal - Body ground
    		20°C (68°F)
    		Approximately 1 Ω

    1. If the result is not as specified, replace the glow plug assembly.

    NOTICE:
    1. Be careful not to damage the glow plug, as this may cause an open circuit or shorten the life of the glow plugs.
    2. Do not allow oil or gasoline to contact the glow plug when cleaning it.
    3. Clean any oil on the terminal and bakelite washer with a dry cloth.
    4. Do not apply more than 11 V to the glow plug, as this may cause an open circuit.
