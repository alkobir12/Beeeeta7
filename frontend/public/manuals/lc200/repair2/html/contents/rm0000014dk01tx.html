SEAT BELT WARNING SYSTEM > Driver Side Seat Belt Warning Light does not Operate

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When the engine switch is on (IG), the center airbag sensor transmits front seat inner belt state signals to the combination meter through the CAN. If the driver seat belt is not fastened, the combination meter blinks the driver side seat belt warning light. If the seat belt is fastened, the warning light goes off.
