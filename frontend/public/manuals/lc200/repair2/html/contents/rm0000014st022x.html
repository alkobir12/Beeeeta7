NAVIGATION SYSTEM > Microphone Circuit between Overhead J/B and Multi-display

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This circuit sends a microphone signal from the microphone in the map light to the multi-display.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK MICROPHONE



  1. Check the waveform of the microphone using an oscilloscope.
    Measurement Condition 
    Item
    		Content
    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		R7-27 (MI1+) - R7-26 (MIC-)
    Tool Setting
    		0.2 V/DIV., 0.2 μ/DIV.
    Condition
    		Engine switch on (ACC), and "Voice" switch of steering pad switch pressed

    OK:
    Waveform that matches sound spoken into microphone is output.



NG
Go to step 4
OK


2.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (MAP LIGHT - MULTI-DISPLAY)



  1. Disconnect the R7 map light connector.

  1. Disconnect the F27 display connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F27-9 (MACC) - R7-25 (ACC)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    F27-11 (MIN+) - R7-27 (MI1+)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    F27-10 (MIN-) - R7-26 (MIC-)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    F27-9 (MACC) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    F27-11 (MIN+) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    F27-10 (MIN-) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
