1. INSPECT INTAKE AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR




  1. Partially immerse the sensor in water and warm up the water.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		Approximately 20°C (68°F)
    		2.21 to 2.65 kΩ
    1 - 2
    		Approximately 60°C (140°F)
    		0.55 to 0.61 kΩ

    If the result is not as specified, replace the sensor.

    NOTICE:
    When checking the sensor in water, keep the terminals dry. After the check, wipe the sensor dry.
