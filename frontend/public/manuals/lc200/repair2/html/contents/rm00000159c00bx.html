VACUUM PUMP (for 1VD-FTV) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR HOSE



  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the No. 2 air hose.



2. REMOVE NO. 4 AIR TUBE



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the suction hose.




  1. Loosen the hose clamp and remove the bolt and No. 4 air tube.



3. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the ventilation hose from the oil separator.

  1. Loosen the hose clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and No. 2 air cleaner pipe.



4. REMOVE V-RIBBED BELT (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Loosen the lock nut and turn the bolt counterclockwise.

  1. Remove the V-ribbed belt.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 IDLER PULLEY (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Remove the bolt, cover, No. 2 idler pulley and collar.



6. REMOVE NO. 2 IDLER PULLEY BRACKET (w/ Viscous Heater)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and idler pulley bracket.



7. DISCONNECT UNION TO CONNECTOR TUBE HOSE



  1. Disconnect the hose from the vacuum pump.



8. REMOVE VACUUM PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and vacuum pump.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings.
