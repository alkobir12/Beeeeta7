FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER ASSEMBLY > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL EXTENSION FLANGE TUBE



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, remove the differential tube.



2. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR SHAFT OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST, tap out the oil seal.

    SST
    09308-00010  



3. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE FRONT NUT



  1. Using SST and a hammer, unstake the nut.

    SST
    09930-00010  




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, remove the nut.

    SST
    09330-00021   (09330-00030)



4. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE PINION COMPANION FLANGE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, remove the companion flange from the differential carrier.

    SST
    09950-30012   (09951-03010, 09953-03010, 09954-03010, 09955-03030, 09956-03020)



5. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST, remove the oil seal.

    SST
    09308-10010  



6. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION OIL SLINGER


7. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CROSS SHAFT BEARING RETAINER



  1. Remove the bolts labeled B and front differential front support assembly LH.

  1. Remove the bolts labeled A.

  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap out the bearing retainer.



8. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 DIFFERENTIAL CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the differential case from the differential carrier.



9. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE PINION



  1. Set the differential carrier to an overhaul stand as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a press, press out the drive pinion.

    NOTICE:
    Do not drop the drive pinion.



10. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE PINION FRONT RADIAL BALL BEARING

  1. Remove the bearing (inner race) from the differential carrier.



11. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL BREATHER PLUG OIL DEFLECTOR



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and oil deflector.



12. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL UNION



  1. Remove the front differential union from the differential carrier.



13. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE PINION REAR TAPERED ROLLER BEARING



  1. Using SST and a press, press out the bearing (inner race) from the drive pinion.

    SST
    09950-00020  
    If the drive pinion or ring gear is damaged, replace them as a set.



14. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE PINION REAR TAPERED ROLLER BEARING



  1. Using a brass bar and hammer, tap out the bearing (outer race) and washer.



15. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE PINION FRONT RADIAL BALL BEARING



  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap out the bearing (outer race) from the differential carrier.

    SST
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)
    09950-60010   (09951-00450)
    09950-60020   (09951-00710, 09952-06010)



16. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CASE BEARING

HINT:
  1. Measure the plate washer and note its thickness.
  2. Tag the bearing outer races to show the location for reassembly.




  1. Using SST and a press, press out the bearing (outer race) and plate washer from the bearing retainer.

    SST
    09950-60020   (09951-00680)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)




  1. Using SST and a press, press out the bearing (outer race) and plate washer from the differential carrier.

    SST
    09950-60020   (09951-00680)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)



17. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL RING GEAR



  1. Place matchmarks on the ring gear and differential case.

  1. Remove the 12 bolts.

  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap on the ring gear to disconnect it from the differential case.



18. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CASE BEARING



  1. Using SST, remove the 2 bearings (inner race).

    SST
    09950-60010   (09951-00390)
    09950-40011   (09951-04020, 09952-04010, 09953-04030, 09954-04010, 09955-04061, 09957-04010, 09958-04011)

    HINT:
    Fix the claws of SST into the notch in the differential case assembly.



19. DISASSEMBLE DIFFERENTIAL CASE



  1. Using a pin punch and hammer, tap out the straight pin.

  1. Remove these parts from the differential case assembly.

    1. 2 differential side gears

    2. 2 differential side gear thrust washers

    3. Differential pinion shaft

    4. 2 differential pinion gears

    5. 2 differential pinion gear thrust washers



20. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL GEAR KIT

  1. Check that there is no damage to the pinion gears and side gears.
    If a pinion gear and/or side gear is damaged, replace the differential gear kit.



21. INSPECT FRONT NO. 1 DIFFERENTIAL CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Check that the differential case is not damaged.
    If the differential case is damaged, replace it.



22. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR SHAFT OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST, tap out the oil seal from the flange tube.

    SST
    09308-00010  



23. REMOVE DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR SHAFT SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Using a snap ring pliers, remove the snap ring.




  1. Using a plastic-faced hammer, tap out the side gear shaft.



24. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL SIDE GEAR SHAFT RH BEARING



  1. Using a snap ring expander, remove the snap ring.




  1. Using SST and a press, press out the bearing.

    SST
    09950-00020  
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)
    09950-60010   (09951-00450)

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the bearing.



25. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL CARRIER STRAIGHT PIN



  1. Remove the straight pins from the bearing retainer.



26. REMOVE FRONT DIFFERENTIAL TUBE STRAIGHT PIN



  1. Remove the straight pins from the bearing retainer.
