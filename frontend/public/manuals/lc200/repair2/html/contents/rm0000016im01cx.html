1. INSPECT HIGH PITCHED HORN ASSEMBLY (for Spiral Type)



  1. Apply battery voltage and check operation of the horn, as shown in the table.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1
    Battery negative (-) → Body ground
    		Horn sounds

    If the result is not as specified, replace the horn.
