FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 engine under cover.



5. DRAIN DIFFERENTIAL OIL

  1. Stop the vehicle on a level place.




  1. for Front Differential:

    1. Remove the engine under cover.

    2. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, remove the filler plug and gasket.

    3. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the oil.

    4. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and the drain plug.

      Torque:
      39 N*m{ 400 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

  1. for Rear Differential:

    1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.

    2. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the oil.

    3. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

      Torque:
      49 N*m{ 500 kgf*cm , 36 ft.*lbf }



6. REMOVE FRONT AXLE ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the front axle assembly (Click here).



7. REMOVE FRONT DRIVE SHAFT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using SST, remove the drive shaft.

    SST
    09520-01010  
    09520-24010   (09520-32040)

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the dust cover and oil seal.
