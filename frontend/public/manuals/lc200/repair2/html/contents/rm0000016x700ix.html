FRONT AXLE HUB > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here


NOTICE:
Do not allow foreign matter, etc. to contact the ABS rotor bearing.



1. REMOVE FRONT AXLE WITH ABS ROTOR BEARING ASSEMBLY

  1. Gently fix the front axle hub in a vise.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the threads of the hub bolts.




  1. Using SST, remove the bearing.

    SST
    09950-40011   (09951-04020, 09952-04010, 09953-04020, 09954-04010, 09955-04061, 09957-04010, 09958-04011)
    09950-60010   (09951-00550)




  1. If part of the bearing remains in the front axle hub, use SST to remove it.

    SST
    09950-00020  
    09950-00030  



2. REMOVE FRONT AXLE HUB OIL SEAL



  1. Using a screwdriver, pry off the oil seal.
