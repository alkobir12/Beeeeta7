FRONT AXLE HUB > REASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here


NOTICE:
Do not allow foreign matter, etc. to contact the ABS rotor bearing.



1. INSTALL FRONT AXLE HUB OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST and a press, press in a new oil seal.

    SST
    09608-36010  
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the oil seal.



2. INSTALL FRONT AXLE WITH ABS ROTOR BEARING ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST and a press, press in a new bearing.

    SST
    09608-32010  

    NOTICE:
    1. Insert 4 bolts into the bearing before installing the axle hub.
    2. Make sure to securely fit the bearing to the axle hub.
