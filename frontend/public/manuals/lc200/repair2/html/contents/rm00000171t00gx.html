HYDRAULIC BRAKE BOOSTER (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


NOTICE:
  1. Be sure not to allow the pump to contact the floor; support it with the booster body. If the pump contacts the floor, the red brake tube between the pump and booster body may be deformed, the operating sound of the pump may become loud enough to hear within the cabin, and fluid may leak.
  2. When installing, coat the parts indicated by arrows with lithium soap base glycol grease (Click here).
  3. As high pressure is applied to the brake actuator tube No. 1, never deform it.
  4. Until the work is over, do not turn the engine switch on (IG).
  5. Before starting the work, make sure that the engine switch is off and depress the brake pedal more than 20 times.

HINT:
When pressure in the power supply system is released, the reaction force becomes light and the stroke becomes longer.



1. DRAIN BRAKE FLUID

NOTICE:
Wash off brake fluid immediately if it comes into contact with a painted surface.



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH


4. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the under cover and disconnect the connectors.



5. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



6. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



7. REMOVE DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and driver side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



8. REMOVE PUSH ROD PIN



  1. Remove the clip and push rod pin from the brake pedal lever.



9. REMOVE HYDRAULIC BRAKE BOOSTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 3 connectors from the hydraulic brake booster.




  1. Use tags or make a memo to identify the place to reconnect.




  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the 4 brake lines from the hydraulic brake booster.

  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the clamp from the hydraulic brake booster assembly.




  1. Remove the 4 nuts and pull out the hydraulic brake booster assembly.



10. REMOVE BRAKE BOOSTER GASKET

  1. Remove the brake booster gasket from the hydraulic brake booster assembly.
