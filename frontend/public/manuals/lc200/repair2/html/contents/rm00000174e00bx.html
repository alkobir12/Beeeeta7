CLUTCH PEDAL (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the clutch master cylinder assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL BRACKET SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the clutch start switch connector.

  1. w/ Cruise Control System:
    Disconnect the clutch switch connector.




  1. Remove the bolt and clutch pedal bracket.



3. REMOVE TURNOVER SPRING



  1. Move the pedal to the full stroke position.

  1. Remove the turnover spring.



4. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SPRING



  1. Remove the E-ring and spring.



5. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the nut, washer and bolt.

  1. Remove the clutch pedal from the clutch pedal bracket.



6. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL PAD

  1. Remove the clutch pedal pad from the clutch pedal.



7. REMOVE NO. 2 CLUTCH PEDAL CUSHION



  1. Remove the clutch pedal cushion from the clutch pedal.



8. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SHAFT COLLAR



  1. Remove the clutch pedal shaft collar from the clutch pedal.



9. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL BUSH



  1. Remove the 2 bushes from the clutch pedal.



10. REMOVE CLUTCH PEDAL SPRING HOLDER



  1. Remove the clutch pedal spring holder from the clutch pedal bracket.
