COUNTER GEAR AND REVERSE IDLER GEAR > INSPECTION

1. INSPECT COUNTER GEAR



  1. Using a dial indicator and 2 V-blocks, measure the shaft runout.

    Maximum Runout:
    0.03 mm (0.00118 in.)

    If the runout is more than the maximum, replace the counter gear.




  1. Using a micrometer, measure the journal diameter.

    Standard Diameter:
    35.957 to 35.970 mm (1.41562 to 1.41613 in.)

    Minimum Diameter:
    35.957 mm (1.41562 in.)

    If the diameter is less than the minimum, replace the bearing or counter gear shaft.



2. INSPECT REVERSE IDLER GEAR



  1. Using a dial indicator, measure the reverse idler gear radial clearance.

    Standard Clearance:
    0.015 to 0.059 mm (0.000591 to 0.00232 in.)

    Maximum Clearance:
    0.059 mm (0.00232 in.)

    If the clearance is more than the maximum, replace the reverse idler gear or reverse idler gear shaft.
