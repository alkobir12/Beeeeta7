DTC    P0405    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Sensor "A" Circuit Low

DTC    P0406    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Sensor "A" Circuit High

DTC    P0407    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Sensor "B" Circuit Low

DTC    P0408    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Sensor "B" Circuit High

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The EGR valve position sensor is mounted on the EGR valve and used for detecting the lift amount of the valve. The lift amount detected by the sensor is provided to the ECM as feedback. The ECM then regulates the lift amount of the valve in accordance with engine running conditions.

P0405 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
Engine switch on (IG) for 5 seconds
No. 1 EGR valve position sensor output voltage is less than 0.1 V for 5 seconds (1 trip detection logic).
  1. Open in No. 1 EGR valve position sensor circuit
  2. No. 1 EGR valve (EGR valve position sensor)
  3. ECM

P0406 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
Engine switch on (IG) for 5 seconds
No. 1 EGR valve position sensor output voltage is more than 4.9 V for 5 seconds (1 trip detection logic).
  1. Short in No. 1 EGR valve position sensor circuit
  2. No. 1 EGR valve (EGR valve position sensor)
  3. ECM

P0407 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
Engine switch on (IG) for 5 seconds
No. 2 EGR valve position sensor output voltage is less than 0.1 V for 5 seconds (1 trip detection logic).
  1. Open in No. 2 EGR valve position sensor circuit
  2. No. 2 EGR valve (EGR valve position sensor)
  3. ECM

P0408 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
Engine switch on (IG) for 5 seconds
No. 2 EGR valve position sensor output voltage is more than 4.9 V for 5 seconds (1 trip detection logic).
  1. Short in No. 2 EGR valve position sensor circuit
  2. No. 2 EGR valve (EGR valve position sensor)
  3. ECM

MONITOR DESCRIPTION

When the output voltage of the EGR valve position sensor deviates from the normal operating range of 0.1 to 4.9 V for more than 5 seconds, the ECM interprets this as a malfunction of the sensor circuit, and illuminates the MIL.
