DTC    P0105    Manifold Absolute Pressure / Barometric Pressure Circuit

DTC    P0107    Manifold Absolute Pressure / Barometric Pressure Circuit Low Input

DTC    P0108    Manifold Absolute Pressure / Barometric Pressure Circuit High Input

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION


The manifold absolute pressure sensor detects the intake manifold pressure by using its built-in sensor unit. The ECM determines the basic injection duration and injection advance timing based on the voltage output by the manifold absolute pressure sensor.
The manifold absolute pressure sensor monitors the absolute pressure inside the intake manifold (default is 0 kPa [0 mmHg, 0 in.Hg]). As a result the ECM controls the air-fuel ratio at the proper level under any driving conditions.

P0105 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
After engine is started, race engine for 1 second
After the engine is started, condition (a) continues for more than 2.0 seconds (1 trip detection logic):
(a) Manifold absolute pressure sensor voltage is 0.1 V or less, or 4.8 V or more.
  1. Open or short in manifold absolute pressure sensor circuit
  2. Manifold absolute pressure sensor
  3. ECM

HINT:
  1. If DTC P0105, P0107 and/or P0108 is stored, the following symptoms may appear:
    1. Misfire
    2. Combustion noise
    3. Black smoke
    4. White smoke
    5. Lack of power
  2. When DTC P0105, P0107 or P0108 is stored, check the intake manifold pressure by entering the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Data List / MAP.

8.CONFIRM WHETHER MALFUNCTION HAS BEEN SUCCESSFULLY REPAIRED

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Clear the DTCs (Click here).

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) for 3 seconds and turn the tester on.

  1. After the engine is started, race the engine for 1 second.

  1. Confirm that the DTC is not output again.

    HINT:
    Perform the following procedure using the tester to determine whether or not the DTC judgment has been carried out.

    1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Utility / All Readiness.

    2. Input DTC P0105, P0107 and/or P0108.

    3. Check that STATUS is NORMAL. If STATUS is INCOMPLETE or UNKNOWN, race the engine for 1 minute and then idle the engine for 5 minutes.
