DTC    P0400    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Flow

DTC    P1248    Exhaust Gas Recirculation Flow Bank 2

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The EGR system recirculates exhaust gases. The recirculated gas mingles with the intake air so that the EGR system can slow combustion speed and keep the combustion temperature down. This helps reduce NOx emissions.
In order to increase EGR circulation efficiency, the ECM adjusts the lift amount of the EGR valve and the throttle valve angle.

P0400 (No. 1 EGR valve malfunction) 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
After warming up engine and idling for 60 seconds, maintain engine speed at 2500 rpm for 40 seconds, drive vehicle and perform engine break deceleration by fully closing accelerator when engine speed is 2000 rpm or more
The target and actual positions of the No. 1 EGR valve assembly are different for 30 seconds or more (1 trip detection logic).
  1. EGR valve stuck
  2. EGR valve does not move smoothly
  3. Open or short in EGR valve circuit
  4. EGR valve deposit
  5. EGR valve passage
  6. ECM

P0400 (Flow malfunction) 
DTC Detection Drive Pattern
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
Decelerate from a speed of 50 km/h (80 mph) or more (release accelerator pedal for approximately 5 seconds)
Mass air flow rate is not changed when turning on the electric EGR control valve while decelerating (2 trip detection logic).
  1. EGR valve stuck
  2. EGR valve does not move smoothly
  3. Open or short in EGR valve circuit
  4. EGR valve deposit
  5. EGR valve passage
  6. ECM

HINT:
If DTC P0400 and/or P1238 is stored, the following symptoms may appear:
  1. Stuck closed malfunction
    1. Intake booming noise
    2. Slight combustion noise
  2. Stuck open malfunction
    1. Black smoke
    2. Lack of power
    3. Vibration at engine stop
    4. Hesitation


MONITOR DESCRIPTION

  1. When the target and actual positions of the EGR valve are different, the ECM interprets this as a malfunction of the EGR valve and illuminates the MIL (1 trip detection logic).
  2. If the electric EGR control valve is forcibly operated but the intake air amount does not vary, the ECM determines that the electric EGR control valve is malfunctioning. The ECM then illuminates the MIL (2 trip detection logic).

HINT:
  1. The Data List item Actual EGR Valve Pos. changes to follow Target EGR Position. When the engine switch is on (IG) (engine stopped), the EGR valve is fully closed (Actual EGR Valve Pos. = 0), and when idling, the EGR valve is open partway (neither fully closed nor fully open).
  2. Read freeze frame data using the intelligent tester. Freeze frame data records the engine condition when malfunctions are detected. When troubleshooting, freeze frame data can help determine if the vehicle was moving or stationary, if the engine was warmed up or not, and other data from the time the malfunction occurred.
