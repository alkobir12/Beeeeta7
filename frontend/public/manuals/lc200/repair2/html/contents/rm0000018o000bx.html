1VD-FTV VANE PUMP > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here


NOTICE:
When using a vise, do not overtighten it.



1. REMOVE POWER STEERING SUCTION PORT UNION

  1. Remove the bolt and suction port union from the vane pump.

  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the O-ring from the suction port union.



2. REMOVE FLOW CONTROL VALVE



  1. Remove the pressure port union.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the pressure port union.

  1. Remove the flow control valve and compression spring.



3. REMOVE VANE PUMP GEAR



  1. Using soft jaws on a vise, clamp the vane pump.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the vane pump.

  1. Using SST, remove the gear.

    SST
    09950-30012   (09951-03010, 09953-03010, 09954-03010, 09955-03011)



4. REMOVE REAR VANE PUMP HOUSING



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear housing from the front housing.




  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the O-ring from the rear housing.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



5. REMOVE VANE PUMP ROTOR, 10 VANE PUMP PLATES AND VANE PUMP CAM RING

  1. Remove the 10 vane pump plates.

    NOTICE:
    Take care not to drop the vane pump plates.

  1. Remove the rotor.

  1. Remove the cam ring from the front housing.



6. REMOVE VANE PUMP FRONT SIDE PLATE

  1. Remove the front side plate from the front housing.




  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the O-ring from the front side plate.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Remove the O-ring from the front housing.



7. REMOVE VANE PUMP SHAFT WITH VANE PUMP SHAFT BEARING



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the snap ring.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the front housing.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Tape the serrated part of the vane pump shaft.




  1. Using a press, press out the shaft with bearing.



8. REMOVE VANE PUMP SHAFT BEARING



  1. Using 2 screwdrivers, remove the snap ring from the shaft.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the shaft.




  1. Using a press, press out the bearing from the shaft.



9. REMOVE VANE PUMP HOUSING OIL SEAL



  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the front housing.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
