FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 engine under cover.



5. REMOVE PROPELLER SHAFT HEAT INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and insulator.



6. REMOVE FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY


  1. for 1GR-FE, 2UZ-FE and Center Grease Fitting type:




    1. Place matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and transfer flange.

    2. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers.




    3. Place matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and differential.

    4. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers.

    5. Remove the front propeller shaft.



  1. for 1VD-FTV:




    1. Place matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and transfer flange.

    2. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers.




    3. Place matchmarks on the propeller shaft flange and differential.

    4. Remove the 4 nuts and 4 washers.

    5. Remove the front propeller shaft.
