AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY (for 1GR-FE) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



6. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



7. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



8. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL FRONT RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and fender apron seal.



9. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



10. DRAIN AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION FLUID

  1. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the ATF.

  1. Install a new gasket and the drain plug.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



11. REMOVE FRONT PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the front propeller shaft (Click here).



12. REMOVE PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the propeller shaft (Click here).



13. REMOVE CENTER EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the bolt and clamp, and disconnect the center exhaust pipe from the tailpipe assembly.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts with the 2 compression springs.

  1. Disconnect the center exhaust pipe from the 3 exhaust pipe supports.

  1. Remove the 3 gaskets from the front exhaust pipe, front No. 2 exhaust pipe, and center exhaust pipe.



14. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the gasket from the front exhaust pipe.



15. REMOVE FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and front exhaust pipe.

  1. Remove the gasket from the front exhaust pipe.



16. DISCONNECT FLOOR SHIFT GEAR SHIFTING ROD SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clip and pin, and disconnect the gear shifting rod from the transmission control shaft lever RH.



17. REMOVE STARTER ASSEMBLY (for 1.6 kW Type)



  1. Remove the terminal cap.

  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the starter wire.

  1. Disconnect the starter connector.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and starter.



18. REMOVE STARTER ASSEMBLY (for 2.0 kW Type)

  1. Disconnect the terminal cap.




  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the starter wire.

  1. Disconnect the starter connector.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and starter.



19. REMOVE DRIVE PLATE AND TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH SETTING BOLT



  1. Remove the flywheel housing side cover.




  1. Turn the crankshaft to gain access to the 6 bolts and remove each bolt while holding the crankshaft pulley setting bolt with a wrench.



20. SUPPORT AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



  1. Support the transmission with a transmission jack. Lift the transmission slightly from the crossmember.



21. REMOVE NO. 2 FRAME CROSSMEMBER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the engine mounting hole cover.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts of the rear engine mounting insulator.




  1. Remove the 4 nuts, 4 bolts and frame crossmember.



22. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear engine mounting insulator from the transmission.



23. DISCONNECT WIRE HARNESS AND CONNECTOR



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground cable.




  1. Tilt the transmission downward.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure the cooling fan does not contact the fan shroud.



  1. Disconnect the park/neutral position switch connector, transmission wire connector, 2 transmission speed sensor connectors and 2 transfer control side connectors.



    HINT:
    Detach the claw, press down the lever, and then disconnect the transmission wire connector.

  1. Disconnect the 2 connector clamps and 6 harness clamps.

  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the wire harness.



24. REMOVE MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and stay.



25. REMOVE NO. 2 MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and stay.



26. DISCONNECT BREATHER PLUG HOSE



  1. Disconnect the breather plug hose and detach the transfer breather hose.



27. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 hoses from the transmission, and separate the oil cooler tube from the engine.



28. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER TUBE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Disconnect the 2 hoses from the transmission oil thermostat.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts, and separate the oil cooler tube from the engine.



29. DISCONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 water bypass hoses from the transmission oil cooler, and separate the by-pass pipe from the automatic transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Use a container to catch any coolant which flows out of the water by-pass hoses.



30. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 9 bolts and transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Do not use excess force when prying the transmission assembly.



31. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER ASSEMBLY (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Disconnect the 2 oil cooler hoses.




  1. Remove the 3 bolts and transmission oil cooler with transmission oil thermostat.

  1. Remove the 2 O-rings.



32. REMOVE HARNESS CLAMP BRACKET

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 brackets.



33. REMOVE TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 breather hose clamps from the automatic transmission breather tube.

  1. Disconnect the clamp of the ground cable from the transfer case lower protector.




  1. Remove the 7 bolts and transfer case lower protector.




  1. Remove the 8 bolts and transfer.



34. REMOVE TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY


35. INSPECT TORQUE CONVERTER CLUTCH ASSEMBLY

  1. Inspect the torque converter clutch (Click here).
