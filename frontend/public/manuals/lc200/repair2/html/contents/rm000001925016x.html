BACK WINDOW GLASS > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. CLEAN BACK DOOR GLASS

  1. Using a scraper, remove the damaged clips, spacers and adhesive sticking to the glass.




  1. Clean the outer circumference of the glass with non-residue solvent.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not touch the glass surface after cleaning it.
    2. Be careful not to damage the glass.
    3. Even if using new glass, clean the glass with non-residue solvent.



2. INSTALL BACK WINDOW GLASS CLIP RH

  1. Apply Primer G to the glass where the clip will be installed.

    HINT:
    If the primer is applied to an area that is not specified, apply non-residue solvent to a clean cloth and wipe off the excess primer before it dries.




  1. Remove the peeling paper from a new clip. Install a new clip onto the glass as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    1. Allow the primer to dry for 3 minutes or more.
    2. Throw away any leftover primer.
    3. Do not apply too much primer.

7. INSTALL BACK WINDOW GLASS



  1. Using a brush or sponge, apply Primer M to the exposed part of the vehicle body.

    NOTICE:
    1. Allow the primer to dry for 3 minutes or more.
    2. Do not apply primer to the adhesive.
    3. Throw away any leftover primer.
    4. Do not apply too much primer.



  1. Using a brush or sponge, apply Primer G to the contact surface of the back window glass.



    Standard:
    Area
    		Specified Condition
    a
    		11.0 mm (0.433 in.)
    b
    		8.0 mm (0.315 in.)
    c
    		13.0 mm (0.512 in.)
    d
    		5.0 mm (0.197 in.)

    HINT:
    If primer is applied to an area that is not specified, wipe off the primer with a non-residue solvent before it dries.

    NOTICE:
    1. Allow the primer to dry for 3 minutes or more.
    2. Throw away any leftover primer.
    3. Do not apply too much primer.



  1. Apply adhesive to the back window glass.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Windshield Glass Adhesive or equivalent


    1. Cut off the tip of the cartridge nozzle as shown in the illustration below.

      HINT:
      After cutting off the tip, use all adhesive within the time written in the table below.

      Usage Time Frame:
      Temperature
      		Usage Time Frame
      35°C (95°F)
      		15 minutes
      20°C (68°F)
      		1 hour 40 minutes
      5°C (41°F)
      		8 hours

    2. Load the sealer gun with the cartridge.



    3. Apply adhesive to the back window glass as shown in the illustration.



      Standard:
      Area
      		Specified Condition
      a
      		12.0 mm (0.472 in.)
      b
      		8.0 mm (0.315 in.)
      c
      		11.0 mm (0.433 in.)
      d
      		13.0 mm (0.512 in.)
      e
      		5.0 mm (0.197 in.)



  1. Install the back window glass to the vehicle body.

    1. Hold the back window glass securely in place with tape or equivalent until the adhesive has hardened.

      NOTICE:
      1. Allow the primer coating to dry for 3 minutes or more.
      2. Check that the clips are attached to the vehicle body correctly.
      3. Check the clearance between the vehicle body and glass.

    2. Lightly press the front surface of the back window glass to ensure that the back window glass is securely fit to the vehicle body.




    3. Using a scraper, remove any excess or protruding adhesive.

      HINT:
      Apply adhesive onto the glass rim.

      NOTICE:
      Do not drive the vehicle within the time written in the table below.

      Minimum time:
      Temperature
      		Minimum Time Prior to Driving Vehicle
      35°C (95°F)
      		1 hour 30 minutes
      20°C (68°F)
      		5 hours
      5°C (41°F)
      		24 hours

    4. Connect the connectors.



8. INSTALL REAR WIPER MOTOR ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Wiper)



  1. Temporarily install the rear wiper motor assembly with the 3 bolts.

  1. Tighten the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    5.5 N*m{ 56 kgf*cm , 49 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the connector.



9. INSTALL REAR WIPER MOTOR GROMMET (w/ Rear Wiper)



  1. Install the rear wiper motor grommet with the position mark facing upward as shown in the illustration.



10. INSTALL REAR WIPER ARM (w/ Rear Wiper)

  1. Operate the rear wiper, and stop the rear wiper motor at the automatic stop position.




  1. Clean the wiper pivot serration with a wire brush.




  1. Set the head of the blade on the defogger line.

  1. Install the nut and rear wiper arm.

    Torque:
    5.5 N*m{ 56 kgf*cm , 49 in.*lbf }

  1. Close the cover.



11. CHECK FOR LEAK AND REPAIR

  1. Conduct a leak test after the adhesive has completely hardened.

  1. Seal any leaks with auto glass sealer.



12. INSTALL REAR SPOILER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Spoiler)



  1. Attach the 3 clips to install the rear spoiler.




  1. Install the 4 bolts.
