DESCRIPTION

The diagnostic trouble code (DTC) P2431 indicates that the pressure sensor of the secondary air injection (AIR) system malfunctions. The engine control module (ECM) monitors the sensor value to detect the malfunction. If the sensor value reaches the criteria (below 45 kPa or higher than 135 kPa), the ECM illuminates the malfunction indicator lamp (MIL), and stores a DTC.
The DTC P2432 and P2433 indicate an open or short circuit in the pressure sensor circuit. The ECM monitors the pressure sensor voltage to detect the open or short circuit. If the voltage reaches the criteria (below 0.1 V or higher than 4.8 V), the ECM illuminates the malfunction indicator lamp (MIL), and stores a DTC.
The pressure sensor detects the air pressure via a piezo element. The ECM applies 5 volts to the sensor, and it is varied with the air pressure. When the pressure is high, the voltage is high. When the pressure is low, the voltage is low.
The secondary air injection (AIR) system introduces fresh air into the exhaust manifold in order to reduce carbon monoxide (CO) and hydrocarbon (HC) in the exhaust emission. This system is activated just after engine start, and is stopped when the catalyst converter is warmed-up. This system consists of the following components: 1) the air pump which sends fresh air to the exhaust manifold, 2) the electric air switching valve (E-ASV) which switches the fresh air flow, 3) the air switching valve (ASV) which switches the air passage to the exhaust manifold of each bank, 4) the vacuum switching valve (VSV) which switches the vacuum (intake manifold pressure) to operate the ASV, 5) the pressure sensor which detects the fresh air pressure and exhaust pulse, 6) the air injection control driver which controls the power sources of the air pump and E-ASV, 7) the engine control module (ECM) which controls and monitors this system.

When troubleshooting, freeze frame data can help determine if the vehicle was running or stopped, if the engine was warmed up or not, if the air-fuel ratio was lean or rich, and other data from the time the malfunction occurred.
