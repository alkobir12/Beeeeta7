POWER BACK DOOR MAIN SWITCH > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE CENTER BACK DOOR GARNISH



  1. Detach the 5 clips and 2 claws, and remove the back door garnish.



2. REMOVE BACK DOOR SIDE GARNISH LH



  1. Detach the 3 clips and remove the back door side garnish.



3. REMOVE BACK DOOR SIDE GARNISH RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE ASSIST GRIP



  1. Remove the 2 screws and assist grip.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 BACK DOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the back door service hole cover.

  1. Disconnect connector.



6. REMOVE POWER BACK DOOR MAIN SWITCH



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the power back door main switch.
