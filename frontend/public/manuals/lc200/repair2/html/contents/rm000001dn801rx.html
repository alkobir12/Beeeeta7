DESCRIPTION

When the engine switch is turned on (IG), battery voltage is applied to terminal IGSW of the ECM. The output signal from the MREL terminal of the ECM causes a current to flow to the coil, closing the contacts of the integration relay (EFI relay) and supplying power to either terminal +B or +B2 of the ECM.
