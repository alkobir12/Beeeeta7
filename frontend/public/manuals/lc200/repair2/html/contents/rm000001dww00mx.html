DTC CHECK / CLEAR (SST CHECK WIRE)



  1. DTC check

    1. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of the DLC3.

      SST
      09843-18040  

    2. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    3. Read the DTCs from the ABS warning light, VSC OFF indicator light*1, or multi-information display*2 on the combination meter.

      HINT:
      1. If no DTC is output, inspect the TC and CG terminal circuit, and the ABS warning light and VSC OFF indicator light*1 circuits.

      2. If 2 or more malfunctions are indicated at the same time, the lowest numbered DTC is output first.
      3. *1: w/o Multi-information Display
      4. *2: w/ Multi-information Display

    5. Refer to the Diagnostic Trouble Code Chart (Click here) for DTC information.

    6. After completing the check, turn the engine switch off and remove SST from the DLC3.



  2. DTC clear




    1. Using SST, connect terminals 13 (TC) and 4 (CG) of the DLC3.

      SST
      09843-18040  

    2. Turn the engine switch on (IG).




    3. Clear the DTCs stored in the ECU by depressing the brake pedal 8 times or more within 5 seconds.

    4. Check that "DIAG VSC OK" is displayed on the multi-information display*1 and check that the ABS warning light and VSC OFF indicator light*2 output a normal system code.

      HINT:
      *1: w/ Multi-information Display
      *2: w/o Multi-information Display

    5. Remove SST from the terminals of the DLC3.

      HINT:
      Clearing the DTCs cannot be performed by disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal or removing the ECU-IG fuse.


DTC CHECK / CLEAR (INTELLIGENT TESTER)

  1. DTC check

    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the intelligent tester on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / DTC.

    5. Read the DTCs by following the directions on the intelligent tester screen.

  2. DTC clear

    1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    2. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    3. Turn the intelligent tester on.

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / DTC.

    5. Clear the DTCs by following the directions on the intelligent tester screen.


END OF DTC CHECK / CLEAR

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  2. Check that the ABS warning light and VSC OFF indicator light* go off within approximately 3 seconds.

    HINT:
    *: w/o Multi-information Display
