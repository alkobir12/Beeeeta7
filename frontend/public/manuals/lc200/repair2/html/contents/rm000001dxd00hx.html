DTC    C1246/46    Master Cylinder Pressure Sensor Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The master cylinder pressure sensor is connected to the skid control ECU in the master cylinder solenoid.



DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C1246/46
When any of the following conditions is detected:
1. All of the following conditions continue for at least 30 seconds.
  1. The vehicle speed is more than 7 km/h (4 mph).
  2. The PM/C1 terminal voltage exceeds 0.86 V.
  3. The master cylinder pressure does not change by more than 0.048 MPa (0.5 kgf/cm2, 7.0 psi).
2. The PM/C1 terminal receives interference at least 7 times within 5 seconds.
3. Both of the following conditions continue for at least 1.2 seconds.
  1. The stop light switch is off.
  2. The PM/C1 terminal voltage is higher than 0.86 V or below 0.3 V.
4. The following condition continues for at least 1.2 seconds.
  1. The PM/C1 terminal voltage is not between 0.19 and 4.57 V.
  1. Master cylinder pressure sensor
  2. Stop light switch
  3. Harness or connector
  4. Master cylinder solenoid
