DTC    C1202/58    Brake Fluid Level Low / Open Circuit in Brake Fluid Level Warning Switch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The brake fluid level warning switch sends the appropriate signal to the skid control ECU when the brake fluid level drops.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
C1202/58
When either of the following conditions is detected:
  1. The fluid level of the reservoir is LOW for 4 seconds, or 40 seconds after the engine switch is turned on (IG), or for 7 seconds during pump motor operation.
  2. With the ECU terminal IG1 voltage at 9.5 V to 17.0 V, an open in the brake fluid level warning switch circuit continues for 2 seconds or more.
  1. Brake fluid level
  2. Brake fluid level warning switch
  3. Harness or connector
  4. Master cylinder solenoid (Skid control ECU)

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

NOTICE:
When replacing the master cylinder solenoid, perform zero point calibration (Click here).

HINT:
When C1241/41 is output together with C1202/58, inspect and repair the trouble areas indicated by C1241/41 first.

1.CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEVEL

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Depress the brake pedal 20 times or more (until the pedal reaction feels light and pedal stroke becomes longer).

  1. Check the amount of fluid in the brake reservoir.

    HINT:
    When the engine switch is turned on (IG), brake fluid is sent to the accumulator and the fluid level decreases by approximately 10 mm (0.394 in.) from the level when the engine switch is off (normal).

    OK:
    Brake fluid level is normal.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    HINT:

    2. The warning switch is switched on/off at +/-5 mm (+/-0.197 in.) from the MIN level.
