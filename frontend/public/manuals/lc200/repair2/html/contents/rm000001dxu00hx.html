ANTI-LOCK BRAKE SYSTEM > TC and CG Terminal Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

DTC output mode is set by connecting terminals TC and CG of the DLC3.
The DTCs are output by the blinking of the ABS warning light.

WIRING DIAGRAM



HINT:
When each warning light stays blinking, a ground short in the wiring of terminal TC of the DLC3 or an internal ground short in each ECU is suspected.
