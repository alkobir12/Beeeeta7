AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM > PTC Heater Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

PTC heater relays are closed in accordance with signals from the air conditioning amplifier assembly and power is supplied to the quick heater assembly installed on the radiator heater unit.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT PTC HEATER RELAY (PTC HTR1)

  1. Remove the PTC HTR1 relay from the engine room relay block.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    3 - 5
    		Battery voltage not applied between terminals 1 and 2
    		10 kΩ or higher
    Battery voltage applied to terminals 1 and 2
    		Below 1 Ω
