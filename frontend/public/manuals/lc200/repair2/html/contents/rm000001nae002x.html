INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK OPERATION

  1. Press "EJECT" and check the operation.

    1. Press the cassette tape EJECT switch of the radio receiver assembly for 2 seconds or more and check that the cassette tape is ejected.

      OK:
      The cassette tape is ejected.
