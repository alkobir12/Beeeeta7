LIGHTING SYSTEM > Back-up Light Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The park/neutral position switch turns on when the shift lever is moved to R, causing the back-up lights to illuminate.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT FUSE (BK/UP LP)

  1. Remove the BK/UP LP fuse from the instrument panel junction block.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    BK/UP LP fuse
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω



NG
REPLACE FUSE
OK


2.INSPECT REAR COMBINATION LIGHT



  1. Remove the rear combination light (Click here).

  1. Apply battery voltage to the terminals of the connector, and check the LED illumination condition.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 3 (T/P)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 1 (E)
    		Light illuminates

4.INSPECT PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH

  1. Remove the park/neutral position switch (Click here).

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Connection
    2 (RB) - 1 (RL)
    		Shift lever in R
    		Below 1 Ω
    2 (RB) - 1 (RL)
    		Shift lever not in R
    		10 kΩ or higher



NG
REPLACE PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH (Click here)
OK


5.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (PARK/NEUTRAL POSITION SWITCH - BODY GROUND)

  1. Disconnect the C24 park/neutral position switch connector.

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    C24-2 (RB) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK
