BRAKE PEDAL (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



2. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the under cover and disconnect the connectors.



4. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



5. REMOVE DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and driver side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



6. REMOVE PUSH ROD PIN



  1. Remove the clip and push rod pin from the brake pedal lever.



7. REMOVE STOP LIGHT SWITCH ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the stop light switch connector.

  1. Remove the stop light switch (Click here).



8. REMOVE BRAKE PEDAL SUPPORT SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt from the brake pedal support reinforcement.




  1. Remove the 4 nuts and brake pedal support sub-assembly.
