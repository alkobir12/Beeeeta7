TRANSFER ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN TRANSFER OIL



  1. except 1GR-FE:
    Remove the 2 bolts and transfer dynamic damper.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and transfer heat insulator.




  1. Remove the 7 bolts and lower transfer case protector.




  1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.




  1. Remove the drain plug and gasket, and drain the transfer oil.



2. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION WITH TRANSFER ASSEMBLY (for A750F)

  1. for 1GR-FE:
    Remove the automatic transmission with transfer assembly (Click here).

  1. for 2UZ-FE:
    Remove the automatic transmission with transfer assembly (Click here).



3. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION WITH TRANSFER ASSEMBLY (for AB60F)

  1. Remove the automatic transmission with transfer assembly (Click here).



4. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION WITH TRANSFER ASSEMBLY (for H150F)

  1. Remove the manual transmission with transfer assembly (Click here).



5. REMOVE TRANSFER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 8 transfer adaptor rear mounting bolts.

  1. Pull the transfer straight up and remove it from the transmission.

    NOTICE:
    Take care not to damage the adaptor oil seal with the transfer input shaft spline.
