THEFT DETERRENT SYSTEM > Intrusion Sensor Cancel Switch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

  1. When the intrusion sensor cancel switch on the map light is pressed, the sensor off signal is sent to the certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) which causes the intrusion sensor to stop operating.

  1. Turn the intrusion sensor off by pressing the intrusion sensor cancel switch. Check if the switch operates normally by checking that the theft deterrent system does not operate when you move your hand in front of the sensor.
