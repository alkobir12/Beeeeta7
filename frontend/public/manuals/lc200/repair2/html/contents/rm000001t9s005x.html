1. INSPECT HEADLIGHT CLEANER CONTROL RELAY



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    6 (PB)- 4 (E)
    		When battery voltage is not applied between terminals 3 (IG) and 2 (H)
    		10 kΩ or higher
    When battery voltage is applied between terminals 3 (IG) and 2 (H)
    		Below 1 Ω (0.8 to 0.9 seconds, then no continuity exists)

    If the result is not as specified, replace the headlight cleaner control relay.
