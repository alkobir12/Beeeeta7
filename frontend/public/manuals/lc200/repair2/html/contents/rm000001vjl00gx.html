CHECK SLIDING ROOF DRIVE GEAR SUB-ASSEMBLY (SLIDING ROOF ECU)


  1. Disconnect the R5 ECU connector.

  2. Measure the resistance and voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbols)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    R5-1 (B) - R5-2 (E)
    		L - W-B
    		+B power supply
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    R5-5 (IG) - R5-2 (E)
    		G - W-B
    		Power supply
    		Engine switch off
    		Below 1 V
    R5-5 (IG) - R5-2 (E)
    		G - W-B
    		Power supply
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    R5-7 (OPN) - R5-2 (E)
    		P - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor open
    		OPEN switch off
    		10 kΩ or higher
    R5-7 (OPN) - R5-2 (E)
    		P - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor open
    		 OPEN switch on
    		Below 1 Ω
    R5-9 (CLS) - R5-2 (E)
    		G - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor close
    		CLOSE switch off
    		10 kΩ or higher
    R5-9 (CLS) - R5-2 (E)
    		G - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor close
    		CLOSE switch on
    		Below 1 Ω
    R5-10 (UP) - R5-2 (E)
    		R - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor up
    		UP switch off
    		10 kΩ or higher
    R5-10 (UP) - R5-2 (E)
    		R - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor up
    		UP switch on
    		Below 1 Ω
    R5-8 (DWN) - R5-2 (E)
    		R - W-B
    		Sliding roof motor down
    		DOWN switch off
    		10 kΩ or higher
    R5-8 (DWN) - R5-2 (E)

    R5-2 (E) - Body ground

    		Always
