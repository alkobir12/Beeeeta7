AUDIO AND VISUAL SYSTEM (w/o Multi-display) > Steering Pad Switch Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This circuit sends an operation signal from the steering pad switch to the radio receiver.
If there is a short in the circuit, the resulting condition is the same as if the switch were continuously depressed.
