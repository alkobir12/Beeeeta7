AUDIO AND VISUAL SYSTEM (w/o Multi-display) > Mute Signal Circuit between Radio Receiver and Stereo Component Amplifier

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This circuit sends a signal to the stereo component amplifier to mute noise. Because of that, the noise produced by changing the sound source ceases.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (RADIO RECEIVER - STEREO COMPONENT AMPLIFIER)



  1. Disconnect the F1 receiver connector.

  1. Disconnect the F18 amplifier connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F1-7 (MUTE) - F18-1 (MUTE)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    F1-7 (MUTE) - Body Ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
