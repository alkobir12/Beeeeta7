THEFT DETERRENT SYSTEM > Glass Breakage Sensor Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When the glass breakage sensor detects that the back door glass, quarter window glass LH or quarter window glass RH is tapped or broken, the sensor will set off the alarm for 30 seconds.
