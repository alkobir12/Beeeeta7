1. INSPECT PTC HEATER ASSEMBLY



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A-1 - B-1
    		23 to 25°C (73 to 77°F)
    		0.5 to 2.5 Ω
    A-2 - B-1
    		23 to 25°C (73 to 77°F)
    		0.5 to 2.5 Ω
    A-2 - B-2
    		23 to 25°C (73 to 77°F)
    		0.5 to 2.5 Ω
    A-3 - B-2
    		23 to 25°C (73 to 77°F)
    		0.5 to 2.5 Ω

    1. If the result is not as specified, replace the PTC heater assembly.
