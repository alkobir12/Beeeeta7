DTC    74-40    Short in Speaker Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION


DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
74-40
Short is detected in the speaker output circuit.
  1. Harness or connector
  2. Speaker
  3. Stereo component amplifier assembly

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
After the inspection is completed, clear the DTCs.

1.CHECK DTC

  1. Disconnect the F3 and F37 amplifier connectors.

  1. Clear the DTCs.

  1. Check if DTC 74-40 is output.

    OK:
    DTC 74-40 is not output.
