DTC    B1422/22    Compressor Lock Sensor Circuit

for Preparation Click here


SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

The ECM sends the engine speed signal to the air conditioning amplifier assembly via CAN communication.
The air conditioning amplifier assembly reads the difference between compressor speed and engine speed. When the difference becomes too large, the air conditioning amplifier assembly determines that the compressor is locked, and turns the magnetic clutch off.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1422/22
An open or short in the compressor lock sensor circuit.
  1. Cooler compressor assembly (A/C lock sensor)
  2. Compressor drive belt
  3. Harness or connector between cooler compressor assembly and air conditioning amplifier assembly
  4. Air conditioning amplifier assembly
  5. CAN communication line
