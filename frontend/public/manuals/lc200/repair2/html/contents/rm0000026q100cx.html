STARTER (for 2.0 kW Type) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



4. REMOVE STARTER ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the terminal cap.




  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the starter wire.

  1. Disconnect the starter connector.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and starter.
