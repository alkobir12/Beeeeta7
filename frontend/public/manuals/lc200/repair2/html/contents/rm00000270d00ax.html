REAR AXLE SHAFT > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL REAR AXLE SHAFT OIL SEAL LH



  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in a new oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Do not allow foreign matter, etc. to contact the axle shaft housing hole.

    SST
    09950-60020   (09951-00770)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)



2. INSTALL REAR AXLE SHAFT LH

  1. Install the O-ring to the axle housing.




  1. Install the rear axle shaft and parking brake plate with the 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    60 N*m{ 612 kgf*cm , 44 ft.*lbf }



3. CONNECT REAR SPEED SENSOR LH



  1. Install the speed sensor with the nut.

    Torque:
    8.3 N*m{ 85 kgf*cm , 73 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure there are no pieces of iron or other foreign matter attached to the sensor tip.
    2. While inserting the speed sensor into the knuckle hole, do not strike or damage the sensor tip.
    3. After installing the speed sensor, make sure there is no clearance or foreign matter between the sensor stay part and the knuckle.
    4. Make sure there is no foreign matter attached to the speed sensor rotor.



4. CONNECT NO. 3 PARKING BRAKE CABLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the No. 3 parking brake cable with the bolt.

    Torque:
    8.0 N*m{ 82 kgf*cm , 71 in.*lbf }



5. INSTALL PARKING BRAKE SHOE LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Apply high temperature grease to the parking brake anchor block.

  1. Install the parking brake shoe lever to the No. 3 parking brake cable.

    NOTICE:
    Be carefully to distinguish between the parking brake shoe lever RH and LH.



6. INSTALL NO. 2 PARKING BRAKE SHOE ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Apply high temperature grease to the areas of the backing plate that contact the shoe.




  1. Using SST, install the No. 2 parking brake shoe with the shoe hold down spring pin, compression spring and shoe hold down spring cup.

    SST
    09718-00011  



7. INSTALL NO. 1 PARKING BRAKE SHOE ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Apply high temperature grease to the areas of the backing plate that contact the shoe.




  1. Apply high temperature grease to the thread and all joining areas of the parking brake shoe adjuster screw set.




  1. Set the No. 1 parking brake shoe and shoe adjuster screw set in place.




  1. Connect the tension spring.




  1. Using SST, install the shoe hold down spring pin, compression spring and shoe hold down spring cup.

    SST
    09718-00011  



8. INSTALL PARKING BRAKE SHOE RETURN TENSION SPRING LH



  1. Using SST, install the shoe return spring.

    SST
    09703-30011  



9. CHECK PARKING BRAKE INSTALLATION


  1. Check that each part is installed properly.





10. INSTALL REAR DISC LH



  1. Align the matchmarks, and then install the rear disc.

    HINT:
    When replacing the rear disc with a new one, select the installation position where the rear disc has the minimum runout.



11. CONNECT REAR DISC BRAKE CYLINDER ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Connect the rear disc brake cylinder and install 2 new bolts.

    Torque:
    95 N*m{ 969 kgf*cm , 70 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not twist the flexible hose.
    2. Make sure that the bolts are free from damage and foreign matter.
    3. Do not overtighten the bolts.



12. CONNECT REAR BRAKE FLEXIBLE HOSE

  1. Set the flexible hose to the connecting point with the brake tube, and then install a new clip.




  1. Using SST, connect the flexible hose to the brake tube while holding the flexible hose with a wrench.

    Torque:
    without union nut wrench:
    15 N*m{ 155 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
    with union nut wrench:
    14 N*m{ 145 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the brake tube.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the brake tube from the connecting point.



13. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



14. FILL RESERVOIR WITH BRAKE FLUID

Fluid:
SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT3



15. BLEED AIR FROM BRAKE LINE


  1. Bleed air from the front brake system.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

      HINT:
      Perform this procedure with the engine switch on (IG), as the large piston of the master cylinder does not stroke with the engine switch off.




    2. Connect a vinyl tube to the brake caliper.

    3. Depress the brake pedal several times, then loosen the bleeder plug with the pedal held down (procedure "A").

      NOTICE:
      While performing the air bleeding procedure, make sure that the amount of fluid in the reservoir remains above the MIN line.

    4. At the point when the fluid stops coming out, tighten the bleeder plug, then release the brake pedal (procedure "B").

      Torque:
      11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

    5. Repeat procedure "A" to "B" until all the air in the fluid has been bled out.

    6. Repeat the above procedures to bleed the other front brake line.



  1. Bleed air from the rear brake system.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and depress the brake pedal.




    2. Connect a vinyl tube to the brake caliper.

    3. Loosen the bleeder plug and release air.

      NOTICE:
      While performing the air bleeding procedure, make sure that the amount of fluid in the reservoir remains above the MIN line.

      HINT:
      Brake fluid is sent through the pump, so keep the brake pedal depressed until the air is completely bled out.

    4. When the air is completely bled out of the brake fluid through the bleeder plug, tighten the bleeder plug.

      Torque:
      11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

    5. Repeat the above procedures to bleed the other rear brake line.

  1. Connect the intelligent tester.

    1. Turn the engine switch off.

    2. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    3. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    4. Enter the following menus: Chassis / ABS/VSC/TRC / Utility / Air Bleeding.

      HINT:
      Please refer to the intelligent tester operator's manual for further details.

  1. Bleed front right brake line.

    1. Select "FR Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "FR Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, depress the brake pedal and hold it to bleed the front right brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed front left brake line.

    1. Select "FL Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "FL Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, depress the brake pedal and hold it to bleed the front left brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed rear right brake line.

    1. Select "RR Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "RR Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, bleed the left brake line.

    3. Repeat the step above until there are no more air bubbles in the fluid.

  1. Bleed rear left brake line.

    1. Select "RL Line" on the intelligent tester.

    2. With "RL Line" turned on with the intelligent tester, bleed the left brake line.

  1. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.

  1. Clear the DTCs (Click here).



16. CHECK BRAKE FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR



  1. When inspecting or refilling with the engine switch off, depress the brake pedal 40 times or more (there will be less pedal resistance and the pedal stroke will lengthen), and then add fluid until the fluid level is at the MAX line.

    HINT:
    When the engine switch is turned on (IG), fluid is sent to the accumulator causing the fluid level to decrease 10 mm (0.394 in.) from the level when the engine switch is off (this is normal).

    Brake Fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS NO. 116 DOT3



17. CHECK FOR BRAKE FLUID LEAK

19. INSPECT DIFFERENTIAL OIL



  1. Add differential oil so that the oil level is between 0 to 5 mm (0 to 0.197 in.) from the bottom lip of the differential filler plug hole.

    NOTICE:
    1. Too much or too little oil will lead to differential problems.
    2. After changing the oil, drive the vehicle and then check the oil level again.

    Front differential oil type and viscosity:
    Toyota Genuine Differential gear oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent

    Rear Differential Oil Type and Viscosity:
    Item
    		Oil Type and Viscosity
    w/o LSD
    		Toyota Genuine Differential gear oil LT 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent
    w/ LSD
    		Toyota Genuine Differential gear oil LX 75W-85 GL-5 or equivalent

    Front differential capacity:
    1.85 to 1.95 liters (1.96 to 2.06 US qts., 1.63 to 1.71 Imp. qts.)

    Rear Differential Capacity:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    Standard
    		4.15 to 4.25 liters (4.39 to 4.49 US qts., 3.66 to 3.74 Imp. qts.)
    w/ LSD
    		4.10 to 4.20 liters (4.34 to 4.43 US qts., 3.60 to 3.69 Imp. qts.)
    w/ Differential lock

  1. for Front Differential:

    1. Using a 10 mm hexagon wrench, install a new gasket and the filler plug.

      Torque:
      39 N*m{ 400 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

    2. Install the engine under cover.

  1. for Rear Differential:

    1. Install a new gasket and the filler plug.

      Torque:
      49 N*m{ 500 kgf*cm , 36 ft.*lbf }

21. CHECK PARKING BRAKE LEVER TRAVEL

  1. Fully pull the parking brake lever to engage the parking brake.

  1. Release the lever to disengage the parking brake.

  1. Slowly pull the parking brake lever all the way, and count the number of clicks.

    Standard Parking Brake Lever Travel when Pulled with a Force of 200 N (20 kgf, 45 lbf):
    5 to 7 clicks

    If the parking brake lever travel is not as specified, adjust the parking brake shoe clearance and parking brake lever travel.



22. ADJUST PARKING BRAKE LEVER TRAVEL

  1. Remove the console cup holder box sub-assembly (Click here).

  1. Completely release the parking brake lever.

  1. Loosen the adjusting nut to completely release the parking brake cable.




  1. Temporarily install the hub nuts.

  1. Remove the hole plug.




  1. Insert an adjustment tool into the adjustment hole of the disc. Rotate the adjustment wheel in the "X" direction until the shoes are locked. Then rotate the adjustment wheel in the "Y" direction 8 notches.

  1. Check that the disc can be rotated lightly. If not, rotate the adjustment wheel in the "Y" direction and check again.

  1. Install the hole plug.

  1. Remove the hub nuts.




  1. Turn the adjusting nut until the parking brake lever travel becomes correct.

    Standard Parking Brake Lever Travel when Pulled with a Force of 200 N (20 kgf, 45 lbf):
    5 to 7 clicks

  1. Operate the parking brake lever 3 to 4 times, and check the parking brake lever travel.

    Standard Parking Brake Lever Travel when Pulled with a Force of 200 N (20 kgf, 45 lbf):
    5 to 7 clicks

  1. Check whether the parking brake drags or not.

  1. When operating the parking brake lever, check that the brake warning light comes on.

    Standard Condition:
    The brake warning light always illuminates at the first click.

  1. Install the console cup holder box sub-assembly (Click here).



23. CHECK SRS WARNING LIGHT

  1. Check the SRS warning light (Click here).



24. CHECK SPEED SENSOR SIGNAL

  1. Check the speed sensor signal (Click here).



25. MEASURE VEHICLE HEIGHT (w/ KDSS)

NOTICE:
  1. Perform the inspection on a level surface.
  2. Perform the inspection with the vehicle empty.
  3. Ensure that the wheels are on the ground and facing straight ahead.
  4. Perform the inspection with the vehicle load completely on the suspension.

  1. Set the tire pressure to the specified value(s) (Click here).

  1. Bounce the vehicle to stabilize the suspension.




  1. Measure the distance from the ground to the top of the bumper and calculate the difference in the vehicle height between left and right. Perform this procedure for both the front and rear wheels.

    Height difference of left and right sides:
    15 mm (0.591 in.) or less

    HINT:
    If not as specified, perform the vehicle tilt calibration.



26. CLOSE STABILIZER CONTROL WITH ACCUMULATOR HOUSING SHUTTER VALVE (w/ KDSS)

NOTICE:
  1. Perform the inspection on a level surface.
  2. Perform the inspection with the vehicle empty.
  3. Ensure that the wheels are on the ground and facing straight ahead.
  4. Perform the inspection with the vehicle load completely on the suspension.

  1. Using a 5 mm hexagon socket wrench, tighten the lower and upper chamber shutter valves of the stabilizer control with accumulator housing.

    Torque:
    14 N*m{ 143 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }



27. INSTALL STABILIZER CONTROL VALVE PROTECTOR (w/ KDSS)



  1. Install the valve protector with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Attach the clamp, and connect the connector to the valve protector.
