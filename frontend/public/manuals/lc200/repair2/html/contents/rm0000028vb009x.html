1. INSTALL FUEL INJECTOR ASSEMBLY

NOTICE:
  1. Be careful not to drop the injectors when installing the delivery pipes.
  2. Do not apply any load to the injector in the horizontal direction.




  1. Install a new grommet to each injector.

  1. Apply a light coat of gasoline to each new O-ring and install one to each injector.




  1. Install the 8 injectors.

    1. While turning each injector clockwise and counterclockwise, push it onto the delivery pipes.

    2. Position the injector connectors outward.

  1. Place 8 new insulators on the intake manifold.



2. INSTALL FUEL DELIVERY PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY

NOTICE:
Be careful not to drop the injectors when installing the delivery pipes.




  1. Place the 2 delivery pipes and injector assemblies on the lower intake manifold.

  1. Temporarily install the 4 nuts.

  1. Install the No. 1 fuel pipe and 4 new gaskets with the bolt and 2 union bolts.

    Torque:
    for union bolt:
    39 N*m{ 398 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }
    for bolt:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }




  1. Connect the No. 3 fuel pipe with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }




  1. Connect the fuel return hose to the fuel pressure regulator.




  1. Check that the injectors rotate smoothly.

    HINT:
    If the injectors do not rotate smoothly, a probable cause is incorrect installation of the O-rings. Replace the O-ring of any injector that does not rotate smoothly.

  1. Position the injector connectors outward.

  1. Tighten the 4 nuts holding the delivery pipes to the lower intake manifold.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



3. INSTALL VACUUM CONTROL VALVE SET (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install the vacuum control valve set with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 3 vacuum hoses.

  1. Connect the 2 VSV connectors.



4. CONNECT ENGINE WIRE



  1. Install the engine wire protector with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    8.2 N*m{ 84 kgf*cm , 73 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 8 injector connectors.




  1. Connect the 2 wire clamps to the wire clamp brackets on the delivery pipe.




  1. Connect the engine wire to the wire bracket and engine hanger.

  1. Connect the VSV connector (for ACIS).



5. INSTALL PURGE VSV



  1. Install the purge VSV to the intake manifold with the bolt.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the purge line hose.

  1. Connect the purge VSV connector.



6. INSTALL NO. 1 VENTILATION HOSE



  1. Install the No. 1 ventilation hose to the intake manifold and ventilation valve.



7. INSTALL NO. 2 VENTILATION HOSE



  1. w/ Secondary Air Injection System:

    1. Install the vacuum transmitting hose to the fuel pressure regulator.

    2. Install the clamp.

    3. Install the No. 2 ventilation hose to the cylinder head cover.




  1. w/o Secondary Air Injection System:

    1. Install the vacuum transmitting hose to the fuel pressure regulator.

    2. Install the No. 2 ventilation hose to the cylinder head cover.



8. INSTALL NO. 2 FUEL HOSE


  1. for engine side:




    1. Connect the fuel hose to the No. 3 fuel pipe.

      HINT:
      Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

      NOTICE:
      1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
      2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.

    2. Attach the lock claws to the connector by pushing down on the cover, as shown in the illustration.

    3. Attach the fuel hose clamp.



  1. for body side:




    1. Connect the fuel hose to the fuel pipe.

      HINT:
      Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

      NOTICE:
      1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
      2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.



9. CONNECT FUEL HOSE



  1. Connect the fuel hose.

    HINT:
    Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

    NOTICE:
    1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
    2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.

  1. Install the fuel hose clamp.



10. INSTALL NO. 2 WATER BY-PASS PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the No. 2 water by-pass pipe with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the 4 water hoses.



11. INSTALL NO. 2 V-BANK COVER BRACKET



  1. Install the bracket with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }



12. INSTALL AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the air cleaner hose so that the protrusion of the air cleaner cap aligns with the groove of the hose as shown in the illustration.

  1. Tighten the 2 clamps.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the vacuum transmitting hose.

  1. Connect the No. 2 ventilation hose.



13. INSTALL V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Attach the 2 V-bank cover hooks to the bracket.

  1. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

15. ADD ENGINE COOLANT

  1. Add engine coolant.

    Standard Capacity:
    Item
    		Specified Condition
    w/o Transmission oil cooler
    		w/o Rear Heater
    		13.4 liters (14.2 US qts, 11.8 Imp. qts)
    w/ Rear Heater
    		16.3 liters (17.2 US qts, 14.3 Imp. qts)
    w/ Transmission oil cooler
    		w/o Rear Heater
    		14.1 liters (14.9 US qts, 12.4 Imp. qts)
    w/ Rear Heater
    		16.9 liters (17.9 US qts, 14.9 Imp. qts)

    HINT:
    TOYOTA vehicles are filled with TOYOTA SLLC at the factory. In order to avoid damage to the engine cooling system and other technical problems, only use TOYOTA SLLC or similar high quality ethylene glycol based non-silicate, non-amine, non-nitrite, non-borate coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology (coolant with long-life hybrid organic acid technology consists of a combination of low phosphates and organic acids).




  1. Slowly pour coolant into the radiator reservoir until it reaches the F line.

  1. Install the radiator reservoir cap.

  1. Press the inlet and outlet radiator hoses several times by hand, and then check the coolant level. If the coolant level is low, add coolant.

  1. Install the radiator cap.

  1. Start the engine and warm it up until the thermostat opens.

    NOTICE:
    Switch off the A/C.

  1. Maintain the engine speed at 2000 to 2500 rpm.

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure that the radiator reservoir still has some coolant in it.
    2. Pay attention to the needle of the water temperature meter. Make sure that the needle does not show an abnormally high temperature.
    3. If there is not enough coolant, the engine may burn out or overheat.
    4. Immediately after starting the engine, if the radiator reservoir does not have any coolant, perform the following: 1) stop the engine, 2) wait until the coolant has cooled down, and 3) add coolant until the coolant is filled to the F line.
    5. Run the engine at 2000 rpm until the coolant level has stabilized.

  1. Press the inlet and outlet radiator hoses several times by hand to bleed air.

    CAUTION:
    1. Wear protective gloves.
    2. Be careful as the radiator hoses are hot.
    3. Keep your hands away from the cooling fan.

  1. Stop the engine, and wait until the engine coolant cools down to the ambient temperature.

    CAUTION:
    Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Check that the coolant level is between the F and L lines. If the coolant level is below the L line, repeat all of the procedures above. If the coolant level is above the F line, drain coolant so that the coolant level is between the F and L lines.



16. INSPECT FOR COOLANT LEAKS



  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and attach a radiator cap tester.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Using the radiator cap tester, increase the pressure inside the radiator to 123 kPa (1.3 kgf/cm2, 18 psi), and check that the pressure does not drop.
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks. If no external leaks are found, check the heater core, cylinder block and head.



17. INSPECT FOR FUEL LEAK

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and intelligent tester ON.

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine.

  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Active Test / Activate the Fuel Pump Speed Control.

  1. Check that there are no fuel leaks after doing maintenance anywhere on the fuel system.
