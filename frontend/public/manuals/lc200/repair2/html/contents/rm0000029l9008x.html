1. INSPECT AIR PUMP



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		20°C (68°F)
    		0.4 to 1.0 Ω
    1 - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 - Body ground

    If the result is not as specified, replace the air pump.




  1. Check operation of the air pump.

    1. Check that air does not flow from port A to port B.
      If the result is not as specified, replace the air pump.




    2. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the air pump operation.

      NOTICE:
      1. Do not operate the air pump continuously for 2 minutes or more.
      2. When operating the air pump, wait 5 minutes until the pump has cooled down between each operation. If the pump has not cooled down sufficiently, the motor will burn out.

      OK:
      Measurement Condition
      		Specified Condition
      Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1
      Battery negative (-) → Terminal 5
      		Air flows from port A to port B

      If the result is not as specified, replace the air pump.
