AIR SWITCHING VALVE (w/ Secondary Air Injection System) > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Measure the resistance of the valve.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1- 2
      		20°C (68°F)
      		4.5 to 5.5 Ω
      1 - Body ground
      		Always
      		1 MΩ or higher
      2 - Body ground
      		Always
      		1 MΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the air switching valve assembly.



  1. Check the valve operation.




    1. Blow air into port A and check that air is not discharged from port B.




    2. Apply battery voltage across the terminals.

    3. Blow air into port A and check that air is discharged from port B.
      If the result is not as specified, replace the air switching valve assembly.



2. INSPECT NO. 1 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Blow air into port B and check that air is not discharged from port C.




  1. Apply vacuum of 33 kPa (250 mmHg, 9.83 in.Hg) to port A, blow air into port B and check that air is discharged from port C.
    If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 1 air switching valve assembly.



3. INSPECT NO. 2 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Blow air into port B and check that air is not discharged from port C.




  1. Apply vacuum of 33 kPa (250 mmHg, 9.83 in.Hg) to port A, blow air into port B and check that air is discharged from port C.
    If the result is not as specified, replace the No. 2 air switching valve assembly.
