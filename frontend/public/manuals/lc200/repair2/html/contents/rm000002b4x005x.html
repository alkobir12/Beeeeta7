REAR CRANKSHAFT OIL SEAL > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY (for Automatic Transmission)

  1. Remove the automatic transmission (Click here).



2. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transmission)

  1. Remove the manual transmission (Click here).



3. REMOVE DRIVE PLATE AND RING GEAR SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Automatic Transmission)



  1. Using SST, hold the crankshaft.

    SST
    09213-54015   (91651-60855)
    09330-00021  




  1. Remove the 8 bolts, rear spacer, drive plate and front spacer.

    NOTICE:
    Do not reuse the bolts.



4. REMOVE CLUTCH COVER ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Put matchmarks on the clutch cover and flywheel.

  1. Loosen each set bolt one turn at a time until spring tension is released.

  1. Remove the 6 set bolts, and pull off the clutch cover.

    NOTICE:
    Do not drop the clutch disc.



5. REMOVE CLUTCH DISC ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transmission)

NOTICE:
Keep the lining part of the clutch disc, pressure plate and surface of the flywheel away from oil and foreign matter.



6. REMOVE FLYWHEEL SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Using SST, hold the crankshaft.

    SST
    09213-54015   (91651-60855)
    09330-00021  




  1. Remove the 8 bolts and flywheel.

    NOTICE:
    Do not reuse the bolts.



7. REMOVE REAR CRANKSHAFT OIL SEAL



  1. Using a knife, cut off the lip of the oil seal.

  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the crankshaft. Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
