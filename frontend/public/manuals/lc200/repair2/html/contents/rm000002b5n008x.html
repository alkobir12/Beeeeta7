OIL PUMP > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT OIL PUMP RELIEF VALVE



  1. Coat the relief valve with engine oil and drop it into the relief valve hole.

  1. Check that the relief valve falls in smoothly by its own weight.
    If it does not, replace the relief valve. If necessary, replace the oil pump assembly.



2. INSPECT OIL PUMP ROTOR SET



  1. Apply engine oil to the oil pump rotor set.

  1. Install the oil pump rotor set to the oil pump body and check that it rotates smoothly.

    HINT:
    Face the marks on the oil pump rotor set towards the oil pump cover side.




  1. Inspect the rotor tip clearance.

    1. Using a feeler gauge, measure the clearance between the drive and driven rotor tips.

      Standard tip clearance:
      0.06 to 0.18 mm (0.00236 to 0.00709 in.)

      Maximum tip clearance:
      0.18 mm (0.00709 in.)

      If the tip clearance is more than the maximum, replace the oil pump assembly.




  1. Inspect the rotor side clearance.

    1. Using a feeler gauge and precision straightedge, measure the clearance between the rotors and precision straightedge.

      Standard side clearance:
      0.03 to 0.09 mm (0.00118 to 0.00354 in.)

      Maximum side clearance:
      0.09 mm (0.00354 in.)

      If the side clearance is more than the maximum, replace the oil pump assembly.




  1. Inspect the rotor body clearance.

    1. Using a feeler gauge, measure the clearance between the driven rotor and body.

      Standard body clearance:
      0.250 to 0.325 mm (0.00984 to 0.0128 in.)

      Maximum body clearance:
      0.325 mm (0.0128 in.)

      If the body clearance is more than the maximum, replace the oil pump assembly.
