CYLINDER HEAD > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE VALVE

HINT:
Arrange the valves, inner compression springs, valve spring retainers and valve spring retainer locks in the correct order.




  1. Place the cylinder head on a wooden block.

  1. Using SST, compress the inner compression spring and remove the valve spring retainer lock.

    SST
    09202-70020   (09202-00010)

  1. Remove the inner compression spring, valve spring retainer and valve.



2. REMOVE VALVE STEM OIL SEAL



  1. Using pliers, remove the valve stem oil seal.



3. REMOVE VALVE SPRING SEAT PLATE WASHER



  1. Using compressed air and a magnetic finger, remove the valve spring seat plate washer by blowing air.



4. REMOVE NOZZLE HOLDER CLAMP SEAT
