1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



6. REMOVE V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



7. REMOVE AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the vacuum transmitting hose and No. 2 ventilation hose.

  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps.

  1. Remove the air cleaner hose.



8. REMOVE AIR CLEANER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the clamp and MAF connector.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and air cleaner.



9. REMOVE FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT



  1. Loosen the belt tension by turning the belt tensioner counterclockwise, and then remove the fan and generator V belt.

    HINT:
    The pulley bolt for the belt tensioner has left-hand thread.



10. REMOVE NO. 2 FUEL HOSE


  1. for engine side:




    1. Detach the fuel hose clamp.

    2. Detach the lock claw by lifting up the cover, as shown in the illustration.

    3. Pinch and pull the No. 2 fuel hose connector to disconnect the connector from the No. 3 fuel pipe.

      NOTICE:
      1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
      2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
      3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
      4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
      5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
      6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.



  1. for body side:




    1. Pinch and pull the No. 2 fuel hose connector to disconnect the connector from the fuel pipe.

      NOTICE:
      1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
      2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
      3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
      4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
      5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
      6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.



11. DISCONNECT FUEL HOSE



  1. Remove the fuel hose clamp.

  1. Pinch and pull the fuel hose connector to disconnect the connector from the fuel pipe.

    NOTICE:
    1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
    2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
    3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
    4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
    5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
    6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.



12. REMOVE FUEL PRESSURE PULSATION DAMPER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the fuel injector connector.

  1. Remove the pulsation damper, upper gasket, fuel hose and lower gasket.

    NOTICE:
    1. Put a cloth or equivalent under the delivery pipe.
    2. Slowly loosen the pulsation damper.



13. DISCONNECT WIRE HARNESS



  1. Disconnect the 2 air injection control driver connectors.

  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the ground wire.

  1. Disconnect the wire harness clamp.




  1. Disconnect the 2 clamps and 2 connectors from the engine room relay block.

  1. Disconnect connector A from the engine room relay block.

  1. Disconnect the wire harness clamp from the cylinder head cover LH.



14. REMOVE IGNITION COIL ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 8 ignition coil connectors.

  1. Remove the 8 bolts and pull out the 8 ignition coils.



15. REMOVE SPARK PLUG

  1. Remove the 8 spark plugs.



16. DISCONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/ Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the water by-pass pipe from the cylinder head cover RH.



17. REMOVE CYLINDER HEAD COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 9 bolts, cylinder head cover and gasket.



18. REMOVE CYLINDER HEAD COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 9 bolts, cylinder head cover and gasket.



19. REMOVE SPARK PLUG TUBE GASKET



  1. Bend the 8 ventilation case claws of the cylinder head cover to an angle of 90° or more.

  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the gaskets.

    NOTICE:
    1. Be careful not to damage the cylinder head cover.
    2. Be careful not to damage the gasket when removing it, as the removed gasket needs to be reused when installing a new one.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



20. REMOVE SEMICIRCULAR PLUG

  1. Remove the 4 semicircular plugs from the cylinder heads.



21. DISCONNECT OIL COOLER PIPE



  1. Remove the cap nut and bolt. Then disconnect the oil cooler pipe.



22. REMOVE NO. 3 TIMING BELT COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Detach the 3 wire clamps and disconnect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector and control motor connector.




  1. Disconnect the camshaft position sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the camshaft position sensor wire from the 2 wire clamps on the timing belt cover.

  1. Remove the wire grommet from the timing belt cover.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Pull the cover outward, pull the camshaft position sensor wire out of the cover, and remove the cover.



23. REMOVE NO. 3 TIMING BELT COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts, nut and timing belt cover.



24. SET NO. 1 CYLINDER TO TDC/COMPRESSION



  1. Turn the crankshaft damper to align its notch with timing mark "0" of the No. 1 timing belt cover.

  1. Check that the timing marks of the camshaft timing pulleys and timing belt rear plates are aligned.
    If not, turn the crankshaft 1 complete revolution (360°) and align the marks as above.



25. INSPECT VALVE CLEARANCE



  1. Check only the valves indicated.

    1. Using a feeler gauge, measure the clearance between the valve lifter and camshaft.

      Standard Valve Clearance (Cold):
      Item
      		Specified Condition
      Intake
      		0.15 to 0.25 mm (0.00591 to 0.00984 in.)
      Exhaust
      		0.25 to 0.35 mm (0.00984 to 0.0138 in.)

    2. Record the out-of-specification valve clearance measurements. They will be used later to determine the required replacement adjusting shim.




  1. Turn the crankshaft 1 complete revolution (360°) and align the camshaft timing marks.

  1. Check only the valves indicated in the illustration.

26. ADJUST VALVE CLEARANCE

  1. Remove the timing belt (Click here).

  1. Remove the camshaft (Click here).

  1. Remove the valve lifter and adjusting shim.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to drop the adjusting shim into the cylinder head.

    HINT:
    Arrange the removed parts in the correct order.

  1. Using a micrometer, measure the thickness of the removed shim.




  1. Determine the replacement adjusting shim size according to the formula or charts below.

    1. Calculate the thickness of a new shim so that the valve clearance comes within the specified value.

      T:
      Thickness of removed shim

      A:
      Measured valve clearance

      N:
      Thickness of new shim

      Intake:
      N = T + (A - 0.20 mm (0.00787 in.))

      Exhaust:
      N = T + (A - 0.30 mm (0.0118 in.))



    2. Select a new shim with a thickness as close as possible to the calculated value.

      HINT:
      1. Shims are available in 41 increments of 0.020 mm (0.000787 in.), from 2.00 mm (0.0787 in.) to 2.80 mm (0.1102 in.) and 33 increments of 0.025 mm (0.000984 in.), from 2.50 mm (0.0984 in.) to 3.30 mm (0.1299 in.).
      2. Refer to the Adjusting Shim Selection Chart.




      Intake valve clearance (Cold):
      0.15 to 0.25 mm (0.00591 to 0.00984 in.)
      EXAMPLE:
      The 2.300 mm (0.0906 in.) shim is installed, and the measured clearance is 0.440 mm (0.0173 in.). Replace the 2.300 mm (0.0906 in.) shim with a No. 54 shim.

28. INSTALL NO. 3 TIMING BELT COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Pass the camshaft position sensor wire through the cover hole.

  1. Install the timing belt cover with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

  1. Install the wire grommet to the timing belt cover.

  1. Connect the sensor connector.

  1. Install the sensor wire to the 2 wire clamps on the cover.




  1. Connect the engine wire to the 3 wire clamps.

  1. Connect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector and control motor connector.



29. CONNECT OIL COOLER PIPE



  1. Install the oil cooler pipe with the cap nut and bolt.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }



30. INSTALL SPARK PLUG TUBE GASKET



  1. Using a cutter knife, cut off the seal part of the removed gasket.




  1. Using the removed gasket and a hammer, tap in a new gasket until it stops.

    NOTICE:
    Do not tap on the oil seal at an angle.

    HINT:
    If the removed gasket does not fit on the new one, correct the removed one with pliers.

  1. Apply a light coat of MP grease to the lip of the gasket.

    NOTICE:
    Keep the lip free from foreign matter.

  1. Return the 8 ventilation case claws to the original positions.



31. INSTALL SEMICIRCULAR PLUG



  1. Apply seal packing to the semicircular plug grooves.

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

  1. Install the 4 semicircular plugs to the cylinder heads.

    NOTICE:
    Install the cylinder head cover within 3 minutes after applying seal packing.



32. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install a new gasket to the cylinder head cover.

  1. Apply seal packing to the cylinder head as shown in the illustration.

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any oil from the contact surface.
    2. Install the cylinder head cover within 3 minutes after applying seal packing.




  1. Install the cylinder head cover with the 9 bolts. Uniformly tighten the bolts in several steps.

    Torque:
    6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }



33. INSTALL CYLINDER HEAD COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install a new gasket to the cylinder head cover.

  1. Apply seal packing to the cylinder head as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:

  1. Install the cylinder head cover with the 9 bolts. Uniformly tighten the bolts in several steps.

    Torque:
    6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }

37. CONNECT WIRE HARNESS



  1. Connect connector A to the engine room relay block.

  1. Connect the 2 connectors and 2 clamps to the engine room relay block.

  1. Connect the wire harness clamp to the cylinder head cover LH.




  1. Connect the wire harness clamp.

  1. Connect the ground wire with the bolt.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 air injection control driver connectors.



38. INSTALL FUEL PRESSURE PULSATION DAMPER ASSEMBLY


  1. Install 2 new gaskets and the fuel hose with the fuel pressure pulsation damper.



    NOTICE:
    Make sure to install the gaskets in the order shown in the illustration, as the shape of the upper side and lower side gaskets is different.

  1. Tighten the fuel pressure pulsation damper by hand.




  1. Using SST, tighten the union bolt.

    SST
    09612-24014   (09617-24011)

    Torque:
    with SST:
    33 N*m{ 340 kgf*cm , 24 ft.*lbf }
    without SST:
    39 N*m{ 398 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).
    2. Make sure SST and the wrench are connected in a straight line.




  1. Connect the fuel injector connector.



39. CONNECT FUEL HOSE



  1. Connect the fuel hose.

    HINT:
    Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

    NOTICE:
    1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
    2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.

  1. Install the fuel hose clamp.



40. INSTALL NO. 2 FUEL HOSE


  1. for engine side:




    1. Connect the fuel hose to the No. 3 fuel pipe.

      HINT:
      Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

      NOTICE:
      1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
      2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.

    2. Attach the lock claws to the connector by pushing down on the cover, as shown in the illustration.

    3. Attach the fuel hose clamp.



  1. for body side:




    1. Connect the fuel hose to the fuel pipe.

      HINT:
      Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

      NOTICE:
      1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
      2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.



41. INSTALL FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT



  1. Set the V belt onto every part except the belt tensioner, as shown in the illustration.

  1. Loosen the V belt by turning the belt tensioner counterclockwise.

  1. Set the V belt to the belt tensioner.




  1. Check that the belt fits properly in the ribbed grooves.

    HINT:
    Make sure to check by hand that the belt has not slipped out of the grooves on the bottom of the pulley.




  1. Check that the arrow on the belt tensioner is within area "A".
    If the arrow is outside area "A", replace the fan and generator V belt.

    HINT:
    When a new belt is installed, the arrow should be within area "B". If not, the fan and generator V belt is not installed correctly.

  1. Connect the No. 2 ventilation hose.
