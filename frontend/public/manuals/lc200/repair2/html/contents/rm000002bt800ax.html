STARTER (for 1.4 kW Type) > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL STARTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Connect the starter wire with the nut.

    Torque:
    9.8 N*m{ 100 kgf*cm , 87 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the starter connector.

  1. Connect the starter to the cylinder block.

  1. Connect the ground wire with the bolt.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 173 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the engine wire protector to the starter with the bolt.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 173 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the starter with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    25 N*m{ 255 kgf*cm , 18 ft.*lbf }



2. INSTALL REAR WATER BY-PASS JOINT




  1. Install 2 new gaskets and the water by-pass joint with the 4 nuts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    With the flat-bottom of each new gasket facing downward as shown in the illustration, install the 2 gaskets to the engine rear side.



3. INSTALL NO. 1 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install a new gasket and the No. 1 air switching valve to the rear water by-pass joint with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the vacuum switching valve hose to the No. 1 air switching valve.



4. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install a new gasket and the No. 2 air switching valve to the rear water by-pass joint with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the vacuum switching valve hose to the No. 2 air switching valve.



5. INSTALL NO. 3 AIR TUBE (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install a new gasket and connect the No. 3 air tube to the exhaust manifold with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install a new gasket and connect the No. 3 air tube to the No. 2 air switching valve with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



6. INSTALL AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install a new gasket and connect the air tube to the exhaust manifold with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install a new gasket and connect the air tube to the No. 1 air switching valve with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



7. CONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Connect the water by-pass pipe to the cylinder head cover RH with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }



8. INSTALL NO. 2 AIR INJECTION SYSTEM HOSE (w/ Secondary Air Injection System)



  1. Install the hose.



9. INSTALL WATER BY-PASS PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Apply soapy water to the O-ring.

  1. Install a new O-ring to the water by-pass pipe.




  1. Push in the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Install the water by-pass pipe with the bolt.

    Torque:
    18 N*m{ 184 kgf*cm , 13 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the 2 vacuum switching valve hoses to the water by-pass pipe clamp.

  1. Connect the wire harness clamp.

  1. Connect the connector.



10. INSTALL INTAKE MANIFOLD

  1. Install the intake manifold (Click here).
