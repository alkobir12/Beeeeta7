STARTER (for 2.0 kW Type) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE STARTER DUST PROTECTOR

  1. Remove the 2 dust protectors.



2. REMOVE STARTER YOKE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the lead wire from terminal C.

  1. Remove the 2 through bolts.

  1. Pull out the starter yoke with the armature from the magnet starter switch.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the starter yoke.



3. REMOVE STARTER CLUTCH SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws.




  1. Remove the return spring, idler gear, clutch roller, retainer, O-ring and starter clutch from the starter housing.




  1. Using a magnetic finger, remove the steel ball from the clutch shaft hole.



4. REMOVE STARTER BRUSH HOLDER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws, 2 O-rings and starter commutator end frame from the starter yoke.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the starter yoke.




  1. Using a screwdriver, hold the spring back and disconnect the brush from the brush holder.

  1. Disconnect the 4 brushes and remove the brush holder.

    NOTICE:
    Check that the positive (+) lead wires are not grounded.



5. REMOVE STARTER ARMATURE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the starter armature from the starter yoke.
