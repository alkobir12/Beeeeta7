DESCRIPTION

This circuit sends a DVD image signal from the radio receiver to the multi-display.

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (MULTI-DISPLAY - RADIO RECEIVER)



  1. Disconnect the F29 display connector.

  1. Disconnect the F25 receiver connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    F29-4 (VV-) - F25-2 (GND)

    F29-1 (VV+) - F25-1 (NTSC)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    F29-4 (VV-) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    F29-1 (VV+) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    F25-3 (SLD) - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
