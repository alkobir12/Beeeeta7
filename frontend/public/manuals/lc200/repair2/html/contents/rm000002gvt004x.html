CAMSHAFT OIL CONTROL VALVE > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install a new O-ring to the camshaft timing oil control valve.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install the oil control valve with the bolt.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the oil seal face of the oil control valve (connecting surface with cylinder head cover).
    2. Be careful that the O-ring is not cracked when installing the oil control valve.

  1. Connect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector.



2. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install a new O-ring to the camshaft timing oil control valve.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install the camshaft timing oil control valve with the bolt.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not allow foreign matter to contact the oil seal face of the oil control valve (connecting surface with cylinder head cover).
    2. Be careful that the O-ring is not cracked when installing the oil control valve.

  1. Connect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector and wire harness clamp.



3. INSTALL AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the air cleaner hose so that the protrusion of the air cleaner cap aligns with the groove of the hose as shown in the illustration.

  1. Tighten the 2 clamps.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the vacuum transmitting hose.

  1. Connect the No. 2 ventilation hose.



4. INSTALL V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Attach the 2 V-bank cover hooks to the bracket.

  1. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }
