CAMSHAFT OIL CONTROL VALVE > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



2. REMOVE AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the vacuum transmitting hose and No. 2 ventilation hose.

  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps.

  1. Remove the air cleaner hose.



3. REMOVE CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Disconnect the oil control valve connector and wire harness clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt and camshaft timing oil control valve.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the camshaft timing oil control valve.



4. REMOVE CAMSHAFT TIMING OIL CONTROL VALVE ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Disconnect the oil control valve connector.

  1. Remove the bolt and camshaft timing oil control valve.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the camshaft timing oil control valve.
