CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



6. DRAIN ENGINE COOLANT


CAUTION:
Do not remove the radiator cap while the engine and radiator are still hot. Pressurized, hot engine coolant and steam may be released and cause serious burns.




  1. Loosen the radiator drain cock plug.

  1. Remove the radiator cap. Then drain the coolant.

    HINT:
    Collect the coolant in a container and dispose of it according to the regulations in your area.

  1. Loosen the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs. Then drain the coolant from the engine.

  1. Tighten the 2 cylinder block drain cock plugs.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 130 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the radiator drain cock plug by hand.



7. REMOVE FAN AND GENERATOR V BELT



  1. Loosen the belt tension by turning the belt tensioner counterclockwise, and then remove the fan and generator V belt.

    HINT:
    The pulley bolt for the belt tensioner has left-hand thread.



8. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIATOR HOSE



  1. Using needle-nose pliers, grip the claws of the clamps and slide the clamps to remove the radiator hose.



9. REMOVE OIL COOLER PIPE



  1. Remove the cap nut and bolt.

  1. Using needle-nose pliers, grip the claws of the clamps and slide the clamps to disconnect the 3 water by-pass hoses and remove the oil cooler pipe.



10. REMOVE NO. 3 TIMING BELT COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Detach the 3 wire clamps and disconnect the camshaft timing oil control valve connector and control motor connector.




  1. Disconnect the camshaft position sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the camshaft position sensor wire from the 2 wire clamps on the timing belt cover.

  1. Remove the wire grommet from the timing belt cover.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Pull the cover outward, pull the camshaft position sensor wire out of the cover, and remove the cover.



11. REMOVE CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt, stud bolt and camshaft position sensor.



12. REMOVE INTAKE MANIFOLD

  1. Remove the intake manifold (Click here).



13. REMOVE VVT SENSOR



  1. Disconnect the 2 sensor connectors.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and 2 sensors.
