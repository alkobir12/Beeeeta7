KNOCK SENSOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE INTAKE MANIFOLD

  1. Remove the intake manifold (Click here).



4. REMOVE WATER BY-PASS PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Disconnect the wire harness clamp from the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Disconnect the 2 vacuum switching valve hoses from the water by-pass pipe clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt.

  1. Pull out the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the water by-pass pipe.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR INJECTION SYSTEM HOSE



  1. Remove the No. 2 air injection system hose from the rear water by-pass joint and air switching valve.



6. REMOVE AIR PUMP WITH BRACKET AND AIR SWITCHING VALVE



  1. Disconnect the air switching valve connector.

  1. Disconnect the air pressure sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and air pump with bracket.



7. REMOVE KNOCK SENSOR



  1. Disconnect the 2 sensor connectors.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts and 2 sensors.
