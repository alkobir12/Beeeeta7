FUEL INJECTOR > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



3. REMOVE AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the vacuum transmitting hose and No. 2 ventilation hose.

  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps.

  1. Remove the air cleaner hose.



4. INSPECT FUEL INJECTOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 8 injector connectors.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		20°C (68°F)
    		11.6 to 12.4 Ω

    If the resistance is not as specified, replace the injector.

  1. Reconnect the 8 injector connectors.



5. INSTALL AIR CLEANER HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the air cleaner hose so that the protrusion of the air cleaner cap aligns with the groove of the hose as shown in the illustration.

  1. Tighten the 2 clamps.

    Torque:
    5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }

  1. Connect the vacuum transmitting hose.

  1. Connect the No. 2 ventilation hose.



6. INSTALL V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Attach the 2 V-bank cover hooks to the bracket.

  1. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }



7. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).
