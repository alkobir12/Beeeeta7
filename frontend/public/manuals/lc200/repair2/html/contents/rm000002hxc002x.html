1.AIR INDUCTION SYSTEM

  1. Removal of the engine oil dipstick, oil filler cap, PCV hose, etc. may damage the engine.

  2. Disconnection, looseness or cracks in the parts of the air induction system between the throttle body and cylinder head may result in air suction and damage the engine.
