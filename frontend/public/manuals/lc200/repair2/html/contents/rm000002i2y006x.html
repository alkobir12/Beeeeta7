IGNITION COIL AND SPARK PLUG > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE V-BANK COVER



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and V-bank cover.



2. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CLEANER HOSE



  1. Loosen the 2 hose clamps and remove the air cleaner hose.



3. REMOVE AIR CLEANER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Unfasten the 2 hook clamps and then remove the air cleaner cap.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 SURGE TANK STAY



  1. for Automatic Transmission:
    Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 surge tank stay.




  1. for Manual Transmission:
    Remove the 2 bolts, nut and No. 2 surge tank stay.



5. REMOVE IGNITION COIL ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the 6 ignition coil connectors.




  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 6 ignition coils.



6. REMOVE SPARK PLUG

  1. Remove the 6 spark plugs.
