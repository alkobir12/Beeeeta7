1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

    1. Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).

36. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY RH



HINT:
When removing the front quarter trim panel, operate the reclining adjuster release handle and move the No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.




  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




  1. Remove the bolt and rear No. 1 seat belt anchor.




  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




    2. Remove the bolt and rear No. 2 seat belt anchor.



  1. w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Remove the clip and bolt.

    2. Detach the 16 clips and 2 claws.

    3. w/o Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Disconnect the rear seat lock control lever cable and then remove the quarter trim panel.



    4. w/ Rear Air Conditioning System:
      Disconnect the thermistor connector and rear seat lock control lever cable, and then remove the quarter trim panel.





  1. w/o Rear No. 2 Seat or w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, for Face to Face Seat Type:

    1. Remove the clip.



    2. Detach the 16 clips and 2 claws, and remove the quarter trim panel.





  1. w/ Tonneau Cover:

    1. Remove the screw and clip.



    2. Detach the 16 clips and 2 claws, and remove the quarter trim panel.





37. REMOVE FUEL FILLER OPENING LID LOCK RETAINER



  1. Turn the cable counterclockwise and remove the cable from the retainer.




  1. Turn the lock retainer counterclockwise and remove the lock retainer.



38. REMOVE FUEL LID LOCK OPEN LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 claws.

  1. Disconnect the fuel lid lock control cable and remove the fuel lid lock open lever.



39. REMOVE FUEL LID LOCK CONTROL CABLE SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the clamps shown in the illustration.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Remove the lock control cable.
