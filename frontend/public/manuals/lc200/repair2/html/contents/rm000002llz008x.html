DESCRIPTION

This circuit consists of the No. 2 air conditioning control assembly* and the air conditioning amplifier assembly. When the No. 2 air conditioning control assembly is operated, signals are transmitted to the air conditioning amplifier assembly through the LIN communication system.
If the LIN communication system malfunctions, the air conditioning amplifier assembly does not operate even if the No. 2 air conditioning control assembly is operated.

HINT:
*: w/ Rear Heater

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.
