1. INSPECT WINDSHIELD WASHER MOTOR AND PUMP ASSEMBLY

HINT:
This check should be performed with the windshield washer motor and pump installed to the washer jar.




  1. Fill the washer jar with washer fluid.

  1. Apply battery voltage to the washer motor and check the operation of the washer motor.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2
    		Windshield washer motor operation is normal

    If the result is not as specified, replace the windshield washer motor and pump assembly.
