RELAY (w/ Headlight Cleaner System) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MUDGUARD LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 4 screws and mudguard.



2. REMOVE SIDE STEP ASSEMBLY LH


  1. Remove the 6 bolts and side step.





3. REMOVE BODY ROCKER PANEL MOULDING ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the 8 side mudguard retainers and screw.



  1. Detach the 10 clips and remove the rocker panel moulding.





4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER LINER LH

  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 2 screws.



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 grommets, 12 clips and fender liner.





5. DRAIN WASHER FLUID



  1. Remove the washer hose from the windshield washer motor and pump, and drain the washer fluid.



6. REMOVE WASHER INLET SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the clip and washer inlet.



7. REMOVE WINDSHIELD WASHER JAR ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the clamp.

  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

  1. Disconnect the headlight cleaner hose.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts. Then remove the windshield washer jar and pump.



8. REMOVE HEADLIGHT CLEANER CONTROL RELAY

  1. Disconnect the connector.




  1. Remove the headlight cleaner control relay in the direction shown in the illustration.
