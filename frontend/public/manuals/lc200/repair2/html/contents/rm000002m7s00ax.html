HOOD LOCK CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here




1. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL


  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.





2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MAIN SEAL LH (for LHD)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 clips and remove the fender main seal.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER MAIN SEAL RH (for RHD)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 clips and remove the fender main seal.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



4. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Put protective tape around the radiator grille.

  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 8 claws, and remove the radiator grille.



5. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL CABLE COVER



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the cable cover.



6. REMOVE HOOD LOCK NUT CAP



  1. Using a screwdriver, remove the hood lock nut cap as shown in the illustration.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



7. REMOVE HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and nut.

  1. Remove the hood lock.

  1. Disconnect the connector.




  1. Disconnect the hood lock control cable.



8. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL LEVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 claws.

  1. Disconnect the hood lock control cable and remove the hood lock lever.



9. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY (for LHD)




  1. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the clamps shown in the illustration.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Pull the cable from the engine room and remove it.



10. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL CABLE ASSEMBLY (for RHD)




  1. Using a screwdriver, disconnect the clamps shown in the illustration.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Pull the cable from the engine room and remove it.
