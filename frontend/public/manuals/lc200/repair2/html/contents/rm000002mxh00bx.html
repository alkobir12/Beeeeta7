DTC    U0122    Lost Communication with Vehicle Dynamics Control Module

DTC    U0129    Lost Communication with Brake System Control Module

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

If 2 or more DTCs are output during the DTC check, one side of the CAN branch wire may be open (One side of the CANH [branch wire]/CANL [branch wire] of the ECU and/or sensor is open).

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
U0122
There is no communication from the skid control ECU.
  1. Power source circuit of skid control ECU
  2. One side of CAN branch wire of other systems
  3. Skid control ECU
  4. Skid control ECU branch wire or connector
U0129
There is no communication from the skid control ECU.
  1. Power source circuit of skid control ECU
  2. One side of CAN branch wire of other systems
  3. Skid control ECU
  4. Skid control ECU branch wire or connector

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal before measuring the resistances of the main wire and the branch wire.

    CAUTION:
    Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).


NEXT


2.CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS WIRE (SKID CONTROL ECU BRANCH WIRE)



  1. Disconnect the A24 skid control ECU connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A24-11 (CANH) - A24-25 (CANL)
    		Engine switch off
    		54 to 69 Ω



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE SKID CONTROL ECU BRANCH WIRE OR CONNECTOR (CANH, CANL)
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (SKID CONTROL ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND)

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).




  1. Disconnect the A24 and A25 skid control ECU connectors.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A24-1 (GND1) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    A24-32 (GND2) - Body ground
    A25-4 (GND3) - Body ground

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A24-2 (+BM1) - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    A24-31 (+BS) - Body ground
    A25-2 (+BM2) - Body ground
    A24-46 (IG1) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    A25-1 (IG2) - Body ground



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK
