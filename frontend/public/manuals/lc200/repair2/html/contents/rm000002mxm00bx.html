DTC    U1002 (CAN MS BUS)    Lost Communication with Gateway Module (Main body ECU)

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

If 2 or more DTCs are output during the DTC check, one side of the CAN branch wire may be open (One side of the CANH [branch wire]/CANL [branch wire] of the ECU and/or sensor is open).

DTC Code
Symptom
Trouble Area
U1002 (CAN MS BUS)
Lost communication with the gateway module (main body ECU).
  1. MS bus circuit
  2. Main body ECU main wire or connector
  3. Windshield wiper ECU branch wire or connector*1
  4. Position control ECU and switch branch wire or connector*2
  5. Certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly) branch wire or connector
  6. Multiplex tilt and telescopic ECU branch wire or connector*3
  7. Outer mirror control ECU branch wire or connector*2
  8. Combination meter main wire or connector
  9. Main body ECU
  10. Windshield wiper ECU*1
  11. Position control ECU and switch*2
  12. Certification ECU (smart key ECU assembly)
  13. Multiplex tilt and telescopic ECU*3
  14. Outer mirror control ECU*2
  15. Combination meter
  16. No. 8 junction connector
  17. No. 9 junction connector
  18. No. 10 junction connector
  19. No. 2 CAN junction connector

HINT:
*1: w/ Rain Sensor
*2: w/ Seat Memory
*3: w/ Power Tilt and Power Telescopic Steering Column System
