DTC    B1630/23    Rear Airbag Sensor RH Circuit Malfunction

DTC    B1695/16    Door Side Airbag Sensor LH Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

HINT:
"Door side airbag sensor LH" refer to the side airbag sensor LH.

  1. The side airbag sensor LH or rear airbag sensor RH consists of the safing sensor, the diagnostic circuit, the lateral deceleration sensor, etc.
    If the center airbag sensor receives signals from the lateral deceleration sensor, it determines whether the SRS should be activated.
    DTC B1630/23 or B1695/16 is stored when a malfunction is detected in the side airbag sensor LH or rear airbag sensor RH circuit.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1630/23
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. A rear airbag sensor RH malfunction.
  2. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. Rear airbag sensor RH
  2. Center airbag sensor assembly
B1695/16
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. A side airbag sensor LH malfunction.
  2. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. Side airbag sensor LH
  2. Center airbag sensor assembly

2.CHECK REAR AIRBAG SENSOR RH

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.

  1. Temporarily replace the rear airbag sensor RH with a new one.

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

  1. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (Click here).

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

  1. Check for DTCs (Click here).

3.CHECK SIDE AIRBAG SENSOR LH

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.

  1. Interchange the side airbag sensor RH with LH and connect the connectors to them.

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 2 seconds.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

  1. Clear the DTCs stored in the memory (Click here).

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG), and wait for at least 60 seconds.

  1. Check for DTCs (Click here).
