SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

The outer rear view mirror assembly has a mirror position sensor. The position control ECU and switch sends instructions to the outer mirror control ECU. Then the outer mirror control ECU receives mirror information from the mirror position sensors, and memorizes the mirror positions.
