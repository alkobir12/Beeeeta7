KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (for Driver Side) > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. CHECK DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (VEHICLE NOT INVOLVED IN COLLISION)



  1. Perform a diagnostic system check (Click here).

  1. With the driver side knee airbag installed on the vehicle, perform a visual check. If there are any defects as mentioned below, replace the driver side knee airbag with a new one:
    Cuts, minute cracks or marked discoloration on the driver side knee airbag.



2. CHECK DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (VEHICLE INVOLVED IN COLLISION AND AIRBAG HAS NOT DEPLOYED)



  1. Perform a diagnostic system check (Click here).

  1. With the driver side knee airbag removed from the vehicle, perform a visual check. If there are any defects as mentioned below, replace the driver side knee airbag with a new one:

    1. Cuts, minute cracks or marked discoloration on the driver side knee airbag.
    2. Cracks or other damage to the connector.
    3. Deformation or cracks on the instrument panel reinforcement.

    NOTICE:
    For removal and installation procedures of the driver side knee airbag, be sure to follow the correct procedure.
