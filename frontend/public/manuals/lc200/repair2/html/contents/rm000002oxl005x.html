INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.PERFORM ACTIVE TEST USING INTELLIGENT TESTER (FRONT WIPER MOTOR)

  1. Select the Active Test, use the intelligent tester to generate a control command, and then check the front wiper motor.

    NOTICE:
    When a Body No. 5 (windshield wiper ECU) related front wiper switch is on, Active Test items related to the front wiper switch are prohibited (double operation prohibition). Check that the front wiper switches are off before performing Active Test.

2.INSPECT FRONT WIPER MOTOR



  1. Remove the front wiper motor (Click here).

  1. Apply battery voltage to the front wiper motor and check the speed of the front wiper motor.
