WIPER AND WASHER SYSTEM (w/ Rain Sensor) > Washer Motor Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The windshield wiper ECU receives washer switch information from the front wiper switch, and operates the washer motor.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT FRONT WASHER MOTOR



  1. Remove the front washer motor (Click here).

  1. Apply battery voltage to the windshield washer motor and check the operation of the windshield washer motor.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2
    		Front washer motor operation is normal



NG
REPLACE FRONT WASHER MOTOR (Click here)
OK


2.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (FRONT WASHER MOTOR - BODY GROUND)



  1. Disconnect the A16 front washer motor connector.

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A16-1 - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH - BODY GROUND)



  1. Disconnect the E16 windshield wiper switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E16-2 - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK


4.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (FRONT WASHER MOTOR - WINDSHIELD WIPER SWITCH)



  1. Disconnect the E15 windshield wiper switch connector.

  1. Disconnect the A16 washer motor connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    for LH Side 
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A16-2 - E15-3 (WF)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    A16-2 - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
    for RH Side 
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A16-2 - E15-1 (WF)
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    A16-2 - Body ground
    		Always
    		10 kΩ or higher
