DTC    U0208    Lost Communication with "Seat Control Module A"

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION


DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
U0208
There is no communication from the position control ECU and switch.
  1. Power source circuit of position control ECU and switch
  2. Position control ECU and switch
  3. Position control ECU and switch branch wire or connector

HINT:
For vehicles with the seat memory only.


WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal before measuring the resistances of the main wire and the branch wire.

    CAUTION:
    Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).


NEXT


2.CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS WIRE (POSITION CONTROL ECU AND SWITCH BRANCH WIRE)



  1. Disconnect the c27 position control ECU and switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    c27-5 (MPX1) - c27-1 (MPX2)
    		Engine switch off
    		54 to 69 Ω



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE POSITION CONTROL ECU AND SWITCH BRANCH WIRE OR CONNECTOR (MPX1, MPX2)
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (POSITION CONTROL ECU AND SWITCH - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND)

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).




  1. Disconnect the c26 position control ECU and switch connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    c26-1 (GND) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    c26-5 (+B) - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
