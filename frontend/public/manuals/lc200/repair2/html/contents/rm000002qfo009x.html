ELECTRICAL KEY OSCILLATOR (for Front Floor) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



2. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



4. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



5. REMOVE SHIFT LEVER KNOB SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. Twist the shift lever knob in the direction indicated by the arrow and remove it.





6. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



7. REMOVE REAR UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 9 claws and remove the panel.



8. REMOVE CONSOLE CUP HOLDER BOX SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the box.



9. REMOVE UPPER CONSOLE PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 14 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the console panel.



10. REMOVE INDOOR ELECTRICAL KEY OSCILLATOR



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the electrical key oscillator.
