ELECTRICAL KEY OSCILLATOR (for Rear Floor) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (except Face to Face Seat Type)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly LH (Click here).



3. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH (except Face to Face Seat Type)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly RH (Click here).



4. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (for Face to Face Seat Type)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly LH (Click here).



5. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH (for Face to Face Seat Type)

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly RH (Click here).



6. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the rear No. 1 seat assembly LH (Click here).



7. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH

  1. Remove the rear No. 1 seat assembly RH (Click here).



8. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).



9. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY RH

  1. Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).



10. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT PROTECTOR



  1. Detach the 10 claws and remove the 2 seat protectors.



11. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT PROTECTOR



  1. Detach the 10 claws and remove the 2 seat protectors.



12. REMOVE INDOOR ELECTRICAL KEY OSCILLATOR

  1. Fold back the front floor carpet assembly.




  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the electrical key oscillator.
