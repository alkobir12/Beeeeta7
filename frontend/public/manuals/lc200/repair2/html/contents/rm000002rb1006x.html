FRONT POWER SEAT CONTROL SYSTEM (w/ Seat Position Memory System) > Front Power Seat does not Operate with Front Power Seat Switch

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

When a signal is input into the position control ECU, the ECU manages the signals received from the front power seat switch LH, and operates each motor. When 2 or more signals are input, the motors only operate when the signals are from the slide switch and reclining switch.

  1. Remove the FR P/SEAT LH*1, FR P/SEAT RH*2 and ECU-B2 fuses from the main body ECU.

    HINT:
    *1: for LHD
    *2: for RHD
