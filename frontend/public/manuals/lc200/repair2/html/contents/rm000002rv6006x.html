1. INSPECT HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 (HT1B) - 2 (+B)
    for Bank 1
    		20°C (68°F)
    		11 to 16 Ω
    1 (HT2B) - 2 (+B)
    for Bank 2
    		20°C (68°F)
    		11 to 16 Ω

    If the result is not as specified, replace the sensor.
