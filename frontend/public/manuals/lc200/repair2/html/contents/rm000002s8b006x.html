DTC    B1206    P/W Master Switch Communication Stop

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

This DTC is stored when LIN communication between the master switch and main body ECU (cowl side junction block LH) stops for 10 seconds or more.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1206
No communication between master switch and main body ECU (cowl side junction block LH) for 10 seconds or more.
  1. Master switch
  2. Main body ECU (cowl side junction block LH)
  3. Harness or connector

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

NOTICE:
When using the intelligent tester with the engine switch off to troubleshoot:
Connect the intelligent tester to the vehicle, and turn a courtesy switch on and off at 1.5 second intervals until communication between the intelligent tester and vehicle begins.
