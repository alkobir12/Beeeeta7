ECD SYSTEM > Injector Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The injector driver drives the injectors at high speeds with a high-voltage DC/DC converter. The ECM constantly monitors the injector driver and stops the engine if an abnormal condition is detected.

WIRING DIAGRAM

Refer to DTC P062D (Click here).
