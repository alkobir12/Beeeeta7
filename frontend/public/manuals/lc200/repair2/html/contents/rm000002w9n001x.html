HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the sensor connector.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and No. 2 exhaust pipe assembly.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the front No. 2 exhaust pipe.



2. REMOVE HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR (for Bank 1 Sensor 2)



  1. Disconnect the sensor connector.




  1. Using SST, remove the sensor.

    SST
    09224-00010  



3. REMOVE HEATED OXYGEN SENSOR (for Bank 2 Sensor 2)



  1. Using SST, remove the sensor.

    SST
    09224-00010
