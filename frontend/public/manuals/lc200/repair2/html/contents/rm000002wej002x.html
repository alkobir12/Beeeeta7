FUEL PRESSURE PULSATION DAMPER > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL FUEL PRESSURE PULSATION DAMPER ASSEMBLY


  1. Install 2 new gaskets and the fuel hose with the fuel pressure pulsation damper.



    NOTICE:
    Make sure to install the gaskets in the order shown in the illustration, as the shape of the upper side and lower side gaskets is different.

  1. Tighten the fuel pressure pulsation damper by hand.




  1. Using SST, tighten the union bolt.

    SST
    09612-24014   (09617-24011)

    Torque:
    with SST:
    33 N*m{ 340 kgf*cm , 24 ft.*lbf }
    without SST:
    39 N*m{ 398 kgf*cm , 29 ft.*lbf }

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.).
    2. Make sure SST and the wrench are connected in a straight line.




  1. Connect the fuel injector connector.



2. CONNECT NO. 2 FUEL HOSE



  1. Connect the No. 2 fuel hose to the No. 3 fuel pipe.

    HINT:
    Push the parts together firmly until a "click" sound is heard.

    NOTICE:
    1. Before installing the tube connectors to the pipes, check if there is any damage or foreign matter in the connectors.
    2. After the connection, check if the connectors and pipes are securely connected by trying to pull them apart.



3. INSTALL V-BANK COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Attach the 2 V-bank cover hooks to the bracket.

  1. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }



4. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



5. INSPECT FOR FUEL LEAK

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG) and intelligent tester ON.

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine.

  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Active Test / Activate the Fuel Pump Speed Control.

  1. Check that there are no fuel leaks after doing maintenance anywhere on the fuel system.




  1. Check that the pulsation damper screw rises up when the fuel pump operates.
    If the operation is not as specified, check the following parts:

    1. Fusible link
    2. Fuses
    3. Circuit opening relay (C/OPN)
    4. Fuel pump
    5. ECM
    6. Wiring connections

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the intelligent tester from the DLC3.
