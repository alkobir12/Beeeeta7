LIGHTING SYSTEM > Front Fog Light Circuit

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The main body ECU receives a front fog light switch information signal from the light control switch (front fog switch), and illuminates the front fog lights.

6.CHECK FRONT FOG LIGHT

  1. Disconnect the E1 ECU connector.

  1. Using a service wire, connect terminal E1-4 (FFGO) on the wire harness side and body ground.

  1. Check that the front fog light illuminates.

    OK:
