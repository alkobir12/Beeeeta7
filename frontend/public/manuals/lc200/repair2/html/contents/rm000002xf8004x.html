FRONT SEAT CUSHION HEATER (w/ Seat Heater System) > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT FRONT SEAT CUSHION HEATER ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Heat the thermostat with a light.

  1. Check the seat cushion heater.

    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      A-1 - B-1
      		Seat cushion heater temperature 25°C (77°F)
      		3.05 to 3.65 Ω
      A-4 - B-2
      A-1 - A-4
      A-2 - A-3
      		8 to 12 kΩ

      If the result is not as specified, replace the seat cushion heater assembly.
