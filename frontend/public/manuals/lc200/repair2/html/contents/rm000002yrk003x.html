EXHAUST MANIFOLD (w/ Secondary Air Injection System) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE INTAKE MANIFOLD

  1. Remove the intake manifold (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL LH (w/ KDSS)



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and fender apron seal.



4. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



5. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL FRONT RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 3 clips and fender apron seal.



6. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



7. DISCONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the water by-pass pipe from the cylinder head cover RH.



8. REMOVE NO. 3 AIR TUBE



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and No. 3 air tube.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets.



9. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and air tube sub-assembly.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets.



10. DISCONNECT COOLER COMPRESSOR



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the bolt. Then disconnect the cooler refrigerant suction hose from the cylinder head cover.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts, nut and No. 1 compressor stay and disconnect the cooler compressor.



11. REMOVE ENGINE OIL LEVEL DIPSTICK GUIDE



  1. Remove the dipstick.

  1. Remove the bolt and dipstick guide.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the dipstick guide.



12. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 engine under cover.



13. REMOVE TAILPIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the bolt, clamp and gasket.

  1. Remove the tailpipe from the 2 exhaust pipe supports.



14. REMOVE CENTER EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Remove the center exhaust pipe from the 3 exhaust pipe supports.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets from the front exhaust pipe and front No. 2 exhaust pipe.



15. REMOVE FRONT EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Detach the heated oxygen sensor wire clamp.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts, front exhaust pipe and gasket.



16. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the air fuel ratio sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the heated oxygen sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 3 nuts, front No. 2 exhaust pipe and gasket.



17. DISCONNECT TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER ASSEMBLY (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the bolt and hose heat protector.




  1. Disconnect the 2 water bypass hoses from the transmission oil cooler.

    NOTICE:
    Use a container to catch any coolant which flows out of the water by-pass hoses.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the transmission oil cooler.



18. REMOVE NO. 1 MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay.



19. REMOVE NO. 1 EXHAUST MANIFOLD HEAT INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and heat insulator.



20. REMOVE EXHAUST MANIFOLD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the 8 nuts and exhaust manifold.




  1. Remove the gasket from the cylinder head RH.



21. REMOVE NO. 2 MANIFOLD STAY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and manifold stay.



22. REMOVE NO. 2 EXHAUST MANIFOLD HEAT INSULATOR



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and heat insulator.



23. REMOVE EXHAUST MANIFOLD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 8 nuts and exhaust manifold.




  1. Remove the gasket from the cylinder head LH.
