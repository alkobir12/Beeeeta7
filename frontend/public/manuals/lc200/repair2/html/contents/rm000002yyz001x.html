ENGINE UNIT > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (for Automatic Transmission)



  1. Disconnect the 2 water by-pass pipe hoses and remove the pipe.



2. DISCONNECT WATER HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Automatic Transmission)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the water hose.



3. DISCONNECT WATER HOSE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the water hose.



4. REMOVE FUEL DELIVERY PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 6 fuel injector connectors.

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and fuel delivery pipe together with the 6 fuel injectors.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to drop the injectors when removing the fuel delivery pipe.



5. REMOVE FUEL INJECTOR



  1. Pull the 6 fuel injectors from the fuel delivery pipe.

  1. Remove the O-ring and injector vibration insulator from each fuel injector.



6. REMOVE INTAKE MANIFOLD



  1. Remove the 10 bolts, intake manifold and 2 gaskets.



7. REMOVE ENGINE WIRE






8. REMOVE IGNITION COIL ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the 6 bolts and 6 ignition coils.



9. REMOVE V-RIBBED BELT TENSIONER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and V-ribbed belt tensioner.



10. REMOVE ENGINE OIL LEVEL DIPSTICK GUIDE



  1. Remove the bolt and dipstick guide.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the dipstick guide.



11. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET RH



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and front No. 1 engine mounting bracket RH.



12. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 ENGINE MOUNTING BRACKET LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and front No. 1 engine mounting bracket LH.



13. REMOVE NO. 1 IDLER PULLEY SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the bolt and No. 1 idler pulley.



14. REMOVE NO. 2 IDLER PULLEY SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 cover plates and 2 idler pulleys.



15. REMOVE FLYWHEEL RING GEAR (for Manual Transmission)

HINT:
If the flywheel ring gear is deformed or the gears are damaged, replace it.

CAUTION:
Wear protective gloves. Hot ares on the flywheel ring gear may injure your hands.




  1. Using a torch, heat the ring gear evenly to approximately 200°C (392°F).

  1. Temporarily install 6 bolts (8 mm x 1.25 pitch) with a length of 70 mm or more to the flywheel.

  1. Uniformly tighten the 6 bolts in the order shown in the illustration.

  1. Remove the ring gear from the flywheel.
