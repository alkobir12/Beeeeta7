for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL OIL PRESSURE SENSOR



  1. Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the oil pressure sensor.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1344, Three Bond 1344 or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    Do not apply adhesive to the oil inlet port of the sensor.




  1. Using a 24 mm deep socket wrench, install the oil pressure sensor.

    Torque:
    15 N*m{ 150 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine within 1 hour of installation.

  1. Connect the oil pressure sensor connector.



2. INSTALL V-BANK COVER



  1. Install the V-bank cover with the 2 cap nuts.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }
