CHECK AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER (w/ Rear air conditioning amplifier)


  1. Disconnect the E36 air conditioning amplifier connector.

  2. Measure the resistance and voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E36-5 (IG+) - E36-1 (GND)
    		G - W-B
    		Ignition power supply
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    Engine switch off
    		Below 1 V
    E36-6 (+B1) - E36-1 (GND)
    		R - W-B
    		Battery power source
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    E36-7 (+B2) - E36-1 (GND)
    		R - W-B
    		Battery power source
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    E36-1 (GND) - Body ground
    		W-B - Body ground
    		Ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

    1. If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction on the wire harness side.

  3. Reconnect the E36 air conditioning amplifier connector.

  4. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Terminal No. (Symbol)
    		Wiring Color
    		Terminal Description
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E36-24 (RDEF) - E36-1 (GND)
    		G - W-B
    		Defogger relay
    		Engine switch on (IG) and defogger switch off
    		11 to 14 V
    Engine switch on (IG) and defogger switch on
    		Below 1 V

    1. If the result is not as specified, the air conditioning amplifier or fuse may have a malfunction.
