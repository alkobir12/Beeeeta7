LIGHTING SYSTEM > Headlight Dimmer Switch Circuit

DESCRIPTION

The turn signal flasher receives a turn signal light information signal from the headlight dimmer switch.

WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.INSPECT HEADLIGHT DIMMER SWITCH (TURN SIGNAL SWITCH)



  1. Remove the headlight dimmer switch (Click here).

  1. for LH Side:
    Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
