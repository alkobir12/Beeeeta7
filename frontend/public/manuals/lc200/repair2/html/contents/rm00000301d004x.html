1. INSPECT SHIFT SOLENOID VALVE SR



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Solenoid Connector (SR) - Solenoid Body (SR)
    		20°C (68°F)
    		11 to 15 Ω

  1. Apply 12 V of battery voltage to the shift solenoid valve and check that the valve moves and makes an operating noise.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1. Battery positive (+) → Shift solenoid valve SR connector
    2. Battery negative (-) → Shift solenoid valve SR body
    		Valve moves and makes an operating noise

    If the result is not as specified, replace the solenoid valve.
