TIMING BELT > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT TIMING BELT

  1. Visually check the timing belt for excessive wear, frayed cords, etc.
    If necessary, replace the timing belt.

    NOTICE:


    1. Do not bend or twist the timing belt or turn it inside out.
    2. Do not allow the timing belt to come into contact with oil, water or steam.



2. INSPECT TIMING BELT TENSIONER ASSEMBLY



  1. Visually check the seal portion of the tensioner for oil leakage.
    If leakage is found, replace the timing belt tensioner assembly.

    HINT:
    If there is only a faint trace of oil on the push rod, the tensioner does not need to be replaced.




  1. Hold the tensioner with both hands and push the push rod strongly as shown to check that it does not move.
    If the push rod moves, replace the timing belt tensioner assembly.

    NOTICE:
    Never face the tensioner push rod downward.




  1. Measure the protrusion of the push rod from the housing end.

    Standard protrusion:
    9.5 to 10.5 mm (0.374 to 0.413 in.)

    If the protrusion is not as specified, replace the timing belt tensioner assembly.
