1. INSPECT FRONT NO. 1 SUSPENSION ARM LOWER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. As shown in the illustration, move the ball joint stud back and forth 5 times before installing the nut.

  1. Using a torque wrench, turn the nut continuously at a rate of 3 to 5 seconds per turn and take the torque reading on the fifth turn.

    Standard turning torque:
    3.0 to 10.0 N*m (31 to 102 kgf*cm, 27 to 89 in.*lbf)

  1. Check for any cracks and grease leaks on the ball joint dust cover.
