TRANSFER OIL > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

1. CHECK TRANSFER OIL



  1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.




  1. Check that the oil level is between 0 to 5.0 mm (0 to 0.197 in.) from the bottom lip of the filler plug hole.
    If the result is not as specified, add transfer oil.

    Oil Grade:
    GL-5

    Viscosity:
    SAE 75W-90

    Standard Capacity:
    1.4 to 1.5 liters (1.47 to 1.59 US qts, 1.23 to 1.32 Imp. qts)

    NOTICE:
    Too much or too little oil will lead to transfer problems.

  1. When the oil level is too low, check for oil leaks.




  1. Install a new gasket onto the filler plug and then tighten the plug.

    Torque:
    37 N*m{ 377 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }



2. ADD TRANSFER OIL



  1. Remove the filler plug and gasket.

  1. Add oil so that the oil level is between 0 to 5.0 mm (0 to 0.197 in.) from the bottom lip of the filler plug hole.

    NOTICE:
    1. Add oil slowly.
    2. Add oil a little at a time, waiting several minutes between each addition of oil.

  1. Wait approximately 5 minutes and check that the oil level has not changed.




  1. Install a new gasket onto the filler plug and then tighten the plug.

    Torque:
    37 N*m{ 377 kgf*cm , 27 ft.*lbf }
