TRANSFER CASE REAR OIL SEAL > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the propeller shaft assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE REAR OUTPUT SHAFT COMPANION FLANGE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST and a hammer, release the staked part of the nut.

    SST
    09930-00010   (09931-00010, 09931-00020)

    NOTICE:
    1. Be sure to use SST with the tapered surface facing the shaft.
    2. Do not grind the tip of SST with a grinder, etc.
    3. Completely loosen the staked part of the nut when removing it.
    4. Do not damage the threads of the rear transfer output shaft.




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, remove the lock nut.

    SST
    09330-00021  




  1. Using SST, remove the companion flange.

    SST
    09950-40011   (09951-04020, 09952-04010, 09953-04030, 09954-04010, 09955-04051, 09957-04010, 09958-04011)

  1. Remove the O-ring from the companion flange.



3. REMOVE TRANSFER CASE REAR OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST, tap out the oil seal.

    SST
    09308-00010  

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the oil seal and rear transfer case contact surface.



4. INSTALL TRANSFER CASE REAR OIL SEAL

  1. Apply transfer oil to the spline of the output shaft.




  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in a new oil seal until its surface is flush with the housing upper surface.

    SST
    09649-17010  
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)



5. INSTALL REAR OUTPUT SHAFT COMPANION FLANGE SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the companion flange onto the output shaft.




  1. Using SST to hold the companion flange, install a new O-ring and a new companion flange lock nut.

    SST
    09330-00021  

    Torque:
    127 N*m{ 1299 kgf*cm , 94 ft.*lbf }




  1. Using a chisel and hammer, stake the companion flange lock nut.



6. INSTALL PROPELLER SHAFT ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the propeller shaft assembly (Click here).
