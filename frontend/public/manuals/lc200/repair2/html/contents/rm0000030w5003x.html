METER / GAUGE SYSTEM > Fuel Receiver Gauge Malfunction

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The fuel sender gauge has a variable resistance mechanism. The resistance decreases when the fuel amount increases, and the resistance increases when the fuel amount decreases. The fuel receiver gauge changes based on the resistance of the fuel sender gauge.
