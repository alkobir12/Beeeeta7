REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY (for Face to Face Seat Type) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE REAR SIDE SEAT LEG PROTECTOR LH



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the protector.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



2. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and rear No. 2 seat.
