DIESEL THROTTLE BODY > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here


NOTICE:
Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to clear the diesel throttle valve fully closed position learned value.



1. INSTALL DIESEL THROTTLE BODY ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install a new gasket to the intake pipe.

  1. Install the throttle body with the 2 bolts and 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the throttle position sensor connector.

  1. Connect the throttle motor connector.



2. INSTALL DIESEL THROTTLE BODY ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install a new gasket to the intake pipe.

  1. Install the throttle body with the 2 bolts and 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Connect the throttle position sensor connector.

  1. Connect the throttle motor connector.



3. INSTALL TUBE CONNECTOR TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Temporarily install the flare nut of the tube connector to flexible hose tube to the clutch tube to release cylinder 2 way by hand.




  1. Temporarily install the tube connector to flexible hose tube with the 2 bolts.

  1. Tighten the bolt labeled A.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    Tighten the bolt labeled B after installing the clutch hose.



4. INSTALL AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Install the air tube to the throttle body.

  1. Align the embossed mark of the throttle body with the paint mark of the No. 4 air hose.




  1. Tighten the hose clamp so that it is 7 to 11 mm (0.276 to 0.433 in.) from the end of the hose as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



5. INSTALL CLUTCH HOSE (for Manual Transmission)



  1. Connect the clutch hose to the air tube with the bolt labeled A.

  1. Temporarily install the clutch hose to the clutch master cylinder tube to flexible hose tube and tube connector to flexible hose tube, and fix it in place with the 2 clips.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }

  1. Tighten the bolt labeled B.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



  1. Tighten the flexible hose tube.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend or damage the flexible hose tube.
    2. Do not allow any foreign matter such as dirt and dust to enter the flexible hose tube from the connecting points.

    HINT:
    1. Use a torque wrench with a fulcrum length of 30 cm (11.8 in.). If using a torque wrench with a length that is not 30 cm (11.8 in.), calculate the torque specification for the torque wrench and SST based on the "without SST" torque specification (Click here).
    2. Make sure union nut wrench and the wrench are connected in a straight line.





    1. Using a 10 mm union nut wrench, tighten the 2 flare nuts of the flexible hose tube.

      Torque:
      without union nut wrench:
      15 N*m{ 154 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
      with union nut wrench:
      14 N*m{ 141 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }




    2. Using a 10 mm union nut wrench, tighten the flare nut of the flexible hose tube.

      Torque:
      without union nut wrench:
      15 N*m{ 154 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }
      with union nut wrench:
      14 N*m{ 141 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }



6. INSTALL AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Install the air tube to the throttle body.

  1. Align the embossed mark of the throttle body with the paint mark of the No. 4 air hose.




  1. Tighten the hose clamp so that it is 7 to 11 mm (0.276 to 0.433 in.) from the end of the hose as shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    6.3 N*m{ 64 kgf*cm , 56 in.*lbf }



7. CONNECT NO. 2 ENGINE OIL LEVEL DIPSTICK GUIDE



  1. Connect the dipstick guide with the bolt.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



8. INSTALL INTERCOOLER ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the intercooler (Click here).



9. FILL BRAKE FLUID RESERVOIR

  1. Fill the reservoir with brake fluid.

    Brake Fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3



10. BLEED CLUTCH LINE



  1. Remove the release cylinder bleeder plug cap.

  1. Connect a vinyl tube to the bleeder plug.

  1. Depress the clutch pedal several times, and then loosen the bleeder plug while the pedal is depressed.

  1. When fluid no longer comes out, tighten the bleeder plug, and then release the clutch pedal.

  1. Repeat the previous 2 steps until all the air in the fluid is completely bled.

  1. Tighten the bleeder plug.

    Torque:
    11 N*m{ 110 kgf*cm , 8 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the bleeder plug cap.

  1. Check that all the air has been bled from the clutch line.



11. CHECK FLUID LEVEL IN RESERVOIR

  1. Check the fluid level.
    If the brake fluid level is low, check for leaks and inspect the disc brake pad. If necessary, refill the reservoir with brake fluid after repair or replacement.

    Brake fluid:
    SAE J1703 or FMVSS No. 116 DOT 3



12. INSPECT FOR CLUTCH FLUID LEAK


13. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



14. CHECK DIESEL THROTTLE BODY ASSEMBLY

  1. Inspect the diesel throttle valve.

    1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    2. Turn the engine switch off and wait 5 seconds.

      HINT:
      Be sure to turn off the engine switch before performing this inspection. The fully closed position of the diesel throttle valve is learned when the engine switch is turned off.

    3. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

    4. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    5. Turn the intelligent tester on.

    6. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Data List / All Data / Diesel Throttle Angle.

    7. Check that the value of "Diesel Throttle Angle" is within the specification.

      Standard Diesel Throttle Angle:
      Tester Display
      		Specified Condition
      Diesel Throttle Angle
      		Throttle Angle: -5 to 5%

      If the result is not as specified, replace the throttle body.

    8. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine / Data List / All Data / Throttle Close Learning Val.

    9. Check that the value of "Throttle Close Learning Val" is within the specification.

      Standard Throttle Valve Fully Closed:
      Tester Display
      		Specified Condition
      Throttle Close Learning Val.
      		14.25 to 21.25 deg

      If the result is not as specified, replace the throttle body.
