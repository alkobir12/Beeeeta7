FUEL SUPPLY PUMP > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Disconnect the cables from the negative (-) main battery and sub-battery terminals.



2. REMOVE THERMOSTAT

  1. Remove the thermostat (Click here).



3. REMOVE FUEL COOLER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the fuel cooler assembly (Click here).



4. REMOVE TIMING GEAR COVER INSULATOR



  1. w/o Viscous Heater:
    Remove the 2 bolts and timing gear cover insulator.




  1. w/ Viscous Heater:
    Remove the timing gear cover insulator.



5. REMOVE TIMING CHAIN COVER PLATE



  1. Remove the 4 bolts, cover plate and gasket.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL PIPE



  1. Remove the union bolt, fuel pipe and 2 gaskets.



7. REMOVE FUEL SUPPLY PUMP



  1. Using a wrench, hold the crankshaft.

    NOTICE:
    Do not turn the crankshaft counterclockwise.
    If it is turned counterclockwise, check that the crankshaft pulley set bolt is not loose. If loose, tighten the bolt (Click here).

    Torque:
    108 N*m{ 1101 kgf*cm , 80 ft.*lbf } or more




  1. Remove the set nut.




  1. Disconnect the fuel temperature sensor connector.




  1. Using a 6 mm hexagon wrench, remove the bolt.

  1. Loosen the 2 nuts.




  1. Disconnect the pump from the supply pump drive gear.

    1. Install SST (No. 1 adapter) to the timing chain cover.

      SST
      09950-50013   (09951-05010, 09952-05010, 09953-05020, 09954-05031, 09955-05010)

      NOTICE:
      Do not use any tools in this procedure.

    2. Rotate the supply pump gear so that the service holes are aligned with SST (No. 1 adapter) as shown in the illustration.

    3. Using SST, disconnect the pump from the supply pump drive gear.

      NOTICE:
      1. When removing or installing SST, do not push the supply pump gear.
      2. Apply lubricant to the threads and tip of SST (center bolt) before using it.

  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

    NOTICE:

    1. Do not hold the supply pump by the parts indicated by the arrows in the illustration, as this may result in deformation, damage, and fuel leakage.



    2. The pump must be kept horizontal.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the supply pump.
