1. INSPECT FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY


  1. Inspect the level warning switch.




    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1 - 2
      		Float upper end position
      		Below 1 Ω
      1 - 2
      		Float lower end position
      		1 MΩ or higher

      If the result is not as specified, replace the fuel filter assembly.



  1. Inspect the clogging switch.




    1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

      Standard Resistance:
      Tester Connection
      		Condition
      		Specified Condition
      1 - 2
      		20°C (68°F)
      		Below 1 Ω

      If the result is not as specified, replace the fuel filter assembly.
