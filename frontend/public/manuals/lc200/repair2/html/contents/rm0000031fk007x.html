FUEL COOLER (for Engine Room Side) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE COMMON RAIL ASSEMBLY LH AND RH

  1. Remove the common rail assembly LH and RH (Click here).



2. REMOVE NO. 1 INTAKE MANIFOLD INSULATOR





3. REMOVE NO. 2 INTAKE MANIFOLD INSULATOR





4. REMOVE INTAKE PIPE



  1. w/ EGR System:
    Remove the 2 nuts, 6 bolts, intake pipe and 2 gaskets.




  1. w/o EGR System:
    Remove the 2 nuts, 4 bolts, intake pipe and gasket.



5. DISCONNECT NO. 2 ENGINE WIRE



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and using a clip remover, detach the 2 wire harness clamps, and then disconnect the No. 2 engine wire.



6. REMOVE NO. 3 INTAKE MANIFOLD



  1. Remove the 16 bolts, No. 3 intake manifold and 2 gaskets.



7. REMOVE INTAKE MANIFOLD INSULATOR (w/ EGR System)





8. REMOVE EGR PIPE INSULATOR (w/ EGR System)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and EGR pipe insulator.



9. REMOVE EGR VALVE ASSEMBLY WITH EGR COOLER (w/ EGR System)



  1. Remove the 10 bolts, EGR valve with EGR cooler and 2 gaskets.




  1. Disconnect the No. 5 water by-pass hose from the water by-pass outlet.



10. REMOVE EGR COOLER INSULATOR (w/ EGR System)





11. REMOVE NO. 3 NOZZLE LEAKAGE PIPE



  1. Disconnect the No. 1 fuel hose from the fuel cooler.

  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 3 nozzle leakage pipe and gasket.

    NOTICE:
    After removing the nozzle leakage pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the nozzle leakage pipe, cover the nozzle leakage pipe ends with plastic bags.



12. REMOVE NO. 4 NOZZLE LEAKAGE PIPE



  1. Disconnect the No. 2 fuel hose from the fuel cooler.

  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 4 nozzle leakage pipe and gasket.

    NOTICE:
    After removing the nozzle leakage pipe, to prevent dirt or foreign objects from entering the nozzle leakage pipe, cover the nozzle leakage pipe ends with plastic bags.



13. DISCONNECT NO. 6 WATER BY-PASS HOSE





14. DISCONNECT NO. 3 FUEL HOSE



  1. Disconnect the fuel hose from the fuel cooler.



15. REMOVE FUEL COOLER ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and fuel cooler.



16. DISCONNECT NO. 7 WATER BY-PASS HOSE



  1. Disconnect the water by-pass hose from the fuel cooler.
