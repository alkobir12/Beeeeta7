1. INSTALL SCAVENGING PUMP ASSEMBLY



  1. Install the scavenging pump with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



2. INSTALL REAR CRANKSHAFT OIL SEAL



  1. Place the oil seal retainer on wooden blocks.

  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in a new oil seal as shown in the illustration.

    SST
    09223-56010  

    Standard depth A:
    2.7 to 3.7 mm (0.106 to 0.147 in.)

    NOTICE:
    1. Keep the lip free from foreign matter.
    2. Do not tap on the seal at an angle.



3. INSTALL REAR ENGINE OIL SEAL RETAINER



  1. Apply seal packing in a continuous line as shown in the illustration.

    Seal diameter:
    3.0 to 5.0 mm (0.118 to 0.197 in.)

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any oil from the contact surface.
    2. Install the rear engine oil seal retainer within 3 minutes and tighten the bolts within 15 minutes after applying seal packing.

  1. Apply engine oil to the oil seal lip.

  1. Make sure that the lip of the oil seal is properly installed.




  1. Install and uniformly tighten the 10 bolts in the order shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. When installing the oil seal retainer, make sure the lip of the oil seal is not damaged.
    2. When installing the oil seal retainer, make sure the lip of the oil seal is not folded incorrectly.



4. INSTALL NO. 1 OIL PAN SUB-ASSEMBLY


NOTICE:
When the contact surfaces shown below are wet, wipe them with an oil-free cloth before applying seal packing.







  1. Install the cylinder block oil hole gasket.




  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to 3 new O-rings and set the 3 O-rings to the oil regulator and scavenging pump.




  1. Apply seal packing to the No. 1 oil pan as shown in the illustration.

    Standard seal diameter:
    3.5 to 4.0 mm (0.134 to 0.157 in.)

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    1. For the 3 bolt holes labeled with arrows, apply the seal packing on the outer edges. For the other bolt holes, apply the seal packing on the inner edges.
    2. Make sure to apply a continuous bead of seal packing as shown in the illustration. If the bead breaks, overlap 3 mm (0.118 in.) of seal packing and continue the bead.
    3. Remove any oil and old seal packing from the contact surface.
    4. Install the No. 1 oil pan within 3 minutes and tighten the bolts and nuts within 15 minutes after applying seal packing.
    5. Do not start the engine for at least 2 hours after installing.




  1. Install the No. 1 oil pan and oil reflector plate with the 20 bolts and 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    25 N*m{ 250 kgf*cm , 18 ft.*lbf }

    Bolt Length 
    Item
    		Quantity
    		Length
    Bolt A
    		7
    		70 mm (2.76 in.)
    Bolt B
    		8
    		20 mm (0.787 in.)
    Bolt C
    		5
    		30 mm (1.18 in.)



5. INSTALL OIL STRAINER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to a new O-ring, and install it to the oil strainer.

  1. Install the oil strainer with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



6. INSTALL NO. 2 OIL PAN SUB-ASSEMBLY


NOTICE:
When the contact surfaces shown below are wet, wipe them with an oil-free cloth before applying seal packing.







  1. Apply seal packing to the No. 2 oil pan as shown in the illustration.

    Standard seal diameter:
    5.0 to 6.0 mm (0.197 to 0.236 in.)

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    1. For the bolt and pin holes, make sure to apply seal packing to the inner sides.
    2. Make sure to apply a continuous bead of seal packing as shown in the illustration. If the bead breaks, overlap 3.0 mm (0.118 in.) of seal packing and continue the bead.
    3. Remove any oil from the contact surface.
    4. Install the No. 2 oil pan within 3 minutes and tighten the bolts and nuts within 10 minutes after applying seal packing.
    5. Do not start the engine for at least 2 hours after installing.




  1. Install the No. 2 oil pan with the 10 bolts and 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



7. INSTALL ENGINE OIL LEVEL SENSOR



  1. Install a new gasket and the sensor with the 4 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



8. INSTALL OIL FILTER BRACKET SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to 2 new O-rings, and set them to the oil filter bracket.




  1. Install the oil filter bracket with the 3 bolts and 2 nuts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the 2 wire harness clamps.



9. INSTALL OIL FILTER ELEMENT



  1. Clean the inside of the oil filter cap, its threads and its O-ring groove.

  1. Apply a small amount of engine oil to a new O-ring and install it to the oil filter cap.

  1. Set a new oil filter element in the oil filter cap.

  1. Remove any dirt or foreign matter from the installation surface of the engine.

  1. Apply a small amount of engine oil to the O-ring again and temporarily install the oil filter cap.




  1. Using SST, tighten the oil filter cap.

    SST
    09228-06501  

    Torque:
    25 N*m{ 255 kgf*cm , 18 ft.*lbf }

    NOTICE:
    1. Make sure that the oil filter is installed securely as shown in the illustration.
    2. Be careful that the O-ring does not get caught between any surrounding parts.




  1. Install the No. 2 engine under cover seal to the No. 2 engine under cover with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



10. INSTALL TIMING GEAR COVER SPACER



  1. Install the timing gear cover spacer with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



11. INSTALL CRANKSHAFT PULLEY

NOTICE:
This procedure is intended for removal/installation the crankshaft pulley only. Do not procedure for removal/installation the flywheel and the drive plate and ring gear.




  1. Align the crankshaft pulley and the crankshaft knock pin, and temporarily install the crankshaft pulley with the 3 bolts.

  1. Install the 2 bolts to the bolt holes of the crankshaft rear side.

  1. Using a bar, turn the crankshaft until the crankshaft pulley service hole is a little to the right of bottom dead center.

  1. Install a 14 mm x 1.5 pitch service bolt with a length of 70 mm or more to the crankshaft pulley service hole, and hold the crankshaft using the timing chain cover protrusion.




  1. Uniformly tighten the 3 bolts in 2 passes in the order shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    115 N*m{ 1168 kgf*cm , 84 ft.*lbf }

  1. Remove the service bolt.

    Torque:

    43 N*m{ 438 kgf*cm , 32 ft.*lbf }

    Bolt B

    Bolt C
    		2

  1. Install the No. 1 idler pulley with the bolt.

    Torque:
    43 N*m{ 438 kgf*cm , 32 ft.*lbf }




  1. Install the V-ribbed belt tensioner bracket with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



13. CONNECT NO. 2 OIL COOLER HOSE



  1. Align the white paint marks on the oil cooler hose and oil filter bracket and connect the hose.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure to maintain a space between the oil cooler hose and crankshaft pulley and the oil cooler hose and V-ribbed belt idler pulley.



14. CONNECT NO. 1 OIL COOLER HOSE

  1. Face the pink paint mark on the oil cooler hose toward the front side of the engine and connect the hose to the oil filter bracket.

    NOTICE:
    Make sure to maintain a space between the oil cooler hose and crankshaft pulley.



15. INSTALL STIFFENER INSULATOR RH



  1. Install the stiffener insulator RH with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



16. INSTALL NO. 2 INLET TURBO OIL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Install a new gasket and connect the No. 2 inlet turbo oil pipe with the union bolt.

    Torque:
    29 N*m{ 296 kgf*cm , 21 ft.*lbf }



17. CONNECT NO. 2 OUTLET TURBO OIL HOSE

HINT:

Align the white paint marks on the oil hose and pipe, and connect the hose.



18. INSTALL NO. 1 ENGINE OIL LEVEL DIPSTICK GUIDE



  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to a new O-ring, and install it to the No. 1 engine oil level dipstick guide.

  1. Install the No. 1 engine oil level dipstick guide with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }



19. CONNECT NO. 1 OUTLET TURBO OIL HOSE





20. INSTALL GENERATOR ASSEMBLY



  1. Temporarily install the generator with the 3 bolts and 2 nuts.

  1. Uniformly tighten the 3 bolts and 2 nuts in the order shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }




  1. Connect the generator cable and install the nut and bolt.

    Torque:
    for bolt:
    13 N*m{ 133 kgf*cm , 10 ft.*lbf }
    for nut (130A Type and 150A Type:):
    9.8 N*m{ 100 kgf*cm , 87 in.*lbf }
    for nut (180A Type:):
    12 N*m{ 122 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



21. INSTALL NO. 1 INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR PIPE



  1. Install the No. 1 intake air connector pipe with the bolt. Then tighten the hose clamp.

    Torque:
    for bolt:
    21 N*m{ 214 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }
    for clamp:
    6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }




  1. Connect the 4 connectors and attach the 5 wire harness clamps.
