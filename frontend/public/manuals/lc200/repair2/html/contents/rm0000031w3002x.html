1. INSTALL FRONT CRANKSHAFT OIL SEAL

  1. Apply engine oil to the lip of a new oil seal.




  1. Using SST and a hammer, tap in the oil seal as shown in the illustration.

    SST
    09316-12010  
    09950-70010   (09951-07100)
    09950-60020   (09951-00890)

    Standard depth A:
    0.6 to 1.4 mm (0.0236 to 0.0551 in.)

    NOTICE:
    Make sure that the lip of the oil seal is properly installed.



2. INSTALL CRANKSHAFT PULLEY SUB-ASSEMBLY

NOTICE:
This procedure is intended for removal/installation the crankshaft pulley only. Do not procedure for removal/installation the flywheel and the drive plate and ring gear.




  1. Align the crankshaft pulley and the crankshaft knock pin, and temporarily install the crankshaft pulley with the 3 bolts.

  1. Install the 2 bolts to the bolt holes of the crankshaft rear side.

  1. Using a bar, turn the crankshaft until the crankshaft pulley service hole is a little to the right of bottom dead center.

  1. Install a 14 mm x 1.5 pitch service bolt with a length of 70 mm or more to the crankshaft pulley service hole, and hold the crankshaft using the timing chain cover protrusion.




  1. Uniformly tighten the 3 bolts in 2 passes in the order shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    115 N*m{ 1168 kgf*cm , 84 ft.*lbf }

  1. Remove the service bolt.
