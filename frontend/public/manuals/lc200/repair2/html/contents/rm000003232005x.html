1. INSPECT ENGINE OIL LEVEL SENSOR

  1. Heat the switch to higher than 60°C (140°F) in an oil bath.




  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - 2
    		ON at 20°C (68°F)
    		Below 1 Ω
    OFF at 20°C (68°F)
    		Below 1 Ω
    ON at 60°C (140°F)
    		Below 1 Ω
    OFF at 60°C (140°F)
    		10 kΩ or higher

    If the result is not as specified, replace the engine oil level sensor.
