DTC    P0606    ECM / PCM Processor

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The ECM continuously monitors its main and sub CPUs. This self-check ensures that the ECM is functioning properly. If outputs from the CPUs are different and deviate from the standard, the ECM will illuminate the MIL and store a DTC immediately.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
P0606
When either condition below is met:
  1. An ECM main CPU error.
  2. An ECM sub CPU error.
ECM

INSPECTION PROCEDURE

HINT:
Read freeze frame data using the intelligent tester. Freeze frame data records the engine condition when malfunctions are detected. When troubleshooting, freeze frame data can help determine if the vehicle was moving or stationary, if the engine was warmed up or not, if the air-fuel ratio was lean or rich, and other data from the time the malfunction occurred.

1.CHECK DTC

  1. Clear DTC (Click here).

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal and wait for 1 minute.

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

  1. Check for DTC.
