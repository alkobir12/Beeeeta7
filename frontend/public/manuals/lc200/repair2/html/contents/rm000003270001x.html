WINCH UNIT > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL

  1. for Gasoline Engine:
    Remove the upper radiator support seal (Click here).

  1. for Diesel Engine:
    Remove the upper radiator support seal (Click here).



5. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER WINCH COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY


6. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Put protective tape around the radiator grille.

  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 8 claws, and remove the radiator grille.



7. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER COVER


  1. Put protective tape around the bumper cover.





  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 6 screws.



  1. Remove the 3 clips, 4 screws and 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 10 claws.

  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Fog Light:
    Disconnect the winch control wire connector and remove the bumper cover.



  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System or w/ Fog Light:
    Disconnect the No. 4 engine room wire connector and winch control wire connector and remove the bumper cover.





8. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER EXTENSION LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bumper extension.



9. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER EXTENSION RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



10. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. for Standard:
    Remove the headlight assembly LH (Click here).

  1. for HID Headlight:
    Remove the headlight assembly LH (Click here).



11. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



12. REMOVE FRONT UPPER CENTER BUMPER RETAINER



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bumper retainer.



13. REMOVE RADIATOR SIDE DEFLECTOR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips, and remove the side deflector.



14. REMOVE RADIATOR SIDE DEFLECTOR RH (w/o Oil Cooler)



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips, and remove the side deflector.



15. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL COOLER AIR DUCT (w/ Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 4 bolts and oil cooler air duct.



16. REMOVE WINCH CONTROL WIRE



  1. Remove the 2 nuts and winch control wire.



17. REMOVE WINCH ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 9 bolts and mounting bracket.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and move the cover in the direction indicated by the arrow shown in the illustration.




  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the nut and disconnect the wire.




  1. Using a crane or equivalent, remove the winch assembly.

    CAUTION:
    Be careful as the winch assembly is very heavy.
