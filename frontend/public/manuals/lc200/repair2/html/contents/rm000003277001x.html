1. INSPECT WINCH MAIN RELAY



  1. Push in the plunger and release it. Check that it returns quickly to its original position.




  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A - B
    		Battery voltage is not applied to terminals 1 and 2
    		10 MΩ or higher
    Battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2
    		Below 1 Ω

    If the result is not as specified, replace the winch main relay.
