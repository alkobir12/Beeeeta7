EXHAUST MANIFOLD W/ TURBOCHARGER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Disconnect the cables from the negative (-) main battery and sub-battery terminals.



2. REMOVE ENGINE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the engine assembly from the vehicle (Click here).



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE OIL LEVEL DIPSTICK GUIDE (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 1 engine oil level dipstick guide.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the No. 1 engine oil level dipstick.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 INTAKE AIR CONNECTOR PIPE (for Bank 1)



  1. Disconnect the 4 connectors.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 5 wire harness clamps.




  1. Loosen the hose clamp, and remove the bolt and No. 1 intake air connector pipe.



5. DISCONNECT NO. 2 VENTILATION HOSE (for Bank 1)





6. DISCONNECT NO. 2 TURBO WATER HOSE (for Bank 1)





7. DISCONNECT BREATHER PLUG RH (for Bank 1)





8. REMOVE NO. 1 EXHAUST MANIFOLD HEAT INSULATOR (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and No. 1 exhaust manifold heat insulator.



9. DISCONNECT NO. 1 OUTLET TURBO OIL HOSE (for Bank 1)





10. REMOVE NO. 1 TURBO WATER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 1)



  1. Disconnect the water hose.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts, 2 nuts, No. 1 turbo water pipe and gasket.



11. REMOVE NO. 1 TURBOCHARGER STAY (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 1 turbocharger stay.



12. REMOVE NO. 1 VENTILATION TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 1)



  1. Disconnect the vacuum hose.

  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 1 ventilation tube and gasket.



13. REMOVE NO. 1 TURBOCHARGER SUB-ASSEMBLY WITH EXHAUST MANIFOLD RH (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the union bolt and gasket, and disconnect the No. 1 inlet turbo oil pipe from the cylinder block.




  1. Remove the 10 nuts, 10 collars and No. 1 turbocharger with exhaust manifold RH.




  1. Remove the gasket.




  1. Remove the 2 nuts and bolt, and separate the No. 1 turbocharger and exhaust manifold RH.

  1. Remove the gasket from the No. 1 turbocharger.



14. REMOVE NO. 1 INLET COMPRESSOR ELBOW (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, No. 1 inlet compressor elbow and gasket.



15. REMOVE NO. 1 TURBO OIL PIPE (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, No. 1 turbo oil pipe and gasket.



16. REMOVE NO. 1 INLET TURBO OIL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 1 inlet turbo oil pipe and gasket.



17. DISCONNECT NO. 3 VENTILATION HOSE (for Bank 2)





18. DISCONNECT BREATHER PLUG LH (for Bank 2)





19. DISCONNECT NO. 2 TURBO WATER HOSE (for Bank 2)





20. REMOVE NO. 2 EXHAUST MANIFOLD HEAT INSULATOR (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and No. 2 exhaust manifold heat insulator.



21. REMOVE NO. 3 TURBO WATER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, union bolt, No. 3 turbo water pipe and gasket.



22. REMOVE FRONT WATER BY-PASS JOINT (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the front water by-pass joint and gasket.



23. REMOVE NO. 2 TURBO WATER PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 2)



  1. Disconnect the water hose.

  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 2 turbo water pipe and gasket.



24. DISCONNECT NO. 2 OUTLET TURBO OIL HOSE (for Bank 2)





25. REMOVE NO. 2 TURBOCHARGER STAY (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and No. 2 turbocharger stay.



26. REMOVE NO. 2 VENTILATION TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 2)



  1. Disconnect the No. 2 vacuum transmitting hose.

  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 2 ventilation tube and gasket.



27. REMOVE NO. 2 TURBOCHARGER SUB-ASSEMBLY WITH EXHAUST MANIFOLD LH (for Bank 2)



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors from the turbocharger.




  1. Remove the union bolt and gasket, and disconnect the No. 2 inlet turbo oil pipe.




  1. Remove the 10 nuts, 10 collars and No. 2 turbocharger with exhaust manifold LH.




  1. Remove the gasket.




  1. Remove the 3 nuts, and separate the No. 2 turbocharger and exhaust manifold LH.

  1. Remove the gasket from the turbocharger.



28. REMOVE NO. 2 INLET COMPRESSOR ELBOW (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, No. 2 inlet compressor elbow and gasket.



29. REMOVE NO. 2 TURBO OIL PIPE (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, No. 2 turbo oil pipe and gasket.



30. REMOVE NO. 2 INLET TURBO OIL PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the union bolt, bolt, No. 2 inlet turbo oil pipe and gasket.



31. REPLACE STUD BOLT

HINT:
If a stud bolt is deformed or the threads are damaged, replace it.

  1. for No. 1 Turbocharger:
    Using an E10 "TORX" wrench, replace the stud bolts labeled A and B shown in the illustration below.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }



  1. for No. 1 Turbocharger:
    Replace the stud bolts labeled C shown in the illustration below.

    Torque:
    6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }







  1. for No. 2 Turbocharger:
    Using an E10 "TORX" wrench, replace the stud bolt.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }




  1. for Exhaust Manifold RH:
    Using an E10 "TORX" wrench, replace the stud bolt.

    Torque:
    20 N*m{ 204 kgf*cm , 15 ft.*lbf }
