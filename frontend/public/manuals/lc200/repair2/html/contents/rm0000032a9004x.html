4. DISCONNECT NO. 2 OIL COOLER HOSE


5. REMOVE CRANKSHAFT PULLEY

NOTICE:

These procedures is intended for removal/installation the crankshaft pulley only. Do not procedures for removal/installation the flywheel and drive plate and ring gear.

  1. Install the 2 bolts to the bolt holes of the crankshaft rear side.

  1. Using a bar, turn the crankshaft until the crankshaft pulley service hole is a little to the left of bottom dead center.

  1. Install a 14 mm x 1.5 pitch service bolt with a length of 70 mm or more to the crankshaft pulley service hole, and hold the crankshaft using the timing chain cover protrusion.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and crankshaft pulley.

    NOTICE:
    If the crankshaft pulley cannot be removed, temporarily install a bolt to the crankshaft so that the pulley does not fall, and lightly tap the outer edge of the mass damper with a plastic-faced hammer to remove the pulley. Do not tap the pulley V-ribbed belt ribs, as the crankshaft may be damaged.



6. REMOVE TIMING GEAR COVER SPACER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and timing gear cover spacer.



7. REMOVE OIL FILTER CAP WITH ELEMENT



  1. Remove the oil filter drain plug.




  1. Connect a hose with an inside diameter of 15 mm (0.591 in.) to the pipe.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the oil filter drain plug and install it to the drain pipe.




  1. Install the oil filter drain pipe to the oil filter cap and drain the engine oil.

  1. Remove the oil filter drain pipe.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to a new O-ring, and install it to the oil filter drain plug.




  1. Install the oil filter drain plug to the oil filter cap.

    Torque:
    13 N*m{ 127 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }




  1. Using SST, remove the oil filter cap.

    SST
    09228-06501  

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the draining oil. After the oil filter cap is loosened approximately 4 turns and the cap ribs are vertical, engine oil drains from the gap between the oil filter cap and oil pan.




  1. Remove the oil filter element and O-ring from the oil filter cap.

    NOTICE:
    Be sure to remove the cap O-ring by hand, without using any tools, to prevent damage to the cap O-ring groove.

  1. Remove the cylinder block oil hole gasket.

33. REMOVE FUEL INJECTOR RH (for Bank 1)



  1. Remove the union bolt, 4 hollow screws, 5 gaskets and nozzle leakage pipe.

    NOTICE:
    1. When removing the nozzle leakage pipe, place a cushion under the pipe.
    2. Be careful not to deform or scratch the union seal surface.
    3. After removing the nozzle leakage pipe, put it in a plastic bag to prevent foreign matter from contaminating its injector inlet.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts, 4 washers, 4 nozzle holder clamps and 4 injectors.

    HINT:
    Arrange the injectors, holder clamps, washers and bolts in the correct order.

  1. Remove the O-ring from each injector.

  1. Remove the 4 injection nozzle seats from the cylinder head.



34. REMOVE FUEL INJECTOR LH (for Bank 2)



  1. Remove the union bolt, 4 hollow screws, 5 gaskets and No. 2 nozzle leakage pipe.

    NOTICE:
    1. When removing the nozzle leakage pipe, place a cushion under the pipe.
    2. Be careful not to deform or scratch the union seal surface.
    3. After removing the nozzle leakage pipe, put it in a plastic bag to prevent foreign matter from contaminating its injector inlet.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts, 4 washers, 4 nozzle holder clamps and 4 injectors.

    HINT:
    Arrange the injectors, holder clamps, washers and bolts in the correct order.

  1. Remove the O-ring from each injector.

  1. Remove the 4 injection nozzle seats from the cylinder head.

  1. Place the timing chain cover on wooden blocks.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



39. SET NO. 1 CYLINDER TO TDC/COMPRESSION

  1. Temporarily install the 2 crankshaft pulley set bolts to the crankshaft.

  1. Turn the crankshaft clockwise to set the No. 1 cylinder to TDC.



  1. With the crankshaft key 45° counterclockwise from the top position, check that the timing marks of the RH and LH camshaft timing gears are aligned. If not as specified, turn the crankshaft 1 revolution (360°) and align the timing marks as shown below.



  1. Remove the 2 crankshaft pulley set bolts.

51. REMOVE IDLE GEAR ASSEMBLY

    SST
    09960-10010   (09962-01000, 09963-00700)

79. REPLACE STUD BOLT

HINT:

If the stud bolt is deformed or the threads are damaged, replace it.

  1. Intake Pipe:

    1. Using an E8 "TORX" wrench, replace the 4 stud bolts.



  1. No. 3 Intake Manifold:




    1. Using an E8 "TORX" wrench, replace the 2 stud bolts labeled A.

      Torque:
      6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }

    2. Using an E6 "TORX" wrench, replace the 2 stud bolts labeled B.

      Torque:
      5.0 N*m{ 51 kgf*cm , 44 in.*lbf }

    3. Using an E6 "TORX" wrench, replace the stud bolt labeled C.

      Torque:
      4.0 N*m{ 41 kgf*cm , 35 in.*lbf }



  1. Oil Filter Bracket:




    1. Using an E8 "TORX" wrench, replace the 2 stud bolts.

      Torque:
      6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 63 in.*lbf }



  1. No. 1 Oil Pan:




    1. Using an E8 "TORX" wrench, replace the 2 stud bolts labeled A.

      Torque:
      6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }

    2. Using an E7 "TORX" wrench, replace the 2 stud bolts labeled B.

      Torque:
      5.2 N*m{ 53 kgf*cm , 46 in.*lbf }



  1. Timing Chain Cover:




    1. Using an E7 "TORX" wrench, replace the 2 stud bolts labeled A.

      Torque:
      6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }

    2. Replace the 2 stud bolts labeled B.

      Torque:
      12 N*m{ 120 kgf*cm , 9 ft.*lbf }



  1. Timing Gear Case:




    1. Using an E7 "TORX" wrench, replace the 4 stud bolts.

      Torque:
      6.0 N*m{ 61 kgf*cm , 53 in.*lbf }




    2. Remove the 2 stud bolts.

    3. Remove the adhesive from the threads of the timing gear case.

    4. Apply adhesive to 2 or more threads of the 2 stud bolts.

    5. Install the 2 stud bolts.

      Torque:
      10 N*m{ 102 kgf*cm , 7 ft.*lbf }
