CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DRAIN CLUTCH FLUID


2. DISCONNECT CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER TO FLEXIBLE HOSE TUBE



  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.



3. DISCONNECT CLUTCH RESERVOIR TUBE



  1. Using pliers, grip the claws of the clip, slide the clip and disconnect the clutch reservoir tube.

    HINT:
    Use a container to catch the fluid.



4. REMOVE CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER ASSEMBLY



  1. Loosen the clutch pedal bracket bolt.




  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  1. Disconnect the clutch pedal from the clevis, and remove the master cylinder.

  1. Remove the gasket.
