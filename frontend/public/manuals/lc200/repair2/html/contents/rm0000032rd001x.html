for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE CLUTCH RELEASE CYLINDER KIT

  1. Remove the boot from the cylinder body.

  1. Remove the push rod from the cylinder body.




  1. Using compressed air, remove the piston together with the spring from the cylinder body.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the inside of the cylinder body.

  1. Remove the 2 cylinder cups from the piston.



2. REMOVE RELEASE CYLINDER BLEEDER PLUG

  1. Remove the bleeder plug from the cylinder body.
