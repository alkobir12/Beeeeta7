CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER (for RHD) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE CLUTCH RESERVOIR GROMMET



  1. Fix the master cylinder in a vise.

  1. Using a pin punch and hammer, tap out the slotted spring pin.

  1. Remove the inlet union and grommet.



2. REMOVE CLUTCH MASTER CYLINDER PUSH ROD



  1. Loosen the lock nut, and then remove the clevis.

  1. Remove the lock nut from the push rod.

  1. Remove the boot.

  1. Using snap ring pliers, remove the snap ring and plate.

  1. Remove the push rod.



3. REMOVE PISTON AND SPRING

  1. Remove the piston and spring from the master cylinder.
