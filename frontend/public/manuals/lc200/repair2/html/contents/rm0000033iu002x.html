OIL LEVEL SENSOR > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE OIL FILLER CAP SUB-ASSEMBLY


2. REMOVE NO. 2 ENGINE UNDER COVER



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and under cover.



3. DRAIN ENGINE OIL



  1. Remove the oil drain plug and gasket, and drain the engine oil into a container.



4. REMOVE ENGINE OIL LEVEL SENSOR



  1. Disconnect the oil level sensor connector.

  1. Remove the 4 bolts and oil level sensor.




  1. Cut off the gasket as shown in the illustration.

  1. Remove the gasket from the oil level sensor.

    HINT:
    After cutting away the parts of the gasket shown in the illustration, remove only the outer part of the gasket.
