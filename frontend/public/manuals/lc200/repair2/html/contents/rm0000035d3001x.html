1.POINTS TO NOTE WHEN SERVICING

  1. The maximum steering angle setting must be carried out with the engine start. Apply the parking brake, depress the brake pedal, move the shift lever to N, and ensure that the vehicle is not moving.

  2. Pay attention to the following points when servicing.

    1. When disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, "SYSTEM INITIALIZING" appears on the multi-display. In this case, correct the steering angle and steering angle neutral point, and adjust the line display position (Click here).

    2. When replacing the parking assist ECU, "SYSTEM NOT READY" appears on the multi-display. In this case, set the maximum steering angle and steering angle neutral point, and adjust the line display position (Click here).

    3. When the rear television camera is replaced or its installation angle changes, readjustment of the line display position is required (Click here).
