1. INSTALL TRANSMITTER BATTERY

HINT:
  1. Type A: for Australia.
  2. Type B: except Australia.




  1. Install the battery (lithium battery: CR1632) with the positive (+) side facing upward, as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not bend the transmitter battery electrode during insertion.
    2. Keep the transmitter cover interior free of dust and oil.




  1. Install the transmitter housing cover by pressing down on it as shown in the illustration.




  1. Insert the Emergency key into the transmitter.

  1. Press one of the transmitter switches and check that the LED illuminates.
