AIR SWITCHING VALVE (w/ Secondary Air Injection System) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR LH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER APRON SEAL REAR RH



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 4 clips and fender apron seal.



3. REMOVE INTAKE MANIFOLD

  1. Remove the intake manifold (Click here).



4. REMOVE WATER BY-PASS PIPE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Disconnect the wire harness clamp from the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Disconnect the 2 vacuum switching valve hoses from the water by-pass pipe clamp.

  1. Remove the bolt.

  1. Pull out the water by-pass pipe.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the water by-pass pipe.



5. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR INJECTION SYSTEM HOSE



  1. Remove the No. 2 air injection system hose from the rear water by-pass joint and air switching valve.



6. REMOVE AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the air pressure sensor connector.

  1. Disconnect the air switching valve connector.

  1. Remove the vacuum hose from the air pressure sensor and air switching valve.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and air pressure sensor from the air switching valve.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and air switching valve from the air pump bracket.

  1. Remove the air switching valve from the No. 1 air injection system hose.



7. DISCONNECT NO. 1 WATER BY-PASS PIPE (w/o Air Cooled Transmission Oil Cooler)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the water by-pass pipe from the cylinder head cover RH.



8. REMOVE AIR TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and air tube sub-assembly.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets.



9. REMOVE NO. 3 AIR TUBE



  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 nuts and No. 3 air tube.

  1. Remove the 2 gaskets.



10. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the vacuum switching valve hose from the No. 2 air switching valve.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts, gasket and No. 2 air switching valve from the rear water by-pass joint.



11. REMOVE NO. 1 AIR SWITCHING VALVE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the vacuum switching valve hose from the No. 1 air switching valve.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts, gasket and No. 1 air switching valve from the rear water by-pass joint.
