DTC    B1603/83    Front Satellite Sensor Bus RH Initialization Incomplete

DTC    B1612/83    Lost Communication with Front Airbag Sensor RH

DTC    B1613/83    Front Airbag Sensor RH Initialization Incomplete

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

The front airbag sensor RH circuit consists of the center airbag sensor and front airbag sensor RH.
The front airbag sensor RH detects impacts to the vehicle and sends signals to the center airbag sensor to determine if the airbag should be deployed.
DTC B1603/83, B1612/83 or B1613/83 is stored when a malfunction is detected in the front airbag sensor RH circuit.

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
B1603/83
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. The center airbag sensor receives a line short circuit signal, an open circuit signal, a short circuit to ground signal or a short circuit to B+ signal in the front airbag sensor RH circuit for 2 seconds.
  2. A front airbag sensor RH malfunction.
  3. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. Instrument panel wire
  2. Engine room main wire
  3. Front airbag sensor RH
  4. Center airbag sensor assembly
B1612/83
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. The center airbag sensor receives a line short circuit signal, an open circuit signal, a short circuit to ground signal or a short circuit to B+ signal in the front airbag sensor RH circuit for 2 seconds.
  2. A front airbag sensor RH malfunction.
  3. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. Instrument panel wire
  2. Engine room main wire
  3. Front airbag sensor RH
  4. Center airbag sensor assembly
B1613/83
When one of the following conditions is met:
  1. The center airbag sensor receives a line short circuit signal, an open circuit signal, a short circuit to ground signal or a short circuit to B+ signal in the front airbag sensor RH circuit for 2 seconds.
  2. A front airbag sensor RH malfunction.
  3. A center airbag sensor malfunction.
  1. Instrument panel wire

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal while performing repairs, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

1.CHECK CONNECTION OF CONNECTORS

  1. Turn the engine switch off.

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal, and wait for at least 90 seconds.

  1. Check that the connectors are properly connected to the center airbag sensor and the front airbag sensor RH.

    OK:
    The connectors are properly connected.

  1. Check that the connectors (on the center airbag sensor side and front airbag sensor RH side) are not damaged.
