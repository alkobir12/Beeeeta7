FRONT BUMPER (for Standard) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL (for HID Headlight)

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL


  1. Remove the 7 clips and radiator support seal.





5. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Put protective tape around the radiator grille.

  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 8 claws, and remove the radiator grille.



6. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER COVER

HINT:
For the front bumper cover with garnish, use the procedures described below.



  1. Put protective tape around the bumper cover.





  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 6 screws.



  1. Remove the 3 clips, 4 screws and 4 bolts.

  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Fog Light:
    Detach the 10 claws and remove the bumper cover.



  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System or w/ Fog Light:
    Detach the 10 claws, disconnect the No. 4 engine room wire connector and remove the bumper cover.






  1. w/ Headlight Cleaner System:
    Disconnect the headlight cleaner hose and remove the bumper cover.



7. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER ENERGY ABSORBER



  1. Remove the energy absorber.



8. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER REINFORCEMENT SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 6 nuts and reinforcement.



9. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 BUMPER EXTENSION SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 4 nuts and bumper extension.



10. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 BUMPER EXTENSION SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



11. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER SIDE SUPPORT LH



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 clips and remove the side support.



12. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER SIDE SUPPORT RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



13. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. for Standard:
    Remove the headlight assembly LH (Click here).

  1. for HID Headlight:
    Remove the headlight assembly LH (Click here).



14. REMOVE HEADLIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



15. REMOVE FRONT UPPER CENTER BUMPER RETAINER



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bumper retainer.
