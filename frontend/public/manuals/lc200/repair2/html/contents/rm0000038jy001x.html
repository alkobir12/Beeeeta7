FRONT BUMPER (for Standard) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE NO. 4 ENGINE ROOM WIRE


  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/ Fog Light:
    Remove the No. 4 engine room wire.

    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.



    2. Detach the 9 clamps and remove the engine room wire.





  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/ Fog Light:
    Remove the No. 4 engine room wire.

    1. Disconnect the 4 connectors.



    2. Detach the 9 clamps and remove the engine room wire.





  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Fog Light:
    Remove the No. 4 engine room wire.

    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.



    2. Detach the 9 clamps and remove the engine room wire.





2. REMOVE NO. 1 ULTRASONIC SENSOR (w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System)

HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the No. 1 ultrasonic sensor on the other side.




  1. While pushing down on the lever with a finger to release the one claw, detach the claw on the other side to remove the ultrasonic sensor.

    HINT:
    The type of claw on the top and bottom of the sensor are different. Observe the claws carefully before removing the sensor.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 ULTRASONIC SENSOR RETAINER (w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System)

HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the No. 1 ultrasonic sensor retainer on the other side.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the ultrasonic sensor retainer from the front bumper cover.



4. REMOVE FOG LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Fog Light)



  1. Detach the 2 clamps.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and fog light.



5. REMOVE FOG LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH (w/ Fog Light)

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



6. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER HOLE COVER LH (w/o Fog Light)



  1. Remove the 3 screws and bumper hole cover.



7. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER HOLE COVER RH (w/o Fog Light)

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



8. REMOVE LOWER RADIATOR GRILLE LH



  1. w/ Headlight Cleaner System:
    Detach the 2 headlight cleaner hose clamps.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the lower radiator grille.



9. REMOVE LOWER RADIATOR GRILLE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



10. REMOVE TYPE 1 HEADLIGHT WASHER NOZZLE SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Headlight Cleaner System)

HINT:
Use the same procedures for the RH side and the LH side.




  1. Extend the headlight washer nozzle.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the washer nozzle.



11. REMOVE TYPE 1 HEADLIGHT WASHER NOZZLE SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (w/ Headlight Cleaner System)

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



12. REMOVE HEADLIGHT WASHER ACTUATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Headlight Cleaner System)

HINT:
Use the same procedures for the RH side and the LH side.




  1. Disconnect the headlight washer hose.




  1. Remove the bolt.

  1. Disengage the 2 claws and remove the headlight washer actuator sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.



13. REMOVE HEADLIGHT WASHER ACTUATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY RH (w/ Headlight Cleaner System)

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



14. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER EXTENSION MOUNTING BRACKET



  1. for Type A:
    Remove the front bumper extension mounting bracket.

    1. Remove the 2 bolts and bracket.




  1. for Type B:
    Remove the front bumper extension mounting bracket.

    1. Remove the 2 bolts.

    2. Detach the 2 claws and remove the bracket.



15. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER GUARD (w/ Garnish)



  1. Remove the 4 nuts.

  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the bumper guard.



16. REMOVE FRONT BUMPER PROTECTOR



  1. Remove the 2 bumper protectors.



17. REMOVE FRONT NO. 2 BUMPER PROTECTOR



  1. Remove the 2 bumper protectors.
