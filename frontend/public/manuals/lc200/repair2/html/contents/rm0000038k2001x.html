REAR BUMPER (for Standard) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER HINGE BRACKET (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the bracket.



2. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  1. Detach the clip and remove the cover.



3. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Tire Carrier)

  1. Remove the spare tire.



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and spare wheel carrier.





4. REMOVE REAR QUARTER PANEL MUDGUARD LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 6 screws and mudguard.



5. REMOVE REAR QUARTER PANEL MUDGUARD RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 7 screws and mudguard.



6. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 BUMPER PLATE

  1. Put protective tape around the bumper plate.



  1. Detach the 20 claws and remove the bumper plate.





7. REMOVE REAR BUMPER COVER

HINT:
For the rear bumper cover with tire carrier, use the procedures described below.



  1. Put protective tape around the bumper cover.





  1. Open the tail gate and remove the 4 clips.



  1. Close the tail gate.

  1. Remove the 7 bolts, 6 screws and 2 clips.

  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Rear Fog Light:
    Detach the 14 claws and remove the bumper cover.



  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System or w/ Rear Fog Light:
    Detach the 14 claws, disconnect the No. 2 frame wire connector and remove the bumper cover.





8. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the rear combination light.

  1. Disconnect the connector.



9. REMOVE REAR COMBINATION LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



10. REMOVE REAR UPPER BUMPER RETAINER LH



  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the bumper retainer.



11. REMOVE REAR UPPER BUMPER RETAINER RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



12. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SIDE SUPPORT LH



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 6 claws and remove the bumper side support.



13. REMOVE REAR BUMPER SIDE SUPPORT RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



14. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 BUMPER REINFORCEMENT



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and bumper reinforcement.



15. REMOVE REAR BUMPER ARM BRACKET



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the bumper arm bracket on the other side.

  1. Remove the bolt and bumper arm bracket.



16. REMOVE REAR BUMPER ARM BRACKET LH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and bumper arm bracket.



17. REMOVE NO. 2 LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT SIDE COVER PROTECTOR



  1. Remove the 2 screws, 2 grommets and protector.



18. REMOVE NO. 1 LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT SIDE COVER PROTECTOR

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.
