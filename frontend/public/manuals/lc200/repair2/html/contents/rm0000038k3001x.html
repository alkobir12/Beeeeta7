REAR BUMPER (for Standard) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE NO. 2 FRAME WIRE


  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/ Rear Fog Light:
    Remove the No. 2 frame wire.

    1. Disconnect the 6 connectors.



    2. Detach the 16 clamps and remove the frame wire.





  1. w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/o Rear Fog Light:
    Remove the No. 2 frame wire.

    1. Disconnect the 4 connectors.



    2. Detach the 14 clamps and remove the frame wire.





  1. w/o TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System, w/ Rear Fog Light:
    Remove the No. 2 frame wire.

    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.



    2. Detach the 13 clamps and remove the frame wire.





2. REMOVE NO. 2 ULTRASONIC SENSOR (w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the No. 2 ultrasonic sensor on the other side.

  1. While pushing down on the lever with a finger to release the one claw, detach the claw on the other side to remove the ultrasonic sensor.

    HINT:
    The type of claw on the top and bottom of the sensor are different. Observe the claws carefully before removing the sensor.



3. REMOVE NO. 3 ULTRASONIC SENSOR (w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the No. 3 ultrasonic sensor on the other side.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the ultrasonic sensor as shown in the illustration.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 ULTRASONIC SENSOR RETAINER (w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the No. 2 ultrasonic sensor retainer on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the ultrasonic sensor retainer from the rear bumper cover.



5. REMOVE NO. 3 ULTRASONIC SENSOR RETAINER (w/ TOYOTA Parking Assist-sensor System)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the No. 3 ultrasonic sensor retainer on the other side.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the ultrasonic sensor retainer from the rear bumper cover.



6. REMOVE REAR BUMPER ENERGY ABSORBER



  1. Detach the 8 claws and remove the energy absorber.



7. REMOVE REAR FOG LIGHT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 nuts.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the fog light.



8. REMOVE REAR FOG LIGHT ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



9. REMOVE NO. 2 REAR BUMPER SIDE BRACKET (w/ Tire Carrier)



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and 2 clips, and remove the No. 2 and No. 3 brackets.
