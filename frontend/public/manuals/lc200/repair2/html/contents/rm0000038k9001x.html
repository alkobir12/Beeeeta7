RADIATOR GRILLE > REASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
When installing the emblem, heat the radiator grille and emblem using a heat light.

Standard Heating Temperature:
Item
Temperature
Radiator grille
20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)
Emblem
20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)

NOTICE:
Do not heat the radiator grille and emblem excessively.



1. INSTALL RADIATOR GRILLE (OR FRONT PANEL) EMBLEM

  1. Clean the radiator grille surface.

    1. Using a heat light, heat the radiator grille surface.

    2. Remove the double-sided tape from the radiator grille.

    3. Wipe off any tape adhesive residue with cleaner.



  1. Install a new emblem.

    1. Using a heat light, heat the radiator grille and a new emblem.

    2. Remove the peeling paper from the face of the emblem.

      HINT:
      After removing the peeling paper, keep the exposed adhesive free from foreign matter.




    3. Align the boss positions and install the emblem.

      HINT:
      Press the emblem firmly to install it.



2. INSTALL UPPER RADIATOR SEAL



  1. Attach the 14 clips to install the radiator seal.
