1. INSPECT REAR SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Compress and extend the shock absorber rod 4 times or more. Check that there is no abnormal resistance or unusual sound during the operation.
    If there is any abnormality, replace the shock absorber assembly with a new one.

    NOTICE:
    When disposing of the shock absorber, see Disposal (Click here).
