1. INSPECT VISOR ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    2 (SW) - 1 (GND)
    		Visor raised
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 (SW) - 1 (GND)
    		Visor lowered and mirror cover closed
    		10 kΩ or higher
    2 (SW) - 1 (GND)
    		Visor lowered and mirror cover open
    		Below 1 Ω

    If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction in the visor assembly.
