1. INSPECT HEADLIGHT RELAY (HEAD HI, HEAD LO)



  1. Remove the headlight relay from the engine room relay box.




  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    3 - 5
    		When battery voltage is not applied to terminals 1 and 2
    		10 kΩ or higher
    When battery voltage is applied to terminals 1 and 2
    		Below 1 Ω

    If the result is not as specified, replace the relay.

  1. Install the headlight relay to the engine room relay box.



2. INSPECT FOG LIGHT RELAY (FR FOG)



  1. Remove the fog light relay from the engine room relay box.




  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition

    		When battery voltage is not applied to terminals 1 and 2
    		10 kΩ or higher

3. INSPECT STOP LIGHT CONTROL RELAY ASSEMBLY

  1. w/ Floor Under Cover:
    Remove the No. 1 under cover (Click here).




  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    A39-6 (+B) - A39-4 (GND)
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    A39-2 (STP) - Body grand
    		Stop light switch on
    		11 to 14 V
    Stop light switch off
    		Below 1 V
    A39-8 (OUT) - Body grand
    		Stop light switch on
    		11 to 14 V
    Stop light switch off
    		Below 1 V

    If the result is not as specified, replace the stop light control relay.

  1. w/ Floor Under Cover:
    Install the No. 1 under cover (Click here).
