FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY (for Manual Seat) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here


CAUTION:
Wear protective gloves. Sharp areas on the parts may injure your hands.

HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE VERTICAL ADJUSTER COVER LH (for 8 Way Seat Type)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and remove the vertical adjuster cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



2. REMOVE VERTICAL ADJUSTING HANDLE LH (for 8 Way Seat Type)



  1. Remove the 2 screws and vertical adjusting handle.



3. REMOVE VERTICAL SEAT ADJUSTER KNOB (for 8 Way Seat Type)



  1. Remove the snap ring and vertical seat adjuster knob.



4. REMOVE RECLINING ADJUSTER RELEASE HANDLE LH



  1. Lift the reclining adjuster release handle.

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw and remove the reclining adjuster release handle.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



5. REMOVE FRONT SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH



  1. w/ Seatback Board:
    Remove the 2 rubber bands from the hooks.




  1. w/o Seatback Board:
    Remove the 2 rubber bands from the hooks.




  1. for 4 Way Seat Type:

    1. Remove the 2 screws.

    2. Detach the 3 claws in the order shown in the illustration, and then remove the cushion shield.




  1. for 8 Way Seat Type:

    1. Remove the 3 screws.

    2. Detach the 3 claws in the order shown in the illustration, and then remove the cushion shield.

  1. w/ Lumbar Support:
    Disconnect the lumbar switch connector.



6. REMOVE LUMBAR SWITCH (w/ Lumbar support)



  1. Remove the 2 screws and switch.



7. REMOVE FRONT SEAT INNER BELT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Disconnect the connectors and detach the clamps.




  1. Remove the nut and front seat inner belt assembly.



8. REMOVE FRONT INNER SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and clip, and then remove the cushion shield.



9. REMOVE SEAT CUSHION COVER WITH PAD



  1. Using a clip remover, remove the 2 clips.




  1. Detach the wire harness clamp.




  1. for Front Passenger Side:

    1. Disconnect the seat belt warning sensor connector.

    2. Detach the 2 clamps and remove the seat belt warning sensor connector.




  1. Bend the 2 seat frame claws upward.

  1. Detach the hooks and remove the seat cushion cover with pad.



10. REMOVE FRONT SEPARATE TYPE SEAT CUSHION COVER



  1. Remove the hog rings and front seat cushion cover.



11. REMOVE FRONT SEATBACK BOARD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH (w/ Seatback Board)



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Move the seatback board in the direction of the arrow to detach the 2 hooks and remove the seatback board.



12. REMOVE FRONT SEAT HEADREST SUPPORT (w/ Active Headrest)



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 headrest supports.



13. REMOVE SEATBACK COVER WITH PAD


  1. w/ Seatback Board:




    1. Remove the 3 hog rings and detach the 9 hooks.




    2. w/ Front Seat Side Airbag:
      Remove the nut and seatback cover bracket from the seat frame.



  1. w/o Seatback Board:




    1. Remove the 3 hog rings.




    2. Open the 2 fasteners, and then open the seatback cover.




    3. w/ Front Seat Side Airbag:
      Remove the nut and seatback cover bracket from the seat frame.




    4. Detach the 4 claws and remove the 2 headrest supports.

  1. Remove the seatback cover with pad from the seatback frame.



14. REMOVE FRONT SEPARATE TYPE SEATBACK COVER



  1. w/ Front Seat Side Airbag:
    Disconnect the seatback cover bracket from the seatback pad.




  1. Remove the hog rings.

  1. Detach the fastening tape and remove the seatback cover.



15. REMOVE INSIDE RECLINING ADJUSTER COVER LH



  1. Detach the wire harness clamp.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the reclining adjuster cover.



16. REMOVE INSIDE RECLINING ADJUSTER COVER RH

  1. Detach the wire harness clamp.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the reclining adjuster cover.



17. REMOVE OUTSIDE RECLINING ADJUSTER COVER LH



  1. Remove the screw and reclining adjuster cover.



18. REMOVE OUTSIDE RECLINING ADJUSTER COVER RH

  1. Remove the screw and reclining adjuster cover.



19. REMOVE LUMBAR SUPPORT ADJUSTER ASSEMBLY LH (w/Lumbar Support)



  1. Remove the 4 springs.

  1. Detach the 4 hooks and remove the seatback spring.




  1. Disconnect the lumbar support adjuster connector.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and lumbar support adjuster.




  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the bush from the seat frame.



20. REMOVE LOWER ACTIVE HEADREST UNIT (w/ Active Headrest)



  1. Remove the 8 nuts, and then remove the lower active headrest unit together with the upper active headrest unit.



21. REMOVE UPPER ACTIVE HEADREST UNIT (w/ Active Headrest)



  1. Remove the nut and cable clamp to remove the upper active headrest unit from the lower active headrest unit.



22. REMOVE FRONT LOWER SEAT CUSHION SHIELD LH



  1. Detach the claw and remove the lower seat cushion shield.



23. REMOVE FRONT LOWER SEAT CUSHION SHIELD RH

  1. Detach the claw and remove the lower seat cushion shield.



24. REMOVE SEAT POSITION SENSOR



  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the screw and seat position sensor with sensor protector.

  1. Disconnect the connector.




  1. Remove the seat slide position sensor protector from the seat position sensor.



25. REMOVE FRONT SEAT WIRE LH


26. REMOVE FRONT INNER SEAT TRACK ASSEMBLY LH


  1. for 4 Way Seat Type:




    1. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 2 bolts and inner seat track bracket.




    2. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 4 bolts and seat frame with adjuster.




    3. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 4 bolts and reinforcement.




  1. for 8 Way Seat Type:

    1. Remove the 2 springs.

    2. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 3 bolts and inner seat track bracket.




    3. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 4 bolts and seat frame with adjuster.




    4. Using a T40 "TORX" socket, remove the 2 bolts and reinforcement.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 springs and remove the seat track adjusting handle as shown in the illustration.



27. REMOVE FRONT OUTER SEAT TRACK ASSEMBLY LH
