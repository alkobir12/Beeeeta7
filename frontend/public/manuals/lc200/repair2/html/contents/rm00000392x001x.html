SEAT MEMORY SWITCH (w/ Seat Position Memory System) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE FRONT LOWER DOOR FRAME BRACKET GARNISH LH



  1. Detach the clip and claw, remove the front lower door frame bracket garnish.



2. REMOVE FRONT DOOR INSIDE HANDLE BEZEL LH



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the inside handle bezel.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE FRONT DOOR ARMREST BASE PANEL ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 5 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the front door armrest base panel and multiplex network master switch.



4. REMOVE DOOR ASSIST GRIP COVER LH



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 8 claws and remove the door assist grip cover.



5. REMOVE FRONT DOOR TRIM BOARD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Remove the 13 clips, 4 claws and trim board.

  1. Disconnect the connector.




  1. Disconnect the 2 cables from the inside handle.



6. REMOVE SEAT MEMORY SWITCH



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the screw and seat memory switch.
