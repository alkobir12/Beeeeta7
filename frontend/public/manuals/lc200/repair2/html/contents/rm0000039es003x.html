REAR NO. 2 SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly (Click here).



2. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



3. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



4. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Detach the 7 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



5. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT COVER BEZEL



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the rear No. 2 seat cover bezel.



6. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH




HINT:
When installing the front quarter trim panel, operate the reclining adjuster release handle and move the No. 1 rear seat to the position shown in the illustration.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear seat outer belt floor anchor.




  1. for LH Side:

    1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear No. 2 seat outer belt floor anchor LH.




  1. for RH Side:

    1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the cover.




    2. Remove the bolt and disconnect the rear seat outer belt floor anchor RH.

  1. Remove the clip and bolt.



  1. Detach the 18 clips and 2 claws.



  1. w/o Rear Air Conditioning System:
    Disconnect the rear seat lock control lever cable and then remove the quarter trim panel.

  1. w/ Rear Air Conditioning System:
    Disconnect the thermistor connector and rear seat lock control lever cable, and then remove the quarter trim panel.



7. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT OUTER BELT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the seat belt shoulder anchor.

    1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and open the seat belt anchor cover as shown in the illustration.

      HINT:
      Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




    2. Remove the bolt and seat belt shoulder anchor.




  1. Remove the bolt and rear No. 2 seat outer belt.
