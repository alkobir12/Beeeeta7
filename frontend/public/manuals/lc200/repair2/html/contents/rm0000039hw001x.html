CIGARETTE LIGHTER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



2. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



4. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



5. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



6. REMOVE CIGARETTE LIGHTER COVER



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the cover.



7. REMOVE CIGARETTE LIGHTER ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the cigarette lighter knob and element assembly.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the claw, and remove the cigarette lighter socket and retainer assembly.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



8. REMOVE CIGARETTE LIGHTER BEZEL STAY



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the bezel stay.
