INSTRUMENT PANEL SAFETY PAD > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE CENTER HEATER TO REGISTER DUCT



  1. Remove the 3 screws and duct.



2. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 AIR DUCT



  1. Remove the 2 screws and duct.



3. REMOVE FRONT PASSENGER AIRBAG ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the claw and remove the wire.

  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors and remove the instrument panel wire from the front passenger airbag.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.




  1. Remove the 2 screws.




  1. Detach the 5 hooks (A).

  1. Detach the 5 hooks (B) and remove the front passenger airbag from the instrument panel.



4. REMOVE DEFROSTER NOZZLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 11 claws and remove the nozzle.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 SIDE DEFROSTER NOZZLE



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the nozzle.



6. REMOVE NO. 2 SIDE DEFROSTER NOZZLE



  1. Detach the 3 claws and remove the nozzle.



7. REMOVE NAVIGATION ANTENNA ASSEMBLY (w/ Multi-display)



  1. Detach the 2 clamps.

  1. Remove the 2 screws and antenna.



8. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Multi-display)


  1. for LHD:
    Detach the 8 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





  1. for RHD:
    Detach the 6 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





9. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/o Multi-display)


  1. for LHD:
    Detach the 7 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





  1. for RHD:
    Detach the 5 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





10. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE (for LHD)


  1. Detach the wire from the clamps and remove the wire from the instrument panel.





11. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL WIRE (for RHD)


  1. Detach the wire from the clamps and remove the wire from the instrument panel.
