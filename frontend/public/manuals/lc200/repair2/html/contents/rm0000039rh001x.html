ENGINE HOOD COURTESY SWITCH > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
  2. The procedures listed below are for LHD vehicles.



1. REMOVE UPPER RADIATOR SUPPORT SEAL

  1. for Gasoline Engine:
    Remove the upper radiator support seal (Click here).

  1. for Diesel Engine:
    Remove the upper radiator support seal (Click here).



2. REMOVE RADIATOR GRILLE ASSEMBLY



  1. Put protective tape around the radiator grille.

  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and 8 claws, and remove the radiator grille.



3. REMOVE HOOD LOCK CONTROL CABLE COVER



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the cover.



4. REMOVE HOOD LOCK ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the hood lock nut cap.

  1. Remove the 2 bolts and nut.

  1. Disconnect the connector.




  1. Disconnect the hood lock control cable and remove the hood lock.
