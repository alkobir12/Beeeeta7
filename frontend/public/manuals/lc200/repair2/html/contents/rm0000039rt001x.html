GENERAL

  1. By operating the seat heater switch which is located in the console box, the temperature can be controlled within the range of 30 to 40°C (86 to 104°F).

  2. Whether the seat heater is on or off can be determined by checking if the seat heater switch indicator light is on or off.

  3. The seat heaters of both seats are controlled by the seat heater controls, which are located under the respective seat.
