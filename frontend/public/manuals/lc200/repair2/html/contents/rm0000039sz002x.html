STEERING LINKAGE > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


CAUTION:
Some of these service operations affect the SRS airbag system. Read the precautionary notices concerning the SRS airbag system before servicing the steering column (Click here).



1. PLACE FRONT WHEELS FACING STRAIGHT AHEAD


2. REMOVE ENGINE ASSEMBLY

  1. for 2UZ-FE:
    Remove the engine (Click here).

  1. for 1GR-FE:
    Remove the engine (Click here).

  1. for 1VD-FTV:
    Remove the engine (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT WHEELS


4. LOOSEN NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BRACKET LH

  1. w/ KDSS:
    Loosen the bracket (Click here).

  1. w/o KDSS:
    Loosen the bracket (Click here).



5. LOOSEN NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BRACKET RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



6. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY LH

  1. w/ KDSS:
    Remove the front stabilizer link (Click here).

  1. w/o KDSS:
    Remove the front stabilizer link (Click here).



7. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



8. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 STABILIZER BRACKET LH

  1. w/ KDSS:
    Remove the stabilizer bracket (Click here).

  1. w/o KDSS:
    Remove the front bracket (Click here).



9. REMOVE FRONT NO. 1 STABILIZER BRACKET RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



10. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER BAR

  1. w/ KDSS:
    Remove the stabilizer bar (Click here).

  1. w/o KDSS:
    Remove the stabilizer bar (Click here).



11. DISCONNECT NO. 2 STEERING INTERMEDIATE SHAFT

  1. for Manual Tilt and Telescopic:
    Disconnect the No. 2 steering intermediate shaft (Click here).

  1. for Power Tilt and Power Telescopic:
    Disconnect the No. 2 steering intermediate shaft (Click here).



12. DISCONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY LH

  1. Remove the cotter pin and nut.




  1. Using SST, disconnect the tie rod end LH from the steering knuckle.

    SST
    09610-20012  

    NOTICE:
    1. Do not damage the front disc brake dust cover.
    2. Do not damage the ball joint dust cover.
    3. Do not damage the steering knuckle.



13. DISCONNECT TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



14. REMOVE TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Put matchmarks on the tie rod end LH and steering rack end.

  1. Measure length A and record the measurement.

  1. Remove the tie rod end LH.



15. REMOVE TIE ROD END SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



16. DISCONNECT PRESSURE FEED TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the clamp and disconnect the pressure feed tube (return tube side) from the power steering link.




  1. Using a union nut wrench, disconnect the pressure feed tube (pressure feed tube side) from the power steering link.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the pressure feed tube clamp.



17. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION REBOUND STOPPER SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and suspension rebound stopper LH.



18. REMOVE FRONT SUSPENSION REBOUND STOPPER SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and suspension rebound stopper RH.



19. REMOVE POWER STEERING LINK ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 3 bolts, 3 nuts and power steering link.
