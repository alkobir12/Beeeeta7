FUEL PUMP > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



NOTICE:
Do not disconnect the hoses indicated in the illustration.




1. REMOVE FUEL SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the fuel sender gauge connector.




  1. Press down on the sender gauge claw labeled A. Then slide the sender gauge upward to remove it.



2. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL SUB-TANK (for Single Tank Type)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws from the claw holes to remove the fuel sub-tank.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the fuel sub-tank.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL SUB-TANK (for Double Tank Type)



  1. Disconnect the fuel pump connector.




  1. Using a small screwdriver, detach the claw from the sub-tank.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws from the claw holes to remove the fuel sub-tank.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the fuel sub-tank.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



4. REMOVE FUEL PUMP (for Double Tank Type)



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws from the claw holes and remove the fuel pump.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.




  1. Disconnect the fuel pump wire harness connector from the fuel pump.




  1. Remove the O-ring and spacer from the fuel pump.
