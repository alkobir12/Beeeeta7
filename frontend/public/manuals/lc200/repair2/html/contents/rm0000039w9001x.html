1. DISCHARGE FUEL SYSTEM PRESSURE

  1. Discharge the fuel system pressure (Click here).



2. REMOVE FUEL TANK CAP ASSEMBLY


3. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TANK PROTECTOR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and fuel tank protector.



4. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Single Tank Type)



  1. Detach the fuel tube clamp.

  1. Pull up the retainer and disconnect the fuel tank main tube.

    NOTICE:
    1. Check for foreign matter in the pipe and around the connector. Clean if necessary. Foreign matter may damage the O-ring or cause leaks in the seal between the pipe and connector.
    2. Do not use any tools to separate the pipe and connector.
    3. Do not forcefully bend or twist the nylon tube.
    4. Check for foreign matter on the pipe seal surface. Clean if necessary.
    5. Put the pipe and connector ends in plastic bags to prevent damage and foreign matter contamination.
    6. If the pipe and connector are stuck together, pinch the connector between your fingers and turn it carefully to disconnect it.

6. DRAIN FUEL

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.

  1. Connect the intelligent tester to the DLC3.

  1. Turn the engine switch on (IG).

    NOTICE:
    Do not start the engine.

  1. Turn the intelligent tester ON.



  1. Enter the following menus: Powertrain / Engine and ECT / Active Test / Control the Fuel Pump / Speed.

    CAUTION:
    1. Do not smoke or be near an open flame when working on the fuel system.
    2. Secure good ventilation.
    3. Keep gasoline away from rubber or leather parts.


    HINT:

    for Single Tank Type:
    If the fuel pump does not operate, remove the fuel tube joint clip and No. 1 fuel tube joint, and drain fuel from the port shown in the illustration.
    for Double Tank Type:
    If the fuel pump does not operate, remove the fuel tube joint clip and disconnect the fuel tank return tube, and drain fuel from the port shown in the illustration.

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

33. REMOVE FUEL TANK SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Place a transmission jack under the fuel tank.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts, 2 clips, 2 pins and 2 fuel tank bands.



34. REMOVE FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY AND FUEL TANK RETURN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Single Tank Type)



  1. Remove the 3 fuel tube joint clips and pull out the 2 fuel tubes and No. 1 fuel tube joint.

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any dirt and foreign matter on the fuel tube joint before performing this work.
    2. Do not allow any scratches or foreign matter on the parts when disconnecting them, as the fuel tube joint contains the O-rings that seal the plug.
    3. Perform this work by hand. Do not use any tools.
    4. Do not forcibly bend, twist or turn the nylon tube.
    5. Protect the disconnected part by covering it with a plastic bag and tape after disconnecting the fuel tubes.




  1. Remove the 2 fuel tubes from the fuel tank.



35. REMOVE FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE, FUEL TANK RETURN TUBE AND NO. 2 FUEL TANK MAIN TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY (for Double Tank Type)



  1. Remove the 3 fuel tube joint clips and pull out the 3 fuel tubes.

    NOTICE:
    1. Remove any dirt and foreign matter on the fuel tube joint before performing this work.
    2. Do not allow any scratches or foreign matter on the parts when disconnecting them, as the fuel tube joint contains the O-rings that seal the plug.
    3. Perform this work by hand. Do not use any tools.

  1. Remove the 3 fuel tubes from the fuel tank.



36. REMOVE FUEL SUCTION WITH PUMP AND GAUGE TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Using SST, loosen the retainer.

    SST
    09808-14020   (09808-01410, 09808-01420, 09808-01430)

    HINT:
    Fit the tips of SST onto the ribs of the retainer.

    NOTICE:
    1. When the retainer is loosened, be careful as the pump and gauge tube will spring upward from the force of the spring.
    2. Remove foreign matter around the fuel suction with pump and gauge before this operation.

  1. Remove the retainer.

  1. Remove the fuel suction with pump and gauge tube assembly from the fuel tank.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to bend the arm of the fuel sender gauge.

  1. Remove the gasket from the fuel tank.



37. REMOVE FUEL TANK TO FILLER PIPE HOSE



  1. Remove the hose from the fuel tank.



38. REMOVE NO. 1 FUEL TANK HEAT INSULATOR



  1. Remove the fuel tube clamp from the heat insulator.

  1. Using needle-nose pliers, remove the 4 clips shown in the illustration and then remove the heat insulator.
