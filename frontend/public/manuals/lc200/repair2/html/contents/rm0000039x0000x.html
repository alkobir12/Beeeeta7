FUEL SUB TANK > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Disconnect the cables from the negative (-) main battery and sub-battery terminals.



2. REMOVE SPARE TIRE


3. REMOVE TAILPIPE ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the bolt, clamp and gasket.

  1. Disconnect the tailpipe from the 2 exhaust pipe supports.



4. REMOVE SPARE WHEEL CARRIER CROSSMEMBER AND SPARE WHEEL CARRIER BRACKET


  1. Remove the bolt labeled A, and then loosen the 4 bolts labeled B.



  1. Slightly raise the spare wheel carrier crossmember, and then remove the 6 bolts labeled C, spare wheel carrier crossmember and 2 brackets.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 SPARE WHEEL STOPPER



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and spare wheel stopper.



6. REMOVE FUEL TANK CAP ASSEMBLY


7. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK EVAPORATION VENT TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the 2 fuel tank evaporation vent tubes.



8. DISCONNECT FUEL HOSE



  1. Disconnect the 3 fuel hoses.



9. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK BREATHER HOSE



  1. Disconnect the breather hose from the filler pipe.



10. DISCONNECT NO. 3 FUEL HOSE



  1. Disconnect the fuel hose from the filler pipe.



11. DISCONNECT FUEL TANK TO FILLER PIPE HOSE



  1. Disconnect the hose from the filler pipe.



12. REMOVE FUEL SUB TANK SUB-ASSEMBLY

  1. Disconnect the floor No. 3 wire connector.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 3 wire harness clamps.

  1. Place a transmission jack under the fuel sub tank.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts and disconnect the 2 fuel tank bands.

  1. Lower the transmission jack to remove the fuel sub tank from the vehicle.

    NOTICE:
    Do not allow the fuel sub tank to contact the vehicle, especially the differential.

    HINT:
    Lower the transmission jack slowly.



13. REMOVE FLOOR NO. 3 WIRE



  1. Disconnect the sender gauge connector.

  1. Using a clip remover, detach the wire harness clamp.



14. REMOVE FUEL SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 5 screws and sender gauge.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to bend the arm of the fuel sender gauge.

  1. Remove the gasket from the sender gauge.



15. REMOVE FUEL HOSE



  1. Remove the 3 fuel hoses.



16. REMOVE FUEL TANK EVAPORATION VENT TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 fuel tank evaporation vent tubes.



17. REMOVE FUEL AND EVAPORATION VENT TUBE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 6 bolts and fuel and evaporation vent tube.

  1. Remove the gasket from the fuel and evaporation vent tube.



18. REMOVE FUEL TANK BREATHER HOSE



  1. Remove the breather hose from the fuel sub tank.



19. REMOVE NO. 3 FUEL HOSE



  1. Remove the fuel hose from the fuel sub tank.



20. REMOVE FUEL TANK TO FILLER PIPE HOSE



  1. Remove the hose from the fuel sub tank.
