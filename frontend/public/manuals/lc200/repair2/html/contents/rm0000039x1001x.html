TURN SIGNAL FLASHER ASSEMBLY (for LHD) > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT TURN SIGNAL FLASHER ASSEMBLY



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E19-7 (E) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E19-1 (IG) - E19-7 (E)
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V
    E19-2 (LR) - E19-7 (E)
    		Hazard warning switch off
    		Below 1 V
    Hazard warning switch on
    		11 to 14 V
    E19-3 (LL) - E19-7 (E)
    		Hazard warning switch off
    		Below 1 V
    Hazard warning switch on
    		11 to 14 V
    E19-4 (B) - E19-7 (E)
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    E19-5 (EL) - E19-7 (E)
    		Headlight dimmer switch (TURN L) off
    		9 V or higher
    Headlight dimmer switch (TURN L) on
    		Below 1 V
    E19-6 (ER) - E19-7 (E)
    		Headlight dimmer switch (TURN R) off
    		9 V or higher
    Headlight dimmer switch (TURN R) on
    		Below 1 V
    E19-8 (HAZ) - E19-7 (E)
    		Hazard warning switch off
    		9 V or higher
    Hazard warning switch on
    		Below 1 V

    If the result is not as specified, there may be a malfunction in the turn signal flasher.
