BLACK OUT TAPE (for Rear Door) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the RH side and LH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.
  3. When removing the black out tape and window frame moulding, heat the vehicle body, black out tape and window frame moulding using a heat light.

Standard Heating Temperature:
Item
Temperature
Vehicle Body
40 to 60°C (104 to 140°F)
Black Out Tape
20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)
Window Frame Moulding
20 to 30°C (68 to 86°F)

NOTICE:
Do not heat the vehicle body, black out tape and window frame moulding excessively.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE REAR DOOR INSIDE HANDLE BEZEL LH



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 4 claws and remove the bezel.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



3. REMOVE REAR DOOR ARMREST BASE PANEL ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the rear door armrest base panel and rear power window regulator switch assembly.



4. REMOVE ASSIST GRIP COVER LH



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 9 claws and remove the door assist grip cover.



5. REMOVE REAR DOOR TRIM BOARD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 screws.

  1. Remove the 9 clips, 4 claws and trim board.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the trim board.




  1. Disconnect the 2 cables from the inside handle.



6. REMOVE REAR INNER DOOR GLASS WEATHERSTRIP LH



  1. Remove the weatherstrip by pulling it upward in the direction indicated by the arrow.



7. REMOVE REAR DOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER LH



  1. Using a clip remover, detach the 6 clamps.

  1. Remove the bolt and disconnect the 2 connectors.

  1. Remove the service hole cover.

    HINT:
    Remove the remaining tape on the door.



8. REMOVE REAR DOOR GLASS RUN LH



  1. Remove the rear door glass run from the window frame.



9. REMOVE REAR DOOR REAR LOWER WINDOW FRAME SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Remove the screw and window frame.



10. REMOVE REAR DOOR QUARTER WINDOW GLASS LH



  1. Remove the rear door quarter window glass and rear door quarter window weatherstrip as a unit as shown in the illustration.



11. REMOVE REAR DOOR GLASS SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the rear door glass sub-assembly from the rear door window regulator sub-assembly as shown in the illustration.

    NOTICE:
    Do not damage the door glass.



12. REMOVE REAR DOOR BELT MOULDING ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Put protective tape around the belt moulding.




  1. Detach the claw and remove the belt moulding.



13. REMOVE REAR DOOR FRONT WINDOW FRAME MOULDING LH



  1. Put protective tape around the window frame moulding.

  1. Detach the clip and remove the double-sided tape to remove the window frame moulding.



14. REMOVE REAR DOOR FRAME GARNISH LH



  1. Detach the clip and remove the rear door frame garnish.

    HINT:
    This clip needs to be replaced with a new one because the clip will break when removing the rear door frame garnish.



15. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 DOOR FRAME GARNISH LH



  1. Detach the clip and remove the rear door frame garnish.

    HINT:
    This clip needs to be replaced with a new one because the clip will break when removing the rear door frame garnish.



16. REMOVE REAR DOOR WEATHERSTRIP LH



  1. Detach the 4 clips, and remove the upper part of the rear door weatherstrip so that the black out tape can be removed.



17. REMOVE NO. 2 BLACK OUT TAPE LH



  1. Pull back an edge of the black out tape and pull it parallel to the vehicle body to remove it.



18. REMOVE NO. 6 BLACK OUT TAPE LH



  1. Pull back an edge of the black out tape and pull it parallel to the vehicle body to remove it.



19. REMOVE UPPER INNER BLACK OUT TAPE LH



  1. Pull back an edge of the black out tape and pull it parallel to the vehicle body to remove it.



20. REMOVE REAR INNER BLACK OUT TAPE LH



  1. Pull back an edge of the black out tape and pull it parallel to the vehicle body to remove it.
