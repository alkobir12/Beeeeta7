1. REMOVE HEIGHT CONTROL HOSE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the clamp from the reservoir, then remove the 2 clips and hose.



2. REMOVE HEIGHT CONTROL CAP TO RESERVOIR HOSE



  1. Remove the 3 clips, 2 breather plugs, and hose from the reservoir hose.




  1. Pull the connector down to detach it and remove the hose from the reservoir tank.



3. DISCONNECT NO. 4 FRAME WIRE



  1. Disconnect the 2 connectors, clamp and frame wire.



4. REMOVE HEIGHT CONTROL COMPRESSOR BRACKET ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 3 nuts and bracket from the pump and motor.



5. REMOVE HEIGHT CONTROL RESERVOIR ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a 5 mm hexagon wrench, remove the 5 bolts and reservoir from the pump and motor.



6. REMOVE NO. 1 HEIGHT CONTROL BOLT CUSHION

  1. Remove the 3 bolt holders and 3 bolt cushions from the pump and motor.
