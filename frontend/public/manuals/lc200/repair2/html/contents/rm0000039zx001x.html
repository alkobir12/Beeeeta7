1. DISPOSE OF REAR SUSPENSION CONTROL ACCUMULATOR



  1. Place the accumulator in a vise and saw a hole between A and B shown in the illustration using a saw to discharge the gas inside.

    CAUTION:
    1. The gas is colorless, odorless and harmless.
    2. Saw a hole while covering the saw with a cloth because chips may fly up.



2. DISPOSE OF REAR NO. 3 SUSPENSION CONTROL ACCUMULATOR



  1. Place the gas camber in a vise and saw a hole in the hatched area shown in the illustration using a saw to discharge the gas inside.

    CAUTION:
    1. The gas is colorless, odorless and harmless.
    2. Saw a hole while covering the saw with a cloth because chips may fly up.
