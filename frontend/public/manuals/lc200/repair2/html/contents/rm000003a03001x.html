DAMPING FORCE CONTROL ACTUATOR (for Rear Side) > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE REAR QUARTER PANEL MUDGUARD RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket, remove the 7 screws and mudguard.



2. REMOVE FUEL TANK FILLER PIPE PROTECTOR



  1. Using a T30 "TORX" socket wrench, remove the 3 screws, grommet and pipe protector.



3. INSPECT REAR SUSPENSION CONTROL ACCUMULATOR ASSEMBLY LH

NOTICE:
Perform the following after inspecting and adjusting the vehicle height.




  1. With the vehicle empty, start the engine and set the vehicle height to LOW.

  1. Record the reservoir tank fluid level (A).

  1. Set the vehicle height to HIGH.

    HINT:
    Make sure to check the fluid level approximately 30 seconds after the vehicle height changes to HIGH.

  1. Record the reservoir tank fluid level (B), and check that A minus B is within the specified value.

    Specified Value:
    A - B = 5 or more

    1. If the result is not as specified, replace all suspension control accumulators.
