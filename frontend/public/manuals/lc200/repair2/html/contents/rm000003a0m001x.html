OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE OUTER REAR VIEW MIRROR GLASS

  1. Push the upper part of the mirror surface and tilt it.




  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 4 claws and separate the outer rear view mirror glass from the mirror body.

    HINT:
    Apply protective tape to the outer rear view mirror to prevent it from being damaged.




  1. w/ EC Mirror:
    Disconnect the connector and remove the mirror glass.




  1. w/ Mirror Heater:
    Disconnect the 2 connectors and remove the mirror glass.



2. REMOVE OUTER MIRROR COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 8 claws and remove the mirror cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.
