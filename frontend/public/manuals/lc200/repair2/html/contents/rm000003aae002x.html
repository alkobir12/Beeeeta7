1. INSTALL CAMSHAFT SUB-GEAR



  1. Install the gear spring, sub-gear and wave washer.

    HINT:
    Attach the pins on the gears to the gear spring ends.




  1. Using snap ring pliers, install the snap ring.




  1. Mount the hexagonal portion of the camshaft in a vise.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the camshaft.




  1. Using SST, align the holes of the driven gear and sub-gear by turning the sub-gear clockwise, and temporarily install a service bolt.

    SST
    09960-10010   (09962-01000, 09963-00500)

    Recommended Service Bolt:
    Thread Diameter
    		Thread Pitch
    		Bolt Length
    6 mm
    		1.0 mm
    		16 to 20 mm (0.630 to 0.787 in.)

  1. Align the gear teeth of the driven gear and sub-gear, and tighten the service bolt.



2. INSTALL CAMSHAFT DRIVE GEAR



  1. Align the timing tube knock pin with the knock pin groove of the drive gear, and temporarily install the drive gear with the 4 bolts.

  1. Using SST and a 5 mm hexagon wrench, uniformly tighten the 4 bolts in several steps, in the sequence shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }

    SST
    09960-10010   (09962-01000, 09963-00500)

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the timing tube.



3. INSTALL CAMSHAFT TIMING TUBE ASSEMBLY



  1. Mount the hexagonal portion of the camshaft in a vise.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the camshaft.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the camshaft and camshaft timing tube.




  1. Align the camshaft knock pin with the knock pin hole of the timing tube, and push the timing tube by hand until the camshaft knock pin touches the bottom of the timing tube knock pin hole.

    NOTICE:
    Do not forcefully push on the camshaft timing tube. This may cause the camshaft knock pin tip to damage the installation surface of the camshaft timing tube.

  1. Using a 10 mm socket hexagon wrench, install the bolt.

    Torque:
    78 N*m{ 795 kgf*cm , 58 ft.*lbf }

  1. Install the seal washer and screw plug.

    Torque:
    15 N*m{ 153 kgf*cm , 11 ft.*lbf }



4. INSTALL CAMSHAFT (for Bank 1)

NOTICE:
As the thrust clearance of the camshaft is small, the camshaft must be kept level while it is being installed. If the camshaft is not kept level, the portion of the cylinder head which receives the shaft thrust may crack or be damaged. To avoid this, the following steps should be carried out.

HINT:
When performing this procedure to adjust the valve clearance, it is not necessary to apply seal packing to the No. 5 bearing cap and camshaft housing plug.




  1. Check that the timing mark of the crankshaft timing pulley is in the position shown in the illustration, and that the piston is below the TDC of compression.

    NOTICE:
    Having the crankshaft pulley at a wrong angle can cause the piston head and valve head to come into contact with each other when removing the camshaft. Always set the crankshaft pulley at the correct angle as shown in the illustration.

  1. Apply a light coat of engine oil to the cam lobe and gear of the camshaft and the journal of the cylinder head.




  1. Using a wrench, align the timing marks (2 dot marks each) of the camshaft drive and driven gears, and set the No. 3 and No. 4 camshafts in place.

    HINT:
    The position shown in the illustration is the most stable position for the exhaust camshaft.




  1. Apply seal packing to the camshaft housing plug groove.

    HINT:
    Remove any old packing (FIPG) material before applying new seal packing.

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    1. Be careful of the installation direction.
    2. Remove any oil from the contact surface.




  1. Install the camshaft housing plug and oil control valve filter to the cylinder head as shown in the illustration.

    HINT:
    Align the protrusion on the camshaft housing plug with the groove of the cylinder head and install the plug.




  1. Apply seal packing to the No. 5 bearing cap as shown in the illustration.

    Seal packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing Black, Three Bond 1207B or equivalent

    Standard seal diameter:
    1.5 to 2.0 mm (0.0591 to 0.0787 in.)

    NOTICE:
    Install the No. 5 bearing cap and housing plug within 3 minutes.




  1. Install a new seal washer to each bearing cap bolt indicated by an arrow in the illustration, and apply engine oil to the threads of the bolts. Then temporarily install them.

  1. Apply engine oil to the threads and washer part of the bearing cap bolts not indicated in the illustration.

  1. Temporarily install the oil feed pipe and bearing caps with the 18 bearing cap bolts, as shown in the illustration.

    Bolt Length:
    Item
    		Length
    Bolt A with seal washer
    		94 mm (3.70 in.)
    Bolt B with seal washer
    		72 mm (2.84 in.)
    Bolt C
    		25 mm (0.984 in.)
    Bolt D
    		55 mm (2.17 in.)
    Bolt E
    		40 mm (1.58 in.)

    HINT:
    Face the arrow imprinted on the oil feed pipe to the exhaust side.




  1. Push in the camshaft setting oil seal.




  1. Uniformly tighten the 22 bearing cap bolts in several steps, in the sequence shown in the illustration.

    Torque:
    for bolt 21, 22:
    7.5 N*m{ 76 kgf*cm , 66 in.*lbf }
    for other bolt:
    16 N*m{ 163 kgf*cm , 12 ft.*lbf }




  1. Bring the service bolt installed in the driven sub-gear upward by turning the hexagonal portion of the camshaft with a wrench.

  1. Remove the service bolt.

10. INSTALL TIMING BELT



  1. Set the No. 1 cylinder to TDC/compression.

    1. Turn the hexagonal portion of the camshaft to align the timing marks of the camshaft timing pulleys and timing belt plates.




    2. Using the pulley bolt, turn the crankshaft to align the timing marks of the crankshaft timing pulley and oil pump body.




  1. Install the timing belt.

    1. Remove any oil or water from each pulley, and keep them clean.

      NOTICE:
      Only wipe the pulleys. Do not use any cleansing agent on the pulleys.

    2. Face the front mark (arrow) on the timing belt forward.

    3. Align the timing marks on the timing belt and crankshaft timing pulley, and the timing belt and camshaft timing pulleys, and install the timing belt.




  1. Install the belt tensioner.

    1. Remove the dust seal from the belt tensioner.

    2. Using a press, slowly press in the push rod using a force of 981 to 9807 N (100 to 1000 kgf, 220.5 to 2204.7 lbf).

    3. Align the holes of the push rod and housing, and pass a 1.5 mm hexagon wrench through the holes to keep the setting position of the push rod.

    4. Release the press.

    5. Install the dust seal to the belt tensioner.




    6. Temporarily install the belt tensioner with the 2 bolts.

    7. Tighten the 2 bolts.

      Torque:
      26 N*m{ 265 kgf*cm , 19 ft.*lbf }

    8. Using pliers, remove the 1.5 mm hexagon wrench from the timing belt tensioner.



11. CHECK NO. 1 CYLINDER TO TDC/COMPRESSION



  1. Using the pulley bolt, slowly turn the crankshaft timing pulley 2 revolutions from the TDC to TDC.

    NOTICE:
    Always turn the crankshaft timing pulley clockwise.

  1. Check that each pulley is aligned with each timing mark as shown in the illustration.
    If the timing marks are not aligned, remove the timing belt and reinstall it.

  1. Remove the pulley bolt.

13. ADJUST VALVE CLEARANCE

  1. Remove the timing belt (Click here).

  1. Remove the camshaft (Click here).

  1. Remove the valve lifter and adjusting shim.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to drop the adjusting shim into the cylinder head.

    HINT:
    Arrange the removed parts in the correct order.

  1. Using a micrometer, measure the thickness of the removed shim.




  1. Determine the replacement adjusting shim size according to the formula or charts below.

    1. Calculate the thickness of a new shim so that the valve clearance comes within the specified value.

      T:
      Thickness of removed shim

      A:
      Measured valve clearance

      N:
      Thickness of new shim

      Intake:
      N = T + (A - 0.20 mm (0.00787 in.))

      Exhaust:
      N = T + (A - 0.30 mm (0.0118 in.))



    2. Select a new shim with a thickness as close as possible to the calculated value.

      HINT:
      1. Shims are available in 41 increments of 0.020 mm (0.000787 in.), from 2.00 mm (0.0787 in.) to 2.80 mm (0.1102 in.) and 33 increments of 0.025 mm (0.000984 in.), from 2.50 mm (0.0984 in.) to 3.30 mm (0.1299 in.).
      2. Refer to the Adjusting Shim Selection Chart.




      Intake valve clearance (Cold):
      0.15 to 0.25 mm (0.00591 to 0.00984 in.)

14. INSTALL SPARK PLUG TUBE GASKET



  1. Using a cutter knife, cut off the seal part of the removed gasket.




  1. Using the removed gasket and a hammer, tap in a new gasket until it stops.

    NOTICE:
    Do not tap on the oil seal at an angle.

    HINT:
    If the removed gasket does not fit on the new one, correct the removed one with pliers.

  1. Apply a light coat of MP grease to the lip of the gasket.

    NOTICE:
    Keep the lip free from foreign matter.

  1. Return the 8 ventilation case claws to the original positions.

  1. Slowly pour coolant into the radiator reservoir until it reaches the F line.

  1. Press the inlet and outlet radiator hoses several times by hand to bleed air.

  1. Check that the coolant level is between the F and L lines. If the coolant level is below the L line, repeat all of the procedures above. If the coolant level is above the F line, drain coolant so that the coolant level is between the F and L lines.

  1. Warm up the engine, then stop the engine and wait for 5 minutes.

  1. Check that the engine oil level is between the low level and full level marks on the dipstick.

    NOTICE:
    Do not fill engine oil above the full level mark.



54. INSPECT FOR COOLANT LEAK



  1. Fill the radiator with coolant and attach a radiator cap tester.

  1. Warm up the engine.

  1. Using the radiator cap tester, increase the pressure inside the radiator to 123 kPa (1.3 kgf/cm2, 18 psi), and check that the pressure does not drop.
    If the pressure drops, check the hoses, radiator and water pump for leaks. If no external leaks are found, check the heater core, cylinder block and head.
