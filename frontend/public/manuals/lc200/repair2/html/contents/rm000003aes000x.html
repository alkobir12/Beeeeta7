TRANSMISSION CASE OIL SEAL > REPLACEMENT

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the manual transmission (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT BEARING RETAINER (MTM)



  1. Remove the 8 bolts and front bearing retainer.



3. REMOVE TRANSMISSION FRONT BEARING RETAINER OIL SEAL



  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the oil seal and front bearing retainer contact surfaces.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



4. INSTALL TRANSMISSION FRONT BEARING RETAINER OIL SEAL



  1. Using SST and a press, drive in a new oil seal.

    SST
    09950-60010   (09951-00460)
    09950-70010   (09951-07150)

    Standard Drive in Depth:
    15.4 to 16.2 mm (0.607 to 0.637 in.)

  1. Apply gear oil to the lip of the oil seal.



5. INSTALL FRONT BEARING RETAINER (MTM)



  1. Apply seal packing to the front bearing retainer, as shown in the illustration.

    Seal Packing:
    Toyota Genuine Seal Packing 1281, Three Bond 1281 or equivalent

    NOTICE:
    Parts must be assembled within 10 minutes of application. Otherwise, the packing (FIPG) material must be removed and reapplied.

  1. Apply adhesive to 2 or 3 threads of the bolts.

    Adhesive:
    Toyota Genuine Adhesive 1324, Three Bond 1324 or equivalent




  1. Install the front bearing retainer with the 8 bolts.

    Torque:
    17 N*m{ 170 kgf*cm , 12 ft.*lbf }



6. INSTALL MANUAL TRANSMISSION ASSEMBLY

  1. Install the manual transmission (Click here).
