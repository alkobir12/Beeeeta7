1. INSPECT BLOWER RESISTOR



  1. Measure resistance between terminals 1 and 4.

    Standard Resistance:
    2.0 to 2.4 kΩ

    If resistance is not as specified, replace the blower resistor.

  1. Inspect blower resistor.

    1. Connect the positive (+) lead to terminal 4 through a 12 V to 3.4 W test bulb and negative (-) lead to terminal 2.

    2. Check the test bulb lights up when another positive (+) lead is connected to terminal 2 through a 12 V to 3.4 W test bulb.
      If operation is not as specified, replace the blower resistor.
