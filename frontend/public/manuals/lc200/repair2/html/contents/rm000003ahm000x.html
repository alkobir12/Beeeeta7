OIL PUMP > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER OIL SEAL



  1. Using a screwdriver, pry out the oil pump cover oil seal.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the oil seal and oil pump cover contact surfaces.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



2. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP DRIVE ROTOR



  1. Remove the oil pump drive rotor.



3. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP DRIVEN ROTOR



  1. Remove the oil pump driven rotor.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 MANUAL TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER PLUG



  1. Using a hexagon wrench, remove the oil pump cover plug.



5. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER COMPRESSION SPRING



  1. Using a magnetic finger, remove the compression spring.



6. REMOVE TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER BALL



  1. Using a magnetic finger, remove the ball.



7. REMOVE OIL PUMP RELIEF VALVE SEAT



  1. Using a magnetic finger, remove the valve seat.



8. REMOVE MANUAL TRANSMISSION OIL PUMP COVER O-RING

  1. Remove the O-ring from the valve seat.
