RADIO RECEIVER (w/ Multi-display) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



3. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



5. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



6. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



7. REMOVE NO. 1 SPEAKER OPENING COVER ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 8 claws and remove the opening cover.



8. REMOVE NO. 3 INSTRUMENT PANEL REGISTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the register and then disconnect the connector.



9. REMOVE NO. 4 INSTRUMENT PANEL REGISTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the register and then disconnect the connector.



10. REMOVE MULTI-DISPLAY ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 screws and 2 bolts.




  1. Pull the radio receiver to detach the 2 clips and 6 claws on the backside of the display.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the display.



11. REMOVE RADIO RECEIVER ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 screws and 2 bolts.




  1. Pull the radio receiver to detach the 5 claws on the backside of the radio receiver.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the radio receiver.



12. REMOVE NO. 1 RADIO BRACKET



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bracket.



13. REMOVE NO. 2 RADIO BRACKET



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and bracket.
