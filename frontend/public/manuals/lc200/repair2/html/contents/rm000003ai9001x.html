STEREO COMPONENT AMPLIFIER > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY LH

  1. for Manual Seat:
    Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).

  1. for Power Seat:
    Remove the front seat assembly LH (Click here).



3. REMOVE FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY RH

  1. for Manual Seat:
    Remove the front seat assembly RH (Click here).

  1. for Power Seat:
    Remove the front seat assembly RH (Click here).

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE REAR NO. 3 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 6 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



5. REMOVE REAR NO. 5 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 6 claws and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



6. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Operate the reclining adjuster release handle to move the seat into the position shown in the illustration.




  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and 2 clips, and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



7. REMOVE REAR NO. 4 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws and 2 clips, and remove the cover.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



8. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY LH



  1. w/ Rear Seat Side Airbag:
    Disconnect the connector.

  1. w/ Seat Heater System:
    Disconnect the connector.




  1. Remove the 4 bolts and seat assembly.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to damage the vehicle body.



9. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT CUSHION HINGE COVER

13. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH

14. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL LH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



15. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH (w/o Airbag Cut Off Switch)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



16. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH (w/ Airbag Cut Off Switch)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the side panel and disconnect the connector.



17. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Detach the 7 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



18. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

19. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Floor Under Cover)

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

34. REMOVE FRONT FLOOR CARPET ASSEMBLY


  1. Detach the 2 clips and 8 claws.






  1. Remove the carpet from the floor, open the back door and tail gate, and remove the carpet from the rear of the vehicle.



35. REMOVE STEREO COMPONENT AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET



  1. Disconnect the 3 connectors.

  1. Remove the 3 bolts and remove the amplifier with bracket.



36. REMOVE AMPLIFIER COVER



  1. Detach the 3 clips and remove the cover.



37. REMOVE NO. 1 AMPLIFIER BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.



38. REMOVE NO. 2 AMPLIFIER BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.
