INJECTOR DRIVER > INSTALLATION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSTALL INJECTOR DRIVER

NOTICE:
Be careful not to drop or strike the injector driver.




  1. Install the No. 2 injector driver and injector driver bracket to the No. 2 injector driver bracket with the 4 screws.

    Torque:
    2.0 N*m{ 20 kgf*cm , 18 in.*lbf }




  1. Install the No. 1 injector driver and injector driver bracket to the No. 1 injector driver bracket with the 4 screws.

    Torque:
    2.0 N*m{ 20 kgf*cm , 18 in.*lbf }




  1. Install the No. 2 injector driver bracket to the No. 1 injector driver bracket with the 2 bolts.

    Torque:
    3.0 N*m{ 31 kgf*cm , 27 in.*lbf }




  1. Install the injector driver assembly with the 3 bolts.

    Torque:
    8.0 N*m{ 82 kgf*cm , 71 in.*lbf }

  1. Attach the fuel hose clamp and wire harness clamp.




  1. Connect the 4 injector driver connectors.



2. CONNECT CABLE TO NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Connect the cables to the negative (-) main battery and sub-battery terminals.
