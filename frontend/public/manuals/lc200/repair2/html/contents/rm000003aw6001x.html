1. INSPECT FRONT NO. 3 SPEAKER ASSEMBLY

  1. When there is a malfunction such as noise from a speaker or no sound at all, replace the speaker with a new one and check that the malfunction disappears.

    OK:
    Malfunction disappears.

    HINT:
    1. Connect all the connectors to the front No. 3 speakers.
    2. When there is a possibility that either the right or left rear speaker is defective, inspect by interchanging the right one with the left one.
    3. Perform the inspection above on both LH and RH sides.
