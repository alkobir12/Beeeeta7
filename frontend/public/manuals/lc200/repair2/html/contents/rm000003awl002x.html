RADIO ANTENNA CORD > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the roof headlining assembly (Click here).

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the roof headlining assembly (Click here).



3. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY


  1. w/o Sliding Roof:



    1. Detach the 2 clamps and remove the antenna from the notch on the rear of the roof headlining.



    2. Remove the antenna cord from the roof headlining.

    3. Remove the double-sided tape from the roof headlining.



  1. w/ Sliding Roof:



    1. Detach the 4 clamps and remove the antenna from the notch on the rear of the roof headlining.



    2. Remove the antenna cord from the roof headlining.

    3. Remove the double-sided tape from the roof headlining.



4. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL SAFETY PAD ASSEMBLY

  1. Remove the instrument panel safety pad (Click here).



5. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Multi-display)


  1. for LHD:
    Detach the 8 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





  1. for RHD:
    Detach the 6 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





6. REMOVE ANTENNA CORD SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/o Multi-display)


  1. for LHD:
    Detach the 7 clamps and remove the antenna cord.





  1. for RHD:
    Detach the 5 clamps and remove the antenna cord.
