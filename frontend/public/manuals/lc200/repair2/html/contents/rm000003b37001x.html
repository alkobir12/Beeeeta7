1. INSPECT CENTER STOP LIGHT ASSEMBLY



  1. Apply battery voltage to the connector, and check the LED illumination condition.

    OK:
    Measurement Condition
    		Specified Condition
    Battery positive (+) → Terminal 1 (STP)
    Battery negative (-) → Terminal 2 (E)
    		Light illuminates

    If the result is not as specified, replace the center stop light assembly.
