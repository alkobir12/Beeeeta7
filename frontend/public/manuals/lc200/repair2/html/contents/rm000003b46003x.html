AIR CONDITIONING PANEL (w/o Multi-display) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LHD vehicle and RHD vehicle.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LHD vehicle.



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE NO. 1 SPEAKER OPENING COVER ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 8 claws and remove the opening cover.



3. REMOVE NO. 3 INSTRUMENT PANEL REGISTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the register and then disconnect the connector.



4. REMOVE NO. 4 INSTRUMENT PANEL REGISTER ASSEMBLY



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the register and then disconnect the connector.



5. REMOVE RADIO RECEIVER ASSEMBLY WITH BRACKET

  1. w/ Cassette Tape Player:
    Remove the radio receiver assembly with bracket (Click here).

  1. w/o Multi-dsplay:
    Remove the radio receiver assembly with bracket (Click here).



6. REMOVE CENTER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL


7. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



8. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



9. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



10. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



11. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



12. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL ASSEMBLY



  1. Pull the radio receiver to detach the 5 claws on the backside of the radio receiver.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the radio receiver.
