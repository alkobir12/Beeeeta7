FUEL SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY (for Fuel Sub Tank) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY RH (for 60/40 Split Seat Type 40 Side)

  1. Remove the rear No. 1 seat assembly RH (Click here).



3. REMOVE REAR NO. 1 SEAT ASSEMBLY LH (for 60/40 Split Seat Type 60 Side)

  1. Remove the rear No. 1 seat assembly LH (Click here).



4. REMOVE REAR NO. 2 SEAT ASSEMBLY

  1. for Face to Face Seat Type:
    Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly (Click here).

  1. except Face to Face Seat Type:
    Remove the rear No. 2 seat assembly (Click here).



5. REMOVE REAR STEP COVER



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the step cover on the other side.

  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the step cover.



6. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 3 claws and 4 clips, and remove the scuff plate.



7. REMOVE REAR DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



8. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT REAR SUPPORT PLATE



  1. Detach the 6 clips and remove the support plate.



9. REMOVE REAR SEAT COVER CAP (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat, except Face to Face Seat Type)



HINT:
Use the same procedure to remove the rear seat cover cap on the other side.

  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 3 claws and remove the rear seat cover cap.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.



10. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY LH

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly LH (Click here).



11. REMOVE FRONT QUARTER TRIM PANEL ASSEMBLY RH

  1. w/ Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly RH (Click here).

  1. w/o Sliding Roof:
    Remove the front quarter trim panel assembly RH (Click here).



12. REMOVE AIR DUCT PLUG (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the plug.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures for both sides.



13. REMOVE REAR AIR DUCT GUIDE (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the claw and remove the guide.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures for both sides.



14. REMOVE REAR FLOOR MAT ASSEMBLY



  1. w/ Rear Air Conditioning System:
    Detach the 5 clips and fold back the floor mat toward the front of the vehicle.

    HINT:
    Fold back the floor mat so that the rear No. 7 air duct can be removed.

  1. w/o Rear Air Conditioning System:
    Detach the 5 clips and fold back the floor mat toward the front of the vehicle.

    HINT:
    Fold back the floor mat so that the service hole cover can be removed.



15. REMOVE REAR NO. 7 AIR DUCT (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Detach the 4 clips and remove the air duct.



16. REMOVE REAR FLOOR SERVICE HOLE COVER





17. REMOVE FUEL SENDER GAUGE ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the sender gauge connector.

  1. Remove the 5 screws and sender gauge.

    NOTICE:
    Be careful not to bend the arm of the fuel sender gauge.

  1. Remove the gasket from the sender gauge.
