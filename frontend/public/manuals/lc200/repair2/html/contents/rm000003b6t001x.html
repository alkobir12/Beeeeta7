FRONT CONSOLE BOX (w/ Cool Box) > DISASSEMBLY

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE NO. 2 AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Air Conditioning System)



  1. Remove the 4 screws.

  1. Detach the 5 claws and remove the air conditioning control.



2. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear Cooler)



  1. Remove the 4 screws.

  1. Detach the 5 claws and remove the air conditioning control.



3. REMOVE REAR COOLER SWITCH HOLE COVER (w/o Rear Cooler)



  1. Remove the 4 screws.

  1. Detach the 5 claws and remove the hole cover.



4. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL HOLE COVER



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the hole cover.



5. REMOVE REAR CONSOLE BOX MOUNTING BRACKET



  1. Remove the 4 screws and bracket.



6. REMOVE CONSOLE COMPARTMENT DOOR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 6 screws and door.



7. REMOVE CONSOLE COMPARTMENT DOOR LOCK SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 12 screws, lower box and door lock.



8. REMOVE CONSOLE COMPARTMENT DOOR HINGE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 12 claws and remove the inner door.




  1. Remove the 4 screws and door hinge.



9. REMOVE CONSOLE COMPARTMENT DOOR LOCK



  1. Remove the 2 screws and door lock.



10. REMOVE COOLING BOX ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 4 screws.

  1. Detach the 10 claws and remove the cooling box.



11. REMOVE AIR CONDITIONING AMPLIFIER ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the screw and amplifier.



12. REMOVE COOLER CONTROL SWITCH SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 2 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connector and remove the switch.



13. REMOVE NO. 2 COOLING BOX BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 screws and mounting bracket.




  1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.



14. REMOVE COOLING BOX BRACKET



  1. Remove the 2 screws and mounting bracket.




  1. Remove the 2 screws and bracket.



15. REMOVE COOLING BOX BASE



  1. Remove the 10 screws and cooling box base.



16. REMOVE COOLING UNIT CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws and air duct from the case.



17. REMOVE LIQUID HOSE



  1. Remove the bolt and liquid hose.

  1. Remove the O-ring from the liquid hose.



18. REMOVE LOWER COOLING UNIT CASE SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Disconnect the 3 connectors.

  1. Remove the 4 holding springs and lower cooling unit case.



19. REMOVE UPPER COOLING UNIT CASE



  1. Remove the cooling unit case.




  1. Detach the 2 hooks and open the case, as shown in the illustration.



20. REMOVE NO. 3 COOLER THERMISTOR



  1. Remove the cool box insulator.




  1. Remove the thermistor.



21. REMOVE NO. 1 COOLER BLOWER MOTOR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the blower motor.



22. REMOVE NO. 2 COOLER EVAPORATOR SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the evaporator.
