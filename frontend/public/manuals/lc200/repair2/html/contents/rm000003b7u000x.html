CURTAIN SHIELD AIRBAG ASSEMBLY > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here


HINT:
  1. Use the same procedures for the LH side and RH side.
  2. The procedures listed below are for the LH side.



1. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY (w/ Sliding Roof)

  1. Remove the roof headlining (Click here).



2. REMOVE ROOF HEADLINING ASSEMBLY (w/o Sliding Roof)

  1. Remove the roof headlining (Click here).



3. REMOVE CURTAIN SHIELD AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/ Rear No. 2 Seat)



  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.

  1. Remove the 13 bolts and curtain shield airbag.



4. REMOVE CURTAIN SHIELD AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/o Rear No. 2 Seat)


  1. Disconnect the connector.



    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.

  1. Remove the 10 bolts and curtain shield airbag.
