CURTAIN SHIELD AIRBAG ASSEMBLY > ON-VEHICLE INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. CHECK VEHICLE NOT INVOLVED IN COLLISION



  1. Perform a diagnostic system check (Click here).

  1. With the curtain shield airbag installed on the vehicle, perform a visual check. If any of the defects mentioned below are present, replace the curtain shield airbag with a new one:
    Cuts, minute cracks or marked discoloration on the curtain shield airbag.



2. CHECK VEHICLE INVOLVED IN COLLISION AND AIRBAG HAS NOT DEPLOYED



  1. Perform a diagnostic system check (Click here).

  1. With the curtain shield airbag removed from the vehicle, perform a visual check. If there are any defects as mentioned below, replace the curtain shield airbag with a new one:

    1. Cuts, minute cracks or marked discoloration on the curtain shield airbag.
    2. Cracks or other damage to the connectors.

    NOTICE:
    For removal and installation procedures of the curtain shield airbag, be sure to follow the correct procedure.
