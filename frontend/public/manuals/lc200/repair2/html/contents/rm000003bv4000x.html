DTC    U0130    Lost Communication with Steering Effort Control Module

for Preparation Click here


DESCRIPTION

If 2 or more DTCs are output during the DTC check, one side of the CAN branch wire may be open (One side of the CANH [branch wire]/CANL [branch wire] of the ECU and/or sensor is open).

DTC Code
DTC Detection Condition
Trouble Area
U0130
There is no communication from the steering control ECU.
  1. Power source circuit of steering control ECU
  2. One side of CAN branch wire of other systems
  3. Steering control ECU
  4. Steering control ECU branch wire or connector

HINT:
For vehicles with the variable gear ratio steering system only.


WIRING DIAGRAM




INSPECTION PROCEDURE

1.DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

  1. Disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal before measuring the resistances of the main wire and the branch wire.

    CAUTION:
    Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).


NEXT


2.CHECK FOR OPEN IN CAN BUS WIRE (STEERING CONTROL ECU BRANCH WIRE)

  1. Disconnect the E79 steering control ECU connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E79-9 (CANH) - E79-20 (CANL)
    		Engine switch off
    		54 to 69 Ω



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE STEERING CONTROL ECU BRANCH WIRE OR CONNECTOR (CANH, CANL)
OK


3.CHECK HARNESS AND CONNECTOR (STEERING CONTROL ECU - BATTERY AND BODY GROUND)

  1. Connect the cable to the negative (-) battery terminal.

    NOTICE:
    When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).

  1. Disconnect the E77 and E78 steering control ECU connectors.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E77-7 (PGND) - Body ground
    		Always
    		Below 1 Ω
    E78-8 (PGD2) - Body ground

  1. Measure the voltage according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Voltage:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    E77-10 (+B1) - Body ground
    		Always
    		11 to 14 V
    E77-8 (PIG) - Body ground
    E77-3 (IG) - Body ground
    		Engine switch on (IG)
    		11 to 14 V



NG
REPAIR OR REPLACE HARNESS OR CONNECTOR
OK

REPLACE STEERING CONTROL ECU
