DOOR CONTROL RELAY (for RHD) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

CAUTION:
Wait at least 90 seconds after disconnecting the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal to disable the SRS system.

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



2. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



3. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 3 claws and remove the panel garnish.



4. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the panel garnish.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Floor Under Cover)



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the under cover and disconnect the connectors.



6. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



7. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/o Driver Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the DLC3.

  1. Remove the 5 bolts and instrument panel.



8. REMOVE DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/ Driver Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Remove the 5 bolts and driver side knee airbag.

  1. Disconnect the connector.

    NOTICE:
    When handling the airbag connector, take care not to damage the airbag wire harness.



9. REMOVE DOUBLE LOCK DOOR CONTROL RELAY ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the nut and double lock door control relay assembly.
