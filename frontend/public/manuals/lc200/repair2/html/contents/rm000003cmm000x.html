1. TABLE OF BOLT, SCREW AND NUT


HINT:
All bolts, screws and nuts relevant to installing and removing the instrument panel are shown along with their alphabet code in the table below.






2. DISCONNECT CABLE FROM NEGATIVE BATTERY TERMINAL

NOTICE:
When disconnecting the cable, some systems need to be initialized after the cable is reconnected (Click here).



3. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



4. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 8 claws.

  1. Remove the panel pad and disconnect the connectors and 2 clamps.



5. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL CUSHION



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 7 claws and remove the panel cushion.



6. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL PAD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the clip.

  1. Remove the screw.

  1. Detach the 7 claws and remove the panel.



7. REMOVE LOWER CENTER INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Detach the 7 claws.

  1. Disconnect the connectors and remove the panel.



8. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL LH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



9. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 3 claws and remove the panel garnish.



10. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the panel garnish.



11. REMOVE NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Floor Under Cover)



  1. Remove the 2 screws.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the under cover and disconnect the connectors.



12. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE LH


13. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD LH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



14. REMOVE LOWER NO. 1 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Using a screwdriver, detach the 2 claws.

    HINT:
    Tape the screwdriver tip before use.

  1. Open the hole cover.




  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 14 claws.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and remove the sensor.




  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the 2 control cables.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connectors.



15. REMOVE NO. 1 SWITCH HOLE BASE



  1. Detach the 4 claws.

  1. Remove the switch hole base and then disconnect the connectors.



16. REMOVE DRIVER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/ Driver Side Knee Airbag)


17. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/o Driver Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Detach the 2 claws and disconnect the DLC3.

  1. Remove the 5 bolts and instrument panel.



18. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH (w/o Airbag Cut Off Switch)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the side panel.



19. REMOVE INSTRUMENT SIDE PANEL RH (w/ Airbag Cut Off Switch)



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws.

  1. Remove the side panel and disconnect the connector.



20. REMOVE FRONT DOOR SCUFF PLATE RH


21. REMOVE NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY (w/ Floor Under Cover)



  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the under cover.



22. REMOVE COWL SIDE TRIM BOARD RH



  1. Remove the cap nut.

  1. Detach the 2 clips and remove the trim board.



23. REMOVE FRONT PASSENGER SIDE KNEE AIRBAG ASSEMBLY (w/ Passenger Side Knee Airbag)


24. REMOVE LOWER INSTRUMENT PANEL (w/o Passenger Side Knee Airbag)



  1. Remove the 2 bolts.

  1. Detach the 4 claws and remove the instrument panel.



25. REMOVE NO. 3 INSTRUMENT CLUSTER FINISH PANEL GARNISH



  1. Place protective tape as shown in the illustration.

  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 6 claws and remove the panel garnish.



26. REMOVE INSTRUMENT PANEL BOX DOOR KNOB



  1. Using a moulding remover, detach the 2 claws and remove the knob.



27. REMOVE LOWER NO. 2 INSTRUMENT PANEL FINISH PANEL



  1. Remove the 4 bolts.

  1. Detach the 3 claws.

  1. Remove the finish panel and then disconnect the connector.



28. REMOVE STEERING CONTROL ECU WITH JUNCTION BLOCK (for LHD)



  1. Vehicle rear side:

    1. Disconnect the 7 connectors.

    2. Detach the 2 clamps.

    3. Remove the bolt and 2 nuts.

  1. Vehicle front side:

    1. Disconnect the 5 connectors.

  1. Remove the steering control ECU with junction block from the vehicle.



29. REMOVE STEERING CONTROL ECU WITH JUNCTION BLOCK (for RHD)



  1. Vehicle rear side:

    1. Disconnect the 2 connectors.

    2. Remove the bolt and 2 nuts.

  1. Vehicle front side:

    1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the steering control ECU with junction block from the vehicle.



30. REMOVE ACCELERATION SENSOR (for Driver Side)



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the bolt and sensor.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.



31. REMOVE ACCELERATION SENSOR (for Front Passenger Side)



  1. Disconnect the connector.

  1. Remove the bolt and sensor.

    HINT:
    Use the same procedures for LHD and RHD vehicles.
