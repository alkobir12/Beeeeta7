FRONT STABILIZER BAR (w/o KDSS) > REMOVAL

for Preparation Click here



1. REMOVE FRONT WHEEL


2. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the 3 bolts and screw.

  1. Turn the clip indicated by the arrow in the illustration to remove the fender splash shield.



3. REMOVE FRONT FENDER SPLASH SHIELD SUB-ASSEMBLY RH

HINT:
Use the same procedures described for the LH side.



4. REMOVE NO. 1 ENGINE UNDER COVER SUB-ASSEMBLY



  1. Remove the 10 bolts and No. 1 engine under cover.



5. LOOSEN NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BRACKET LH



  1. Loosen the 2 bolts of the front stabilizer brackets.



6. LOOSEN NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BRACKET RH



  1. Loosen the 2 bolts of the front stabilizer brackets.



7. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY LH



  1. Remove the bolt, nut and stabilizer link.

    HINT:
    If the ball joint turns together with the nut, use a 6 mm hexagon wrench to hold the stud.



8. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER LINK ASSEMBLY RH



  1. Remove the bolt, nut and stabilizer link.

    HINT:
    If the ball joint turns together with the nut, use a 6 mm hexagon wrench to hold the stud.



9. REMOVE NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BRACKET LH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and stabilizer bracket from the front frame assembly.



10. REMOVE NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BRACKET RH



  1. Remove the 2 bolts and stabilizer bracket from the front frame assembly.



11. REMOVE FRONT STABILIZER BAR

  1. Remove the front stabilizer bar from the frame assembly.



12. REMOVE NO. 1 FRONT STABILIZER BAR BUSH

  1. Remove the front stabilizer bar bush from the front stabilizer bar.
