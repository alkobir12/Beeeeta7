LUMBAR SWITCH (for Manual Seat) > INSPECTION

for Preparation Click here



1. INSPECT LUMBAR SWITCH LH (for LHD)



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    3 (B) - 4 (H)
    		Hold
    		Below 1 Ω
    1 (R) - 2 (E2)
    1 (R) - 2 (E2)
    		Off
    4 (H) - 5 (E2)
    1 (R) - 3 (B)
    		Release
    4 (H) - 5 (E2)

    If the result is not as specified, replace the lumbar switch.



2. INSPECT LUMBAR SWITCH RH (for RHD)



  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Connection
    		Switch Condition
    		Specified Condition
    3 (B) - 4 (H)
    		Hold
    		Below 1 Ω
    1 (R) - 2 (E)
    1 (R) - 2 (E)
    		Off
    4 (H) - 5 (E)
    1 (R) - 3 (B)
    		Release
    4 (H) - 5 (E)

    If the result is not as specified, replace the lumbar switch.
