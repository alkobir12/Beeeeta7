1. INSPECT NAVIGATION ANTENNA ASSEMBLY



  1. Disconnect the navigation antenna connector.

  1. Measure the resistance according to the value(s) in the table below.

    Standard Resistance:
    Tester Condition
    		Condition
    		Specified Condition
    1 - Shield
    		Always
    		50 to 500 Ω

    If the result is not as specified, replace the navigation antenna.
